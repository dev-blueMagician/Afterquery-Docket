Before I offer a Docket sketch or question, I run the removal test as delete-and-guess for every ticked document and write the result in the sketch: with that document gone, what would a solver answer from the other documents, and from what it already knows of a well-reported law? If that answer is the gold, the document is not needed, however many reasoning steps the design has. I redo the test every time I narrow or reword the ask.

**Why:** aq-03736, 2026-10-13. The user had asked for Medium or Hard on a bundle holding a Commission proposal and the adopted EU directive. q02 asked which guidance the adopted directive requires of the Commission where the proposal's articles only allowed it (two items). The user ran it and sent five screenshots with no words: Low 2/5, gate failed, "A marked document may not be needed. Your answer can still be reached without document 3." I had narrowed the ask from five discretionary steps to guidance to avoid one arguable item; after the narrowing the gold was simply both optional guidance items in the proposal, so "guidance became a duty" answers it with the adopted text deleted. I had written a necessity paragraph for the broad form and not redone it, and my new step audit (count the steps the question does not point at) said nothing about necessity. One difficulty run was spent finding what the workspace's own removal test would have shown. The same day, on q01, the solver with no documents recalled the directive's entry into force, phase-in dates and thresholds exactly, which made two more sketches unsafe. Mine, not the user's words: the screenshots carried no request; I am reading them as "this should not have reached a run".

**How to apply:** in `D:\Projects\Projects\AfterQuery-Docket\10.8` the test is now in WORKFLOW section 4, `.claude/commands/docket-new.md` step 5 and the sketch template's "Delete-and-guess" line. Two shapes to distrust at once: a gold equal to the whole set one document lists, with the other document only confirming it; and any input that is a headline figure or date of a public law. Do not adopt the page's "What to change" items for this failure (ask for article numbers, put citations in the gold): they are untested wordings and break the short-answer rule. Do not use the one-line reviewer note either; a reviewer returned aq-02501 on it. A closed-book answer that lands within a few percent of the gold on any earlier run counts as reached: change the asked form before the next run instead of writing the risk down. aq-03570 q03, 2026-10-13: Run 3's closed-book attempt gave 1.70 against a gold of 1.65 (a difference of two public figures); I recorded it as a risk at two hand-overs and kept the gold, and Run 4 was spent on "Answerable without the documents" (recalled 3.41 − 1.76 = 1.65, neither figure the documents'). A difference cancels a shared recall error; a ratio or percentage at one decimal does not. Mine, not the user's words: the user's message was only "check arrived verdicts ... and fix".
