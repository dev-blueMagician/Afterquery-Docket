# WORKFLOW section 6 repair order (frozen 2026-10-14)

Moved verbatim from WORKFLOW.md section 6 when the objective became Hard-only. These rules were measured
against the Low and Medium solvers (2026-10-10 to 2026-10-13). They are history: the current rules are in
[hard-playbook.md](../reference/hard-playbook.md). Use them only if a bundle has an Easy or Medium tier open.

Repair order for a gold that has to be derived (measured on aq-03120, seven versions of one hook):

1. Read the strongest solver's every-document attempts before anything else. If they take the same
   reading as the weaker solvers' misses, the wording is open, and the counts measured the wording.
   Replace the open term with a description that has one reading. Change nothing else.
2. Do not add a cue to calculate. A percentage form, "on reported figures" and "stated or implied"
   were each quick-test too easy; the same question with one reading and no cue measured Medium.
   The step that holds the easier solver is a subtraction the wording does not ask for.
3. To move a fair design from Low 1/5 to Low 0/5, lengthen the step that places the period, not the
   arithmetic: name the period by an event dated after it ("that last ended before ..."), with the
   date in a footnote. A plain event inside the period was placed four times in five.
4. Keep the answer in the form of the printed look-alikes (a dollar amount beside printed dollar
   amounts). The form's "Answer matches a stated figure" advisory then warns; it is advisory.
5. A list or base with one member outside a complete-looking table follows the same rule (measured on
   aq-02933, six versions of one hook): before the first run, name the universe the member sits in
   ("whose 2019 total appears in the notice"). Cue words of different strength (none, "every",
   "wherever", "only in text") gave Too hard, a form block, Between levels and Easy; the structural
   scope gave Medium. Screen it in the form's Checks before a run. Measured again on aq-03169 q02 (one
   list member stated only in a response's prose, the rest in its annex table): "say anywhere in its
   response" Between levels, the scope dropped on the platform's suggested wording "Answer not
   supported" (the high model stayed in the table), "does <respondent>'s response say" Hard. When a cue
   word is removed, put the scope back as the name of the document; the floor WARNs `cue` on
   "anywhere" and "wherever".
6. A posed case run through stated rules (measured on aq-03736 q01: "In band — Easy tier", Low 1/5): before
   the first run, list the steps and mark each one that a word in the question points at (a size figure, a
   date, a status, a financial year). The easier solver cleared every such step, a two-threshold trap
   included. Count only the steps nothing points at: that design had one, an exception closing a long
   sentence, which held four of five low attempts and no more. A second such step must sit in a different
   passage; a step added inside the same sentence makes solvers read it to its end (as on aq-03143). With
   one unpointed step, quick-test other hooks before spending a difficulty run on it.
7. "Between levels" names two solvers; read which, and repair toward the open tier, not toward the page's
   heading (measured on aq-03707, two hooks). "Too hard for the low model but too easy for the medium model"
   (q02, Medium 4/7): the one unpointed step was a figure a persistent re-read of its document finds; with no
   sound second step in another passage the hook was dropped. "Too hard for the medium model but too easy for
   the high model" (q03 rev 0, Medium 1/7 · High 3/3): the page says "Make it a little harder", which aims at
   Hard; with Medium open the repair went one notch the other way. The membership test was restated in the
   documents' own kind of term, "a submission" became "any submission", and no clause was added (the Coach's
   "(however phrased)" was not used): rev 1 measured "In band — Medium tier", Medium 2/6. One run each, and
   the right attempts' text was not recorded, so treat it as the order to try, not as a cause. The changed
   sentence goes back to the user as a sketch for a quick test before any run.
