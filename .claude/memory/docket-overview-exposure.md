## Current rule

At sketch time, read the capture's "## Bundle overview" summaries for the documents a sketch
relies on. The page marks them "for navigation only, not evidence". If the summaries alone
yield the answer, the question is exposed: for example, both conditions of a filter in exactly
the qualifying summaries, a named survivor, or the needed figure. Prefer a sketch whose
deciding fact the summaries leave out or blur: an unnamed party, a state-versus-federal
distinction, the scope of a motion, a buried list item. Record any exposure in the question's
`## Lever checks` and say so at hand-over.

Exposure breaks no rule; it only lowers difficulty. Use it as a tie-breaker between otherwise
equal fair sketches, not as a reason to drop one. Whether the platform's screener or reviewer
sees the overview is unknown.

**Why:** in aq-docket-trial-05, local review found that q02's whole filter ("with prejudice"
plus "lacked jurisdiction") appeared in the overview summaries of exactly the two qualifying
rulings, Doc 2 and Doc 4, and in no others. q01's deciding facts were not in the overview: who
moved against every claim, Edwards's name, and the federal-law test. Doc 10's summary even
repeated q01's decoy line. So q01 became the primary hand-over.

**How to apply:** for each sketch, search the capture's overview block for the question's key
terms and each answer item before quick-testing. Note hits in `notes/ideas.md`.
See also [[docket-local-solver-calibration]].
