## Question

Which of the short-term discount types Woolworths lists for Countdown does Consumer NZ also say Countdown offers?

## Answer

Specials, Club Prices and Multibuys

## Evidence

- D09 (answer to question 45, L1411): "In addition, we market “Club Prices”, which are short term promotions/savings offered only to Onecard members"
- D09 (answer to question 45, L1409): "Within our standard shelf prices, we will specifically identify “Great Price” items"
- D01 (paragraph 61, L199): "Countdown has “Specials”, “Great Price” (a long-term everyday price) and deals available only for Onecard members."
- D01 (paragraph 61, L205): "In addition, all stores offer multibuys."
