## Question

Using the store counts Woolworths gives, how many supermarkets trade under banners that any retailer's submission says have no loyalty programme?

## Answer

376 supermarkets

## Evidence

- D09 (executive summary, L31): "56 PAK’nSAVE, 138 New World, and 245 Four Square individually owner-operated supermarkets"
- D09 (executive summary, L20): "We are also the franchisor for 71 SuperValue and FreshChoice supermarkets"
- D09 (other supermarkets, L284): "Raeward Fresh (with four retail stores across Christchurch, Nelson, and Queenstown)"
- D09 (answer on loyalty programmes, L1435): "Four Square banners do not offer loyalty programmes, nor does Chemist Warehouse"
- D09 (answer on loyalty programmes, L1437): "There are not any banner-wide loyalty programmes at SuperValue stores"
- D04 (answer on loyalty programmes, L558): "Four Square and Raeward Fresh also do not operate loyalty schemes."
- D04 (answer on loyalty programmes, L559): "It is our understanding that FreshChoice and SuperValue do not operate loyalty schemes."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. Five supermarket banners are said to run no loyalty programme: PAK'nSAVE and Four Square (Doc 7
and Doc 3), SuperValue (Doc 7 and Doc 3), FreshChoice and Raeward Fresh (Doc 3 only). Doc 7 holds every store count:
56, 245, one figure of 71 for SuperValue and FreshChoice together, and four for Raeward Fresh in a list of small
rivals 33,000 characters after the Foodstuffs counts. 56 + 245 + 71 + 4 = 376.

Ambiguity: 392 adds Chemist Warehouse's 16 stores -> fails "supermarkets" (Doc 7 L292 lists it under "Health and
beauty retailers and pharmacies", L530 "discount chemist"; no document calls it a supermarket). 372 leaves out
Raeward Fresh -> fails "any retailer's submission" (Doc 3 L558) and "the store counts Woolworths gives" (Doc 7 L284,
under "Other supermarkets"). 305 or 301 leaves out the 71 -> Doc 3 L559 covers both franchise banners, so the whole
figure counts. PAK'nSAVE's fuel dockets (Doc 7 fn 140) -> Doc 7, Doc 2 and Doc 3 all still say PAK'nSAVE has no
loyalty programme. Tai Ping, Moore Wilson's, the two Metros: no submission says anything about a programme, so
they are outside "says have no". Countdown, New World, Farro Fresh, Huckleberry: said to have one.

Necessity: Doc 7 -> every store count (56, 245, 71, four); no other document gives any of them (searched "56",
"245", "71", "Raeward", "SuperValue", "FreshChoice", "Four Square" in all nine) -> without it no total. Doc 3 ->
the status of Raeward Fresh and FreshChoice; Doc 7 speaks only for SuperValue among its franchise banners and
never mentions Raeward Fresh's programme; Doc 2 covers only "the other FSNI brands" -> without Doc 3 the total
stops at 301, or 372 on an assumption about FreshChoice, never 376.

After clarification: "any retailer's submission" states the counting rule (a rival's statement counts) without
naming a banner, a document or a figure. Two documents still needed.

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Scatter the pieces; Bury one list item late (Raeward Fresh); Match, then
exclude (Chemist Warehouse fails "supermarket"). Wrong-route totals: 301, 305, 372, 392; none equals 376.

Source risks: both retailers hedge their statements about rivals' banners ("To the best of our knowledge", "It is
our understanding"); the question asks what the submissions say. Doc 7's SuperValue sentence ends in a redaction.

Fresh) -> 56 + 245 = 301; 301 + 71 = 372; 372 + 4 = 376 supermarkets. Whole stores, no rounding. Checked by script
