## Question

Calculated the way Iridium defines ARPU, by how much did its commercial IoT data ARPU for the second half of 2025 exceed its ARPU for the first half of 2025? Answer in dollars to the nearest cent.

## Answer

$0.14

## Evidence

- D06 (Summary Revenue and OEBITDA Highlights, three and nine months ended September 30, 2025): "IoT data(2) 46,707 43,695 7% 135,303 124,759 8%"
- D06 (Subscriber Highlights, as of September 30): "IoT data 1,991 1,902 5%"
- D06 (Net Billable Subscriber Additions, three and nine months): "IoT data 67 65 3% 104 193 -46%"
- D07 (Summary Revenue and OEBITDA Highlights, year ended December 31, 2025): "IoT data(2) 46,077 41,407 11% 181,380 166,166 9%"
- D07 (Subscriber Highlights, as of December 31): "IoT data 1,998 1,887 6%"
- D07 (footnote 4): "ARPU is calculated by dividing revenue in the respective period by the average of the number of billable subscribers at the beginning of the period and the number of billable subscribers at the end of the period and then dividing the result by the number of months in the period."

Steps:

1. Fix the two half-years and their subscriber endpoints: December 31, 2024 1,887 (D07 prior-year column, or D06 1,991 - 104); June 30, 2025 = September 30 1,991 - Q3 net additions 67 = 1,924 (D06); December 31, 2025 1,998 (D07).
2. Revenue: first half = nine months 135,303 - Q3 46,707 = 88,596 (D06); second half = Q3 46,707 + Q4 46,077 (D07; or full year 181,380 - nine months) = 92,784 (thousands).
3. Apply the footnote to each half: 88,596 / ((1,887 + 1,924) / 2) / 6 = 7.749; 92,784 / ((1,924 + 1,998) / 2) / 6 = 7.886; difference 0.137, $0.14.

Decoy: $0.04 (averaging the quarterly ARPUs: (7.95 + 7.70) / 2 - (7.75 + 7.83) / 2)

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h3. Iridium prints quarterly, nine-month and full-year ARPU but never a half-year one; the footnote averages only the beginning and end counts of the period, so a half-year ARPU is not the mean of its two quarterly ARPUs.
Hard design (playbook section 4): L1: the printed quarterly ARPUs invite averaging; the footnote's two-point average settles it, and the printed nine-month and full-year ARPUs confirm the method (9M 135,303 / 1,939 / 9 = 7.753; FY 181,380 / 1,942.5 / 12 = 7.781). L2: no release reports a half-year; the June 30 count is printed nowhere (D06 prose gives June 30 totals for all subscribers, commercial and government only) and must come from September 30 less Q3 additions. "ARPU", "IoT" and "half" do not lead to the settling rows; the footnote sits in all three releases.
