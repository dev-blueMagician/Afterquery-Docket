## Question

Which BHP-operated assets had production for the year ended 30 June 2026 outside the FY26 production guidance range that applied on the day BHP completed its divestment of Carajás? List them in any order, or answer none.

## Answer

Pampa Norte (Spence), NSW Energy Coal

## Evidence

- D01 (Carajás, L123): "The divestment of Carajás to a subsidiary of CoreX Holding was completed on 2 April 2026"
- D01 (Spence, L107): "Production guidance for FY26 has been lowered to between 210 and 220 kt (from between 230 and 250 kt previously)."
- D01 (NSWEC, L138): "Production guidance for FY26 remains unchanged at between 14 and 16 Mt"
- D01 (L480): "Our non-operated assets include Antamina, Resolution, Samarco and Vicuña."
- D02 (Pampa Norte, L153): "Pampa Norte 213 kt"
- D02 (NSWEC, L187): "NSWEC exceeding the top end of production guidance in FY26"
