## Question

Freeport-McMoRan raised its ownership interest in Cerro Verde during 2026. South America copper sales in the first six months of 2026 equalled what percentage of the full-year 2026 South America copper sales projection in force when that increase happened? Answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

47.0%

## Evidence

- D04 (NCI section, L921): "In May 2026, FCX increased its ownership interest in Cerro Verde from 55.08% to 55.66%."
- D03 (South America, L227): "Copper sales from South America operations are expected to approximate 1.05 billion pounds for the year 2026."
- D03 (South America, L227): "FCX’s consolidated copper sales volumes from South America operations of 248 million pounds in first-quarter 2026"
- D04 (South America, L221): "FCX’s consolidated copper sales volumes from South America operations of 245 million pounds in second-quarter 2026"
