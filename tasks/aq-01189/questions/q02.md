## Question

Which BHP-operated assets had production for the year ended 30 June 2026 outside the FY26 production guidance range that applied on the day BHP completed its divestment of Carajás? List them in any order, or answer none.

## Answer

Pampa Norte (Spence), NSW Energy Coal

## Components

- c1: Pampa Norte (Spence)
- c2: NSW Energy Coal

## Reasoning

1. Doc 3 is BHP's operational review dated "22 April 2026". It records that "The divestment of Carajás to a subsidiary of CoreX Holding was completed on 2 April 2026". The guidance changes announced in this review came after that day, so the ranges that applied on 2 April are the ranges as they stood before the review.
2. Doc 3 lists the assets BHP does not operate: "Our non-operated assets include Antamina, Resolution, Samarco and Vicuña." The BHP-operated assets with FY26 production guidance are therefore Escondida, Pampa Norte (Spence), Copper South Australia, WAIO, BMA and NSWEC; Carajás had no guidance.
3. Doc 3 gives those ranges: Escondida between 1,200 and 1,275 kt; Spence "lowered to between 210 and 220 kt (from between 230 and 250 kt previously)", so 230 to 250 kt applied on 2 April; Copper South Australia between 310 and 340 kt; WAIO between 251 and 262 Mt (284 and 296 Mt, 100% basis); BMA between 18 and 20 Mt (36 and 40 Mt, 100% basis); NSWEC "between 14 and 16 Mt".
4. Doc 4 is BHP's review for the year ended 30 June 2026, dated "16 July 2026". FY26 production: Escondida 1,261.2 kt; Pampa Norte (Spence) 212.6 kt; Copper South Australia 320.7 kt; WAIO 256.9 Mt (291.2 Mt, 100% basis); BMA 18.6 Mt (37.3 Mt, 100% basis); NSWEC 16.4 Mt, with "NSWEC exceeding the top end of production guidance in FY26".
5. Comparing the two: Pampa Norte (Spence) at 212.6 kt is below 230 kt and NSWEC at 16.4 Mt is above 16 Mt. Escondida, Copper South Australia, WAIO and BMA are inside their ranges on both bases.
6. Decoy, Doc 4: its FY26e guidance column repeats the updated Spence range of 210 to 220 kt, which puts Spence inside and leaves only NSWEC. Skipping the exclusion would also add the non-operated Antamina and Samarco.
