## Question

In the first six months of 2026, Freeport-McMoRan's South America operations sold what percentage of the full-year 2026 copper sales volume the company was projecting for them at the time it raised its ownership interest in Cerro Verde, to one decimal place?

## Answer

47.0%

## Components

- c1: 47.0%

## Reasoning

1. Doc 2 is Freeport's release dated "July 23, 2026". It reports the stake increase: "In May 2026, FCX increased its ownership interest in Cerro Verde from 55.08% to 55.66%." Its own projection, "Copper sales from South America operations are expected to approximate 1.0 billion pounds for the year 2026.", was issued on July 23, 2026, after the increase.
2. Doc 1 is the release dated "April 23, 2026". Its projection was the current one until the July release, so it was in force when the stake was raised: "Copper sales from South America operations are expected to approximate 1.05 billion pounds for the year 2026."
3. Doc 1: South America sold "248 million pounds in first-quarter 2026".
4. Doc 2: South America sold "245 million pounds in second-quarter 2026".
5. Doc 1 and Doc 2: first-half South America copper sales were 248 + 245 = 493 million pounds.
6. Doc 1 and Doc 2: 493 million / 1.05 billion = 47.0% (to one decimal place).
7. Decoy, Doc 2: the July projection gives 493 million / 1.0 billion = 49.3%, but that projection did not exist in May 2026.
