## Question

EOG declared a $1.02-per-share dividend alongside its agreement to buy Encino. In the calendar quarter after the one in which that dividend was payable, how much did the change in production volumes alone move EOG's diluted earnings per share from the prior quarter, according to EOG? Give the sign.

## Answer

-$0.11 per diluted share

## Evidence

- D03 (line 47): "today declared a dividend of $1.02 per share"
- D03 (line 47): "The dividend will be payable October 31, 2025"
- D06 (line 479, per-share walk, Volumes step): "Less: 4Q 2025 Crude Oil Equivalent Volumes (MMBoe)"
- D06 (line 487, Volumes step result): "Change in Diluted Earnings per Share (0.11)"
