## Question

Over the four calendar quarters following the quarter in which EOG declared the dividend payable to stockholders of record on October 17, 2025, how much did EOG declare in dividends per share?

## Answer

$3.06

## Evidence

- D03 (line 13, release date): "May 30, 2025"
- D03 (line 47, dividend declaration): "The Board of Directors today declared a dividend of $1.02 per share on EOG’s common stock."
- D03 (line 47, payable and record dates): "The dividend will be payable October 31, 2025, to stockholders of record as of October 17, 2025."
- D07 (line 85, income statements, 1Q 2025-2Q 2026 and 2026 year to date): "Dividends Declared per Common Share | 0.9750 | 1.9950 | — | 1.0200 | 3.9900 | 1.0200 | 1.0200 | 2.0400"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: i10 family (event-document forward value as decoy, the aq-00964 shape), re-anchored
on a fact only D03 states. The dividend with the October 17, 2025 record date was declared "today"
in the May 30, 2025 release, so in 2Q 2025; D03 adds "The indicated annual rate is $4.08." beside
it. The four following quarters are 3Q 2025-2Q 2026. D07's declared-per-share row prints no
declaration ("—") for 3Q 2025 (the 4Q-payable dividend was declared early, in 2Q 2025, which is why
2Q 2025 shows 1.9950 = 0.975 + 1.02) and 1.0200 for 4Q 2025, 1Q 2026 and 2Q 2026: 3.06.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: a same-document forward value as decoy (indicated annual
rate $4.08 = 4 x 1.02, printed beside the anchor); temporal alignment (declaration quarter vs
record/payable quarter); aggregation over a window containing a quarter with no declaration.
Wrong routes: $4.08 (indicated rate, or 3Q 2025 misread as 1.02); $4.035 (window taken as 2Q
2025-1Q 2026, counting the declaration quarter); $3.99 (calendar 2025 total, the FY column); $5.055
(2Q 2025 through 2Q 2026 inclusive); anchoring on the record/payable quarter (4Q 2025) gives 1Q
2026-4Q 2026, which no document covers.
Necessity: D03 alone dates the declaration and states the record date (no series edition prints
record dates, payable dates, "$4.08" or "indicated"); D07 alone prints 2Q 2026 (D06 ends at 1Q
2026). Source risks: (1) without D03, a strong model could infer 2Q 2025 from 2Q 2025's 1.9950
and the Jun 30, 2025 dividends payable (1,081, two unpaid declarations); (2) without D07, a model
could assume 2Q 2026 = 1.02 and reach 3.06 (removal-test risk; the form's one-line reviewer note
is the fallback). Closed-book: the May 2025 raise to $1.02 is public, but the skipped 3Q 2025
declaration is not, so recall should land on 4.08. The task page overview lists the October 17,
2025 date for Doc 3 but no total.
Author calculation check and counting convention: 3Q 2025 "—" = 0; 0 + 1.02 + 1.02 + 1.02 = 3.06;
check: FY 2025 3.99 - 0.975 - 1.995 = 1.02 (3Q + 4Q 2025), plus 2026 year to date 2.04 = 3.06.
