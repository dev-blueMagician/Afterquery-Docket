## Question

In which calendar quarter did EOG's reported crude oil output first include production from Bahrain, and in which did it first include production from the United Arab Emirates? Give the Bahrain quarter first.

## Answer

4Q 2025, then 2Q 2026

## Evidence

- D06 (line 79, Other International crude MBbld: 1Q25, 2Q25, 3Q25, 4Q25, FY25, 1Q26, YTD): "Other International (B) — — — 0.1 — 0.1 0.1"
- D06 (line 125, note B): "Production volumes from Bahrain operations"
- D07 (line 79, same row plus 2Q26 and YTD): "Other International (B) — — — 0.1 — 0.1 0.5 0.3"
- D07 (line 123, note B): "Crude oil and condensate volumes are from UAE and Bahrain operations"
- D07 (line 923, endnote 1): "2025 volumes are from Bahrain operations"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. Classification inferred across two editions. D06 attributes all of its
Other International volumes, including 1Q 2026, to Bahrain; D07 is the only source naming the
UAE, with a row-wide note and a 2025-only endnote. Together they place the first UAE crude in
2Q 2026; D07 alone leaves 1Q 2026 or 2Q 2026 open.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: same series, right edition (D07's row-wide note vs D06's
1Q26 note); mentions it vs does it (D05 names Bahrain in 3Q25 for gas only, no crude row).
Wrong routes: D07-only reading of note B as covering 2026 gives "4Q 2025, 1Q 2026"; treating
Bahrain's first appearance (3Q25 gas) as crude gives "3Q 2025, 2Q 2026".
Source risks: hidden ranges in D06/D07 next to these rows are numeric volume tables (natural gas
and BOE volumes); no country prose expected there. "—" also prints for FY25 although 4Q25 is 0.1,
so "—" means nothing reported at one decimal, not provably zero; D05 has no Other International
crude row at all for 3Q25. The latest-edition heuristic (UAE named first in the 2Q26 edition)
reaches the UAE part without D06.
