## Question

By what percentage did EOG's total debt grow from the end of 2019 to June 30, 2026? Round to one decimal place.

## Answer

53.2%

## Evidence

- D01 (line 165, net debt reconciliation): "Current and Long-Term Debt (GAAP) - (b)"
- D01 (line 41, prose decoy): "total debt outstanding was $5.2 billion"
- D07 (line 834, net debt table, June 30, 2026 column): "Current and Long-Term Debt (GAAP) - (b) | 7,926"
