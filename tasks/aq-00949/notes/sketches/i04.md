## Question

EOG's March 2020 plan update named the cash measure it expected to fund both its capital spending and its dividend payments. Using that measure and the plan's definition of capital spending, in which calendar quarter from 1Q 2025 through 2Q 2026 did the measure fall short of the two combined?

## Answer

2Q 2025

## Evidence

- D01 (line 35): "Net cash from operating activities is expected to fund both capital expenditures and dividend payments"
- D01 (line 35): "and excluding acquisitions"
- D07 (line 278, cash flow statement): "Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities | 2,289 | 2,032 | 3,111 | 2,612"
- D07 (line 744, capital expenditure reconciliation): "Total Capital Expenditures (Non-GAAP) (2) (1,484) (1,523) (1,648) (1,639) (6,294) (1,636) (1,587)"
- D07 (line 297, cash flow statement): "Dividends Paid | (538) | (528) | (545) | (550) | (2,161) | (544) | (540)"
