## Question

By how many percentage points did EOG's cash, as a percentage of its total debt, rise from December 31, 2019 to June 30, 2026? Round to one decimal place.

## Answer

22.7 percentage points

## Components

- c1: 22.7 percentage points

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 1 (March 16, 2020): at December 31, 2019, the net debt reconciliation lists current and long-term debt of 5,175 and cash of 2,028 (in millions), which the release text rounds to "$5.2 billion" of total debt and "$2.0 billion" of cash. 2,028 / 5,175 = 39.19%.
Step 2. Doc 7: at June 30, 2026, the same reconciliation lists current and long-term debt of 7,926 and cash of 4,907 (in millions). 4,907 / 7,926 = 61.91%.
Step 3. Doc 1 and Doc 7: 61.91% - 39.19% = 22.7 percentage points (to one decimal place).
