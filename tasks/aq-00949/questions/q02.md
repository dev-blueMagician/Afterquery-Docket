## Question

EOG declared a $1.02-per-share dividend alongside its agreement to buy Encino. In the calendar quarter after the one in which that dividend was payable, how much did the change in production volumes alone move EOG's diluted earnings per share from the prior quarter, according to EOG? Give the signed amount in dollars to the nearest cent.

## Answer

-$0.11 per diluted share

## Components

- c1: -$0.11

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 3 (May 30, 2025): the Board "today declared a dividend of $1.02 per share", and "The dividend will be payable October 31, 2025".
Step 2. October 31, 2025 falls in the fourth quarter of 2025, so the calendar quarter after it is the first quarter of 2026.
Step 3. Doc 6 (first quarter 2026 supplemental data) holds EOG's per-share walk from 4Q 2025 to 1Q 2026. Its Volumes step takes 1Q 2026 volumes of 124.5 MMBoe less 4Q 2025 volumes of 128.7 MMBoe, a subtotal of (4.2), times the 1Q 2026 composite average margin of 18.11 per Boe, giving a change in margin of (76) and (59) after the 22% imputed tax, and prints "Change in Diluted Earnings per Share (0.11)". The adjusted per-share walk in Doc 6 shows the same (0.11).
Step 4. So the change in production volumes moved diluted earnings per share by -$0.11 from 4Q 2025 to 1Q 2026.
Decoy 1. Doc 3's own date, May 30, 2025, is when the dividend was declared, in the second quarter of 2025.
Decoy 2. Doc 5's walk for the quarter after that, 2Q 2025 to 3Q 2025, shows a volume effect of 0.32, which answers the declaration date rather than the payable date.
Decoy 3. Doc 7's walk runs from 1Q 2026 to 2Q 2026 and shows a volume effect of 0.14; there 1Q 2026 is the starting quarter, not the quarter asked about.
