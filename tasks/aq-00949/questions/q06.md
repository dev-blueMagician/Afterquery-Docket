## Question

Using the method of EOG's own net income per share bridges, how much did the change in production volumes from the calendar quarter in which EOG agreed to acquire Encino to the same quarter a year later add to GAAP diluted EPS? Round to the nearest cent.

## Answer

$0.94 per diluted share

## Components

- c1: $0.94 per diluted share

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 3 (May 30, 2025): EOG "today announced a definitive agreement" under which it "will acquire Encino Acquisition Partners", so the agreement fell in the second calendar quarter of 2025.
Step 2. Doc 7: crude oil equivalent volumes were 103.2 MMBoe in 2Q 2025 and 128.3 MMBoe in 2Q 2026, the same quarter a year later: 128.3 - 103.2 = 25.1 MMBoe.
Step 3. Doc 7: EOG's net income per share bridge multiplies the volume change by the current quarter's "Composite Average Margin per Boe (GAAP) (Including Total Exploration Costs)", which is 25.51 for 2Q 2026: 25.1 x 25.51 = 640 ($ millions, the change in margin).
Step 4. Doc 7: the bridge then deducts income tax "Imputed (based on 22%)": 640 x 22% = 141.
Step 5. Doc 7: 640 - 141 = 499 ($ millions, the change in net income).
Step 6. Doc 7: the bridge divides by the quarter's diluted shares, 529 million for 2Q 2026: 499 / 529 = $0.94 per diluted share (to the nearest cent).
Decoy. Doc 7's own bridge prints "Change in Diluted Earnings per Share 0.14" for volumes, but it compares 2Q 2026 with 1Q 2026: 128.3 - 124.5 = 3.8 MMBoe.
