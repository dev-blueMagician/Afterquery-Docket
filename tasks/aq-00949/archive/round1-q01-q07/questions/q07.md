## Question

Over the four calendar quarters following the quarter in which EOG declared the dividend payable to stockholders of record on October 17, 2025, how much did EOG declare in dividends per share? Answer in dollars to the nearest cent.

## Answer

$3.06 per share

## Reasoning

1. ### Failure mode 1
Trap: Look-alike measure
How a careless solver fails: Takes the wrong line item
Where the trap sits: Doc 3, EOG's release dated May 30, 2025, is the only document that identifies the
dividend by its October 17, 2025 record date. Its headline says EOG "Increases Regular Dividend 5%", and
the bullet that begins "Supports return of capital to shareholders with 5% dividend increase" goes on:
"The Board of Directors today declared a dividend of $1.02 per share on EOG’s common stock. The dividend
will be payable October 31, 2025, to stockholders of record as of October 17, 2025. The indicated annual
rate is $4.08." The $4.08 is a dividend figure for a whole year, printed in the sentence right after the
record date the question names, so it looks like the four-quarter total the question asks for. It is a
rate, exactly four times the $1.02 dividend, not a total of the dividends the Board went on to declare.
The wrong answer a careless solver gives: $4.08. The solver searches for the October 17, 2025 record
date, lands on Doc 3's dividend sentences and reads "The indicated annual rate is $4.08" as the dividends
EOG declared over the next four quarters. Because the figure is per share, covers a year and sits beside
the named dividend, the solver answers $4.08 without opening Doc 7's row of dividends declared in each
quarter.
Why the correct answer avoids it: The question asks what EOG declared, so a careful reader dates the
declaration and then reads the declarations themselves. Doc 3 is dated May 30, 2025 and says the Board
"today declared" the dividend, so it was declared in the second quarter of 2025, before the window
opens. The four following
quarters are the third and fourth quarters of 2025 and the first and second quarters of 2026. In Doc 7,
the Second Quarter 2026 supplemental data, the Income Statements row "Dividends Declared per Common
Share" shows 1.9950 for the 2nd Qtr of 2025 (0.9750 plus this $1.02), a dash for the 3rd Qtr of 2025,
and 1.0200 for each of the 4th Qtr of 2025 and the 1st and 2nd Qtrs of 2026. The dash is a zero:
0.9750 + 1.9950 + 1.0200 = 3.9900, the row's 2025 total. The answer is 0 + 1.02 + 1.02 + 1.02 = $3.06.
Reading $4.08 as the answer counts four $1.02 declarations in the window, but Doc 7 shows $1.02 in only
three of the four quarters.
