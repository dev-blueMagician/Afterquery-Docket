## Question

By how many percentage points, to one decimal place, does the share of FTSE 350 audit tenders ending in a new auditor in Deloitte's 2011-2017 figures exceed the share in Grant Thornton's latest yearly review?

## Answer

46.4 percentage points

## Evidence

- D02 (appendix 4.2, L234): "74% of the 213 FTSE 350 companies that tendered between 2011 and 2017 resulted in the appointment of a new audit firm"
- D04 (note 4, L155-156): "350 companies that had tendered their audits in the preceding year had then changed auditors"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h5. Two responses give opposite pictures of how often a tender changes the auditor; one prints a
percentage, the other a count that must be turned into one.
Ambiguity: (a) Deloitte's "eight" of 244 tenders (D02 L274) is tenders won by BDO or Grant Thornton, not switches -> fails
"ending in a new auditor". (b) 213 companies or 244 tenders: only the 213 carry the 74%.
Necessity: D02 -> 74%; D04 -> 8 of 29. Neither figure appears elsewhere.
After clarification: both still needed.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Reconcile documents that disagree; State the rounding (8 / 29 = 27.586%, 46.4).
Source risks: two lookups, one division, one subtraction: the family measured too easy. "Only 8 of 29 FTSE" and "350
companies" sit on two lines of the capture, so the evidence quote starts after the line break.
Author calculation: 8 / 29 = 27.59%; 74 - 27.59 = 46.41, 46.4 points.
