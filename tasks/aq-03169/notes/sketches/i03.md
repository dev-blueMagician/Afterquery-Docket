## Question

How many times the most FTSE 350 audits Grant Thornton thinks non-Big-Four firms can realistically take on would they have to audit under BDO's 2023 client limit, and under ICAEW's individual cap?

## Answer

2.2 times and 1.8 times

## Evidence

- D04 (Q19-Q23, para 23.4, L348): "we think it is currently realistic that non-Big 4 auditors could audit c. 40-50 of the FTSE 350 firms"
- D01 (Q15 item 4, L145): "by 2023 no audit firm can act for more than 60 FTSE 350 audit"
- D06 (Q19, para 87, L282): "each Big Four firm could be restricted to auditing 65 of the FTSE 350 companies"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h3. Two per-firm limits from two respondents, each turned into the audits left for everyone else, against a
third respondent's capacity estimate that sits far from its headline target.
Ambiguity: (a) 1.6 and 1.3: Grant Thornton's "10-20% ... within three to four years" (D04 L40, 70 companies) -> fails
"realistically": L40 lists a successful outcome, L348 says what is "currently realistic". (b) 1.4: BDO's 80% collective
ceiling (D01 L221) -> fails "2023 client limit". (c) 2.8: ICAEW's "around 60%" (D06 L408) -> fails "individual cap".
(d) 2.75 and 2.25 from the bottom of the range -> fails "the most".
Necessity: each document gives one number no other states (60, 65, 40-50); remove one and one ratio cannot be formed.
After clarification: all three still needed.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Scatter the pieces; Calculate across documents. Two parallel asks.
Source risks: every step is a plain sentence; the family "two lookups plus one operation" measured too easy. Horizons differ
(2023 against three to four years); the question compares sizes only.
Author calculation: 350 - 4 x 60 = 110; 110 / 50 = 2.2. 350 - 4 x 65 = 90; 90 / 50 = 1.8.
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: quick-test sketch only.
