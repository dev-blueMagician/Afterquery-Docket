## Question

How many FTSE 350 audits would BDO's 2023 client limit allow the Big Four between them, and what share of the FTSE 250 audit tenders Deloitte expects over five years, to the nearest ten percent, would other firms have to win to bring them within it?

## Answer

240 audits and 100%

## Evidence

- D01 (Q15 item 4, L145): "by 2023 no audit firm can act for more than 60 FTSE 350 audit"
- D02 (appendix 20.4, L616): "Firms outside the four largest currently perform 11 audits of the FTSE 350 today"
- D02 (appendix 20.4, L616): "would need to win about 60 tenders, or 60% of the FTSE250 tenders in this period"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2. The number of tenders in Deloitte's five-year window is not printed: 60 wins are 60% of them, so about
100. BDO's limit needs the other firms at 110 audits, 99 more than they hold: essentially every one of those tenders.
Ambiguity: (a) 90%: Deloitte's 60% scaled by 110/70 = 94% -> fails because wins start from the 11 held, not from zero.
(b) 110%: no subtraction of the 11 -> exceeds the tenders that exist. (c) 60%: BDO's "cannot be greater than 80%" (D01 L221)
taken as the limit -> fails "2023 client limit" (60 clients per firm, L145). (d) Base choice: 99.0%, 100.3%, 98.1%, 100.0%,
100.7% on the stated bases and on "about 60" read as 59: 100% at the nearest ten on all.
Necessity: D01 -> the 60-client limit, stated nowhere else. D02 -> the tender window (60 wins = 60%) and the 11 held;
no other document gives a tender count. Without either the share cannot be formed.
After clarification: both still needed; the wording gives no figure.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Calculate across documents; State the rounding (whole percent would split 99 / 100 / 101).
Source risks: same Deloitte paragraph as i01; one of the two at most. A gold of exactly 100% can look like a guess.
Author calculation: 4 x 60 = 240; 350 - 240 = 110; 110 - 11 = 99 wins; tenders 60 / 0.60 = 100; 99 / 100 = 99%, 100% to the
nearest ten.
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: alternate to i01; platform quick test pending. 2026-10-12: first ask added
(240 audits = 4 x 60) after q01 rev 0's lone "7 years" was guessed with no documents; "100%" alone is one of about ten
answers at that precision.
