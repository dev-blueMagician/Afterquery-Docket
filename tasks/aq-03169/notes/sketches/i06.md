## Question

Which of the CMA's potential measures does Deloitte rate High for costs and Low for benefits, although Grant Thornton's measure-by-measure list of costs and benefits puts greater auditor independence among their benefits?

## Answer

Reform of mandatory tendering and auditor rotation, splitting UK firms into audit-only and non-audit practices, and joint audits

## Evidence

- D02 (appendix 14.4 table, L417): "Reform mandatory tendering and auditor rotation"
- D02 (appendix 14.4 table, L418): "4.34 High Low Medium 14.5.9 Q27"
- D02 (appendix 14.4 table, L427): "4.8(c) High Low High 14.5.11 Exec sum and"
- D02 (appendix 14.4 table, L429): "Joint audits 4.18(a) High Low Medium 14.5.12 Q23"

- D04 (Annex 2, row 1, L833): "Promotes auditor independence"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h10 (round 2; found by an independent author, checked line by line by the caller). Deloitte's table rates
seventeen measures on costs, benefits and implementation challenges; seven are High/Low. Grant Thornton's Annex 2 lists
costs and benefits for the same measures; independence is a listed benefit of three of the seven. One of the three sits
under Deloitte's "appear to have merit" heading, and one gets independence only through a cross-reference to another row.
Alternate, more explicit wording (same gold): "... although greater auditor independence is among the benefits Grant
Thornton's costs-and-benefits list gives them, whether stated outright or carried over from another measure?"
Ambiguity: (a) the seven-measure answer: no Grant Thornton condition -> fails "although ... independence among their
benefits". (b) "Split, joint audits, break the link": Deloitte's "Measures that we do not support" heading taken for the
rating -> fails "High for costs": break the link is "4.40 Low Low High" (D02 L433), and tendering reform is "High Low" under
the merit heading (L404, L418). (c) "Tendering reform and joint audits": the split row read without its cross-reference ->
D04 L845 gives the split "All other benefits of 1 above", and measure 1's benefits begin "Promotes auditor independence"
(L833). (d) A fourth member, prohibiting non-audit services to all large clients and PIEs (D02 L424 High Low High):
Grant Thornton's row 2 (L835-840) lists audit quality and competition, no independence and no cross-reference.
(e) Break-up, insurance, national auditor: no independence benefit (L877, L900-903, L904-910). (f) Shared audits: Grant
Thornton's row 5 covers joint and shared, but Deloitte rates shared audits "Medium Medium Medium" (L407). (g) Column
order: header L392-394 gives costs, benefits, implementation challenges; D02 L443 and L471 confirm it in prose.
(h) Grant Thornton's main text on the split points both ways (D04 L28 "partially address conflicts of interest and
independence"; L295 "independence ... may be further eroded"), so the question names the list of costs and benefits.
Necessity: D02 -> the only ratings (rating triples occur only at D02 L398-435); D04 -> the only per-measure benefit list
(ICAEW's Q14 comments, D06 L172-228, credit independence to all three non-audit-service restrictions and give a different
set). Without D02 no measure is High/Low; without D04 nothing removes four of the seven.
After clarification: both documents still needed; the wording names no measure and no rating beyond the condition.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Match, then exclude; Who belongs where. Which-items question: decoys are break the
link (independence in D04, fails D02's rating) and the all-clients prohibition (passes D02's rating, no independence in D04).
Omitting the exclusion gives seven measures instead of three.
Source risks: both tables are flattened row by row; row 3 of Annex 2 runs across a page break (L845, L849-851). Tendering's
benefit is hedged ("Potentially"), but it is listed as a benefit. A no-document guess at the exact three is unlikely but
not impossible (the measures are public); the form's own solvers may miss the cross-reference on the lean wording.
Author calculation: not applicable (membership); seven High/Low rows re-read 2026-10-12 against L391-436 and L828-912.
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: quick-test sketch, both wordings; no write-up before a survivor.
