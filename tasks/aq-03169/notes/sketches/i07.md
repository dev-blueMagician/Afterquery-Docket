## Question

Hermes would accept an actual or a de facto break-up of the Big Four. How does Deloitte rate the costs, benefits and implementation challenges of the de facto kind?

## Answer

Medium costs, High benefits, Low implementation challenges

## Evidence

- D05 (Breaking up audit firms, L67): "de facto being through prohibiting audit firms from providing consulting services to their audit clients"
- D02 (appendix 14.4 table, L397): "Non-audit services ban to audit clients"
- D02 (appendix 14.4 table, L398): "4.8(a) Medium High Low 14.5.1 Exec sum and"
