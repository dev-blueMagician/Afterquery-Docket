## Question

By what percent did the OCC's merger applications per small entity fall from 2022 to 2023, counting small entities as sized on December 31 of each year? Give two decimals.

## Answer

48.03%

## Evidence

- D01 (fn 32, L257): "The OCC received 51 merger applications in 2019, 36 in 2020, 46 in 2021, 36 in 2022, and 18 in 2023."
- D01 (RFA paragraph, L252): "of which approximately 661 are small entities"
- D01 (fn 31, L255): "The OCC used December 31, 2022, to determine size"
- D02 (RFA paragraph, L369): "of which approximately 636 are small entities"
- D02 (fn 50, L373): "The OCC uses December 31, 2023, to determine size"
