## Question

For the 2024-25 financial year, the Australian Government's tax receipts and its non-tax receipts both exceeded the amounts the 2024-25 Budget had forecast. What is the larger of the two percentages by which they exceeded those forecasts, to one decimal place?

## Answer

5.7%

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1, two heads compared, one number asked. Forecast amounts are not printed
anywhere: tax 657.8 - 15.3 = 642.5, non-tax 59.1 - 3.2 = 55.9. Excess over forecast: tax
15.3 / 642.5 = 2.38%, non-tax 3.2 / 55.9 = 5.72%. Gold: the larger, 5.7% (non-tax receipts).
The printed growth rates rank the other way: tax 3.9 vs 0.6 (gap 3.3 points, ratio 3.3%),
non-tax 7.1 vs 4.4 (2.7 points, 2.6%), because each forecast grew from a 2023-24 estimate that
the outcome revised. Asked as one value (not "which ...") so the which-items format advisories
do not apply; the head is implied by the value.
Readings still possible, with the answer each gives and the words that reject it:
- Growth route: 3.3% (tax, the larger growth gap; or 3.3 percentage points). Rejected by
  "exceeded the amounts ... forecast" / "exceeded those forecasts" (amounts) and "percentages";
  Doc 3's dollar gaps contradict it. This is the lazy default and the declared trap.
- Gaps over outcomes: non-tax 3.2/59.1 = 5.4%, tax 2.3% -> 5.4%. Rejected by the usual sense
  of "exceeded those forecasts by x%"; a careless reader can still take it.
- Only one head worked out: 2.4% (tax by levels) if the solver stops after tax. "the larger of
  the two" asks for both.
- Wrong Table 3.1 column (2025-26 block 699.5 / 60.2 -> non-tax 5.6%). Rejected by the block's
  UCB -10.0 / -0.4 and gross debt 928.6 / 33.4 (Table 3.6, "Actual 2024-25").
- Total receipts (18.5 / 698.5 = 2.6%) is not one of the two named heads.
- Rounding: non-tax from printed inputs 5.72 (exact inputs could give 5.63-5.82).
Necessity: D03 -> both forecast gaps (no other document prints a 2024-25 forecast). D01 -> both
2024-25 outcome levels (only Table 3.1).
Delete-and-guess: without D01 -> growth route only: 3.3% (tax's growth gap, wrong head and
value). Without D03 -> no forecasts at all. Closed book: needs four obscure figures -> not
reachable.
