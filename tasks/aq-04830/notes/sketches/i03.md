## Question

Applying the Reserve Bank's October 2024 Bulletin definition of Australia's average maturity mismatch between indexed and nominal government bonds to the bonds on issue at 1 May 2026, by how many days had the mismatch changed from the Bulletin's figure? Give the sign and one decimal place.

## Answer
