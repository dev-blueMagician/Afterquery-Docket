## Question

By what percentage did the Australian Government's non-tax receipts for the 2024-25 financial year exceed the amount the 2024-25 Budget had forecast for them? Give the answer in per cent to one decimal place.

## Answer

5.7%

Hook/mechanism: h1. Doc 3 prints the 2024-25 forecast error for non-tax receipts two ways in one
sentence pair: as growth rates (7.1% outcome vs 4.4% forecast) and as a dollar gap ($3.2b). No
document prints the forecast amount. The percentage by which the outcome exceeded the forecast
needs the forecast amount = outcome level (Doc 1 Table 3.1, 2024-25 Actual: 59.1) minus 3.2 = 55.9.
Gold 3.2 / 55.9 = 5.72% -> 5.7%. The growth figures do not give it: the forecast grew from the
Budget's estimate of 2023-24 (55.9 / 1.044 = 53.5), the outcome from the 2023-24 outcome
(59.1 / 1.071 = 55.2), so 1.071 / 1.044 - 1 = 2.6% is not the gap between the amounts.
