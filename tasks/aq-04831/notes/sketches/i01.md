## Question

Which hazards in the RBA's RMBS climate value-at-risk data, if any, were absent from its earlier bank-mortgage study's data? List alphabetically.

## Answer

Cyclone wind, surface (flash) flooding

## Evidence

- D04 (physical risks section, line 26): "The XDI-Climate Valuation data assess the climate risks to five hazards in Australia: riverine flooding, coastal inundation, forest wildfires, wind storms (other than cyclones), and ground subsidence in drought."
- D01 (Data section, line 94): "These data were sourced from XDI-Climate Valuation and assess the combined VaR from seven hazards in Australia."
- D01 (endnote 5, line 642): "These are coastal inundation, cyclone wind, extreme wind, forest fire, riverine flooding, soil subsidence, and surface (flash) flooding."
