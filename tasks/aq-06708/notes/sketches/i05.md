## Question

If all of Pinterest's 2031 convertible notes were converted with its Class A stock at 150% of the conversion price, the shares Pinterest delivered would equal what percentage of its Class A and Class B shares outstanding at June 30, 2026? Two decimal places.

## Answer

2.59%

## Evidence

- D02 (Note 5): "Each $1,000 principal amount of the Notes is convertible at an initial conversion rate of 44.0063, which is equivalent to a conversion price of approximately $22.72 per share"
- D02 (Note 5, continued after a page break): "aggregate principal amount of the Notes being converted and deliver shares of our Class A common stock for any conversion value in excess of the principal amount."
- D02 (Note 5): "we issued $1.0 billion in aggregate principal amount of 1.75% convertible senior notes due in 2031"
- D03 (balance sheet, stockholders' equity): "490,712 and 584,866 shares issued and outstanding as of June 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025, respectively; Class B common stock, $0.00001 par value, 1,333,333 shares authorized, 74,785 and 79,680 shares issued and outstanding as of June 30, 2026"

## Lever stack

Shape: Hard shape B (compute, then let a rule change the result)

- L1: Calculate across documents | trap: D02 "Upon conversion of any Note, we will pay or deliver, as the case may be, cash and shares of our Class A common stock, if any." | settles: D02 "aggregate principal amount of the Notes being converted and deliver shares of our Class A common stock for any conversion value in excess of the principal amount" | missed gives: 7.78 (all 44.0 million conversion shares delivered)
- L2: Same series, right edition | trap: D02 "As of April 28, 2026, there were 481,750,308 shares of the Registrant" | settles: D03 "490,712 and 584,866 shares issued and outstanding as of June 30, 2026" | missed gives: 2.62 (the 10-Q's April 28 count; its March 31 balance sheet gives 2.56)

Steps:

1. Conversion terms (D02 Note 5): 44.0063 shares per $1,000, conversion price about $22.72, so 150% is about $34.09; on conversion Pinterest pays cash up to the principal and delivers shares only for the conversion value above it.
2. Shares delivered: each $1,000 note converts to 44.0063 x 34.086 = $1,500 of value; the $500 above principal is paid in shares, 500 / 34.086 = 14.669 shares per note; 1,000,000 notes -> 14.67 million shares (14.66 million at $22.72).
3. Base and ratio (D03): June 30, 2026 Class A 490,712K plus Class B 74,785K = 565,497K; 14.669 / 565.497 = 2.594%, so 2.59%.

Decoy: 7.78% (all 44.0 million shares delivered, as "conversions of the Notes into our Class A common stock" suggests)
