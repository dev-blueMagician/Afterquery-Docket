## Question

As each company measures it in its constant-currency revenue figures, how much more did currency movements add to Snap's revenue than to Pinterest's in the first nine months of 2025? Answer in millions of dollars, to the nearest million.

## Answer

$12 million

- D06 (supplemental table, Constant Currency Revenue row): "|Constant Currency Revenue|$1,370,500|$1,334,606|$1,494,999|$1,695,488|$1,500,374|$1,591,052|"
- D04 (Financial Highlights, footnote 1): "revenue for the three months and year ended December 31, 2025 was $1,305.9 million and $4,205.3 million, respectively, due to favorable impacts of changes in foreign exchange rates of $13.5 million and $16.5 million, respectively"

Steps:

1. Fix the window: January-September 2025 for both calendar-year companies; Pinterest prints only Q4 and full-year currency effects, so its nine-month effect is the full year less Q4: 16.467 - 13.447 = $3.020M (stated 16.5 - 13.5 = 3.0).
2. Take Snap's 2025 currency effect quarter by quarter from the later release's backfilled rows (reported less constant-currency revenue): Q1 -7.283, Q2 +10.324, Q3 +11.840 = +$14.881M, keeping Q1's negative sign.
3. Subtract: 14.881 - 3.020 = 11.861, nearest million 12.
