## Question

From the fourth quarter of 2025 to the first quarter of 2026, by how many percentage points did the year-over-year growth of Snap's revenue excluding Other Revenue change? Round to a whole number and give the sign.

## Answer

-2

## Evidence

- D07 (Q4 2025 highlights): "Other Revenue increased 62% year-over-year to $232 million in Q4, with subscribers growing 71% year-over-year to reach 24 million in Q4."
- D07 (financial summary table): "|Revenue|$1,716,461|$1,557,283|10%|$5,931,447|$5,361,398|11%|"
- D05 (Q1 2026 highlights): "We directly monetized the growing engagement on Snapchat, with Other Revenue increasing 87% year-over-year to $285 million."
- D05 (financial summary table): "|Revenue|$1,528,791|$1,363,217|12%|"

## Lever stack

Shape: 2-like reading of a summary against its detail (the company's own account of accelerating growth), with prior-year figures rebuilt from prose

- L1: Summary vs detail | trap: D05 "we returned to growth in daily active users, accelerated revenue growth" | settles: D05 "Other Revenue increasing 87% year-over-year to $285 million" | missed gives: +2 (total revenue growth, 10% in Q4 2025 to 12% in Q1 2026)
- L2: Calculate across documents | trap: D07 "Other Revenue increased 62% year-over-year to $232 million in Q4" | settles: D07 "Revenue $1,716,461 $1,557,283 10%" | missed gives: +3 (each quarter's Other Revenue subtracted from both years' totals, no prior-year Other Revenue rebuilt)

Steps:

1. Q4 2025 (D07): prior-year Other Revenue 232 / 1.62 = 143.2; revenue excluding Other Revenue 1,716.461 - 232 = 1,484.5 against 1,557.283 - 143.2 = 1,414.1, growth +4.98%.
2. Q1 2026 (D05): prior-year Other Revenue 285 / 1.87 = 152.4; 1,528.791 - 285 = 1,243.8 against 1,363.217 - 152.4 = 1,210.8, growth +2.72%.
3. Change: 2.72 - 4.98 = -2.25 points, whole number -2 (all roundings of $232M, 62%, $285M and 87% keep it between -2.35 and -2.16).

Decoy: +2 (total revenue growth accelerated from 10% to 12%, as the CEO's "accelerated revenue growth" says)
