## Question

By how many percentage points did Snap's gross margin rise from the third quarter to the fourth quarter of 2025? Answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

3.8

## Evidence

- D07 (headline): "Fourth quarter gross margin of 59%, up 4 percentage points sequentially and 2 percentage points year-over-year"

- D07 (supplemental table, Q3 2024 to Q4 2025): "|Revenue|$1,372,574|$1,557,283|$1,363,217|$1,344,930|$1,506,839|$1,716,461|"

Steps:

1. Q4 2025 (D07): (1,716,461 - 702,443) / 1,716,461 = 59.08%.
2. Q3 2025 cost of revenue, printed nowhere: full year 2,669,575 - Q4 702,443 - first half 1,292,912 (D06 six-month 2025 column) = 674,220; revenue 1,506,839 (D07 table), so (1,506,839 - 674,220) / 1,506,839 = 55.26%.
3. Rise: 59.08 - 55.26 = 3.82 points, one decimal 3.8.
