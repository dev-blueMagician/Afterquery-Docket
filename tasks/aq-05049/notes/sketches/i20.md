## Question

How much did Paylocity spend buying back its own shares in calendar 2025 before announcing its fiscal 2025 third-quarter results, to the nearest million dollars?

## Answer

$142 million

## Evidence

- D07 (dateline): "SCHAUMBURG, IL. – May 1, 2025 – Paylocity Holding Corporation"
- D07 (dateline): "today announced financial results for the third quarter of fiscal year 2025, which ended March 31, 2025."
- D07 (CEO quotation): "we continue to return capital to shareholders with $150 million or approximately 800,000 shares repurchased through April of this fiscal year"
- D07 (highlights): "Cash flow from operations for the first nine months of fiscal year 2025 was $331.7 million"
- D07 (cash flow, nine months ended March 31; lure): "|Repurchases of common shares|—|(91,080)|"
- D06 (cash flow header): "||Six Months Ended December 31,||"
- D06 (cash flow, six months ended December 31, 2024): "|Repurchases of common shares|—|(8,395)|"
