## Question

By January 31, 2025, how much cash had Docusign paid for share buybacks under its stock repurchase program, to the nearest million dollars?

## Answer

$892 million

## Evidence

- D03 (Note 10, Stock Repurchase Program): "In March 2022, our board of directors authorized a stock repurchase program of up to $200.0 million of our outstanding common stock"
- D03 (Note 10): "Subsequently, in September 2023, our board of directors authorized an increase to our existing stock repurchase program for an additional amount of up to $300.0 million"
- D03 (Note 10): "In May 2024, our board of directors authorized an increase to our existing stock repurchase program for an additional amount of up to $1.0 billion"
- D03 (MD&A, Liquidity): "which commenced in March 2022 and does not have an expiration date"
- D03 (Part II, Item 2 table): "October 1 - October 31|1,014,980|$64.16|1,014,980|$769,641"
- D03 (Part II, footnote 3): "Amounts presented exclude the 1% excise tax as a result of the IRA"
- D04 (cash flow statement): "|Repurchases of common stock|(161,725)|—|(683,528)|(145,515)|"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. Cumulative buyback cash under the program = authorizations ($200.0M + $300.0M + $1.0B = $1,500.0M, D03) less the authorization still unused at Oct 31, 2024 ($769,641K, D03 Part II, excludes excise tax), which is the program's cash use to that date, plus Q4 FY2025 cash repurchases ($161,725K, D04).
Hard design (playbook kind): complete-looking account over an incidental fact (an absence). The releases give a two-year account (D04: "Repurchases of common stock were $683.5 million compared to $145.5 million in the same period last year"; D03 cash flow 9M FY2025 521,803 and 9M FY2024 145,515) that reads as the program's whole history. The deciding step, which no word of the question asks for: the program began in March 2022 (fiscal 2023), so purchases made before February 2023 exist but are printed nowhere (63,041K by subtraction) and come only from the remaining-authorization arithmetic. Default wrong answer: $829 million (145,515 + 683,528).
Ambiguity:
- 829 (FY2024 + FY2025 cash, D04) -> fails "under its stock repurchase program": D03 says the program "commenced in March 2022", and the authorization arithmetic shows 63,041K used before fiscal 2024.
- 894 (adds the 9M FY2025 excise tax accrual of 1,943K) -> fails "cash ... paid": D03 lists "Excise tax payable on net stock repurchase 1,943" under non-cash activities; the Part II amounts exclude excise.
- 730 (through Oct 31, 2024 only) -> fails "By January 31, 2025".
- about 1,100 or 917 (from D01's "As of June 5, 2025 ... up to $1.4 billion" after a further $1.0B) -> fails the date; D01 is a later edition.
- Withholding on net RSU settlement (213,282K in FY2025) -> not "under its stock repurchase program".
- Trade vs settlement timing: Q3 FY2025 cash (172,665K, D02) equals the table's Sep + Oct usage (1,861,255 x $57.78 + 1,014,980 x $64.16 = 172,664K), so cash and authorization use agree in the bundle; nothing shows unsettled trades at Jan 31, 2025.
Necessity:
- D03: sole source of the authorizations, the March 2022 start and the Oct 31, 2024 remaining authorization (no release prints them; D01 gives only a rounded June 5, 2025 figure after a new $1.0B). Without it the program total and the pre-2023 purchases are unreachable.
- D04: sole source of Q4 FY2025 cash repurchases (161,725; also "$161.7 million" in its highlights). D01 and D02 do not print it; the accumulated-deficit roll would need Q4 net income, also only in D04.
Delete-and-guess:
- D03 deleted: solver sums D04's two years, 829, or 683.5 for FY2025 alone. Not the gold.
- D04 deleted: 1,500.0 - 769.6 = 730.4 through Oct 31; Q4 unknown; D01's June 5 route gives about 1,100 - 183.4 = 917 (assuming no May purchases). Not the gold.
