## Question

What was Paylocity's free cash flow excluding interest income on funds held for clients in the fiscal quarter in which it completed its Airbase acquisition, in millions of dollars to one decimal place?

## Answer

$7.6 million

## Evidence

- D05 (headline bullets): "Acquisition of Airbase, a modern finance and spend management software solution, completed on October 1, 2024"
- D05 (dateline): "today announced financial results for the first quarter of fiscal year 2025, which ended September 30, 2024"
