## Question

How much adjusted cash flow from operations before working capital did Eni need to generate in the second half of 2026 to meet the full-year 2026 guidance for that measure that it had in place at the end of June 2026? Answer in billions of euros to two decimal places.

## Answer

€6.45 billion

## Evidence

- D02 (l.802, net borrowings and cash flow): "Adjusted net cash before changes in working capital at replacement cost was €7,347 mln in IH ’26"
- D02 (l.833): "The main inflows comprised the adjusted operating cash flow (€7.35 bln)"
- D02 (l.110, dateline): "San Donato Milanese, July 29, 2026"
- D02 (l.253, Outlook 2026, issued July 29): "adjusted CFFO is expected to amount to €15 bln"
- D01 (l.74, dateline): "San Donato Milanese, April 24, 2026"
