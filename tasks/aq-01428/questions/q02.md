## Reasoning

Step 3. Doc 2 (Eni's results for the first quarter of 2026, dated April 24, 2026) holds the guidance in place from April 24 until that July update: "adjusted CFFO to amount to €13.8 bln". Doc 2 uses the same measure for actual results: "1Q ’26 Group’s adjusted CFFO before working capital movements was €2.88 bln".

Step 4. Doc 2 and Doc 1: 13.8 - 7.35 = 6.45 (€ billion, two decimal places). Using the exact €7,347 mln instead of the rounded €7.35 bln leaves the two-decimal result unchanged.

Decoy. Doc 1's own outlook, issued on July 29, 2026: 15 - 7.35 = 7.65; it was not yet in place at the end of June.
