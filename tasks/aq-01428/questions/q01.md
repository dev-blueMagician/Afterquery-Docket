## Question

During the month in which Eni secured ten years of regasification capacity at Ravenna, what full-year 2026 proforma adjusted EBIT did the guidance then in force expect from its Global Gas & LNG Portfolio business? Give it in € billion to one decimal place.

## Answer

€1.3 billion

## Components

- c1: €1.3 billion

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 1 (Eni's results for the second quarter and first half of 2026, dated July 29, 2026): "In June, Eni booked regasification capacity at the Ravenna terminal equal to 2 bcm/year for a 10-year term." The booking month is June. The outlook in Doc 1 ("Eni is raising guidance for business performance and cash flow generation") was published on July 29, 2026, after June, so it was not yet Eni's guidance during the booking month.

Step 2. Doc 2 (Eni's results for the first quarter of 2026, dated April 24, 2026) holds the latest outlook published before June: "FY’26 GGP adjusted proforma EBIT guided at around €1.3 bln, up 30% from the initial forecast." So the guidance then in force expected €1.3 billion from the Global Gas & LNG Portfolio business, the figure Doc 2 states to one decimal place.

Decoy. Doc 1's own outlook reads "FY’26 GGP adjusted proforma EBIT guidance is raised to over €1.4 bln, representing an increase of 40% compared with the initial level"; that raise was published on July 29, after the booking month, and the initial level it refers to had already been superseded by the April guidance.
