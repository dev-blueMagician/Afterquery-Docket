## Question

In the 2011 wave of Osaka University's Preference Parameters Study, one group of 40-to-64-year-olds defined by sex, university degree or employment status had the highest rate of non-mandatory health check-ups in the prior 12 months. Among those aged 40 or over in that group, what was the mean number of non-breast, non-uterine cancer examinations? Give two decimals.

## Answer

0.71
