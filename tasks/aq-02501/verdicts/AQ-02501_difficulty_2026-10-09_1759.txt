- accepted in lieu of trial by general court‑martial, or
- for mutiny or spying

(those two bars remain absolute and get no “compelling circumstances” exception).

Specifically, the 2024 rule:

1. Removes the old regulatory bar for discharges based on homosexual acts (the former §3.12(d) homosexual‑acts provision is deleted). So OTH, BCD, dishonorable, dismissal, or uncharacterized separations that were previously barred solely for that reason are now potentially eligible.
2. Adds a “compelling circumstances” exception to the remaining conduct‑based bars for moral turpitude and willful and persistent misconduct (§3.12(d)(2) and §3.12(e)). That means a former service member with an OTH, bad‑conduct, dishonorable, dismissal, or uncharacterized discharge that was based on one of those two types of misconduct can now qualify for VA benefits if mitigating/compelling circumstances outweigh the misconduct.
