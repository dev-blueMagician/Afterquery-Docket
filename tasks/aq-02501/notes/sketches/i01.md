## Question

Which separations does DoD's Discharge Appeal Review Board count as less than honorable but VA automatically accept as other than dishonorable?

## Answer

General (under honorable conditions) discharges and entry-level separations

## Evidence

- D05 (32 CFR 73.2 definitions, L428): "A characterization that is less than honorable includes a general under honorable conditions, other than honorable conditions, uncharacterized, bad-conduct discharge, dishonorable discharge, or a dismissal."
- D05 (73.2, characterization definition, L427): "or can be described as uncharacterized (as in an entry-level separation)"
- D03 (Sec. 3.12(a), L383): "A discharge under honorable conditions is binding on the Department of Veterans Affairs as to character of discharge."
- D03 (preamble, L210): "If a SM has an Honorable or General discharge, VA does not conduct a COD determination"
- D03 (Section IV, L277): "An entry level separation is considered under conditions other than dishonorable"
