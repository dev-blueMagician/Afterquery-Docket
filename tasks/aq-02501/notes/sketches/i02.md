## Question

From proposed to final rule publication, how many more days did VA's character-of-discharge rulemaking take than its 85/15 rulemaking?

## Answer

925 days

## Evidence

- D03 (preamble I, L56): "On July 10, 2020, VA published at 85 FR 41471 its proposal to amend its regulation governing COD determinations"
- D03 (header, L3): "Federal Register, Volume 89 Issue 82 (Friday, April 26, 2024)"
- D02 (preamble, L76): "was published in the Federal Register (Vol. 87, No. 196) on October 12, 2022"
- D02 (header, L3): "Federal Register, Volume 89 Issue 10 (Tuesday, January 16, 2024)"
