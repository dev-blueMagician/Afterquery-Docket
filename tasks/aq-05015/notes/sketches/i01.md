## Question

How much of Valero's 2025 cost of materials and other was incurred in the second half of the year?

## Answer

$50,370 million

## Evidence

- D06 (Financial Highlights, Table Page 1, row "Cost of materials and other"; columns Q4 2025, Q4 2024, FY 2025, FY 2024): "Cost of materials and other (a) 24,238 26,409 101,096 110,616"
- D06 (same table, next row): "Taxes other than income taxes (b) 1,740 1,517 6,720 5,900"
- D08 (Financial Highlights, Table Page 1; columns Q2 2026, Q2 2025, six months 2026, six months 2025): "Cost of materials and other (a) 35,130 24,678 61,315 50,726"
- D08 (same table, next row): "Taxes other than income taxes (b) 1,648 1,654 3,369 3,154"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1, a silent reclassification. From its Q4 2025 release (D06) Valero prints
"Taxes other than income taxes" (note (b): "includes excise taxes on sales by certain of our foreign
operations") on its own line, and D06, D07 and D08 restate their comparatives: old cost = new cost +
taxes in every overlap (Q4 2024 27,926 = 26,409 + 1,517; FY 2024 116,516 = 110,616 + 5,900; Q1 2025
27,548 = 26,048 + 1,500; Q2 2025 26,332 = 24,678 + 1,654; H1 2025 53,880 = 50,726 + 3,154). No release
says the line was reclassified (searched reclass/recast/conform/prior period/presentation/excise: only
the three one-line notes). Q3 2025 (D05: 27,958) is never restated, and no document prints H2 2025.

Ambiguity:
- 47,216 = 101,096 - 53,880 (first half as first printed, D04 Table Page 1): the words do not reject it.
  Only the documents do: D04's first half includes the 3,154 of taxes the full-year figure leaves out
  (D08 restates the same half as 50,726 plus 3,154 of taxes). Main trap; the form may score it.
- 52,196 = 27,958 (Q3 as printed, D05) + 24,238: not a portion of 101,096 (with either printed first
  half the halves do not sum to 101,096); "of Valero's 2025 cost" rejects it, via arithmetic.
- 106,076 as "the 2025 cost" (four quarters as first printed): D06 prints the year as 101,096, the
  only full-year 2025 figure; "2025 cost of materials" points there. Screen note settled: "full-year"
  dropped from the sentence because it echoes the releases' "Full Year Results" label.
- 53,936 (both halves on the old presentation): not a part of 101,096, which excludes those taxes.
- Refining segment line (96,080 - 48,157 = 47,923): "Valero's" names the consolidated line.
- "Second half": July-December 2025; fiscal year = calendar year ("Year Ended December 31").
  Period-word search: "second half" is printed only as "second half of 2026" (St. Charles timing,
  D05 and D06); "twelve months", "half-year", "first half" are not printed.

Necessity:
- D06: the full-year 2025 figure (101,096) and Q4 2025 (24,238) are printed nowhere else.
- D08: the first half of 2025 on the full-year presentation (50,726; Q2 2025 restated 24,678) is
  printed nowhere else. D06 gives nine-month taxes (6,720 - 1,740 = 4,980) and D07 gives Q1 (1,500),
  so Q2 + Q3 taxes = 3,480 can be formed, but not split, without D08.
- Searched substitutes: D04 (H1 2025 as first printed), D05 (Q3 and nine months as first printed),
  D07 (Q1 2025 restated). None gives the first half or Q3 2025 on the new presentation.

Delete-and-guess:

- Without D08: 47,216 (with D04's first half) or 52,196 (D05's Q3 plus D06's Q4). Not the gold.
- Recall: Valero's 2025 cost lines and this restated split are not recallable.
