## Question

## Answer

- D04 (Investing and Financing Activities): "Capital investments totaled $407 million in the second quarter of 2025, of which $371 million was for sustaining the business"
- D05 (Investing and Financing Activities): "Capital investments totaled $409 million in the third quarter of 2025, of which $364 million was for sustaining the business"
- D06 (Investing and Financing Activities): "Capital investments totaled $412 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, of which $368 million was for sustaining the business"
