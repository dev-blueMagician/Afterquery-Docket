## Question

On the day the plaintiff filed his motion asking the court to stop his former employer from answering a Virginia custody-case subpoena, what disability onset date did his Social Security disability application, as it then stood, give?

## Answer

May 31, 2012

## Evidence

- D02 (p6): "The Plaintiff filed his motion for a temporary restraining order on March 4, 2013"
- D08 (p10): "Mr. Mattiaccio applied to the SSA for disability benefits on February 5, 2013."
- D08 (p10): "He claimed that he was “unable to work” due to his “disabling condition on May 31, 2012,”"
- D08 (p10): "On March 19, 2013, he amended the onset date of his disabling condition to May 16, 2012"
