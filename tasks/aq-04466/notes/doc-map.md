## Per document (author, date, what it answers, where the figures are)

- D01 AFCA, October 2023. Complaints data: table by financial year 18-19 to 22-23 (486; 1,290; 772;
  694; 859; total 4,101), AFCA established 1 November 2018, data to 30 June 2023; paused complaints
  against insolvent firms as at 1 June 2023 (4,875 complaints, 54 firms, $693,573,439; MIS operator
  6 firms, 272, $28,074,948; timeshare 1 firm, 13, $61,867); appendix by product type and issue;
  outcome percentages. Names eight failures. Thresholds "should be increased"; "appears reasonable"
  to exclude superannuation and the primary residence. Answers Q1-5 only, as one block.
- D02 AIMA, 29 September 2023. Answers all 24. No increase; could lower product tests "(e.g., to
  AU$250,000)" with consent; indexation in steps (20% CPI); not less than 12 months' prior notice;
  does not support excluding assets, but "if any assets are to be excluded ... supports a
  corresponding reduction"; General Observations (grandfather indefinitely, non-residents, AFSL
  variations). Example of the $6 million mortgage-free home and a $3 million loan. Unlisted
  scheme: special resolution (75% of votes cast). ~4500 compliance plans.
- D03 AIOFP, September 2023. Answers all 24. Product value to $1,000,000 with indexation;
  individual wealth thresholds retained; exclude home and superannuation. Simple majority to
  replace a responsible entity. List of failed and frozen products "January 2006 to August 2022":
  191 numbered rows (summary says 192), day/month/year dates, first row dated 1/01/2000, total
  43,731.42 ($m). Rows from late 2018: 1/11/2018, 18/03/2019, 1/06/2019, 3/11/2021, 1/06/2022,
  6/06/2022, 4/08/2022.
- D04 Allens, 6 October 2023 (118,793 characters). Table of collapses with years; nine reforms
  2012-2021. No view on dollar figures; any change "prospective only" with status "preserved for
  the life of their investment"; review at fixed intervals (footnote 9 prefers it to indexation);
  exclude home and superannuation-sourced money; written warning instead of consent. Numbers its
  last two answers 24 and 25 (paper's 23 and 24). Keeps the extraordinary resolution for unlisted
  schemes; footnote 47 reports CAMAC's simple majority with a 25% turnout floor; footnote 40:
  CCIV needs half, not a majority, of external directors.
- D05 Angas Securities, undated. Numbers its answers 1-23 (consent sits under its 3, so its numbers
  run one behind the paper's from there). Product value: abolish or $20 million; net assets $5
  million with home and superannuation excluded; gross income $2.5 million. Unlisted voting should
  "mirror those of listed schemes". "A 7 day liquidity timeframe" unworkable. PI cover $2.5 million.
- D06 Angel Loop, undated. Chapter 1 only. Supports "$4.5M/$450k" on a condition; no position on
  the product value test or on excluding assets.
- D07 Archangel Ventures, 29 January 2024. Exclude the home "while lowering the threshold";
  suggests exempting early-stage investing from any change. $40M from 150 wholesale investors.
- D08 Arnold Bloch Leibler and Euphemia, 31 January 2024. No increase; exclude the home and reduce
  the threshold; transition of at least 36 months; grandfathering; parental-leave example FY 1 to
  FY 6. Euphemia led Aussie Angels' seed raise and runs a syndicate on its platform.
- D09 ASCF, 29 September 2023. Thresholds adequate; home and superannuation stay in; opposes the
  seven-business-day liquidity test. Its comments on scheme conditions sit under its Question 4(b).
- D10 Aussie Angels, undated. Retain $500,000 and $250,000; net assets to $1.5m excluding the home;
  joint test $2m; two-year transition; grandfathering; education pathway; non-residents. Reuses
  sentences found in D02 and D01.
- D11 Bell Potter, undated. No increase; suitability process; attachment with a table of secondary
  capital raisings 2017-2021 (scan noise).
- D12 BoardRoom, 29 September 2023. 2022 volumes (6,566 applications; 5,728 transfers; 77 clients;
  387 funds). Supports an ordinary resolution for unlisted schemes.
- D13 Brisbane Angels, 22 January 2024. Describes a proposal it attributes to ASIC ($5 million,
  $500,000) and argues for the current thresholds, "or at least ... a more gradual approach".
- D14 Joint Associations, 29 September 2023. $1 million, $4 million, $350,000, each indexed to AWOTE
  in fixed increments; exclude the home and preserved superannuation; adopt the seven-business-day
  liquidity test; answers 20-22 and 13-16 as groups.
- D15 CP Ventures and Saltwater Fund, 31 January 2024. No increase; knowledge test; exclude the
  home "as long as the overall test is then lowered"; foreign investors.
