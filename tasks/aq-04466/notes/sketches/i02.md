## Question

Which submitters, if any, say both that already-qualified investors should stay wholesale for existing investments and that the net assets threshold should fall if the family home is excluded? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

AIMA, Arnold Bloch Leibler and Euphemia, Aussie Angels

## Evidence

- D02 (General Observations): "are deemed to remain a wholesale client, provided they continue to satisfy the current wholesale client test/s at all relevant points in time"
- D02 (Q3): "if any assets are to be excluded from the individual wealth test, then AIMA supports a corresponding reduction in the current tests"
- D08 (overview): "investors who satisfy the current thresholds, and investments in which they have invested and may make re-investments or follow on investments should be grandfathered"
- D08 (Q2, net assets): "With the exclusion of the primary residence, the threshold for the net assets test will need to be reviewed and reduced accordingly"
- D10 (grandfathering): "Investors who meet the current wholesale client tests should be grandfathered and any changes to the wholesale client tests should not be applied retrospectively to any prior investments"
- D10 (summary): "Lower the net assets test to $1.5m, excluding the primary residence"
- D04 (Q1, first bullet; first position only): "should be preserved for the life of their investment in the relevant financial product"
- D15 (Q1.3; second position only): "as long as the overall test is then lowered to compensate"
- D07 (Q3; second position only): "from the calculation while lowering the threshold"
