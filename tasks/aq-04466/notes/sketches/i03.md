## Question

After the borrowing in AIMA's family-home example, what is the highest net assets threshold any submitter supports that the homeowner would meet on that submitter's own terms?

## Answer

$4.5 million

## Evidence

- D02 (Q3): "an owner of say a $6 million mortgage free property would not be classified as a wholesale client if the asset is excluded from the individual wealth test, but could borrow $3 million against that property to invest in financial products (leaving net equity of $3 million in the family home)"
- D06 (Question 2): "Angel Loop supports increasing the threshold to $4.5M/$450k on the condition that"
- D06 (Question 3): "Angel Loop has no position with respect to this question"
- D05 (Q2): "Net assets should be $5 million with certain assets excluded (see below)"
- D05 (Q3): "The family home should be excluded"
- D14 (Q2): "the net assets test should increase to $4 million"
- D14 (Q3): "principal place of residence should be excluded when determining their net assets"
- D13 (Proposed Changes): "Raising the minimum net assets requirement from the existing $2.5 million to $5 million"
- D13 (Economic Implications): "we advocate for keeping the current thresholds"
