## Question

What were American Tower's total New Site Tenant Billings for the latest three-month period whose results still counted the 242 communication sites it bought for about $148 million as new sites?

## Answer

$12 million

## Components

- c1: $12 million

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 2: "During the first quarter of 2025, the Company spent approximately $148 million to acquire 242 communication sites". The release defines New Site Tenant Billings as "Day-one Tenant Billings associated with sites that have been built or acquired since the beginning of the prior-year period", and Tenant Billings Growth compares a current period "with Tenant Billings for the corresponding prior-year period".

Step 2. A quarter's prior-year period is the same quarter a year earlier. For the quarter ended March 31, 2026 that is the first quarter of 2025, when the sites were bought, so they still count as new sites. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026 the prior-year period is the second quarter of 2025, which began after the purchase, so the sites have become organic sites. The most recent quarter that still counted them is therefore the first quarter of 2026.

Step 3. Doc 6: in the segment results for the three months ended March 31, 2026, the New Site Tenant Billings row shows 1 for U.S. & Canada, (0) for Latin America, 8 for Africa & APAC, 3 for Europe and 12 for Total Property, so total New Site Tenant Billings were $12 million.

Decoy: Doc 7's segment results for the three months ended June 30, 2026, the latest period in the bundle, show 13 for Total Property, but in that quarter these sites no longer count as new.
