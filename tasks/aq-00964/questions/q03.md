## Question

By how much did American Tower's actual full-year 2025 Adjusted EBITDA exceed the midpoint of the full-year 2025 outlook the company had in place before it reported its results for January through March 2025?

## Answer

$240 million

## Components

- c1: $240 million

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 2 (first-quarter 2025 release, outlook as of April 29, 2025): the full-year 2025 Adjusted EBITDA outlook is 6,885 to 6,955, a midpoint of (6,885 + 6,955) / 2 = 6,920. The same release says the company is raising the Adjusted EBITDA midpoint by $30 million relative to its prior 2025 outlook, so the April figure is not the outlook that was in place before the release.

Step 2. The outlook in place before the first-quarter release therefore had a midpoint of 6,920 - 30 = 6,890.

Step 3. Doc 5: full-year 2025 "Adjusted EBITDA increased 4.7% to $7,130 million".

Step 4. 7,130 - 6,890 = 240, so actual 2025 Adjusted EBITDA exceeded that midpoint by $240 million.

Decoy: taking the April outlook printed in the first-quarter release as the one in place before it gives 7,130 - 6,920 = 210.
