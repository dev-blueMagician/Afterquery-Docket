## Question

In its July 2026 outlook, by how much did American Tower expect 2026 straight-line revenue to differ from the straight-line revenue it recognized in 2025? Give a signed figure in millions of dollars.

## Answer

-$167 million

## Components

- c1: -$167 million

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 5: full-year 2025 non-cash straight-line revenue "represented approximately $28.0 million and $101.0 million, respectively, in the current periods" for the fourth quarter and full year 2025, so 2025 straight-line revenue was $101.0 million.

Step 2. Doc 7: the July 2026 outlook's components of total property revenue at the midpoint list straight-line revenue of (124) for U.S. & Canada, 3 for Latin America, 33 for Africa & APAC, 3 for Europe, 19 for Data Centers and (66) in total, so the outlook expected 2026 straight-line revenue of minus $66 million.

Step 3. -66 - 101.0 = -167.0, so the outlook expected 2026 straight-line revenue to be $167 million lower than in 2025.

Decoy: Doc 7's reconciliations of the outlook to AFFO list "Straight-line revenue" as a positive adjustment of 66, because a negative revenue item is added back.

Reading that adjustment as positive straight-line revenue gives 66 - 101.0 = -35.0, the wrong sign for the expected level.
