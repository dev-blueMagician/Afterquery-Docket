## Question

Across American Tower's April, July and October 2025 updates to its full-year 2025 outlook, what was the combined change in the total property revenue midpoint once the estimated foreign-exchange effect cited in each update is removed? Give the sign.

## Answer

+$25 million

## Components

- c1: +$25 million

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 2 (outlook as of April 29, 2025): the estimated foreign-exchange effect on total property revenue is approximately $50 million relative to the prior 2025 outlook, and the property revenue midpoint is raised by $50 million. Change net of foreign exchange: 50 - 50 = 0.

Step 2. Doc 3 (outlook as of July 29, 2025): the foreign-exchange effect is approximately $130 million and the property revenue midpoint is raised by $165 million, citing foreign exchange and core outperformance. Change net of foreign exchange: 165 - 130 = 35.

Step 3. Doc 4 (outlook as of October 28, 2025): the foreign-exchange effect is approximately $50 million and the property revenue midpoint is raised by $40 million, "primarily as a result of" foreign exchange. Change net of foreign exchange: 40 - 50 = -10.

Step 4. Combined: 0 + 35 + (-10) = 25, an increase of $25 million.

Decoy: the October raise is described as primarily foreign exchange, but the effect it cites is larger than the raise itself, so its non-currency part is negative. Treating the October raise as entirely foreign exchange, or dropping its negative part, gives 0 + 35 = 35.
