## Question

When American Tower completed the sale of its Philippines subsidiary, what full-year 2026 property revenue midpoint was its then-current outlook projecting for the segment that included that subsidiary?

## Answer

$1,605 million

## Components

- c1: $1,605 million

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 7: "On June 15, 2026, the Company completed the sale of its subsidiary in the Philippines". Its segment table marks the Africa & APAC column with the footnote "Includes results of Bangladesh and the Philippines through dates of sale", so the subsidiary sat in the Africa & APAC segment. Doc 7's own outlook reflects expectations as of July 28, 2026, after the sale, and its chief executive calls that raise the second of the year, so the outlook current at the sale was the previous one.

Step 2. Doc 6: its full-year 2026 outlook reflects expectations as of April 28, 2026, the latest outlook issued before the sale. It includes Africa & APAC property revenue of $1,595 million to $1,615 million: (1,595 + 1,615) / 2 = 1,605.

Conclusion: the then-current outlook projected Africa & APAC property revenue at a midpoint of $1,605 million.

Decoy: Doc 7's own outlook, issued after the sale, gives Africa & APAC property revenue of $1,620 million to $1,640 million: (1,620 + 1,640) / 2 = 1,630.
