## Question

Which of American Tower's property segments had their full-year 2026 property revenue outlook raised at both the April 2026 and the July 2026 outlook updates? List them in any order, or answer none.

## Answer

Latin America, Africa & APAC

## Components

- c1: Latin America
- c2: Africa & APAC

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 5 (initial 2026 outlook, as of February 24, 2026): U.S. & Canada $5,060 million to $5,120 million, Latin America $1,645 million to $1,665 million, Africa & APAC $1,535 million to $1,555 million, Europe $1,025 million to $1,055 million, Data Centers $1,175 million to $1,195 million.

Step 2. Doc 6 (update as of April 28, 2026): U.S. & Canada $5,060 million to $5,120 million and Europe $1,025 million to $1,055 million are unchanged; Latin America rises to $1,730 million to $1,750 million and Africa & APAC to $1,595 million to $1,615 million; Data Centers stays at $1,175 million to $1,195 million.

Step 3. Doc 7 (update as of July 28, 2026): U.S. & Canada and Europe are unchanged again; Latin America rises to $1,790 million to $1,810 million, Africa & APAC to $1,620 million to $1,640 million and Data Centers to $1,200 million to $1,220 million.

Conclusion: only Latin America and Africa & APAC were raised at both updates.

Decoy: the prose names different segments. Doc 6 credits its raise partly to "accelerating straight-line revenue in Latin America" and never mentions Africa & APAC, which was also raised. Doc 7 credits "Data Center outperformance", but Data Centers was not raised in April.
