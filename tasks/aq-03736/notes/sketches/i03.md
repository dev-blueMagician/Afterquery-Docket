## Question

Which kinds of financial undertaking counted as a company under the Commission's 2022 due diligence proposal but are not companies under the directive as adopted? List them in any order.

## Answer

Alternative investment funds, UCITS, and pension institutions running social-security pension schemes

## Evidence

- D01 (Article 3(a)(iv), L601): "pension institutions operating pension schemes which are considered to be social security schemes covered by Regulation (EC) No 883/2004"
- D01 (Article 3(a)(iv), L603): "an alternative investment fund (AIF) managed by an AIFM as defined in Article 4(1), point (b), of Directive 2011/61/EU or an AIF supervised under the applicable national law;"
- D01 (Article 3(a)(iv), L605): "UCITS in the meaning of Article 1(2) of Directive 2009/65/EC;"
- D02 (Article 2(8), L281): "This Directive shall not apply to AIFs, as defined in Article 4(1), point (a), of Directive 2011/61/EU"
- D02 (Article 3(1)(a)(iii), L289): "a management company, as defined in Article 2(1), point (b), of Directive 2009/65/EC;"
