## Question

An EU company that must publish its due diligence statement always has 6,000 employees, EUR 1.2 billion worldwide turnover and accounts closed each 30 June. Had the Commission's proposal entered into force when the adopted directive did, how many months after its first statement deadline under the proposal does the first accounting year its directive statement must cover end?

## Answer

38 months

## Evidence

- D01 (Article 11, L821): "The statement shall be published by 30 April each year, covering the previous calendar year."
- D01 (Article 30(1)(a), L1063): "2 years from the entry into force of this Directive] as regards companies referred to in Article 2(1), point (a), and Article 2(2), point (a);"
- D02 (Article 36(1), L1011): "It shall be published at the earliest possible opportunity after 25 July 2024, but no later than 26 July 2026."
- D02 (Article 37(1)(b), L1039): "with the exception of the measures necessary to comply with Article 16, which Member States shall apply to those companies for financial years starting on or after 1 January 2029"
