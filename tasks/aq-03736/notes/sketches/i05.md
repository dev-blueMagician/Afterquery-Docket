## Question

An EU company that must publish its due diligence statement always has 6,000 employees, EUR 1.2 billion worldwide turnover and a July-June financial year. Had the Commission's proposal entered into force when the adopted directive did, how many months after its first statement deadline under the proposal would the first financial year its statement under the directive must cover end?

## Answer

38 months

## Evidence

- D01 (Article 11, L821): "The statement shall be published by 30 April each year, covering the previous calendar year."
- D01 (Article 30(1)(a), L1063): "2 years from the entry into force of this Directive] as regards companies referred to in Article 2(1), point (a), and Article 2(2), point (a);"
- D01 (Article 2(1)(a), L551): "the company had more than 500 employees on average and had a net worldwide turnover of more than EUR 150 million in the last financial year for which annual financial statements have been prepared;"
- D02 (Article 36(1), L1011): "It shall be published at the earliest possible opportunity after 25 July 2024, but no later than 26 July 2026."
- D02 (Article 37(1)(a), L1037): "that had more than 5 000 employees on average and generated a net worldwide turnover of more than EUR 1 500 000 000 in the last financial year preceding 26 July 2027"
- D02 (Article 37(1)(b), L1039): "from 26 July 2028 as regards companies referred to in Article 2(1), points (a) and (b), which are formed in accordance with the legislation of the Member State and that had more than 3 000 employees on average and generated a net worldwide turnover of more than EUR 900 000 000 in the last financial year preceding 26 July 2028 for which annual financial statements have been or should have been adopted, with the exception of the measures necessary to comply with Article 16, which Member States shall apply to those companies for financial years starting on or after 1 January 2029"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h15 (independent author run 1, 2026-10-13; verified by the caller). Proposal side: point (a) company, rules from 2 years after entry into force (25 July 2024), statement "by 30 April each year": first deadline 30 April 2027. Adopted side: 6,000 employees but turnover below EUR 1 500 000 000, so second group; statement duty "for financial years starting on or after 1 January 2029"; a July-June year running on that date began 1 July 2028, so the first covered year is 1 July 2029 to 30 June 2030. Gap 38 months.
Ambiguity: first proposal deadline read as 30 April 2028 (first full calendar year) -> 26; Article 11 sets the date "each year" with no first-year exemption, and 30 April 2027 falls after the rules start. Year in progress on 1 January 2029 -> 26; rejected by "starting on or after". First group from the head-count alone -> 26; point (a) of Article 37(1) also needs the turnover. Calendar-year assumption -> 32; the question states July-June. Publication deadline instead of the year end -> 50; the question asks when the covered year ends. Group-2 start under the proposal -> 14; the company exceeds point (a)'s thresholds. The 25 or 26 July anniversary does not move either date.
Necessity: D01 -> "30 April" (only occurrence in the bundle) and the 2-year start for point (a) companies; without it the start date is unreachable. D02 -> the entry-into-force date, the size groups and the financial-year rule ("financial years starting" occurs only in Article 37); without it the end date is unreachable.
