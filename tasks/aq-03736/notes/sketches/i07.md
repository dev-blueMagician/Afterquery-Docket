## Question

Which kind of regulated financial undertaking, if any, counts as a company under the adopted due diligence directive but did not under the Commission's proposal?

## Answer

Financial holding companies

## Evidence

- D01 (Article 3(a)(iv), L615): "an insurance holding company as defined in Article 212(1), point (f), of Directive 2009/138/EC or a mixed financial holding company as defined in Article 212(1), point (h), of Directive 2009/138/EC"
- D02 (Article 3(1)(a)(iii), L289): "a financial holding company, as defined in Article 4(1), point (20), of Regulation (EU) No 575/2013, an insurance holding company, as defined in Article 212(1), point (f), of Directive 2009/138/EC, or a mixed financial holding company"
- D02 (Article 3(1)(a)(iii), L289): "a management company, as defined in Article 2(1), point (b), of Directive 2009/65/EC;"
- D01 (Article 3(a)(iv), L593): "management company as defined Article 2(1), point (b), of Directive 2009/65/EC"
