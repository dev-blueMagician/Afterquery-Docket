## Question

Suppose the Commission's proposal had entered into force on the day the adopted directive did. On the Commission's own estimates, how many companies would still have been waiting for its rules to apply to them on the day the adopted directive first applies to any company?

## Answer

4,800 companies

## Evidence

- D01 (Article 30(1), L1063): "2 years from the entry into force of this Directive] as regards companies referred to in Article 2(1), point (a), and Article 2(2), point (a);"
- D01 (Article 30(1), L1065): "4 years from the entry into force of this Directive] as regards companies referred to in Article 2(1), point (b), and Article 2(2), point (b)."
- D01 (footnote 62, L1211): "In group 1: 9 400 companies, in group 2: 3 400 companies."
- D01 (footnote 63, L1213): "In group 1: 2 600 companies, in group 2: 1 400 companies."
- D02 (Article 34(2), L987): "for an indeterminate period of time from 25 July 2024"
- D02 (Article 37(1)(a), L1037): "from 26 July 2027 as regards companies referred to in Article 2(1), points (a) and (b)"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2. Proposal offsets (2 and 4 years) placed on the actual entry into force (25 July 2024) give July 2026 for group 1 and July 2028 for group 2; the adopted directive first applies on 26 July 2027, between the two; group 2 = 3 400 EU + 1 400 third-country companies.
Ambiguity: all companies (16 800, or 17 000 from the body's rounded totals) -> fails "still waiting": group 1's date has passed. Group 1 (12 000) -> the reverse set. One footnote only (3 400 or 1 400) -> Article 30(1)(b) names both Article 2(1)(b) and 2(2)(b). Reading "first applies" as the transposition deadline (26 July 2026) gives the same 4 800; as entry into force gives 16 800, which Article 37's "They shall apply those measures ... from 26 July 2027" rejects. The one-day question on anniversaries (25 or 26 July) does not reach either date.
Necessity: D01 -> offsets and group counts (only source of both). D02 -> the entry-into-force date and the first application date; without it the anchor date cannot be placed between the two offsets. Risk: both dates are widely reported, so the platform's document check may find D02 "not needed".
After clarification: the question gives no date, group or count.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: calculate across documents; exact footnote against rounded body. Wrong routes recomputed: 9 400 + 3 400 + 2 600 + 1 400 = 16 800; 13 000 + 4 000 = 17 000; 9 400 + 2 600 = 12 000.
Source risks: posed counterfactual; each step is of a kind the easier solver has cleared on other bundles.
Author calculation: 3 400 + 1 400 = 4 800 companies; 25 July 2024 + 2 years < 26 July 2027 < 25 July 2024 + 4 years on either anniversary reading.
