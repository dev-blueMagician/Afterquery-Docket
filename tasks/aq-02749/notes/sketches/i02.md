## Question

If EPA had to keep comments open after each of its five State coal-ash hearings for as long as Virginia requires after a hearing on a new permit, by how many days in total would its five comment deadlines fall short?

## Answer

33 days

## Evidence

- D03 (Public Notice and Participation in the CCR Permit Application Process, L195): "the public comment period extending at least 15 days after the public hearing"
- D02 (DATES, L27): "Comments must be received on or before November 3, 2025. Public hearing: EPA will hold a hybrid in-person and online public hearing on October 30, 2025."
- D03 (DATES, L32): "Comments must be received on or before July 6, 2026. Public hearing: EPA will hold a hybrid public hearing on June 24, 2026."
- D04 (DATES, L27): "Comments must be received on or before August 4, 2026. Public hearing: EPA will hold a hybrid (in-person and virtual) public hearing on July 21, 2026."
- D05 (DATES, L27): "Comments must be received on or before September 14, 2026. Public hearing: EPA will hold a virtual public hearing on September 3, 2026."
- D06 (DATES, L27): "Comments must be received on or before November 10, 2026. Public hearing: EPA will hold an online public hearing on November 9, 2026."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h26. One State's rule applied to five federal schedules: each proposal's comment deadline falls 4, 12, 14, 11 or 1 days after its hearing, against Virginia's 15.
Ambiguity: 0 takes Virginia's 45-day minimum length instead of the post-hearing rule the question names (every EPA period runs 60 days or more). 3 answers for the Virginia proposal alone and fails "five". The condition is hypothetical and the question says so ("If EPA had to").
Necessity: each of D02-D06 is the only source of its own hearing and comment dates, and D03 alone states the 15 days (grep "after the public hearing": D03 L195). Dropping any document changes the total, since every shortfall is above zero.
After clarification: the question names which Virginia rule applies, not its length; all five documents stay indispensable.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Calculate across documents; Scatter the pieces. No which-items decoy applies.
Source risks: five plain date gaps and one lookup; aq-02626's five-proposal day sum (307) was quick-test too easy and the low model counted days 5 of 5. Offered only as a free quick test.
Author calculation: shortfalls 15 - 4 = 11, 15 - 12 = 3, 15 - 14 = 1, 15 - 11 = 4, 15 - 1 = 14; 11 + 3 + 1 + 4 + 14 = 33. Python 2026-10-10 (datetime).
