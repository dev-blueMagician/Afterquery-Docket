## Question

Across EPA's five proposed State coal-ash program approvals, how many of the 2024 Legacy Rule's all-unit amendments would EPA approve as the State's own version in exactly three States?

## Answer

10 amendments

- D04 (Louisiana Partial Program, L316): "Therefore, EPA is approving the State's version of these requirements:"
- D04 (Louisiana Partial Program, L338): "20. 40 CFR 257.104(a); this amendment clarifies that all owners or operators of CCR units that are subject to 40 CFR 257.102 are subject to the post-closure care requirements"
