## Question

A utility needs a new coal-ash landfill permit in Wyoming (notices a week apart), a capacity increase at a Virginia landfill and, at an Alabama landfill, a liner redesign and an identical replacement monitoring well. Hearings on the capacity increase and the redesign come 40 days after notice. At minimum, how many public-comment days is that in total?

## Answer

169 days

## Evidence

- D02 (Public Comment Period, L220): "are required to complete two public comment periods during the permitting process"
- D02 (Public Comment Period, L221): "Each public comment period begins on the first day of the notice publication and continues until 30 days after the second publication."
- D03 (Public Notice and Participation in the CCR Permit Application Process, L198): "If the modification would expand a facility or increase capacity, the process follows the same public participation procedures as outlined above for new solid waste permits."
- D03 (Public Notice and Participation in the CCR Permit Application Process, L195): "The regulation requires that the public comment period last at least 45 days, with the public hearing scheduled at least 30 days following the public notice and the public comment period extending at least 15 days after the public hearing."
- D05 (Permit Modifications, L216): "Major modifications require a public notice and public comment period under the same procedures as for issuance of initial permits."
- D05 (Permit Modifications, L218): "addition of new groundwater monitoring wells or replacement of existing wells (of similar design and depth)"
- D05 (Public Comment Period, L240): "The comment period will be extended to include comments submitted at a public hearing, if one is held on a proposed permitting action, if the hearing is scheduled after the end of the original 35-day comment period."
