## Question

What percentage of the 2025 North Atlantic swordfish total allowable catch was U.S. underharvest that could not be carried into 2026? Answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

18.7%

## Evidence

- D06 (North Atlantic Swordfish Annual Quota and Adjustment Process): "were 637.7 mt dw (2,514.9 mt dw less than the 2025 adjusted quota)"
- D06 (same paragraph): "only 440.6 mt dw may be carried forward"
- D06 (Supplementary information): "The conversion factor between dw and ww for swordfish is 1.33"
- D03 (North Atlantic Swordfish Annual Quota and Adjustment Process): "Recommendation 24-10 previously set the TAC for 2025-2027 at 14,769 mt."
- D03 (same passage): "TACs) that range from 4,764 mt ww to 17,628 mt ww"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. The final rule (D06) reports the 2025 U.S. shortfall against the adjusted quota and the capped carryover, both in dressed weight; only the proposal (D03) prints the Atlantic-wide TAC, in whole weight. Three dependent steps: underharvest minus carryover, dressed to whole weight, divide by the TAC.
Ambiguity: 14.0% (2,074.3 / 14,769 with no conversion) -> a percentage needs one weight basis; D03 gives its TACs in "mt ww" and a "3,907 mt allocation out of a 14,769 mt TAC", the whole-weight baseline. 19.1% (2,558.3 from Table 1's 2025 column) -> that column's "Underharvest from Previous Year" is the 2024 shortfall; the question says underharvest not carried into 2026. 22.6% (all of 2,514.9) -> "could not be carried" excludes the 440.6 carried. 4.0% (586 / 14,769) -> that is the part carried. Underharvest against the baseline quota (2,299.9) -> D06 defines the underharvest against the 2025 adjusted quota in the sentence and the table row.
Necessity: D03 -> the 14,769 mt TAC (also 3,907-of-14,769) -> Grep of all seven texts: "14,769" only in D03 (two hits); D06 never prints a TAC -> without D03 no denominator. D06 -> 2025 underharvest 2,514.9 and carryover 440.6 -> "2,514.9", "637.7" only in D06; D03 predates the 2025 catch data (it has only the 15 percent rule) -> without D06 no numerator.
After clarification: the wording supplies no figure; both documents still needed.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Calculate across documents; unit normalization (dw vs ww). Wrong routes recomputed: 14.0, 19.1, 22.6, 4.0.
Source risks: D06's sentence says the 440.6 "may be carried forward to the 2025 fishing year" (a slip for 2026; Table 1's 2026 column and "underharvest from 2025" settle it). The TAC sentence prints "14,769 mt" without "ww"; the range sentence before it and the 3,907 allocation show the basis.
Author calculation: Doc 6 inputs 2,514.9 and 440.6 mt dw; 2,514.9 - 440.6 = 2,074.3 mt dw (derived). 2,074.3 x 1.33 = 2,758.819 mt ww (derived). Doc 3 input 14,769 mt ww. 2,758.819 / 14,769 = 18.68% -> 18.7%. Same result converting the TAC to dw (14,769 / 1.33 = 11,104.5) or using the printed 586 mt ww carryover (3,344.817 - 586 = 2,758.817).
