## Question

At 26 percent of the lowest total allowable catch under ICCAT's North Atlantic swordfish management procedure, what percentage of 2025 U.S. landings would the U.S. quota be, to the nearest percent?

## Answer

146%

## Evidence

- D03 (North Atlantic Swordfish Annual Quota and Adjustment Process): "11 possible North Atlantic-wide total allowable catches (TACs) that range from 4,764 mt ww to 17,628 mt ww"
- D03 (same passage): "17,628 mt ww * 0.26 = 4,583.3 mt ww"
- D03 (Supplementary information): "The conversion factor between dw and ww for swordfish is 1.33"
- D06 (North Atlantic Swordfish Annual Quota and Adjustment Process): "were 637.7 mt dw (2,514.9 mt dw less than the 2025 adjusted quota)"
