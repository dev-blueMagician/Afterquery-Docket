## Question

When PHMSA's gas pipeline advisory committee was scheduled to begin meeting on the agency's leak-detection proposal, in which month and year did DOT's then-current regulatory agenda expect PHMSA to propose its rule on idled pipelines?

## Answer

November 2023

## Evidence

- D03 (L1981, RIN 2137-AF51 abstract): "has scheduled a Gas Pipeline Advisory Committee meeting to discuss the NPRM for November 27 through December 1, 2023"
- D03 (L5, edition date): "Federal Register Volume 89, Number 28 (Friday, February 9, 2024)"
- D01 (L5, edition date): "Federal Register Volume 88, Number 143 (Thursday, July 27, 2023)"
- D01 (L781, RIN 2137-AF52 abstract): "define an idled operational status for natural gas and hazardous liquid pipelines"
- D01 (L787, RIN 2137-AF52 timetable): "NPRM................................ 11/00/23"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2-family edition trap. The bridging event (advisory committee meeting scheduled Nov 27 - Dec 1, 2023) is printed only in the February 9, 2024 agenda (D03). The agenda current on November 27, 2023 is the July 27, 2023 edition (D01); the Fall 2023 agenda was not yet published (FR Feb 9, 2024; posted online in December 2023 in the real world, also after the meeting).
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Same series, right edition; Scatter the pieces. Decoys: D03's own idled-pipeline projection, "NPRM 02/00/24" (L2007), in the same PHMSA section as the event (February 2024); D04's "NPRM 12/00/24" (L663) (December 2024).
Source risks: "scheduled" (D03 does not confirm the meeting occurred; wording keeps "scheduled to begin meeting"). D03's text predates its publication (it calls the meeting scheduled), so a reader could argue its content reflects DOT's expectations at the meeting; "then-current regulatory agenda" means the agenda in effect, which by publication date is D01. Removal: without D03 no document mentions the meeting; without D01 November 2023 is printed nowhere else.
Author calculation check and counting convention: date ordering only: 2023-07-27 < 2023-11-27 < 2024-02-09 < 2024-08-16.
