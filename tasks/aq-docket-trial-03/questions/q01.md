## Question

When PHMSA's gas pipeline advisory committee was scheduled to begin meeting on the agency's leak-detection proposal, in which month and year did DOT's then-current regulatory agenda expect PHMSA to propose its rule on idled pipelines?

## Answer

November 2023

## Components

- c1: November 2023

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 3: in its entry for the gas pipeline leak detection and repair rulemaking, PHMSA "has scheduled a Gas Pipeline Advisory Committee meeting to discuss the NPRM for November 27 through December 1, 2023". Doc 3 itself was published in the Federal Register on Friday, February 9, 2024, after those scheduled dates, so it was not yet DOT's regulatory agenda when the meeting was scheduled to begin.

Step 2. Doc 1: published on Thursday, July 27, 2023, it is the latest agenda edition issued before the scheduled meeting, so it was the then-current agenda. Its entry for the rulemaking that would "define an idled operational status for natural gas and hazardous liquid pipelines" lists the proposed rule for 11/00/23 = November 2023.

Decoy: Doc 3's own entry for the same idled-pipeline rulemaking lists the proposed rule for 02/00/24 = February 2024, but Doc 3 was published after the scheduled meeting dates.
