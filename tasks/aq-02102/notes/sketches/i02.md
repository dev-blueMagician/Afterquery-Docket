## Question

Which unions that accepted the government's 2016 Civil Service compensation offer were engaged during the 2023 pension remedy consultation but sent no written response? Any order.

## Answer

FDA, GMB, Unite, NCOA and the Prison Governors' Association

## Evidence

- D05 (L65, para 1.3): "The offer was ultimately agreed with eight out of ten unions (FDA, Prospect, GMB, UNISON, Unite, NCOA, the Defence Police Federation and PGA)."
- D05 (L67, para 1.4): "The reforms were not agreed by the Prison Officers Association (POA) and the Public and Commercial Services Union (PCS)"
- D03 (L137, para 2.11): "Unions engaged during the consultation process are listed below:"
- D03 (L189, para 3.9): "These consisted of 102 responses from individuals and five responses from organisations."
- D03 (L553-561, Annex A): "Civil Service Pensioners Alliance: Representing over 40,000 pensioner and deferred Civil Service Pension Scheme members."
