## Question

How many percentage points did individuals' share of all responses received fall between the 2021-22 and 2023 Civil Service pension remedy consultations, to two decimal places?

## Answer

3.81 percentage points

## Evidence

- D01 (L174, para 3.7): "The Cabinet Office received 1,268 responses in total from scheme members and trade unions which directly addressed the questions in the consultation."
- D01 (L174, para 3.7): "In addition, a response from Audit Wales was also received. Another 32 responses were received from individuals that did not address the questions posed by the consultation"
- D03 (L189, para 3.9): "These consisted of 102 responses from individuals and five responses from organisations. In addition another ten responses were received from individuals that did not address the questions posed by the consultation"
