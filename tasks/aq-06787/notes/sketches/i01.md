## Question

Using the most recently published figure for each quarter, how many percentage points separated the highest and lowest quarterly margins of U.S. Physical Therapy's industrial injury prevention business over the eight quarters ending June 30, 2026? Answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

3.7

## Evidence

- D07 (l.304, Q&A, Q3 2024 on the post-reallocation basis): "It was 20.3% on the properly adjusted basis in 2024, it was 19.6% in the third quarter of this year."
- D07 (l.86, the reallocation): "we made a small reallocation of amortization between our PT and IIP segments in the third quarter, and then we adjusted the prior year amounts to align with the current year presentation"
- D08 (l.67, Q4 2024 and Q4 2025): "Our IIP margin for the fourth quarter increased from 16.7% in the fourth quarter of 2024 to 17.1% in 4Q25."
- D10 (l.70, Q2 2026 and restated Q2 2025): "IIP margin was 20.4% in Q2 2026, compared to 20.3% in Q2 2025."
