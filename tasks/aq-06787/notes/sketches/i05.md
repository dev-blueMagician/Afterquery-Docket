## Question

From the quarter in which U.S. Physical Therapy acquired its Metro partnership to the earliest quarter in which Metro's quarterly net rate exceeded the company's overall net rate, by how many dollars per visit did Metro's net rate rise? Answer to the nearest cent.

## Answer
