## Question

After the September 2026 rulings on the motions to dismiss, how many counts in total were still pending against at least one defendant in the suits brought by the Maryland State Police lieutenant and the Baltimore police sergeant?

## Answer

9 counts

## Components

- c1: 9

## Reasoning

1. Doc 5: Lt. Duane Hill's complaint pleads four counts against the Maryland Department of State Police. The court grants the motion as to Counts I, II and IV "and will deny the Motion as to Count III", so Count III is the one count still pending.
2. Doc 10: Sgt. Larry Worsley's complaint "alleges twelve counts total". The Officer Defendants moved to dismiss only the civil conspiracy count; the other defendants moved against everything. All counts against the State's Attorney Defendants are dismissed, Counts IV, V, VI, VII, VIII and "X" are dismissed "as to the City and BPD", and the conspiracy count is dismissed "as to all defendants".
3. Doc 10, matching each count to the defendants it names: Counts I and II (Officer Defendants) and the twelfth count (battery, Officer Rinaldo) were never challenged; Counts III, VI, VII and the eleventh count (negligence, labelled "Count X") still stand against the Officer Defendants; Count IX stands against the Officer Defendants, the City and BPD. Counts IV, V and VIII lose every defendant and the tenth count (civil conspiracy) is dismissed outright. Pending counts I, II, III, VI, VII, IX, eleventh and twelfth = 8.
4. Total pending counts in the two suits: 1 + 8 = 9.
5. Decoy (Doc 10): treating every count in the conclusion's list (Counts IV, V, VI, VII, VIII, "X" and the conspiracy count) as gone leaves 12 - 7 = 5 pending counts.
6. Decoy total: 5 + 1 = 6.
