## Question

Which plaintiffs, if any, sued a judge who had ruled against them? List them in any order.

## Answer

Veronica Hernandez, Geneva Langworthy

## Components

- c1: Veronica Hernandez
- c2: Geneva Langworthy

## Reasoning

1. Doc 9: Veronica Hernandez sued four Suffolk County Supreme Court justices. In Justice Liccione's debt-collection matter she learned in March 2025 that a "default judgment had been entered based upon her failure to appear", and "Justice Liccione entered a judgment against Hernandez on July 9, 2025", before her amended complaint of November 14, 2025 that still names Liccione. Justice John J. Leo "issued rulings adverse to Hernandez" in her divorce case. Hernandez qualifies.
2. Doc 7: Geneva Langworthy's earlier suit Langworthy v. Colfax County (filed 1/8/2025, terminated 3/18/2025) was "dismissed by Chief District Judge Kenneth J. Gonzales for failure to state a claim for relief". Her later suit Langworthy v. Gonzales (filed 5/6/25) alleged that she "was denied access to the courts by Chief District Judge Kenneth J. Gonzales". She sued the judge who had dismissed her earlier case, so Langworthy qualifies.
3. Doc 3 (decoy): Ashley Mulvilhill sought a summons for Judge Crandall of the Herkimer County Surrogate Court, but the report says "Presumably, Judge Crandall was somehow involved in the court proceedings" and adds "though Plaintiff does not state so or explain how". No ruling by him against her is shown, so she is excluded.
4. No other plaintiff sued a judge. Richard Cora sued the Commonwealth, its veterans' office, the office's Solicitor and two appointed attorneys; Larry Worsley sued police officers, the police department, the city and two prosecutors; Wagner, Hill, Resper, Howard and Richards sued a college board and its official, a state police department, prison officials or a prison medical contractor.
