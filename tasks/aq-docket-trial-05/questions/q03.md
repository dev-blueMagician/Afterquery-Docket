## Question

In the suits that prisoners brought over their medical care, which individual defendants, if any, had every claim against them dismissed with prejudice? List them in any order.

## Answer

Nurse Supervisor Lock, RNS Money, Britney Huner

## Components

- c1: Nurse Supervisor Lock
- c2: RNS Money
- c3: Britney Huner

## Reasoning

1. Doc 1: Samuel Richards brought claim 2 (equal protection) and claim 3 (ADA) against "Defendants Edwards, Lock and Money", claim 1 against Edwards and claim 4 against Baird. The court dismisses the official-capacity claims, the claims for declaratory and injunctive relief, and the equal protection and ADA claims with prejudice; the only claim left is the Eighth Amendment claim "against Defendants Edwards and Baird". Every claim against Lock and Money was therefore dismissed with prejudice; Edwards and Baird still face a claim.
2. Doc 6: in Leon Howard's suit "the claims against her will be dismissed with prejudice" for Britney Huner, while Dr. Letizio's Eighth Amendment claim proceeds and Wellpath is a company, not an individual.
3. Doc 8 (exclusion): in Wayne Resper's suit the official-capacity claims against Weber and Lasher are dismissed without prejudice, Weber receives summary judgment on the individual-capacity claims, Lasher's individual-capacity claims "will be dismissed without prejudice", and footnote 8 declines to dismiss with prejudice. Mace and Akintade are not ruled on. None of them qualifies.
4. No other ruling concerns a prisoner's medical care. Answer: Lock, Money and Huner.
