## Question

Of all the individuals (not courts or other organizations) named as defendants by the two pro se plaintiffs who sued New York state-court judges, what percentage were judges? Answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

29.4%

## Components

- c1: 29.4%

## Reasoning

1. Doc 9: pro se plaintiff Veronica Hernandez alleged that "state court judges" violated her rights. The individuals she named as defendants are Justice John J. Leo, Justice Maureen T. Liccione, Justice Christopher Modelewski and Justice Aletha V. Fields, Robert Montefusco and Abigail Miller of the "MLG Defendants", Craig Stiller and Ryan Tappan of the "Pressler Defendants", court filer Theresa O'Brien, and Lauren Russo and Richard Mullen, who are listed among the "Bank Defendants". The Suffolk County Supreme Court and the law firms, banks and debt companies are not individuals. Individuals = 11, of whom judges = 4.
2. Doc 3: pro se plaintiff Ashley Mulvilhill named "Ricki and Peter Kuchler" and, "in addition to Ricki and Peter", sought summonses for "Judge Crandall of the Herkimer County Surrogate Court", Camille Kahler, Deborah VanSlyke, Gregory VanSlyke and Custom Wealth Management, a company. The report states "Aside from Judge Crandall, the individuals named in the Complaint all appear to be private parties", and its single mention of "Judge Randall" is a misspelling of Crandall, not another defendant. Individuals = 6, of whom judges = 1.
3. Doc 9 and Doc 3: judges 4 + 1 = 5.
4. Doc 9 and Doc 3: individuals 11 + 6 = 17.
5. Doc 9 and Doc 3: 5 / 17 = 29.4% (to one decimal place).
6. Decoys: counting the Suffolk County Supreme Court as a judge because it is grouped with the justices as the "Judicial Defendants", treating every member of the "Bank Defendants" as an institution, counting "Judge Randall" as a second judge, or leaving out Ricki and Peter Kuchler, who are named before the summons list, each gives a different percentage.
