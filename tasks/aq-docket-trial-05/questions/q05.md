## Question

Which judges, if any, were sued by a litigant whose case they had dismissed, or against whom they had entered a judgment? List them in any order.

## Answer

Kenneth J. Gonzales, Maureen T. Liccione

## Components

- c1: Kenneth J. Gonzales
- c2: Maureen T. Liccione

## Reasoning

1. Doc 7: Geneva Langworthy's suit Langworthy v. Colfax County (filed 1/8/2025; terminated 3/18/2025) was "dismissed by Chief District Judge Kenneth J. Gonzales for failure to state a claim for relief". Her later suit Langworthy v. Gonzales (filed 5/6/25) alleged she "was denied access to the courts by Chief District Judge Kenneth J. Gonzales, District Judge David Urias, and Magistrate Judge Kirtan Khalsa". Gonzales qualifies. The list gives no dismissal or judgment by Urias or Khalsa against her, and Senior District Judge William P. Johnson, who denied her motion to reopen, was not sued.
2. Doc 9: Veronica Hernandez sued Justices Leo, Liccione, Modelewski and Fields. "Justice Liccione entered a judgment against Hernandez on July 9, 2025", so Liccione qualifies. Justice Leo issued adverse rulings but entered no judgment (Hernandez still seeks "a final judgment in her divorce litigation") and dismissed nothing; Justices Modelewski and Fields only granted extensions of time to her adversaries.
3. Doc 3 (decoy): Ashley Mulvilhill named Judge Crandall, but his involvement is only presumed ("Presumably, Judge Crandall was somehow involved in the court proceedings"), so no dismissal or judgment by him is shown.
4. No other judge in these rulings is a defendant. Answer: Gonzales and Liccione.
