## Question

Which state police forces, if any, did a federal court hold to be shielded by Eleventh Amendment immunity from a claim brought against them? List them in any order.

## Answer

Maryland Department of State Police, New Mexico State Police

## Components

- c1: Maryland Department of State Police
- c2: New Mexico State Police

## Reasoning

1. Doc 5: the only defendant in Lt. Hill's suit is the Maryland Department of State Police. On Count IV the court agrees "that MFEPA claims are barred by Maryland's Eleventh Amendment sovereign immunity" and dismisses Count IV "for lack of subject-matter jurisdiction".
2. Doc 7: the court's list of Geneva Langworthy's earlier suits includes "Langworthy v. New Mexico State Police" (filed 8/17/2023; terminated 9/20/2023), "dismissed for lack of subject-matterjurisdiction due to Eleventh Amendment immunity".
3. Doc 10 (decoy): the Baltimore Police Department argued that it was a state agency entitled to state sovereign immunity, but the court found "BPD has failed to establish, at this early stage of the case, that it is entitled to state sovereign immunity". It was not held shielded.
4. The other police references are citations (Will v. Michigan Dep't of State Police, cited for the rule that a state is not a "person" under § 1983) or concern officials, a college board, a state court or prison staff. Answer: the Maryland Department of State Police and the New Mexico State Police.
