## Question

Which state police forces did a federal court hold to be shielded by Eleventh Amendment immunity from a claim brought against them? List them in any order.

## Answer

Maryland Department of State Police, New Mexico State Police

## Evidence

- D03 (L503): "MFEPA claims are barred by Maryland"
- D03 (L505): "dismiss Count IV for lack of subject-matter jurisdiction"
- D05 (L211): "Langworthy v. New Mexico State Police"
- D05 (L211): "due to Eleventh Amendment immunity"
- D04 (L169): "BPD has failed to establish, at this early stage of the case, that it is entitled to state sovereign immunity"
