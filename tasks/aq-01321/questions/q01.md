## Question

How much had Microchip reduced its inventory from its peak at the end of calendar 2024 by the close of its first quarter of fiscal 2027? Give millions of dollars to one decimal place.

## Answer

$309.0 million

## Components

- c1: $309.0 million

## Reasoning

Step 1 (Doc 5): Microchip's release for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 quotes its CFO: "we reduced inventory by $22.3 million and lowered days of inventory from 201 days in December to 185 days in March" and "From the high point at the end of December 2024, we have now reduced the inventory by $320.9 million." So "now" is the March 31, 2026 quarter-end, when inventories were 1,035.4. Peak at the end of calendar 2024: 1,035.4 + 320.9 = 1,356.3.

Step 2 (Doc 6): The first quarter of fiscal year 2027 is the three months ended June 30, 2026. The balance sheet shows inventories of 1,047.3 at June 30, 2026, up from 1,035.4 at the prior quarter-end.

Step 3 (Doc 5 and Doc 6): 1,356.3 - 1,047.3 = 309.0 ($ millions).

The 320.9 in Doc 5 is the reduction up to March 31, 2026; inventory then rose in the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
