## Question

For the twelve months ended June 30, 2026, what was Texas Instruments' combined R&D and SG&A expense divided by its revenue, expressed as a percentage to two decimal places?

## Answer

20.26%

## Components

- c1: 20.26%

## Reasoning

Step 1 (Doc 7): TI's income statement for the year ended December 31, 2025 shows research and development of 2,083 and selling, general and administrative of 1,860 ($ millions). 2,083 + 1,860 = 3,943.

Step 2 (Doc 8): For the first quarter of 2026, R&D was 510 and SG&A was 464. 510 + 464 = 974.

Step 3 (Doc 8): For the first quarter of 2025, R&D was 517 and SG&A was 472. 517 + 472 = 989.

Step 4 (Doc 9): For the second quarter of 2026, R&D was 535 and SG&A was 490. 535 + 490 = 1,025.

Step 5 (Doc 9): For the second quarter of 2025, R&D was 527 and SG&A was 485. 527 + 485 = 1,012.

Step 6 (Doc 8 and Doc 9): First half of 2025: 989 + 1,012 = 2,001.

Step 7 (Doc 8 and Doc 9): First half of 2026: 974 + 1,025 = 1,999.

Step 8 (Doc 7, Doc 8 and Doc 9): July to December 2025: 3,943 - 2,001 = 1,942.

Step 9 (Doc 7, Doc 8 and Doc 9): Twelve months ended June 30, 2026: 1,942 + 1,999 = 3,941.

Step 10 (Doc 9): Revenue for the 12 months ended June 30, 2026 was 19,453.

Step 11 (Doc 7, Doc 8 and Doc 9): 3,941 / 19,453 = 20.26%.

No release prints a twelve-month R&D or SG&A figure. Doc 9's own "Over the past 12 months we invested $3.9 billion in R&D and SG&A" is rounded to a tenth of a billion, too coarse for two decimal places.
