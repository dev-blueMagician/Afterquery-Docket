## Question

By what percentage had Microchip's inventory balance fallen from its end-of-2024 peak by the close of its first quarter of fiscal 2027? Give the answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

22.8%

## Evidence

- D06 (L46, CFO quote, March-quarter release): "From the high point at the end of December 2024, we have now reduced the inventory by $320.9 million."
- D06 (L229, balance sheet, March 31, 2026 / March 31, 2025): "Inventories 1,035.4 1,293.5"
- D04 (L203, balance sheet, June 30, 2026 / March 31, 2026): "Inventories 1,047.3 1,035.4"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1, percentage form of q01 (same facts; not to be submitted alongside q01).
Peak 1,035.4 + 320.9 = 1,356.3; fall to June 30, 2026 = 309.0; 309.0 / 1,356.3 = 22.78% -> 22.8%.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: scatter the pieces; stale same-label figure; state the
rounding. Wrong routes: 320.9 / 1,356.3 = 23.7% (stale numerator); 320.9 / (1,047.3 + 320.9) =
23.5% ("now" read as June 30); (1,293.5 - 1,047.3) / 1,293.5 = 19.0% (earliest printed balance as
peak); 309.0 / 1,047.3 = 29.5% (wrong base).
Source risks: as q01; base must be derived, so every route computes something.
