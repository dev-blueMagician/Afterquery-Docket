## Question

How far below its end-of-2024 peak, in millions of dollars, was Microchip's inventory balance at the close of its first quarter of fiscal 2027?

## Answer

$309.0 million

## Evidence

- D06 (L46, CFO quote, March-quarter release): "From the high point at the end of December 2024, we have now reduced the inventory by $320.9 million."
- D06 (L229, balance sheet, March 31, 2026 / March 31, 2025): "|Inventories|1,035.4|1,293.5|"
- D04 (L203, balance sheet, June 30, 2026 / March 31, 2026): "|Inventories|1,047.3|1,035.4|"
