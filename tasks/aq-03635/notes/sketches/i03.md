## Question

On the Commission's assessment of Z Energy's average earnings and capital employed for 2016 to 2018, how much a year did Z earn above a return at the top of the Commission's reasonable range? Answer to the nearest $ million.

## Answer

$175 million

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1, dollar form. Doc 9 gives the Commission's 2016-2018 average earnings ($283m) and capital employed ($1,254m) for Z; Doc 1 gives the reasonable-return range. The dollar amount above a reasonable return is printed nowhere.
Ambiguity: Z's own assessment (231 and 2,085, D11 118 and 164) fails "on the Commission's assessment" -> 51.7. The rate route from the prose "approximately 22%" (D11 102) is an approximation of the same figures -> (22 - 8.6)% x 1,254 = 168.0, not the amount the labelled earnings and capital give. 6.9 fails "top" -> 196.5. The amount is linear in earnings and capital, so the mean of the yearly amounts equals the amount on the means: yearly rows give 175.2 as well (525.6 / 3).
Necessity: Doc 9 -> 283 and 1,254 -> no other document has Z's earnings or capital employed -> unreachable. Doc 1 -> 8.6% -> D02 485 "a WACC of 8%" gives 182.7, not 175 -> unreachable without Doc 1.
After clarification: both needed; the wording supplies no input.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: calculate across documents; exact table vs rounded prose (22% route 168, labelled figures 175: the lever changes the answer).
Source risks: the waterfall's numbers survive as a jumbled text run; Appendix 2 confirms them (mean of the yearly opening-and-closing averages 1,253.5; mean NOPAT 283). All digits used sit within the first 19,000 characters of Doc 9 and the first 15,000 of Doc 1, so the form's number check can find them. Doc 1's range is stated for returns on new investment; the question applies it to capital employed, which is what the Commission's ROACE comparison does.
Author calculation: 0.086 x 1,254 = 107.844; 283 - 107.844 = 175.156 -> $175 million. With 1,253.5: 175.199. Script check 2026-10-12.
