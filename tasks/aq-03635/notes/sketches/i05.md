## Question

BP counts the new sites four named chains opened over five years. How many of those sites belong to chains among the nine firms the Commission's draft report assessed for its findings on returns?

## Answer

47 sites

## Evidence

- D02 (para 1.11(d), line 60): "Allied opening 16 new South Island sites and 13 new North Island sites"
- D02 (para 1.11(d), line 62): "NPD opening 21 new South Island sites"
- D02 (para 1.11(d), line 64): "Gull opening 12 new North Island sites"
- D02 (para 1.11(d), line 65): "Waitomo opening 14 new North Island sites"
- D07 (Schedule 1, row 3.50, line 298): "these findings relate to an assessment of BP, Chevron (until 2015), GAS, Gull, Mobil, NPD, Shell (until 2010), Waitomo and Z Energy (until 2010)"
