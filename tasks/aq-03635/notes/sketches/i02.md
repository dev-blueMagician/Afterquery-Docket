## Question

On the Automobile Association's tax total, what gross margin per litre did importers earn on regular petrol in the second half of 2018, keeping the Commission's other components? Answer in cents to two decimals.

## Answer

28.65 cents

## Evidence

- D09 (X39, line 137): "the average board price for a litre of regular petrol (91 octane) was $2.20 and the average discount was 6 cents"
- D09 (X39, line 138): "Taxes accounted for roughly 97 cents a litre and the estimated cost of fuel was 83 cents, leaving 34 cents as the gross margin for importers"
- D01 (line 19): "we calculate that total taxes including GST are $1.0235/litre"
- D01 (line 32): "underestimate the total tax amount, and thus overstate the importer margin"
