## Question

Z Energy says the Commission left components out of its capital employed. Keeping the Commission's 2016 to 2018 average earnings for Z but using Z's own average capital employed, by how many percentage points would Z's ROACE exceed the top of the Commission's reasonable-return range? Give one decimal place.

## Answer

5.0 percentage points
