## Question

## Answer

## Components

- c1: $1.16 billion

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 5: its cash-flow footnote refers to "the February 2, 2026 acquisition of OneOncology", which falls in Cencora's fiscal 2026 second quarter, the quarter ended March 31, 2026. Doc 5 reconciles adjusted free cash flow only for the six months ended March 31, 2026: adjusted free cash flows of $(1,280.2)M.

Step 2. Doc 4: its reconciliation for the three months ended December 31, 2025, the fiscal first quarter, gives adjusted free cash flows of $(2,436.7)M.

Step 3. Doc 4 and Doc 5: the second quarter is the six months less the first quarter. The six-month outflow is smaller than the first-quarter outflow, so the second quarter's adjusted free cash flow was 2,436.7 - 1,280.2 = 1,156.5 million dollars.

Step 4. 1,156.5 million dollars = 1.16 billion dollars (two decimal places).

Decoy: Doc 4, the release that first reports the completed acquisition, prints only the first quarter's $(2,436.7)M, and Doc 5 prints only the six-month $(1,280.2)M. Neither is the quarter's figure.
