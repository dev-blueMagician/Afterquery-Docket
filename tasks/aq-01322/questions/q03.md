## Question

Cencora's fiscal 2026 segment restatement reduced the revenue and operating income that U.S. Healthcare Solutions had originally reported. For January 1 to June 30, 2025, what operating margin did the removed amounts represent, as a percentage to one decimal place?

## Answer

5.2%

## Components

- c1: 5.2%

## Reasoning

Step 1. The first half of calendar 2025 is Cencora's fiscal 2025 second quarter, the three months ended March 31, 2025, plus its third quarter, the three months ended June 30, 2025.

Step 2. Doc 1: as originally reported for the three months ended March 31, 2025, U.S. Healthcare Solutions revenue of 68,283,831 and operating income of 1,033,150 (thousands).

Step 3. Doc 5: the restated 2025 column for the three months ended March 31 gives U.S. Healthcare Solutions revenue of 66,819,265 and operating income of 944,969.

Step 4. Doc 2: as originally reported for the three months ended June 30, 2025, revenue of 72,876,563 and operating income of 901,793.

Step 5. Doc 6: the restated 2025 column for the three months ended June 30 gives revenue of 71,342,873 and operating income of 833,650.

Step 6. Doc 1 and Doc 5: revenue removed from the March quarter 68,283,831 - 66,819,265 = 1,464,566.

Step 7. Doc 1 and Doc 5: operating income removed from the March quarter 1,033,150 - 944,969 = 88,181.

Step 8. Doc 2 and Doc 6: revenue removed from the June quarter 72,876,563 - 71,342,873 = 1,533,690.

Step 9. Doc 2 and Doc 6: operating income removed from the June quarter 901,793 - 833,650 = 68,143.

Step 10. Revenue removed for the half year 1,464,566 + 1,533,690 = 2,998,256.

Step 11. Operating income removed for the half year 88,181 + 68,143 = 156,324.

Step 12. 156,324 / 2,998,256 = 5.2% (one decimal place).

Decoy: the six-month tables in Doc 1 and Doc 5 cover Cencora's fiscal first half, October through March, not January through June, and the nine-month tables run from October. Doc 6 also prints Other's restated margin for the June quarter, 4.13%, but Other received operations from International Healthcare Solutions as well.
