## Question

What was Cencora's adjusted free cash flow in the fiscal quarter in which it completed its acquisition of OneOncology? Give billions of dollars to two decimal places.

## Answer

$1.16 billion

## Evidence

- D03 (Doc 5, cash flow footnote L639): "to finance a portion of the February 2, 2026 acquisition of OneOncology in the six months ended March 31, 2026"
- D03 (Doc 5, reconciliation lead-in): "Below is a reconciliation of operating cash flows to adjusted free cash flows for the six months ended March 31, 2026"
- D03 (Doc 5, reconciliation L686): "|Adjusted free cash flows|$(1,280.2)M|"
- D01 (Doc 4, reconciliation L469): "|Adjusted free cash flows|$(2,436.7)M|"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2. February 2, 2026 falls in fiscal Q2 2026 (January-March 2026). Q2 = six
months minus Q1: -1,280.2 - (-2,436.7) = 1,156.5 -> $1.16 billion (thousands: 1,156,483).
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: period identified by a fact; two printed same-label
decoys: D01 Q1 $(2,436.7)M in the release whose headline announces the completion ("Company
Completes Acquisition of OneOncology", no date) and D03's six-month $(1,280.2)M. Q2+Q3 from
D05's nine months would give 3.58.
Source risks: the completion date is plainly printed in D03/D05; YTD de-cumulation is a standard
transform (trial-02 q01 too easy); the sign flip is the remaining trap.
Author calculation check and counting convention: calc via components (OCF 1,338,639, capex
165,618, gains 16,538) agrees; nearest $1M would split (1,156 vs 1,157), so two decimals in billions.
