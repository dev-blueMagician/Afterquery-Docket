## Question

Applying Cencora's own definition of adjusted free cash flow, how much did the company generate over calendar year 2025 (January 1 to December 31)? Give billions of dollars to two decimal places.

## Answer

$3.38 billion

## Evidence

- D06 (Doc 3, non-GAAP section): "For the fiscal year ended September 30, 2025, adjusted free cash flow of $2,970.8 million consisted of net cash provided by operating activities of $3,875.1 million, minus capital expenditures of $668.0 million and the gains from antitrust litigation settlements of $236.4 million"
- D01 (Doc 4, cash flow L404): "|Net cash used in operating activities|(2,305,169)|(2,718,782)|"
- D01 (Doc 4, cash flow L406): "|Capital expenditures|(119,376)|(105,893)|"
- D01 (Doc 4, footnote L200): "Includes a $22.9 million gain from antitrust litigation settlements, a $7.3 million LIFO credit"
- D01 (Doc 4, reconciliation L469): "|Adjusted free cash flows|$(2,436.7)M|"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h3. Calendar 2025 = FY2025 - Q1 FY2025 + Q1 FY2026. Q1 FY2025 adjusted FCF is
printed nowhere: -2,718,782 - 105,893 - 22,870 = -2,847,545. 2,970,767 + 2,847,545 - 2,436,697 =
3,381,615 -> $3.38 billion (every rounding route 3,381.5-3,381.7).
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: method applied to an unreported period; near misses at two
decimals: 3.36 (Q1 FY25 gains not removed), 3.39 (Q1 FY26 unadjusted), 3.41 (FY25 at "$3.0B"
face value), 5.82 (Jan-Sep only), 2.97 (fiscal year).
Source risks: closed-book recall of FY25 (~$3.0B) and the two Q1 figures could approach the gold;
derivative-settlement credit ($86.8M, Q1 FY26) is not excluded in D01's own reconciliation.
Author calculation check and counting convention: calc1.py; calendar quarters = fiscal Q2-Q4 2025
plus fiscal Q1 2026.
