## Question

By how many percentage points did the share of satellite telecommunications providers with 1,500 or fewer employees rise between the FCC's earliest and latest year-end counts? Answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

2.6 percentage points

## Evidence

- D03 (FRFA, Satellite Telecommunications): "as of December 31, 2020, there were 71 providers that reported they were engaged in the provision of satellite telecommunications services. Of these providers, the Commission estimates that approximately 48 providers have 1,500 or fewer employees"
- D07 (FRFA, para 58): "as of December 31, 2023, there were 57 providers that reported they were engaged in the provision of satellite telecommunications services. Of these providers, the Commission estimates that approximately 40 providers have 1,500 or fewer employees"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2. One paragraph repeated in three rules with a different year-end each time (2020 in D03, 2021 in D02, 2023 in D07) and the same closing words ("a little more than half"). The earliest data sit in the later-published of the two 2024 rules.
Ambiguity: 5.6 points (D02 to D07, taking the first-published rule as the earliest count) -> fails "earliest ... year-end": D03's count is as of December 31, 2020, D02's as of December 31, 2021. -3.0 or 3.0 (D03 to D02) -> fails "latest". The 2017 Census firm counts (275 firms) are by revenue and for a whole year, not a year-end count by employees.
Necessity: D03 -> 71 and 48 (only source of the 2020 count); D07 -> 57 and 40 (only source of the 2023 count). D02's 2021 count is the middle edition and is not used.
After clarification: two indispensable documents; the question names no year.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: same series, right edition; exact figures under identical prose.
Source risks: all four inputs are past character 20,000 in their documents (D03 char 36,522; D07 char 63,902), so the form's number check will flag them. "Approximately" 48 and 40 are the Commission's estimates.
Author calculation: 48 / 71 = 67.606%; 40 / 57 = 70.175%; 70.175 - 67.606 = 2.57 -> 2.6 points.
Delete-and-guess (2026-10-13): without D03 the earliest count left is D02's (December 31, 2021): 64.6% to 70.2%, 5.6 points, not the gold. Without D07 the latest count left is D02's: 67.6% to 64.6%, a fall of 3.0, not the gold. No recall route found (Monitoring Report provider counts are not headline figures). Both documents needed.
