## Question

What percentage of all space station regulatory fees the FCC assessed for fiscal year 2024 fell on NGSO payors charged per system, to one decimal place?

## Answer

39.4%

## Evidence

- D05 (III.B.1, para 28): "for FY 2024 there were 16 units of small satellite fee payors, 140 units of GSO space stations, 6 units of"
- D05 (III.B.1, para 28): "and 11 units of other NGSO space stations"
- D05 (II, para 7): "rather than on the per system basis used for"
- D05 (II, para 9): "to 60% of space station regulatory fees being allocated to GSO space station payors and 40% to NGSO space station payors"
- D06 (III.D, para 40): "For fiscal year 2024, fee payors in the NGSO--Other fee category were assessed fees of $964,200 per unit"
- D06 (III.D, para 40): "Payors in the NGSO--Less Complex fee category were assessed per unit fees of $441,925"
- D06 (III.D, para 40): "after subtracting a pro rata amount of the total fees assessed for NGSO--Small Satellites from each category"
- D06 (II, para 7): "for FY 2024 at the level set for FY 2023 ($12,215)"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1c, a carve-out under the printed 40% NGSO share. "Charged per system" names NGSO-Other and NGSO-Less Complex by a property the documents state (small satellites pay per license); those two categories hold the NGSO share less the small-satellite fees.
Ambiguity: 40.0% (the printed NGSO share) -> fails "charged per system": the 40% also covers small satellites, which pay per license (D05 para 7; D06 para 36). 39.8% (small satellites outside the split) -> fails D06 para 40. Reading to report, not cleared: a solver may treat "NGSO payors charged per system" as all NGSO payors and answer 40%; if both of the form's solvers do, "One defensible answer" will block it.
Necessity: as i01 (units only in D05, fees only in D06).
After clarification: two indispensable documents.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: exact figure under a printed round share (redo with the printed 40%: differs from 39.4%).
Source risks: as i01; a higher risk of a "too vague" block than i01 because the lure is printed verbatim.
