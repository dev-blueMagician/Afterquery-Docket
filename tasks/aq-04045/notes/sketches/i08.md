## Question

Which company named in the FCC's 2025 space-fee notice, if any, had joined a petition asking the FCC to reconsider its disclosure rule on deployment devices?

## Answer

Telesat

## Evidence

- D01 (I, para 1): "and Telesat Canada (Telesat) (collectively, Combined Petition)"
- D01 (I, para 1): "asking the Commission to reconsider information disclosure requirements relating to satellite maneuverability, large system disposal reliability, the use of deployment devices, and the use of certain types of persistent liquids"
- D05 (III.C.2, para 53): "Maxar and Telesat propose to create additional tiers of"
