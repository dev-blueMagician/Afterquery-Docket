## Question

How many TWh of the impact assessment's 2050 preferred-option deployment depend on requiring the buildings slated for zero connection charges to connect?

## Answer

10.5 TWh

## Evidence

- D01 (L144, Connecting buildings in zones): "We intend to cap these costs at zero for pre-existing communally heated domestic buildings, ensuring no costs on leaseholders."
- D02 (L632, paragraph 14c): "High (option 3, preferred): all new build, large public sector, large non-domestic and communally heated residential blocks required to connect to heat networks"
- D02 (L51, summary sheet): "Option 2, medium option: all new builds, large non-domestic, and large public sector buildings are required to"
- D02 (L1534-1553, Table 12): "Medium High (preferred) 0.1 0.1 4.2 6.3 8.3 12.5 12.5 18.8 16.6 25.0 20.7 31.2"
