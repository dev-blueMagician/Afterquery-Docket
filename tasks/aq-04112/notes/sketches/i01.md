## Question

At the Association of Labour Providers' unit costs, what would one company pay to recruit, for three years, the staff Nick Allen's example plant lacked?

## Answer

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. Doc 1's Table 3 prices 50 workers over three years line by line and then prints a
rounded shortcut ("£2,834 per worker per annum"). One line, the sponsor application, is charged once per
company (unit cost £1,000, total £1,000). Doc 2 alone gives a plant's missing head count (150). Applied to
150 workers the itemised cost is not three times the printed total and not the printed rate times 450.
Same shape as aq-02157 q04 (a figure derived from a table rule beside printed look-alikes, Medium).

Step audit (WORKFLOW section 6 item 6): pointed at by the question: the Association's table ("unit costs"),
the three years, Allen's plant. Not pointed at: that the sponsor application does not scale with head count.
One unpointed step, in one table. Say so in the hand-over.

Ambiguity (readings reported, not cleared):
- Three times the 50-worker total: 3 × £425,050 = £1,275,150. The words "unit costs" and "one company" reject
  it, and so does the table (the application's total equals its unit cost). It is the natural shortcut,
  because 150 is three times 50; the form's "One defensible answer" check will show whether its solvers take it.
- The printed rate: £2,834 × 150 × 3 = £1,275,300. The rate is the table's own rounding of 425,050 / 150.
- Every unit cost times 150, the application included: £1,422,150. The table charges the application once.
- "example plant" read as one of Allen's automation examples (Q73: an 800-people plant "would have saved
  them 100 people"): those plants are not described as lacking staff; "lacked" fits only Q70.
- "the staff ... lacked" read as 15% or 16% of some other head count: Allen states both 1,000 and 150.

Necessity:
- D03 (Doc 1): only source of the unit costs (searched "£610", "£199", "£624", "Skills Charge" in all three
  documents: Table 3 only). Without it nothing can be priced.
- D01 (Doc 2): only source of the 150 ("150 people" and "1,000 people" occur once, at Q70). Doc 1 repeats
  only "15% to 16% short on staff" (para 5) with no plant size, so the head count cannot be rebuilt from it.
  The question does not print 1,000 for that reason.
- D02 (Doc 3) holds neither input.
