## Question

Had the Migration Advisory Committee's advice on butchers been accepted, by how much would a skilled butcher's quoted starting salary have exceeded the Skilled Worker Visa salary threshold?

## Answer

£6,020

## Evidence

- D01 (Q8, Charlie Dewhirst): "Yes, £26,500 starting salary up to £35,000 to £40,000 once they are"
- D03 (para 54): "on to the SOL, including: veterinary nurses; meat hygiene inspectors; jobs in the fishing industry; and butchers."
- D03 (para 39): "Entry onto the SOL allows the applicable minimum salary requirement for a SWV to be reduced by 20%."
- D03 (para 53): "reducing the general salary threshold of £25,600"
- D03 (para 58): "putting the role on the SOL would not help as the pay being offered by recruiting firms was above £25,600 in any case"

39 and 53): £20,480. The starting salary is only in the first hearing. Same shape as aq-02157 q04 (a floor
derived from a rule beside printed same-label figures, Medium).
