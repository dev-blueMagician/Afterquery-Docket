## Question

What percentage of the NFU's own top estimate of need was the most seasonal worker visas the Government said it might issue for 2022? Give one decimal place.

## Answer

66.7%

## Evidence

- D01 (Q30, Tom Bradshaw): "We are suggesting that it is going to be a figure of between 50,000 and 60,000 that will be required."
- D01 (Q34, Tom Bradshaw): "We have said 50,000 to 60,000."
- D03 (Table 4): "30,000 30,000 28,000"
- D03 (para 62): "might increase by a further 10,000 during 2022"
- D03 (para 64): "A range of figures were given to us by the sector for how many visas were needed, ranging from 50,000 to 80,000."
