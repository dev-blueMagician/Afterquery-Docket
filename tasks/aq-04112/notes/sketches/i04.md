## Question

For every visa issued under the temporary scheme the NFU's witness heard would fill very quickly, how many were issued under the one he said would not work? Give one decimal place.

## Answer

15.4

## Evidence

- D01 (Q16, Tom Bradshaw): "they are very confident they will fill the butcher requirement for pigs extremely rapidly from eastern Europe and the Ukraine"
- D01 (Q20, Tom Bradshaw): "However, it is not going to work for poultry."
- D03 (para 29, Table 2 header): "Visas available Applicants Issued (% of visas available)"
- D03 (Table 2): "Poultry workers 5,500 1,845 1,770 (32%)"
- D03 (Table 2): "Pork butchers 800 170 115 (14%)"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h4. Forecast in one document, outcome in another (the Guide's first worked example). On
26 October the NFU's vice-president passed on labour providers' confidence about the butcher visas and
wrote off the poultry visas. The Home Office figures in the report show the reverse order of take-up:
1,770 poultry visas against 115 for butchers.

Step audit: both schemes are identified by words in the question (pointed); "issued" names the column. No
unpointed step.

- Shares instead of counts: 32% / 14% = 2.3. Applicants: 1,845 / 170 = 10.9. Visas available: 5,500 / 800 =
  6.9. The question says "visa issued".
- Order reversed: 0.1.

Necessity:
