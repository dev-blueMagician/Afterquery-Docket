## Question

At each of the October and November 2021 hearings, one witness said the same Government skills scheme left their sector out. Which scheme, and which two sectors?

## Answer

The lifetime skills guarantee, poultry and hospitality

## Evidence

- D01 (Q10, Graeme Dear): "We cannot, in part, because we are not part of the lifetime skills guarantee"
- D01 (Q40, Graeme Dear): "being excluded from the lifetime skills guarantee is not a good thing"
- D02 (Q118, Kate Nicholls): "The plan for jobs, the initial lifetime skills guarantee, missed out hospitality qualifications altogether."
