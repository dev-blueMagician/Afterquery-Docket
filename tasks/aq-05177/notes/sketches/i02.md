## Question

RadNet's first 2024 revenue guidance for its imaging center segment was a range. Measured against 2023 revenue of the businesses that made up that segment in 2024, what growth did the bottom of the range imply? Give the percentage to one decimal place.

## Answer

5.3%

## Evidence

- D06 (L149, August 2024 update): "For revenue, we increased our guidance ranges by $10 million at the low and high end. For EBITDA, we increased—and that range is now $1.685,000,000 to $1,735,000,000."
- D05 (L138, May 2024 update): "For revenue, we increased our guidance level for the imaging center segment by $25 million, both at the low end and the top end of the range."
- D04 (L143-144, 2023 actual): "Imaging Center reporting segment of $1.604 billion"
- D04 (L167, segment change): "the eRAD software businesses and related health informatics businesses that were reported as part of our Imaging Center segment throughout 2023 will now be combined with our artificial intelligence segment to form a new Digital Health financial reportable segment"
- D04 (L169, moved businesses): "These businesses produced $37.1 million of revenue"
- D04 (L173, check on the top of the range): "we are anticipating revenue growth in 2024 to be as much as 8.5%"
