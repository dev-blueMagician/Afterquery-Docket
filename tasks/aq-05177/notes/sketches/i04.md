## Question

At which of its 2024 guidance updates, if any, did RadNet change at least one guidance range for its Digital Health segment? List them in date order.

## Answer

May 2024, November 2024 (the updates with first- and third-quarter results)

## Evidence

- D05 (L142-143, May 2024): "For the digital health segment, we increased our EBITDA guidance by $1 million both at the low end and the high end of the range to $13 million to $15 million. We increased our non-capitalized R&D expenditures by $1 million to $12 million to $14 million, and all other guidance ranges remain the same for the digital health platform."
- D06 (L151, August 2024): "For the digital health segment, our guidance ranges all remain the same and we are on track to meet the originally anticipated projections."
- D07 (L155, November 2024): "For the digital health segment, our guidance remains substantially the same. We did increase our spend by $1 million on non-capitalized R&D related to the DeepHealth cloud-based OS and generative AI initiatives."
