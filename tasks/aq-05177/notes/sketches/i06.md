## Question

How many fewer de novo imaging centers did RadNet open in 2024 than it expected to open that year when it reported its first-quarter 2024 results?

## Answer

5

## Evidence

- D05 (L79, May 2024): "We currently have 12 de novo facilities in various stages of development which will open for operations throughout the remainder of the year. These are on top of the two de novos which opened in the first quarter."
- D05 (L211, May 2024 Q&A): "We opened up two facilities in the first quarter, one in Maryland, one in California, and we’ve got 12 other facilities that are in various stages of construction and completion, that should open up by year end."
- D08 (L95, March 2025): "During 2024, we opened nine de novo facilities"
