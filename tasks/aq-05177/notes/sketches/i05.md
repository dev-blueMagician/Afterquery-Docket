## Question

Which health systems, if any, were RadNet's partners in the seven-center joint venture it formed when the first quarter of 2024 closed? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

Cedars-Sinai, Providence Health System

## Evidence

- D05 (L85, May 2024): "upon quarter close, we formed a new seven-center joint venture with Providence Health System in the northern and eastern San Fernando Valley of Los Angeles"
- D07 (L82, November 2024): "one inside our three-way joint venture in the San Fernando Valley and Los Angeles with Cedars Sinai and the Providence Health system"
- D07 (L271, November 2024 Q&A): "one here in Los Angeles with Cedars and the Providence Health system"
- D05 (L85, decoy): "we added seven additional centers to our joint venture with Dignity Health in Arizona"
