## Question

On the basis Rio Tinto used to report alumina production in 2025, by what percentage did its alumina production in its highest-output calendar half-year from January 2024 to June 2026 exceed that in its lowest-output one? Give the nearest whole percent.

## Answer

9%

Steps:

1. Build the five calendar halves from the tables: H1 2024 = nine months 2024 less Q3 2024 = 5,310 - 1,770 = 3,540 (D03, printed nowhere); H2 2024 = 1,770 + 1,992 = 3,762; H1 2025 = 3,735 (D02); H2 2025 = 1,888 + 1,969 = 3,857 (printed nowhere as a half); H1 2026 = 4,040 (D02).
2. Put H1 2026 on the 2025 basis: the 2026 reviews count Queensland Alumina at 100% where 2025 (and 2024) counted Rio Tinto's 80%, so H1 2026 = 4,040 - 0.2 x 1,698 = 3,700.4.
3. Highest H2 2025 (3,857), lowest H1 2024 (3,540): 3,857 / 3,540 - 1 = 8.96%, nearest whole percent 9%.

Decoy: 14% (H1 2026's printed 4,040, the record half as printed, against H1 2024)
