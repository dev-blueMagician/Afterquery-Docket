## Question

By what percentage, to one decimal place, did the number of UFLPA-listed entities grow from November 1, 2024 to August 3, 2026?

## Answer

139.7%

## Evidence

- D02 (L77, appendix 1 opening): "This notice supersedes the UFLPA Entity List published in the Federal Register on November 1, 2024 (89 FR 87391)."
- D02 (L21, summary): "This update adds five entities to the section 2(d)(2)(B)(ii) list of the UFLPA and twenty-five entities to the section 2(d)(2)(B)(v) list of the UFLPA."
- D02 (L160, Xinjiang Daqo paragraph): "This company was first listed on the UFLPA Entity List, pursuant to Section 2(d)(2)(B)(i), in June 2022."
- D02 (L164): "No removals are being made to the UFLPA Entity List at this time."
- D02 (L165): "One hundred and seven entities that meet the criteria set forth in the four required lists"
- D06 (L76): "The UFLPA Entity List as of August 3, 2026 is available in this appendix"
- D06 (L182): "One hundred and eighty-seven entities that meet the criteria set forth in the four required lists"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. The November 1, 2024 version is outside the bundle; D02 is the update that superseded it, so its size is D02's 107 minus the entities that update newly added. The update names 5 + 25 = 30 additions, but one (v) addition, Xinjiang Daqo New Energy, had been on the (i) list since June 2022 (D02 L160, and on D02's own (i) list at L177), so only 29 entities were new and the November 1 version held 78. D06 gives 187 on August 3, 2026.
Ambiguity: (a) "number of entities" vs sub-list placements: the question asks for listed entities, and DHS's printed totals count each entity once (D02 107 = 120 placements minus 13 repeat appearances; checked by script, doc-map shared ground); no reading counts placements. (b) Which version on November 1: D02 L77 names the one published that day; no update fell between November 1 and November 25 (D02 supersedes it directly). (c) Which version on August 3, 2026: D06 L76 "as of August 3, 2026". (d) Percent vs points: "by what percentage ... grow" is relative change from the November 1 base; precision stated. Competing answers: 142.9% (Daqo counted as new, base 77), 74.8% (D02's 107 used as the November 1 base), 84.6% (D04's 144 taken as the latest); each fails a condition the question states (the November 1 date or August 3 date) or a fact D02 prints.
Necessity: D02 -> 107, the 30 named additions, Daqo's June 2022 listing, the November 1 supersession -> no other document names the November 1 version or the November 2024 additions (D04 starts from November 25; D06 from January 15, 2025) -> without D02 the base is unknowable. D06 -> 187 on August 3, 2026 -> D02/D04 stop at 144 (January 2025) -> without D06 the end count is unknowable. D04 is not needed (route 187 - 78) and is not marked.
After clarification: the wording supplies only the two dates and the precision; both counts and the Daqo fact come from D02 and D06.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: entity resolution (an addition that was already listed) and right edition (November 1 version vs D02's November 25 total). Not a which-items question; wrong-route recomputations in the author calculation below.
Source risks: D02 and D06 print 107, 187, five and twenty-five only as words, so the form's digit-based reasoning check may flag source steps (unknown whether it reads number words). The November 2024 additions were public in 2024; a solver without D02 could only reach 78 by recalling that update's size (stated Document-check risk; D06's 187 is post-cutoff, so no closed-book route).
Author calculation: base 107 - (5 + 25 - 1) = 78 entities; growth 187 - 78 = 109; 109 / 78 x 100 (conversion to percent) = 139.7436% -> 139.7% at one decimal. Wrong routes: (187 - 77) / 77 = 142.857% -> 142.9%; (187 - 107) / 107 = 74.766% -> 74.8%; (144 - 78) / 78 = 84.615% -> 84.6%. Precision: one decimal; no input rounding.
