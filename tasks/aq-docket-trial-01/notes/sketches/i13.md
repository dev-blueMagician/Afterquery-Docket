## Question

The battery ageing study and the autoimmune subtype study both relied on BIC. What ridge hyper-parameter value did the battery study's algorithm reach at convergence for its full repeated-measurements model, and by exactly how much was the autoimmune study's selected-model BIC below that of its alternative module construction?

## Answer

0.0002 and 248,685

## Evidence

- D04 (Sec. 2.2, text line 121): "such as BIC [xiv], the hyperparameter, λ, can be optimally determined at each iteration"
- D02 (Table 4, text line 585): "MAD-based model 9 8 −504,975.3 1,031,607 19 979"
- D02 (Table 4, text line 587): "WGCNA-based model 4 6 −630,297.4 1,280,292 3 975"
