## Question

Two papers in this collection cite different page ranges of one 2011 publication on mixtures. How many more pages does the range that begins later span than the other range?

## Answer

3 pages

## Evidence

- D01 (References, text line 507): "Dealing with label switching under model uncertainty. Mixtures: estimation and applications, pages 213–239."
- D02 (References, text line 679): "Mixtures of factor analysers for the analysis of high-dimensional data. Mixtures: Estimation and Applications, pages 189–212, 2011."
