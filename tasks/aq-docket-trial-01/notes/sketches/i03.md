## Question

The battery ageing study and the autoimmune subtype study both tuned their models with the Bayesian information criterion. What ridge hyper-parameter value had the battery study's full repeated-measurements model reached at convergence, and how many clusters did the autoimmune study's selected model have?

## Answer

0.0002 and 9 clusters
