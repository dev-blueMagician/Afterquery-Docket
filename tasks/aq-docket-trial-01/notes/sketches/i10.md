## Question

The autoimmune subtype study and the brain spatial-factor study each discarded genes with a threshold of 20: the first dropped genes in undersized modules when building its selected model's modules, the second dropped genes expressed in too few tissue spots. How many more genes did the second rule discard than the first?

## Answer

16,879 genes

## Evidence

- D05 (Sec. 6, text line 783): "3,592 in-tissue Visium spots and 33,538 gene features"
- D05 (Sec. 6, text line 783): "retain genes with positive expression in at least 20 spots"
- D05 (Sec. 6, text line 783): "The low-expression filtering retains 14,638 genes."
- D02 (Appendix A, text line 723): "the top 3000 genes ranked by MAD were retained"
- D02 (Appendix A, text line 725): "20 genes were removed. This resulted in 979 genes grouped into 19 modules."
