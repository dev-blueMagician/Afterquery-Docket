## Question

In its benchmark on the leukemia samples, how many more iterations did the sparse Dirichlet process mixture model run than the battery ageing study's regularised algorithm took to converge when identifying its full repeated-measurements model?

## Answer

9

## Evidence

- D01 (Fig. 5 runtime panel, text line 413): "HDDC (random): 0.13 Sparse DPMM: 65"
- D01 (Sec. 3.2.1, text line 403): "Sparse DPMM is significantly slower (∼ 5.91 seconds per iteration)"
- D04 (Sec. 2.4, text line 379): "The RIGLS algorithm converged after just 2-iterations."
