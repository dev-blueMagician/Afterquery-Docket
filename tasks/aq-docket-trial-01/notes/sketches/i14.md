## Question

The battery ageing study and the autoimmune subtype study both relied on BIC. What ridge hyper-parameter value did the battery study's algorithm reach at convergence when fitting all retained cells together, and by how much was the autoimmune study's selected-model BIC below that of its alternative module construction?

## Answer

0.0002 and 248,685
