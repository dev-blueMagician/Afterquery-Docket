## Question

In the brain spatial-factor study, what prior sill was estimated for the tissue analysis that used exactly as many genes as passed the autoimmune subtype study's gene screen?

## Answer

A sill of 100

## Evidence

- D02 (Sec. 5.1, text line 435): "genes are first screened using the MAD, and only highly variable genes are retained for downstream analysis"
- D02 (Appendix A, text line 723): "the top 3000 genes ranked by MAD were retained"
- D05 (Sec. 6, text line 783): "selecting the top 3,000 HVGs"
- D05 (Sec. 6.2.3, text line 815): "and the estimated sill was φˆ = 500 for the filtered and full-gene analyses, and φˆ = 100 for the 3000-HVG analysis"
