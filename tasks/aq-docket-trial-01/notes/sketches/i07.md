## Question

What percentage of its measured genes passed the autoimmune subtype study's median-absolute-deviation screen, and what percentage did the brain spatial-transcriptomics study keep in its highly-variable-gene analysis? Give both to one decimal place, in that order.

## Answer

31.0% and 8.9%

## Evidence

- D02 (Sec. 5.1, text line 431): "raw expression measurements available for 9,671 genes"
- D02 (Appendix A, text line 723): "the top 3000 genes ranked by MAD were retained"
- D05 (Sec. 6, text line 783): "3,592 in-tissue Visium spots and 33,538 gene features"
- D05 (Sec. 6, text line 783): "selecting the top 3,000 HVGs"
