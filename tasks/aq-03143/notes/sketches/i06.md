## Question

By how much did the estimated yearly cost of simplifying the housing and immigration fee schemes rise once attendance at court was set at 75% of the preparation rate?

## Answer

£0.1 million

## Evidence

- D02 (Doc 2, L474): "estimated to increase the cost of the policy by 1% (£0.2m) compared to the fee uplifts without simplification"
- D03 (Doc 4, L513): "estimated to increase the cost of the policy by 1% (£0.3m) compared to the fee uplifts without simplification"
- D03 (Doc 4, L505): "Following the consultation, this has been revised to 75%, and the updated rate has been incorporated into the costing presented here."
