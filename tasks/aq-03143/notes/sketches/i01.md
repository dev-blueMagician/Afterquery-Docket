## Question

Outside London, what do the final legal aid fees pay in total for 10 hours' preparation on each of a housing and a non-asylum immigration legal help matter, plus 3 hours attending county court with counsel in a housing case?

## Answer

£1,032.50

## Evidence

- D04 (Doc 1, L293): "Most fixed fees have an escape mechanism whereby providers can be remunerated by hourly rates when the claim for work done (calculated in hourly rates) exceeds the escape threshold, which is two or three times the fixed fee."
- D04 (Doc 1, L556): "The escape threshold remains at three times the fixed fee, meaning the escape threshold is increased to £669."
- D04 (Doc 1, L415): "reduction of the escape threshold for legal help cases to twice the value of the fixed fee"
- D04 (Doc 1, L1485, housing standard fee row): "£45.95  42%  £223.00"
- D04 (Doc 1, L1121, non-asylum Stage 1 legal help row): "£48.24  35%  £317"
- D01 (Doc 3, L475): "we propose to raise this fee from 50% to 75% of the corresponding preparation and attendance rate"
- D01 (Doc 3, L1994, attendance at court or conference in the County Court): "£29.25   78%  1(b), 1(c)  £52.00"
