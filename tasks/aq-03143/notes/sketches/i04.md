## Question

Among the areas of law respondents drew on for the remote-advice questions, what do the response counts total for areas where the remote-application limit is still 50% and a full-time office is required?

## Answer

106

## Evidence

- D04 (Doc 1, L639): "to make an application for controlled work must not exceed 50%"
- D04 (Doc 1, L643): "Since August 2024, for Immigration legal aid, the restriction has been increased to allow 75% of Controlled Work Matters"
- D04 (Doc 1, L694): "The exceptions to this are where providers only hold contracts in one or more of the following categories: Claims Against Public Authorities, Clinical Negligence, Community Care, Discrimination, Education and Public Law."
