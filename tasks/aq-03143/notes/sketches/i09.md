## Question

Once the final fee changes are in force, what is a firm outside London paid in total for 10 hours' preparation on a new non-asylum immigration legal help matter, plus 3 hours attending county court with counsel in each of a new housing and a new discrimination case?

## Answer

£897.25

## Evidence

- D04 (Doc 1, L1121): "£48.24  35%  £317"
