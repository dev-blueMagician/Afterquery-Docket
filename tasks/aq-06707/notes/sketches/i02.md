## Question

Dividing Vertex's CASGEVY revenue from launch through September 30, 2025 by the number of patients infused over that period, what was the average revenue per patient? Answer to the nearest thousand dollars.

## Answer

$1,833,000

## Evidence

- D07 (CASGEVY highlights, Q3 2025 release): "39 people with SCD or TDT have received infusions of CASGEVY, including 10 people infused in the third quarter of 2025"
- D07 (Total Revenues table, note 1): "In the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, "Other product revenues" included $16.9 million and $61.5 million from CASGEVY"
- D08 (Product Revenues table, note 1): "The three and twelve month periods ending December 31, 2024, included CASGEVY revenue of $8.0 million and $10.0 million, respectively."

## Lever stack

Shape: Hard shape A (rebuild a figure no document prints) with guide shape 1 (identify the period, then work out the answer)

- L1: Ask about a period no document reports on its own | trap: D07 "In the three and nine months ended September 30, 2024, "Other product revenues" included $2.0 million from CASGEVY" | settles: D08 "The three and twelve month periods ending December 31, 2024, included CASGEVY revenue of $8.0 million and $10.0 million, respectively." | missed gives: $1,628,000
- L2: Like for like across a definition change | trap: D07 "In the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, "Other product revenues" included $16.9 million and $61.5 million from CASGEVY" | settles: D06 "In the third quarter of 2024, Vertex’s product revenue included revenue from the first patient infused with CASGEVY." | missed gives: $1,577,000

Steps:

1. Fix the window: D07's 39 patients are counted "since launch through September 30th, 2025"; the first patient was infused in the third quarter of 2024 (D06), so the revenue window covers the second half of 2024 and the first nine months of 2025.
2. Take revenue on that window from different documents: nine months of 2025 $61.5M (D07 note); all of 2024 $10.0M (D08 note: $8.0M in the fourth quarter and $10.0M for the year), not D07's nine-month 2024 column ($2.0M), which stops before the fourth quarter of 2024.
3. Compute: (10.0 + 61.5) / 39 = $1.8333M -> $1,833,000.

Decoy: $1,628,000 (D07's own two columns: (61.5 + 2.0) / 39)
