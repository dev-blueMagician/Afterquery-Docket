## Question

Dividing each quarter's CASGEVY revenue by the number of patients infused that quarter, how much lower was the figure in Vertex's 2025 quarter with the fewest CASGEVY infusions than in its 2025 quarter with the most? Answer to the nearest thousand dollars.

## Answer

$35,000

## Evidence

- D04 (CASGEVY highlights, Q2 2025 release): "29 patients have received infusions of CASGEVY, including 16 infused in the second quarter of 2025"
- D07 (CASGEVY highlights, Q3 2025 release): "39 people with SCD or TDT have received infusions of CASGEVY, including 10 people infused in the third quarter of 2025"
- D09 (CASGEVY highlights, Q4 2025 release): "This reflects 64 patients receiving infusions of CASGEVY in 2025, including 30 people infused in the fourth quarter of 2025."
- D09 (Total Revenues table, note 1): "“Other product revenues” included $54.3 million and $115.8 million from CASGEVY"
- D01 (Total Revenues table, note 1): "In the first quarter of 2025, "Other product revenues" included $14.2 million from CASGEVY"

- L2: Exact table vs rounded prose | trap: D09 "Vertex recorded fourth quarter 2025 CASGEVY revenue of $54 million" | settles: D09 "“Other product revenues” included $54.3 million and $115.8 million from CASGEVY" | missed gives: $25,000

Steps:

1. Find each 2025 quarter's infusions: Q2 16 (D04), Q3 10 (D07), Q4 30 (D09); Q1 is printed nowhere and is 64 - 30 - 10 - 16 = 8 (D09's calendar-2025 total), not the 13 that D04's since-launch count gives (29 - 16 includes the 5 patients infused in 2024: 39 - (64 - 30)).

3. Compute: Q4 54.3 / 30 = $1.810M; Q1 14.2 / 8 = $1.775M; gap $35,000.

Decoy: $120,000 (Q3 2025, 10 infused, taken as the fewest: 54.3/30 - 16.9/10)
