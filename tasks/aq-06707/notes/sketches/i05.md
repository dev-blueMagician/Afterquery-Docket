## Question

On the balance-sheet presentation Vertex used at December 31, 2025, by how much did its other long-term liabilities change during 2024? Give the signed change in millions of dollars, to one decimal place.

## Answer

-$239.2 million

## Evidence

- D09 (Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets, December 31, 2025 and 2024): "|Other long-term liabilities|1,269.5|1,014.6|"
- D08 (Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheets, December 31, 2024 and 2023): "|Long-term finance lease liabilities|112.8|376.1|"
- D08 (same table): "|Other long-term liabilities|901.8|877.7|"

## Lever stack

Shape: guide shape 2 (compare original and revised figures, then use the right one) with Hard shape A (rebuild a figure no document prints)

- L1: Restated figures | trap: D08 "Other long-term liabilities" | settles: D09 "Other long-term liabilities" | missed gives: +$24.1 million
- L2: Like for like across a definition change | trap: D03 "Other long-term liabilities" | settles: D08 "Long-term finance lease liabilities" | missed gives: +$136.9 million

Steps:

1. Read the named presentation: the year-end 2025 balance sheet (D09) has no long-term finance lease line, and its December 31, 2024 "Other long-term liabilities" is 1,014.6, where the year-end 2024 release (D08) showed 901.8 plus 112.8 of long-term finance lease liabilities (901.8 + 112.8 = 1,014.6): the later presentation folds long-term finance leases into the line.
2. Put December 31, 2023 on that presentation, which no document prints: D08 (or D03, D06) gives 877.7 of other long-term liabilities and 376.1 of long-term finance lease liabilities, so 877.7 + 376.1 = 1,253.8.
3. Change during 2024: 1,014.6 - 1,253.8 = -239.2.

Decoy: +$136.9 million (D09's restated December 31, 2024 figure less D08's December 31, 2023 figure as printed)
