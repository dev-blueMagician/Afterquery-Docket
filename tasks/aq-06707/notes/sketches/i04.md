## Question

From the second quarter of 2024 through the second quarter of 2026, which of its drug candidates, if any, did Vertex stop developing altogether? List their VX codes in ascending order.

## Answer

VX-264, VX-522, VX-634, VX-668

## Evidence

- D01 (Type 1 diabetes pipeline, Q1 2025 release): "Vertex previously announced that it has discontinued development of the VX-264 (“cells plus device” program), as the Phase 1/2 study did not meet its efficacy endpoint."
- D02 (CF pipeline, Q1 2026 release): "Vertex has ended the Phase 1/2 study of VX-522 after observing persistent tolerability issues in the study. The early termination precludes assessment of efficacy and full safety and prevents further development of the VX-522 program."
- D03 (AATD pipeline, Q2 2024 release): "Vertex has determined that VX-634 and VX-668, two investigational small molecule AAT correctors, would not deliver transformative efficacy for people with AATD. As such, Vertex has discontinued development of both molecules."
