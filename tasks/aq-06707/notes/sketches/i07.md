## Question

What net gain or loss did Vertex record from changes in the fair value of its strategic investments in the second half of 2025? State whether it was a gain or a loss, in millions of dollars to one decimal place.

## Answer

Gain of $4.6 million

## Evidence

- D09 (GAAP to non-GAAP reconciliation, three and twelve months ended December 31): "|(Increase) decrease in fair value of strategic investments|(7.2)|7.2|5.0|57.7|"
