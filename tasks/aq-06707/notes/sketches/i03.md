## Question

By what percentage did Vertex's cystic fibrosis product revenue grow in the third quarter of 2024 over the third quarter of 2023? Give the answer to two decimal places.

## Answer

11.53%

## Evidence

- D06 (Product Revenues table, Q3 2024 release): "|Product revenues, net|$2,771.9|$2,483.5|$8,108.1|$7,351.5|"
- D06 (CASGEVY highlights): "In the third quarter of 2024, Vertex’s product revenue included revenue from the first patient infused with CASGEVY."
- D07 (Total Revenues table, note 1, Q3 2025 release): "In the three and nine months ended September 30, 2024, "Other product revenues" included $2.0 million from CASGEVY and no revenues from JOURNAVX."

Steps:

1. Identify what the Q3 2024 product revenue line holds: TRIKAFTA/KAFTRIO plus "Other product revenues", which in Q3 2024 included revenue from the first CASGEVY patient (D06 prose); the amount, $2.0M, is printed only in the next year's release (D07 note 1).
2. Take the CF figures on one basis: Q3 2024 2,771.9 - 2.0 = 2,769.9 (D06 table less D07 note); Q3 2023 2,483.5 (D06 table; no CASGEVY revenue before Q3 2024).
3. Compute: 2,769.9 / 2,483.5 - 1 = 11.532% -> 11.53%.
