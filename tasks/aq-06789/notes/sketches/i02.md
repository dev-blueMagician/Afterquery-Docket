## Question

By what percentage did the number of FEMA's own employees managing and overseeing the National Flood Insurance Program grow from GAO's April 2005 testimony to its December 2007 report? Answer to the nearest whole percent.

## Answer

70%

## Evidence

- D02 (testimony of April 14, 2005): "about 40 FEMA employees are responsible for regulating, managing, and overseeing the program"
- D02 (same paragraph): "FEMA is assisted in this effort by about 170 contractor employees."
- D09 (report of December 28, 2007, Background): "As of September 2007, FEMA had about 68 employees, assisted by about 170 contractor employees"

Steps:

1. Take the April 2005 count of FEMA's own NFIP staff, about 40 (D02), not FEMA's agency-wide 2,500 staff of March 2003.
2. Take the count printed in the December 2007 report, about 68 as of September 2007 (D09), keeping the 170 contractor employees out in both years.
3. Compute (68 - 40) / 40 = 70%.
