## Question

Had the final 111 Contact Code amendments kept their draft's rule on when they start, on what date would they have come into force, and how many days before the grace period ended is that?

## Answer

26 September 2024 and 97 days

## Components

- c1: 26 September 2024
- c2: 97 days

## Reasoning

Step 1. Source input (Doc 12, commencement clause of the draft amending instrument; Doc 11 restates it): the draft says the Amended Code comes into force on a date that is 3 months following the publication of the Amended Code.
Step 2. Source input (Doc 14, cover and status note; Doc 3 carries the same date): the amended Code, with the amendments as finally made, was published on 26 June 2024.
Step 3. Application (Doc 12 and Doc 14): kept by the final amendments, the clause would count from their own publication, and 3 months after 26 June 2024 is 26 September 2024. That is the first answer.
Step 4. Source input (Doc 14, grace period clause; Doc 3 gives the same date): the grace period for complying with the amendments ends on 1 January 2025.
Step 5. Calculation (Doc 12 and Doc 14): September has 30 days, so the days left in it after the 26th number: 30 - 26 = 4
Step 6. Calculation (Doc 12 and Doc 14): October is one day longer than September, so its length in days is: 30 + 1 = 31
Step 7. Calculation (Doc 12 and Doc 14): October and November together hold: 31 + 30 = 61
Step 8. Calculation (Doc 12 and Doc 14): adding December, which is as long as October: 61 + 31 = 92
Step 9. Calculation (Doc 12 and Doc 14): with the days left in September: 92 + 4 = 96
Step 10. Final answer (Doc 12 and Doc 14): the first answer is the date from Step 3, 26 September 2024. Counting 1 January 2025 itself, the days from that date to the end of the grace period, the second answer, number: 96 + 1 = 97
