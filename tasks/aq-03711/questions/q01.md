## Question

At how many of its retirement villages and care homes did Bupa itself owe 111 Contact Code duties on the Commission's preliminary view, and how many under its final decision?

## Answer

39 (preliminary view) and none (final decision)

## Components

- c1: 39 (preliminary view)
- c2: none (final decision)

## Reasoning

Step 1. Source input (Doc 11, "About Bupa"): Bupa operates 35 retirement villages and 39 care homes in New Zealand. Its care homes offer rest home and hospital care, and certain of them also offer dementia and psychogeriatric care.
Step 2. Source input (Doc 11, discussion of how the Code would apply to aged care Facilities): Bupa does not supply landlines to people in independent and assisted living such as its retirement villages, who may contract directly with a retail provider. It offers no landline to dementia and psychogeriatric residents. Rest-home and hospital-level residents may choose to be provided with a landline by Bupa.
Step 3. Source input (Doc 3, context for the decision on health care service providers and retirement villages): the Commission's preliminary view was that the party holding the direct billing relationship with the consumer is subject to the Code. That is the Facility where it sells the landline or bundles it with other costs, and not the Facility whose residents buy their own service from a retail service provider.
Step 4. Application (Doc 11 and Doc 3): on that view Bupa is caught only where it supplies the landline itself. That is its care homes, every one of which offers rest home and hospital care, so 39 sites. Its 35 retirement villages are the case in which residents buy their own service, and the view does not reach Bupa there.
Step 5. Source input (Doc 3, final decision and worked examples; the amended Code in Doc 14 carries the same exclusion in its definition of provider): the final decision takes suppliers of health care services out of the Code. A Facility offering rest home, hospital, dementia and psychogeriatric care has no obligations under the Code even if it provides landlines to its residents. Retirement villages stay within the Code only where the operator provides the landline: a village that offers no landline service has no obligations.
Step 6. Final answer (Doc 11 and Doc 3): under the final decision Bupa's 39 care homes are excluded as health care services, and its 35 retirement villages carry no obligations because Bupa supplies no landlines there. The answer is 39 under the preliminary view and none under the final decision.
