## Question

At how many of its retirement villages and care homes did Bupa itself owe 111 Contact Code duties on the Commission's preliminary view, and how many under its final decision?

## Answer

39 (preliminary view) and none (final decision)

## Evidence

- D04 (L7, para 2): "Bupa operates 35 retirement villages and 39 care homes across New Zealand"
- D04 (L7, para 2): "Bupa’s care homes offer rest home and hospital care, with certain care homes offering dementia and psychogeriatric care."
- D04 (L47, para 12): "We do not supply landlines to these consumers, though they may choose to contract directly with a retail services provider"
- D04 (L48, para 13): "who may choose to be provided with a landline. The Commission appears to be suggesting that the Code should cover our provision of landline services to these residents."
- D06 (L464, para 70): "It was our preliminary view that the party who holds the direct billing relationship with the consumer (ie, the Facility in scenarios A and B) is subject to the Previous Code and must comply with all requirements."
- D06 (L570, para 94.1): "As these are all ‘health care services’, they are excluded from the scope of the Code. As such, the Facility has no obligations under the Code, even if they provide landline services to their residents."
- D06 (L571, para 94.2): "This Facility does not offer landline services to its residents. Residents must source their own residential landline service from a provider. As such, the Facility is not in scope of the Code and has no obligations under the Code."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. One operator's own account of where it supplies landlines, run through the Commission's
preliminary billing-relationship view and then its final decision. The natural reading of the final decision
("the Code does apply to retirement villages") is overturned by a fact only the operator states.

Ambiguity:
- 74 on the preliminary view -> fails "bring under the Code": the view reaches the party with the direct billing
  relationship (D06 L464), and Bupa supplies no landlines in its villages (D04 L47), which is Scenario C (D06 L463).
- 35 on the final decision -> fails the same test: Example 2 (D06 L571) puts a village that offers no landline
  outside the Code; D06 L573 (Example 3) needs the village to sell landlines.
- 39 on the final decision -> fails D06 L529 and L570: providers of health care services are out even where they
  supply landlines.
- Fewer than 39 on the preliminary view (only care homes with a resident who has taken a landline) -> no document
  gives such a number; every care home offers rest-home and hospital care (D04 L7) and landlines are offered to
  those residents (D04 L48), so 39 is the only count the documents support. Open point for the form's "One
  defensible answer" check.
- Residents rather than sites -> the question names villages and care homes.

Necessity:
- D04 (Doc 11): the two site counts and where Bupa supplies landlines. Searched every other document for
  "35 retirement", "39 care", "Bupa": D06 cites Bupa's submission by page (L479, L524) without counts or supply
  facts. Without D04 neither number is reachable.
- D06 (Doc 3): the final decision (health care services excluded; a village offering no landline has no
  obligations). Also in D05 L189 (definition of provider), so Doc 14 is an alternative for the exclusion; group
  "Doc 3 or Doc 14". Without both, "none" is unreachable (D04 predates the decision).
- The preliminary view is in D01 L480, restated in D06 L464 and D04 L41: D01 is not necessary and is not ticked.

After clarification: two indispensable groups remain (Doc 11; Doc 3 or Doc 14). The wording supplies no source fact.

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Who belongs where; same series, right edition. Wrong routes recomputed:
35 + 39 = 74 (every site, preliminary view); 35 (villages, final decision); 39 (care homes, final decision).

Source risks: D06 and D05 both carry the exclusion; the preliminary view sits in three documents. "none" is a
zero, paired here with 39 so that it cannot be guessed alone.

Author calculation: no arithmetic in the gold. Site counts read from D04 L7; 35 + 39 = 74 is the careless total.
