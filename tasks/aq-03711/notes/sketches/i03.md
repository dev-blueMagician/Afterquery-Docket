## Question

Under the draft Code's own commencement clause, on what date would the amendments have come into force, and how many days before the grace period ended is that?

## Answer

26 September 2024 and 97 days

## Evidence

- D08 (clause 5, L37): "This Amended Code comes into force on a date that is 3 months following the publication of the Amended Code."
- D05 (cover, L13): "Date of publication: 26 June 2024"
- D05 (clause 3, L110): "There is a grace period starting on 1 July 2024 and ending on 1 January 2025"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2. A commencement rule only the draft states, applied to the publication date only the final
documents give, then measured against the grace period's end.

Ambiguity:
- 1 October 2024 and 92 days -> fails "following the publication" (D08 L37): the clause counts from publication
  (26 June 2024, D05 L13), not from the date the amendments came into force (1 July 2024, D05 L20).
- 18 March 2024 (three months after the draft's own publication, 18 December 2023) -> the clause speaks of the
  publication of "the Amended Code", which the final documents date 26 June 2024 (D05 L58-59).
- "ended" is the date the Code prints for the end of the grace period, 1 January 2025; no before/after reading is
  needed.

Necessity:
- D08 (Doc 12): the 3-month clause. Also restated in D04 L92 ("will come into force 3 months following
  publication"), so "Doc 12 or Doc 11". Not in D01, D05 or D06 (searched "3 months", "three months").
- D05 (Doc 14): publication date and grace period. Also in D06 L12, L106, so "Doc 14 or Doc 3".

After clarification: two groups remain (Doc 12 or Doc 11; Doc 14 or Doc 3).

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Calculate across documents; proposal versus final dates. Wrong route:
three months from 1 July 2024 = 1 October 2024, 92 days.

Source risks: date-count family, which the easier solver has handled on sister bundles; every input is also

Author calculation: 26 June 2024 + 3 months = 26 September 2024; 1 January 2025 - 26 September 2024 = 97 days
(script, 2026-10-12: 4 remaining days of September + 31 + 30 + 31 + 1).
