## Question

Had the Commerce Commission's final decision come the day submissions closed, what is the earliest date the caps could start while satisfying both ASB's and Westpac's timing requests?

## Answer

1 April 2027

## Evidence

- D13 (Executive summary X17): "Please submit your views by 12pm on Monday 13 July 2026."
- D02 (ASB Recommendation 3): "Set an implementation date of no earlier than 8 months from the announcement date"
- D16 (paragraph 3.21): "Westpac considers that a minimum six-month implementation period, with changes taking effect from 1 March 2027 at the earliest"
- D16 (paragraph 3.23): "any interchange changes should align with a first-of-month effective date."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h13. A scenario dated by an event (submissions closing, 13 July 2026, Doc 1), run through two banks' timing requests whose rules the question does not name: ASB's eight months (Doc 4) lands on 13 March 2027, twelve days past the date Westpac prints (1 March 2027), and Westpac's separate first-of-month request (Doc 14, paragraph 3.23) then moves the start to 1 April 2027. Shape: one case, each party's rule changing the answer, every wrong route with its own date (calibration recipes 2 and 3).
Ambiguity: (a) 1 March 2027 (Westpac's printed date, also six months plus the floor) -> fails "both ASB's": eight months from 13 July 2026 is 13 March 2027. (b) 13 March 2027 -> fails "Westpac's timing requests": not a first of the month (D16 L95). (c) 13 January 2027 (six months) -> fails Westpac's own "from 1 March 2027 at the earliest". (d) Survey closing day 29 June 2026 as the anchor (D13 L1129, D09 L22) -> fails "submissions closed"; it would give 1 March 2027. (e) ASB's "6 months" is the acquirers' share inside the eight (D02 L48), not a second request. (f) Westpac's December-to-February concern (D16 L93) is met by 1 April. "Announcement date" is the final decision (D02 L35: "After the Commission confirms the caps"). Month arithmetic by calendar month: 13 July 2026 + 8 months = 13 March 2027.
Necessity: D13 -> the closing date (stated only in D13, L117, L212, L1132; D05 is merely dated 13 July 2026) -> without it no start date exists. D02 -> the eight months (only D02, L48 and L137) -> without it the answer is Westpac's 1 March 2027. D16 -> the first-of-month request and the 1 March floor (only D16) -> without it the answer is 13 March 2027.
