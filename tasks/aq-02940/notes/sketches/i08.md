## Question

If the Commerce Commission adopted the interim differential Mastercard asks for pending American Express's designation, by how many percentage points would the implied average interchange on foreign-issued commercial credit exceed the domestic one?

## Answer

0.29 percentage points

## Evidence

- D08 (Policy Recommendations, item 3): "Pending designation, any interim differential should match the observed MSF differential between Amex and the regulated networks."
- D08 (Competition and Network Effects): "which on the Commission's own data is in the order of 40 basis points"
- D13 (paragraph 3.7.2): "We have applied 0.10% to reflect this."
- D13 (Table 3.1, domestic total row): "Total 1.39% 0.78% 0.78%"
- D13 (Table 3.1, foreign-issued total row): "Total 1.99% 1.37% NA"
- D13 (paragraph 3.49): "our view is that no additional premiums over personal credit cards are warranted for foreignissued commercial credit cards."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h11 (independent author's second sketch, re-asked as a domestic-to-foreign gap so that two unstated scope facts both decide the answer): Mastercard's 40 basis points replaces only the 0.10% market-stability part of the 0.20% premium (D13 L593), and that part exists only in the domestic caps (D13 L812), so the domestic implied average moves from 0.78% to 1.08% while the foreign-issued 1.37% stays.
Ambiguity: (a) 0.59 (nothing moved, or both moved) -> fails "adopted the interim differential": the domestic caps rise, the foreign ones carry no such allowance. (b) 0.39 (whole 0.20% premium swapped for 0.40%) -> D13 L591-593 splits the premium 0.10% + 0.10% and D08 L17 calls the mitigant "a 10-basis point differential". (c) 0.19 (0.40% added on top) -> D08 L80 asks for the differential to "reflect" the observed gap, i.e. be 40 points, not 50. The 1:1 move of the implied average follows from the Commission's method (D13 L553, fn 69: caps weighted by transaction mix).
Necessity: D08 -> the 40 basis points and the interim request (only D08, L80, L140, L172). D13 -> the foreign-issued 1.37% (only D13, L580, L806); the 0.78% is also in D07 and D04 and the 0.10% split also in D11. Coincidence: ASB's 50 bps over personal caps (D02 L32) gives the same domestic 1.08%.
