## Question

What percentage of classic members' 2021/22 contribution rate at £60,000 was the widows' and widowers' element, to one decimal place?

## Answer

20.4%

## Evidence

- D02 (Doc 1, 3.5): "it is proposed to rollover member contribution rates and salary thresholds from 2021/22 for use in 2022/23"
- D02 (Doc 1, draft reg 4): "£56,001 up to but not including £150,001"
- D02 (Doc 1, draft reg 4): "Member contribution rates 4.6% 5.45% 7.35% 8.05%"
- D03 (Doc 4, 4.9): "The basic 1.5% widows and widowers pension scheme (WPS) contribution that was a feature of the PCSPS classic section was accounted for in the equalised contribution rates"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: n2. Doc 1 prints the 2022/23 bands as the rolled-over 2021/22 bands (7.35% from £56,001 to
£150,000). Doc 4 says classic's 1.5% WPS contribution sits inside the equalised rate. 1.5 / 7.35 = 20.41%.

Ambiguity:
- 16.9% (1.5 / 8.85, WPS added on top of 7.35%): fails Doc 4's "accounted for in the equalised contribution rates".
- 27.5% (1.5 / 5.45, wrong band) or 18.6% (1.5 / 8.05): fail the £60,000 band in Doc 1's table.
- Marginal bands (blended rate on £60,000): the table assigns one rate by the annualised rate of pensionable earnings.
- "2021/22" vs the printed "2022/23" table: Doc 1 3.4-3.5 state the 2022/23 rates and thresholds are 2021/22's rolled
  over, and Doc 2 5.2 confirms the same percentages and bands; no other 2021/22 table exists in the bundle.

Necessity: Doc 1 (D02) is the only source of the rates (Doc 2 confirms "same percentages" without printing them).
Doc 4 (D03) is the only source of the 1.5% WPS element (grep "WPS|widows": no other document). Both remain needed.

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Part vs total; Document convention. Wrong routes above.

Source risks: alpha band rates (4.6/5.45/7.35/8.05) and classic's 1.5% widow's contribution are widely published, so a
closed-book reach is plausible; two lookups plus one division, the profile measured Easy or too easy elsewhere.

Author calculation: 1.5 / 7.35 = 0.204082 -> 20.4% (one decimal). Decoys: 1.5 / 8.85 = 16.95%; 1.5 / 5.45 = 27.52%;
1.5 / 8.05 = 18.63%.

Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: secondary to i01 (closed-book and too-easy risk higher).
