## Question

How many 2016 scheme members were in the only protection group whose pensions in payment change without a remedy choice?

## Answer

54,807

## Evidence

- D01 (Doc 2, EQIA Table 1): "Civil Service Pension Scheme Protection Status at 31st March 2016"
- D01 (Doc 2, EQIA Table 1): "Tapered Protected Members 54,807"
- D03 (Doc 4, 4.45): "Changes to benefits in payment would only be triggered by a member’s immediate section 6 election or by default where the election period ends and no election has been made by a tapered member"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: n7 (new). Rule in the 2023 papers applied to the 2021-22 papers' exact 2016 counts. With no election
by the deadline: PCSPS benefits in payment continue (Doc 3 4.12); all-alpha (unprotected) remediable service gets a
deemed election, so alpha benefits continue unchanged (Doc 3 3.5, 4.9 condition A); taper-protected mixed service
cannot continue in payment and the scheme manager pays the higher of PCSPS or alpha (Doc 3 4.8, 4.18; Doc 4 4.45).
Ineligible groups have no remedy choice. So the only group is tapered protection: 54,807 at 31 March 2016.

Ambiguity:
- 222,424 (Eligible Unprotected): Doc 3 4.9 condition A deems an election for all-alpha service; a careless reader
  takes "deemed election" for a change. Fails Doc 3 3.5 ("won't see a change to their benefits") and Doc 4 4.45 (only
  tapered members by default).
- 105,875 (Protected): fails Doc 3 4.12 (default is to continue PCSPS benefits).
- 277,231 / 160,682 (two groups combined): fail "the only protection group".
- Population: Table 1 counts all scheme members by protection group at 31 March 2016, not pensioners only; the question
  asks for the group's 2016 membership, so 54,807 stands. Doc 1 and Doc 2 print the same Table 1 (group "or").

Necessity: Doc 1 or Doc 2 (Table 1; the only counts by protection group). Doc 3 or Doc 4 (the default rule; Doc 1/2
cover only the 2022 prospective remedy and never discuss missed elections or mixed benefits in payment). Without the
2023 papers a reader could still guess "tapered" from real-world McCloud knowledge (stated risk).

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Applying the right rule (the page's under-supplied idea type); Part vs total
decoys above. Not a which-items list.

Source risks: Doc 4 4.45 states the rule in one plain sentence (the lookup profile measured too easy elsewhere);
Doc 1's flattened Table 1 misaligns labels and values (Doc 2's layout is clean); the table quote spans two lines.
