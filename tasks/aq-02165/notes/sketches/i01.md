## Question

Under the 2017 compensation proposals, how many years before their MOD-funded pension age could MDP officers first get employer-funded pension top-ups?

## Answer

7 years

## Evidence

- D05 (Doc 5, 4.3 proposals): "only to allow employer funded top up to pension from age 55 and for this to track 10"
- D05 (Doc 5, 5.2): "which the proposals increase to age 55, and this is tracked 10 years behind the State Pension Age"
- D01 (Doc 2, 4.9 DPF extract): "The MOD has agreed to fund a lower NPA for MDP officers by paying for an effective pension age equal to three years below State pension age"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: n1. Two age rules from two documents, each stated relative to State Pension age (SPA). Earliest
top-up age = SPA - 10 (55 when SPA is 65; Doc 5 L301-305, L328, L344). MOD-funded effective pension age = SPA - 3
(Doc 2 L359). The officer's SPA cancels: (SPA - 3) - (SPA - 10) = 7 years for every SPA of 65 or more. No document
prints 7 or connects the two rules; the question applies the 2017 rule to the officers Doc 2 describes.

Ambiguity:
- 5 (Home Office police NPA "age 60", Doc 2 L357, minus 55): fails "MOD-funded" and "MDP officers"; Doc 2 L357 is the
  Home Office's 2015 Police Pension Scheme. The "officers who are over age 60" clause (L359) is a hardship group, not a
  pension age.
- 9 (or 8/10): age 55 held fixed against SPA - 3 for an assumed SPA of 67 (66/68). Fails Doc 5's own "track 10 years
  behind State Pension Age"; and without that rule the question has no determinate answer, because no SPA is given.
  At SPA 65 both readings give 7.
- 10 (alpha NPA = SPA, Doc 2 L510, minus SPA - 10): fails "MOD-funded"; the MOD funds the lower effective age, not NPA.
- Open point: "State Pension Age" in Doc 5 is read as the member's own SPA, the same SPA Doc 2's rule uses. A general
  (calendar) SPA reading would break the cancellation; no passage defines it either way. Risk recorded.

Necessity:
- Doc 5 (D05): the only source of the top-up rule (55 / SPA - 10); grep "top up", "behind State", "age 55" finds no
  other document. Without it the top-up age is unknown (recall of the later CSCS terms is the substitute risk).
- Doc 2 (D01): the only source of the MDP arrangement (SPA - 3); Doc 4 L561 names the MDP but gives no pension age;
  Doc 4's EEPA gloss ("between 65 and 60") is a different, non-MDP option. Without it the MDP pension age is unknown
  (a reader might assume police NPA 60, giving 5).
After clarification: both documents still needed; the wording supplies neither rule.

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Applying the right rule; Look-alike measure (60 beside the MDP sentence).
Wrong routes recomputed below. Not a which-items question, so no decoy universe is required.

Source risks: constructed comparison of two rules no document connects (reviewer may call it manufactured); small
integer (closed-book guess); whether the 2017 CSCS proposals cover MDP officers (MDP are civil service scheme members,
Doc 2 L501; the DPF was a party to the 2016 compensation offer, Doc 5 L65); proposal status (2017 proposals, not final).

Author calculation: SPA S in {65, 66, 67, 68}: top-up T = S - 10 = 55, 56, 57, 58; EPA E = S - 3 = 62, 63, 64, 65;
E - T = 7 in every case. Wrong routes: fixed 55 gives E - 55 = 7, 8, 9, 10; police NPA 60 - 55 = 5; alpha NPA S - T = 10.

Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: author checks pass with the SPA-reading risk; mechanical screen next.
