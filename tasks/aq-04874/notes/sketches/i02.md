## Question

How many cents per dollar of superannuation, to one decimal place, does the average superannuation shock in the RBA's 2020 consumption stress test remove from counted wealth when the household head is the 2022/23 median housing-investor age?

## Answer

16.5 cents

## Evidence

- D02 (Doc 1, Section 3.2, l.1114, char ~75,100): "superannuation is only included in households’ assets if the household head is over 40 years old, and it is vested linearly until they are 60 years old"
- D02 (Doc 1, Table 4, l.967-994, char ~56,300): "Superannuation(a) and trust funds"
- D02 (Doc 1, Table 4 note, l.994): "(a) Average change, change depends on household age profile given different returns on different asset classes"
- D03 (Doc 4, l.207, char ~11,720): "45 to 51 years between 1999/2000 and 2022/23"
