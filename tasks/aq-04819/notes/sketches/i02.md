## Question

How many children and teenagers took part in the studies included in the systematic review of Internet gambling marketing in which their parents were also participants?

## Answer

213

## Evidence

- D03 (Doc 5, Table 2A, Pitt et al. (42) row): "Mixed method study of 152 parent/child dyads (children 8 to 16-years old) conducted on Australian football League, national Rugby League, and soccer sporting sites in Australia"
- D03 (Doc 5, Table 2A, Pitt et al. (43) row): "Qualitative study conducted with 59 family groups (at least 1 parent and 1 adolescent 14 to 18-years-old) in Australia"
- D04 (Doc 9, results): "A total of 59 parents and 61 adolescents were interviewed across 59 family groups"
