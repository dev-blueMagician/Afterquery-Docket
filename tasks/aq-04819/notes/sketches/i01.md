## Question

How many children and teenagers in total were surveyed at sports venues in the studies that the systematic review of Internet gambling marketing includes?

## Answer

263

## Evidence

- D03 (Doc 5, Table 2A, Pitt et al. (42) row): "Mixed method study of 152 parent/child dyads (children 8 to 16-years old) conducted on Australian football League, national Rugby League, and soccer sporting sites in Australia"
- D03 (Doc 5, Table 2A, Pitt et al. (42) row): "304 participants were included"
- D03 (Doc 5, Table 2A, Thomas et al. (45) row): "Influence of online gambling advertising on young people (11 to 16-years-old) who were basket-ball fans"
- D10 (Doc 8, abstract): "Interviews were conducted at basketball stadiums between May and July 2018"
- D10 (Doc 8, sampling): "Young people were recruited between May and July 2018 at three local community basketball stadiums in Victoria, Australia"
