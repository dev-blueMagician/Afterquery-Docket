## Question

Relative to nominal capacity, which lithium-ion cells, if any, besides NASA cell B0005 end their constant-voltage charge at a current no higher than B0005's? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

B0006, B0007, PANASONIC NCA cells

## Evidence

- D05 (Doc 1, Research methodology, line 65): "batteries B0005, B0006, and B0007 utilize lithium nickel cobalt aluminum oxide (NCA) as the positive electrode and graphite as the negative electrode, with a nominal capacity of 2 Ah"
- D05 (Doc 1, line 65): "battery B0018 utilizes lithium nickel manganese cobalt oxide (NMC) for the positive electrode and graphite for the negative electrode, with a nominal capacity of 1.35 Ah"
- D05 (Doc 1, line 65): "with a cutoff voltage of 4.2 V and a cutoff current of 20 mA"
- D03 (Doc 7, Table 1, line 49): "|#3|PANASONIC|NCA/ Graphite|3.03|2.5-4.2|2770|1801|"
- D03 (Doc 7, Methods, Battery cycling and dataset generation, line 191): "then hold at 4.2 V until the charging current drops below 0.03 A"
- D03 (Doc 7, Data generation, line 41): "C-rate is a measure of the battery’s charge or discharge current relative to its nominal capacity"
