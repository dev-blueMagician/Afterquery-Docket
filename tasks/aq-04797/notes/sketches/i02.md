## Question

What is the combined nominal capacity of the NASA cells the LSTM–PPO hybrid study models but the DTV-feature PSO-GRU study does not, in ampere-hours to two decimal places?

## Answer

3.35 Ah

## Evidence

- D05 (Doc 1, Research methodology, line 65): "four different models of 18,650 lithium-ion batteries (B0005, B0006, B0007, and B0018)"
- D05 (Doc 1, line 65): "batteries B0005, B0006, and B0007 utilize lithium nickel cobalt aluminum oxide (NCA) as the positive electrode and graphite as the negative electrode, with a nominal capacity of 2 Ah"
- D05 (Doc 1, line 65): "battery B0018 utilizes lithium nickel manganese cobalt oxide (NMC) for the positive electrode and graphite for the negative electrode, with a nominal capacity of 1.35 Ah"
- D06 (Doc 4, 1.1, line 31): "Battery B0005 was selected as the subject of this study"
- D06 (Doc 4, 3.4, line 316): "introduces another independent dataset—the NASA B0007 battery dataset—for cross-validation"
