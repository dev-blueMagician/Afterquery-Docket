## Question

On cells each model never saw, what is the GAT-BiGRU SOH model's average R² minus the fast-charging CNN–self-attention model's, to three decimal places?

## Answer

0.018

## Evidence

- D08 (Doc 6, 4.4 Model Generalization Performance Analysis, line 215): "training the model on data from the first four batteries in Batch1 and testing it on the remaining batteries from the same batch"
- D08 (Doc 6, 4.4, line 215): "The average MAE, RMSE, and R2 values for all batteries are 0.608, 0.802, and 0.977"
- D02 (Doc 5, Transfer learning, line 186): "data from the test set (target domain) was used for fine-tuning"
- D02 (Doc 5, Results of health estimation, line 212): "Before optimization, the average RMSE across 39 batteries was 0.00609 Ah, with an average R2 of 0.959"
- D02 (Doc 5, line 212, the post-fine-tuning figure the trap takes): "R2 increasing to 0.977"
