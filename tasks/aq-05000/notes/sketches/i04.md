## Question

Which, if any, business surveys run by ONS, the US Census Bureau or a regional Federal Reserve Bank have reported AI adoption rates weighted by employment? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

Business Trends and Outlook Survey, Management and Expectations Survey, Survey of Business Uncertainty

## Evidence

- D03 (Doc 1, section 3): "These adoption rates increase for all technologies when looked at by the share of employment, especially robotics and AI, as larger firms are more likely to adopt them. Adoption rates by share of employment are included in Table 1 of the accompanying dataset."
- D04 (Doc 6, Table 1, second BTOS row): "20% (employment-weighted, 6 month lookback)"
- D04 (Doc 6, Results): "This estimate is employment-weighted instead of firm-weighted"
- D05 (Doc 5, Estimated AI Adoption Rates): "The SBU estimates an employment-weighted firm AI adoption rate of around 78 percent"
- D05 (Doc 5, The Surveys): "The final source is the Survey of Business Uncertainty (SBU), which is fielded by the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta"
