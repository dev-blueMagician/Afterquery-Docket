## Question

By how many percentage points did employment weighting alone raise the Census Bureau's September 2023 to February 2024 AI adoption estimate, and the Atlanta Fed's November 2025 one? One decimal each.

## Answer

11.2 and 8.6

## Evidence

- D04 (Doc 6, Table 1, second BTOS row): "20% (employment-weighted, 6 month lookback)"
- D04 (Doc 6, Results): "most of the gap between the 5 percent and 20 percent figure is due to employment weighting with the changes in the survey question accounting for less of the gap."
- D04 (Doc 6, footnote 2): "The firm-weighted (not employment-weighted) uptake rate for the six month lookback detailed question is 8.8 percent."
- D05 (Doc 5, Table 1): "|SBU|Nov. 2025|Firms|Employment-weighted percentage|78||"
- D05 (Doc 5, footnote 9): "The SBU also publishes equal-weighted AI and LLM adoption rates for its sample of 69.4 percent and 46.4 percent."
