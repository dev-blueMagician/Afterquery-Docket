## Question

By how many percentage points, to one decimal place, was the midpoint of the 2026 growth in comparable currency neutral EPS implied by Coca-Cola's July 2026 outlook below the 2025 growth in comparable currency neutral EPS it expected in July 2025?

## Answer

1.5 percentage points

## Evidence

- D02 (Doc 1 L209, Outlook, July 22, 2025): "The company expects to deliver comparable currency neutral EPS (non-GAAP) growth of approximately 8%."
- D03 (Doc 5 L203, Full Year 2026 Guidance, Current): "|Comparable EPS (non-GAAP)|9% to 10% growth, which includes: •Approx. 3% currency tailwind1"
- D03 (Doc 5 L202, Full Year 2026 Guidance, Current): "|Comparable currency neutral EPS excluding acquisitions and divestitures (non-GAAP)|7% to 8% growth|"
- D03 (Doc 5 L472, Definitions): "Comparable currency neutral EPS (non-GAAP) has been adjusted for the impact of fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates, and comparable currency neutral EPS excluding acquisitions and divestitures (non-GAAP) has been adjusted for the impact of fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates and the impact of acquisitions and divestitures"
