## Question

To the nearest cent, how much more per hour did DEA's 2025 VA telemedicine cost estimate apply to patients' travel and wait time than VA's apportionment rule applied to claimants' time?

## Answer

$0.92

## Evidence

- D02 (VI.2.a, line 394): "Therefore, the $33.58 loaded hourly wage represents the hourly average opportunity cost to travel and wait for a practitioner's appointment."
- D02 (Table 2, line 410): "Time cost savings............................................ 33.58 0.75 25.19"
- D04 (PRA, line 177): "The total estimated cost to respondents is reduced to $2,678.12 (82 hours x $32.66/hour"
- D04 (footnote 1, line 180): "the mean hourly wage is $32.66 based on the BLS wage code"
