## Question

What is the least an SSVF grantee with a top-of-range award for grants starting October 1, 2025 could have requested before VA published the next year's notice, missing no spending milestone?

## Answer

$3,441,336

## Evidence

- D01 (I, Funding Details, line 38): "with a range of $118,000 to $22,942,240 in awards"
- D01 (II.C.5.a, line 141): "By the end of the first quarter (December 31, 2025) of the grantee's supportive services annualized grant award period, the grantee's cumulative requests for supportive services grant funds are less than an amount equal to 15% of the total supportive services grant award"
- D01 (II.C.5.b, line 142): "By the end of the second quarter (March 31, 2026)"
- D03 (header, line 3): "Federal Register, Volume 91 Issue 3 (Tuesday, January 6, 2026)"
- D03 (SUMMARY, line 21): "Awards made for grants will fund operations beginning October 1, 2026."
