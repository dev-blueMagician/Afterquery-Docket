## Question

Going by all these submissions together, how many different businesses paid or were ordered to pay a penalty in 2023 for breaches or alleged breaches of the Dairy Code or the Horticulture Code?

## Answer

5 businesses

## Evidence

- D01 (Recommendation 4 discussion, line 215): "In 2023 the ACCC successfully obtained a penalty of $950,000 in the Federal Court against dairy processor Lactalis for"
- D01 (line 217): "In 2023 the ACCC also issued infringement notices to horticulture traders Nutrano and Getfresh Merchants for trading under alleged non-compliant Horticulture Produce Agreements."
- D02 (Horticulture Code section, line 159): "in 2023 fresh fruit and vegetable supplier GetFresh Merchants Pty Ltd (GetFresh) paid penalties totalling $43,150"
- D02 (line 160): "GetFresh was the fourth fruit and vegetable trader to have paid a penalty in 2023 for breaches of the Code. Green Endeavour and Bache Bros each paid a penalty of $13,750"
- D02 (line 160): "Nutrano paid penalties totalling $24,850"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: a count that is the union of two single-document sets, each of which looks complete. The regulator's own submission names three businesses (Lactalis under the Dairy Code; Nutrano and Getfresh Merchants under the Horticulture Code). A wholesaler's submission names four Horticulture Code traders (GetFresh, Green Endeavour, Bache Bros, Nutrano). Two names are common, so the union is five.
Ambiguity: (a) 3 -> the regulator's submission alone; D02 line 160 adds Green Endeavour and Bache Bros; (b) 4 -> D02 alone ("the fourth fruit and vegetable trader"); D01 line 215 adds Lactalis under the Dairy Code; (c) 7 -> adding the two sets without matching Nutrano and Getfresh/GetFresh Merchants; (d) 6 -> adding Coles (D01 line 302, $10 million): that penalty was for unconscionable conduct under the consumer law, is not dated 2023 and is under neither code. An infringement-notice payment is covered by "alleged breaches".
Necessity: D01 -> Lactalis, the only Dairy Code penalty in the bundle (searched every document for infringement notice, fined, penalised, paid a penalty, penalty of/totalling); D02 -> Green Endeavour and Bache Bros, named nowhere else.
Delete-and-guess: without D01 the answer is 4; without D02 it is 3. Closed-book: these are real 2023 enforcement outcomes, so a solver could recall some of them; an exact count of five from recall is possible but not likely. Flag for the "Needs the documents" check.
