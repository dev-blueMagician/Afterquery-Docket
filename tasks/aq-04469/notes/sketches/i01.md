## Question

Roughly how much produce, by yearly dollar value, passes through the Melbourne, Adelaide, Perth and Newcastle central markets combined? Answer in billions of dollars to one decimal place.

## Answer

$3.8 billion

## Evidence

- D11 (1. About Us, line 42): "Australia’s six central fresh fruit and vegetable wholesale Markets (Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne, Adelaide, Perth and Newcastle)"
- D11 (1. About Us, line 47): "have an annual throughput volume of over 4 million tonnes, with a wholesale value now over $8 billion annually"
- D12 (About Freshmark, line 10): "Freshmark is based in the heart of Sydney Markets, the largest central market in Australia, transacting approximately $3 billion in produce per annum."
- D02 (history of the Brisbane Markets, line 85): "Each year, over 700 million kilograms of fresh produce valued at over $1.2 billion pass through the gates."
