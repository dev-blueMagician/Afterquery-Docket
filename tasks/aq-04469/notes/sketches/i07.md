## Question

Which of these submitters, if any, have one of their own submissions mentioned in another submitter's document? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

ACCC, Australian Dairy Farmers, Fresh Markets Australia

## Evidence

- D10 (Q10 answer, line 426): "Australian Dairy Farmers has done an analysis of the content of each of the Dairy Industry Code and the Food and Grocery Industry Code"
- D10 (line 426): "Their analysis was part of their confidential submission to the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission review of the acquisition by Coles of two Saputo dairy processing facilities."
- D10 (para 18, lines 117-118): "This assessment also underpins their 2021 submission to the review of the dairy code"
- D12 (About Freshmark, line 11): "Freshmark has also contributed to and broadly supports a submission developed by Fresh Markets Australia, our national industry body."
- D15 (line 140): "MF&G notes the ACCC’s submission in February 2023 to the review of the Code’s dispute resolution provisions"
- D11 (section 5.3, line 263): "a number of submissions, including the ACCC’s submission, called for the introduction of civil pecuniary penalty provisions"
