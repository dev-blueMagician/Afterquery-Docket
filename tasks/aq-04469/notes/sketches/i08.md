## Question

Other submitters mention submissions the ACCC made to earlier reviews. To how many different reviews were those ACCC submissions made?

## Answer

3

## Evidence

- D11 (section 5.3, line 263): "In the 2018 Final Report - Independent Review of the Food and Grocery Code of Conduct3, it was stated that “a number of submissions, including the ACCC’s submission, called for the introduction of civil pecuniary penalty provisions"
- D10 (para 18, lines 117-118): "Even at its making, the ACCC noted it would not fully correct the bargaining power imbalance, but would merely reduce some of the negative consequences."
- D10 (para 18b, line 118): "This assessment also underpins their 2021 submission to the review of the dairy code"
- D15 (line 140): "MF&G notes the ACCC’s submission in February 2023 to the review of the Code’s dispute resolution provisions"
