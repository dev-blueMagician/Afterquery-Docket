## Question

Which of these submitters, if any, cite figures published by Hort Innovation? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

Australian Fresh Produce Alliance, AUSVEG, Fruit Producers SA

## Evidence

- D06 (Key facts, line 169): "sourced largely from Hort Innovation’s Australian Horticulture Statistics Handbook 2022/233, provide valuable context and a snapshot of the fresh produce industry"
- D06 (footnote 3, line 183): "Australian Horticulture Statistics Handbook 2022/23 - Hort Innovation"
- D09 (line 51): "Harvest to Home citing 78 percent of retail fresh vegetables are sold through Coles, Woolworths, and Aldi"
- D09 (footnote 2, line 58): "‘Harvest to Home’, Hort Innovation."
- D13 (Executive summary, line 10): "These horticultural activities represent over $150 million in value of production (Source: Australian Horticultural Statistics 2022/2023), over 7% of national production in these fruits."

Fruit Producers SA gives its source as "Australian Horticultural Statistics 2022/2023" with no publisher; only AFPA's sentence says that handbook is Hort Innovation's. Fresh Markets Australia names Hort Innovation but cites no figure from it.
Ambiguity: (a) AFPA and AUSVEG only -> D13 line 10 cites the same 2022/23 statistics handbook that D06 line 169 calls Hort Innovation's; (b) adding Fresh Markets Australia -> D11 line 325 only asks the Government to "influence or mandate Hort Innovation to allocate fruit and vegetable levies"; it cites no Hort Innovation figure; (c) adding a submitter whose farm-gate figure has no stated source (AUSVEG line 21, D08, D14) -> no source is given, so only outside knowledge could tie it to Hort Innovation; (d) a strict reader could hold that "Australian Horticultural Statistics 2022/2023" and "Australian Horticulture Statistics Handbook 2022/23" are not shown to be the same publication. Same year and near-identical title; reading still possible.
Necessity: D06 -> AFPA as a member and the only statement that the handbook is Hort Innovation's; D09 -> AUSVEG as a member; D13 -> Fruit Producers SA as a member.
