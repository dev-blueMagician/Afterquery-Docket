## Question

If each UK nation's adult smoking rate repeated its 2021-to-2022 percentage-point change yearly, in what year would one first reach 5% or below?

## Answer

2032

## Evidence

- D05 (L68, ONS Adult smoking habits in the UK: 2021): "Adult smoking habits in the UK: 2021 shows that the adult smoking rate in the UK is the lowest on record"
- D05 (L72): "14.8% in Scotland"
- D02 (L42, ONS Adult smoking habits in the UK 2022): "Adult smoking habits in the UK 2022 reported that 6.4 million people in the UK were current smokers"
- D02 (L50): "13.9% in Scotland"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. Two editions of the ONS nation rates are printed in two different documents, in
different orders (Doc 3: England, Scotland, Wales, NI for 2021; Doc 1: England, Wales, NI, Scotland for
2022). Each nation's one-year change must be built by matching names across the lists, then projected.
Ambiguity: UK aggregate (13.3 -> 12.9, Doc 3/Doc 4 and Doc 1/Doc 2) gives 2042 -> fails "each UK nation";
relative (percent) change gives 2039 -> fails "percentage-point change"; floor of 9.89 years gives 2031 ->
fails "first reach 5% or below" (2031 value 5.8); England alone 2048 -> not the first; Wales (0) and NI
(+0.2) never reach it. No other 2021 or 2022 nation-level adult smoking rates exist in the bundle
(searched each value and "in Scotland/Wales/England/Northern Ireland"; Doc 5 L88 Scotland 11% is maternal
smoking at booking, 2023; Doc 1 L60 NI 21.3% is youth vaping).
Necessity: Doc 3 -> 2021 nation rates (L68-76); substitutes searched: Doc 4 has only UK 2019/2021 and
"Declines were seen across all constituent countries" (L638), Docs 1, 2, 5 none -> without it no change
can be formed. Doc 1 -> 2022 nation rates (L42-50); Doc 2 has only UK 12.9% for 2022 (L417) -> without it
no change can be formed. Recall risk: ONS nation figures for 2021/2022 might be remembered (probe).
After clarification: the wording supplies neither year's values; both documents stay necessary.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Calculate across documents; Who belongs where (repeated list
across two editions, reordered). Misaligned-by-position route recomputed: 2035.
Source risks: England printed "13%" without decimal (not decisive: England cannot be first for any
value 12.95-13.05). Projection is the Guide's own "keep falling by the same amount" shape.
Author calculation: Scotland 13.9 - 14.8 = -0.9; 13.9 - 0.9 x 9 = 5.8 (2031); 13.9 - 0.9 x 10 = 4.9 (2032);
England 12.7 - 13 = -0.3 -> 2048; Wales 0; NI +0.2. Scratchpad aq02157_calc.py (Decimal).
