## Question

By what percentage, to one decimal place, did the minimum excise duty on a 20-pack rise from the pack-insert impact assessment to the smokefree consultation response?

## Answer

21.6%

## Evidence

- D01 (L1204): "Guidance on rates and allowances for the Tobacco Products Duty estimates at least a £8.46 excise duty on a packet of 20 cigarettes"
- D03 (L1806): "£347.86 per 1,000 cigarettes (MET)"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h3, "Picking the right measure": the IA's October 2021 duty table gives the floor (MET, £347.86 per
1,000, £6.9572 a pack) beside its own average-pack duty (£7.98); the smokefree response gives "at least £8.46".
Ambiguity: £7.98 average pack -> 6.0% (not a minimum); Doc 1 £7.87 -> 13.1% (the consultation, not the response);
specific duty alone -> 60.9% (ignores "Highest of"); October 2021 formula at £12.61 -> 15.3% (not minimum).
Necessity: Doc 2 only source of £8.46; Doc 4 only source of a duty table/MET. Doc 1's £7.87 is a different edition.
After clarification: the wording gives no figures; both documents needed.
Selected lever(s): Calculate across documents; Same series, right edition (Doc 1 £7.87 vs Doc 2 £8.46).
Source risks: the IA's paragraph 123 uses £294.72 per 1,000 (inconsistent with its table) for the specific duty only.
Author calculation: 347.86 / 1000 x 20 = 6.9572; 8.46 / 6.9572 - 1 = 21.60% (Python Decimal).
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: promoted as q04 rev 0 (second family after q02 and q03 were dropped).
