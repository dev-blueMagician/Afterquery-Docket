## Question

Among UK nations whose smoking rate fell in 2022, what was the highest share of individuals backing the generational tobacco sales ban?

## Answer

65.5%

## Components

- c1: 65.5%

## Reasoning

Step 1. Source fact (Doc 3, ONS report Adult smoking habits in the UK: 2021): the adult smoking rate was 13% in England, 14.8% in Scotland, 14.1% in Wales and 13.8% in Northern Ireland.

Step 2. Source fact (Doc 1, ONS Adult smoking habits in the UK 2022): the rate was 12.7% in England, 14.1% in Wales, 14.0% in Northern Ireland and 13.9% in Scotland. This list gives the nations in a different order, so each nation is matched by name.

Step 3. Calculation (from Steps 1 and 2; Doc 3 and Doc 1): England: 13 - 12.7 = 0.3 percentage points lower in 2022, so its rate fell.

Step 4. Calculation (from Steps 1 and 2; Doc 3 and Doc 1): Scotland: 14.8 - 13.9 = 0.9 percentage points lower in 2022, so its rate fell.

Step 5. Calculation (from Steps 1 and 2; Doc 3 and Doc 1): Wales: 14.1 - 14.1 = 0, so its rate did not fall.

Step 6. Calculation (from Steps 1 and 2; Doc 3 and Doc 1): Northern Ireland: 14.0 - 13.8 = 0.2 percentage points higher in 2022, so its rate rose and Northern Ireland is excluded.

Step 7. Source fact (Doc 2, Table 7, percentage of those responding as individuals, by where they live, on whether the age of sale should be changed so that anyone born on or after 1 January 2009 will never be legally sold tobacco products): Agree was 61.6 in England, 51.4 in Wales, 65.5 in Scotland and 79.0 in Northern Ireland.

Step 8. Calculation (from Steps 3, 4 and 7; Doc 3, Doc 1 and Doc 2): of the two nations whose rate fell, Scotland's agreement exceeds England's: 65.5 - 61.6 = 3.9 percentage points, so the highest share is Scotland's 65.5%.
