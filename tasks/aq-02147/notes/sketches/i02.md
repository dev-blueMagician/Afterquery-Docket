## Question

In which week of absence would the equality analysis's higher-paid case-study employee first be worse off overall at employers' median suggested rate?

## Answer

Week 3

## Evidence

- D01 (Doc 4 L428, Appendix): "Case study 2: Employee works 5 days per week, and earns £175 per week"
- D03 (Doc 5 L105, Impact on Employers): "The median percentage put forward across all employers was 50%"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2. Doc 4's appendix tracks cumulative SSP for two five-day employees under an 80% rate;
for the £175 employee it concludes they "would always be notionally better off under a rate at 80%"
(80% of £175 is above the flat rate). At the employers' median (50%, Doc 5 only) the weekly SSP is
175 x 50% = £87.50, below the flat £116.75. Cumulative: week 1 87.50 vs 46.70; week 2 175.00 vs 163.45;
week 3 262.50 vs 280.20 -> first worse off overall in week 3. Same at the April 2025 £118.75 (285.00).

Ambiguity: employees' median 90% -> £157.50, above the flat rate -> never worse off -> fails "employers'".
Doc 4's 80% conclusion -> never -> a different rate. Weekly (not cumulative) loss starts in week 2 (87.50 <
116.75) -> fails "overall". Crossing at 2.39 weeks -> the question asks for the week.

Necessity: D01 (Doc 4): the only document with a £175 example or a five-day pattern for it (Doc 3's
examples are £100, £125, £150, £250 without days; Doc 5's are £110 and a two-day £125). D03 (Doc 5): the only
source of the employers' median. Flat rate and waiting days also sit in Docs 3 and 4.

After clarification: neither 50%, £175 nor five days is in the question.

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Calculate across documents; Scatter the pieces. Wrong routes:
Doc 4's 80% statement -> never; employees' 90% -> never; 60% illustration -> week 6.

Source risks: same family as i01 (shared answer path); "3 weeks" is printed as Doc 3 Table 1's 70% break-even,
because 87.50 is close to 70% of £123 (86.10); the break-even depends only on weekly SSP against the flat rate.

Author calculation: 175 x 50% = 87.50; 116.75 / 5 = 23.35; 23.35 x 2 = 46.70; 116.75 x 2 = 233.50;
233.50 + 46.70 = 280.20 > 87.50 x 3 = 262.50; 116.75 + 46.70 = 163.45 < 175.00 -> week 3.
