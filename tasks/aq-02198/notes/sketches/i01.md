## Question

After the 2022 amendments, which Pension Schemes Act 1993 subsection does regulation 142(4) of the 2014 civil service pension regulations cite?

## Answer

Section 95(2)

## Evidence

- D02 (Doc 1, Annex E draft regulation 5): "In regulation 142(4), for “95(2)” substitute “95(2A)”."
- D02 (Doc 1, paragraph 3.3): "amendment to regulation 142 to correct a cross reference to the wrong subsection"
- D01 (Doc 2, paragraph 4.61): "Cabinet Office has decided not to proceed with the amendment of regulation 142"
