## Question

How many days after the 2023 retrospective-remedy consultation opened was the statutory deadline for remediable service statements?

## Answer

757 days

## Evidence

- D03 (Doc 4, paragraph 2.7): "The consultation was launched on 6 March 2023"
- D04 (Doc 3, paragraph 7.14): "This is the day after the end of the 18 month period, which begins on the day that the rollback provisions come into force"
- D04 (Doc 3, paragraph 3.5): "will become effective from 1 October 2023, at which time Section 2 of the PSPJOA 2022 comes into force"
