## Question

The newest federal CCR amendments reflected in Wyoming's and Wisconsin's rules were published how many days apart?

## Answer

76 days

## Evidence

- D02 (L280, Wyoming Partial Program): "The WDEQ's rules adopt the Federal CCR regulations promulgated through December 14, 2020."
- D02 (L127, Background, dated amendment list): "August 28, 2020 (85 FR 53516), November 12, 2020 (85 FR 72506), May 8, 2024 (89 FR 38950)"
- D06 (L308, Wisconsin Partial Program): "Wisconsin's CCR regulations reflect 40 CFR part 257, subpart D, as amended through August 28, 2020, for CCR landfills"
