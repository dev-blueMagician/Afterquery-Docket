## Question

How many more definitions that the 2024 Legacy Rule added would stay federal requirements in Wyoming than in Virginia?

## Answer

3 definitions

## Evidence

- D02 (L280, Wyoming Partial Program): "Wyoming is not seeking approval for the following four requirements of the Federal CCR regulations"
- D02 (L282): "1. Amendments made in the Legacy CCR Surface Impoundments and CCR Management Units Final Rule."
- D03 (L246, Virginia Partial Program): "Therefore, EPA is approving the State's version of the following requirements:"
- D03 (L258, item 11 of the approved list): "this additional definition in 40 CFR 257.53 is consistent with the term's plain meaning and dictionary definitions to assist in the application of closure performance standards"
- D03 (L291): "Thus, any legacy CCR surface impoundments in Virginia will remain subject to the following Federal CCR regulations:"
- D03 (L294): "this added definition to 40 CFR 257.53 specifies a new type of CCR unit that meets certain criteria"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: independent author B's i01 family (definitions the 2024 Legacy Rule added), reframed
by the caller as a Wyoming-Virginia difference so that each named state's document is indispensable.
Virginia (D03) labels ten 2024 Legacy Rule entries as added definitions: three in the all-unit list
EPA approves as Virginia's own version (Contains both CCR and liquids L255, Infiltration L258, Liquids
L259), two legacy-impoundment definitions (L293-L294) and five CCRMU definitions (L307-L311), the last
seven left federal (L291, L305). Wyoming (D02 L280-L285) seeks approval of no Legacy Rule amendment,
so all ten stay federal. 10 - 7 = 3.
Ambiguity: "neither state adopted the 2024 changes" (D03 L245 "VADEQ has not adopted regulations
reflecting the 2024 or 2026 changes") gives 10 - 10 = 0 -> fails because EPA approves Virginia's
version of the three all-unit additions (L246-L259). Counting amended definitions too (Operator,
Owner L279-L280, and the eight amended entries of the approved list) -> fails "added". Wyoming built
from Wisconsin's list (nine additions, no "Contains both") -> fails: the question's Wyoming count rests
on Wyoming's blanket non-adoption applied to the Legacy Rule's additions, which Virginia's list
enumerates.
Necessity: D02 -> Wyoming's blanket non-adoption (only Wyoming source). D03 -> Virginia's split
between approved and federal additions (only Virginia source; Louisiana's identical lists in D04 are
another state). Without D02 a solver would most likely assume Wyoming matches Virginia (0); without
D03, that Virginia matches Wyoming (0) or Alabama (0).
After clarification: wording names no definition and no count.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: mentions it vs does it (Virginia "has not adopted" the 2024
changes, yet EPA approves its version of three additions); scatter the pieces (Wyoming's grouped item,
split only in other documents). Wrong routes: 0, 10 - 9 = 1 (Wisconsin enumeration), 12 - 7 = 5 if
Operator/Owner are counted for Wyoming only.
Source risks: small-integer gold (3). Overview: Doc 2 "exceptions for four specific federal
requirements"; Doc 3 names no list.
Author calculation: grep "additional definition|added definition" on D03 = 10 (L255, L258, L259,
L293, L294, L307-L311); approved three; 10 - 7 = 3.
