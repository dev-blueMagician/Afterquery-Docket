## Question

How many 2024 Legacy Rule amendments would EPA approve in Alabama's state version but not in Wisconsin's?

## Answer

8 amendments

## Evidence

- D05 (L308, Alabama approved list intro): "In the 2024 Legacy Rule, EPA amended certain terms and provisions that apply to all CCR units."
- D05 (L310, item 2 of 17): "2. 40 CFR 257.50(c); this amendment revises the scope of applicability to specify that it includes inactive CCR surface impoundments"
- D05 (L329, item 17 of 17): "17. 40 CFR 257.104(a); this amendment clarifies that all owners or operators of CCR units that are subject to Sec. 257.102 are subject to the post-closure care requirements"
- D06 (L309, Wisconsin approved list intro): "Therefore, EPA is approving the State's version of these requirements:"
- D06 (L314, item 5 of 11): "5. ``Infiltration''; this additional definition in 40 CFR 257.53 is consistent with the term's plain meaning"
