## Question

To the nearest week, how far apart were Wisconsin's deadline for CCR landfills' initial-permitting submittals and Wyoming's for existing CCR impoundments' permit applications?

## Answer

28 weeks

## Evidence

- D02 (L202, Permit Requirements and Permitting Process): "Existing CCR surface impoundments must submit a new permit application within 12 months of the effective date of the State rule."
- D02 (L278, Wyoming CCR Regulations): "chapter 18 became effective on August 19, 2022"
- D06 (L456, Wisconsin CCR Permits): "the six CCR landfills were required to submit a plan of operation modification for initial permitting by February 1, 2023"
