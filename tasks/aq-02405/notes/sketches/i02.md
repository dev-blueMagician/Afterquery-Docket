## Question

Under ATF's 2026 proposals as written, which paragraph of 27 CFR 478.96 would hold the seven-day shipping delay?

## Answer

27 CFR 478.96(d)(4)

## Evidence

- D05 (L487, page Doc 5, proposed 478.96): "(c) Law enforcement notice for NOTC transfers."
- D05 (L498, page Doc 5, proposed 478.96(c)(4)): "(4) Delay shipping or delivering the firearm to the transferee for a period of at least seven days after receiving the return receipt or response under paragraph (c)(2) and (3) of this section."
- D04 (L465, page Doc 4, amendatory instruction 2.b): "Redesignating paragraphs (c) and (d) to (d) and (e), respectively"
- D04 (L468, page Doc 4, amendatory instruction 2.d): "in paragraph (d)(4), redesignating the paragraph reference ``(c)'' as ``(d)''"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2, amendments applied in order. D05 rewrites 478.96 as (a) applicability, (b) NOTC,
(c) law enforcement notice whose item (4) is the seven-day delay, (d) out-of-state (L470-506). D04's
instructions are written against that rewritten text, not the current rule: they insert a new (c) for
NOTC sales needing a NICS check and redesignate (c) and (d) as (d) and (e); the (d) edits ("for" ->
"for all NOTC", the sworn-statement clause, the (d)(4) cross-reference) match D05's (c) word for word,
and the current rule has no paragraph (d) to redesignate. D05 fn8 (L181) says ATF expects to finalize
both at about the same time. So the delay moves from D05's printed (c)(4) to (d)(4).

Ambiguity (competing answer -> condition it fails -> location):
- (c)(4) (D05 as printed) -> "ATF's 2026 proposals as written" (plural): D04 L465 redesignates it.
- D04's new (c)(4) (L485, wait for the NICS response or investigatory period) -> "seven-day".
- 478.96(b) (current rule, D04 L68) -> "proposals as written".
- D05 L536 "seven-day waiting period" (state law, Example 5 of 478.102(c)) -> not 478.96, not a shipping delay.
- "(d)" alone is incomplete; the documents themselves cite items as "paragraph (c)(2)", so the paragraph
  designation includes the item number.
- No other bundle proposal (D01, D02, D03, D06) amends 478.96; out-of-bundle ATF rules named in D05
  (478.130 records, retention periods) are not said to touch 478.96.

Necessity:
- D05 (page Doc 5): the delay's place as item (4) of the law-enforcement-notice paragraph -> substitutes
  searched: D04 states the statutory seven-day wait (L67) and the current rule's 478.96(b) (L68) but
  never prints the rewritten paragraph's items -> without D05 the answer is (b) or unknown.
- D04 (page Doc 4): the redesignation (c) -> (d) -> substitutes: D05 fn8 (L181) only says another
  proposal revises 478.96 -> without D04 the answer is (c)(4).
After clarification: both documents indispensable; wording names neither paragraph.

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Same series, right edition (later amendment governs). Decoy
(c)(4) is the fully printed text; keyword "seven days" lands on it (D05 L498). Not a which-items question.

Source risks: answer is a regulation paragraph (allowed: "A standard or regulation is a fine answer");
