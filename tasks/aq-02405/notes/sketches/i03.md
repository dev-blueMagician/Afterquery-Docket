## Question

Which states does ATF cite both for blocking transfers on checks unfinished after three business days and for exempting some carry-permit applicants from checks, in any order?

## Answer

Delaware, Florida, Maryland

## Evidence

- D05 (L316, page Doc 5, fn 13): "States that have restricted the ability to transfer firearms after three business days without a completed background check include California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Florida, Hawaii, Illinois, Massachusetts, Maryland"
- D03 (L97, page Doc 2, section II, Fourth): "a state might exempt a law enforcement officer or judge from the background check requirement when obtaining a concealed weapons license. See, e.g., Fla. Stat. 790.06(5)(b); Del. Code Ann."
- D03 (L98, page Doc 2): "Md. Pub. Safety Code sec. 5-305(g) (exempting employees of armored car companies)"
