## Question

The 2022 law behind Form 4473's city-limits question added firearm prohibitions from which levels of government to the background-check statute, in any order?

## Answer

Local and Tribal

## Evidence

- D05 (L211, page Doc 5, section II.D.2): "was added because of the 2022 NICS Denial Notification Act (``NDNA''), Public Law 117-103, 136 Stat. 49"
- D01 (L137-138, page Doc 1, fn 16): "Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2022, Public Law 117-103, 136 Stat."
- D01 (L138, page Doc 1, fn 16): "which amended 18 U.S.C. 922(t) to reference firearm prohibitions imposed by"
