## Question

Recomputing ATF's 2026 savings estimates at $26.10 an hour, how much would an individual save per paper Form 1, to the cent?

## Answer

$48.05

## Evidence

- D06 (L200, page Doc 6, fingerprint cost savings): "resulting in a per-application savings of $18 for this two percent of applicants"
- D06 (L220, page Doc 6, photograph cost savings): "could realize a capital savings of $17 per application"
- D06 (L253, page Doc 6, time estimates): "up to 0.25 hours for GCA individual/RP applicants and NFA individual applicants who submit paper applications and would no longer have to obtain paper 2'' x 2'' photographs"
- D02 (L141, page Doc 3, benefits and savings): "ATF estimates that it would take 15 minutes (0.25 hours) for an individual to copy the relevant form prior to submitting the original to ATF"
- D02 (L66, page Doc 3, scope of the copy): "all applicants and responsible persons must currently forward a complete copy of Form 1"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. Two 2026 ATF proposals each cut part of the same applicant's burden: the CLEO
proposal removes the copy for the local CLEO (time only, D02 L141, L165 "$7 (rounded)"); the
fingerprint/photo proposal cuts one paper fingerprint card ($18) and lets a paper filer send an ID copy
instead of a photo ($17 plus 0.25 h) (D06 L200, L220, L253; its own rounded total "$42", L257). The
combined per-application figure is printed nowhere; D06's $42 is the obvious same-label figure.

Ambiguity (competing answer -> condition it fails -> location):
- $41.53 (D06 alone at $26.10) -> "ATF's 2026 savings estimates" (plural): D02's CLEO-copy estimate covers
  the same Form 1 (D02 L66, L141, L165).
- $49 (printed $42 + $7) -> "at $26.10 an hour": each printed figure rounds 0.25 h x $26 = $6.50 up to $7
  (D06 L257 "($7)", "$26 * 0.25 hours, rounded"; D02 L165 "$7 (rounded)").
- $48.00 -> "$26.10" (not the rounded $26 wage, D02 L161 "Rounded hourly leisure wage").
- $49.36 (D02 PRA "12 minutes (overall reduction from 30 minutes, due to conversion to eForm, changes
  proposed in this rule, and other related changes)", L222) -> not ATF's savings estimate for the rule
  alone, and eForm conversion does not apply to a paper filer; the savings estimate is L141/L165.
- $74.15 / $106.15 (adding the RP fingerprint hour or RP cash) -> "an individual": D06 L257 lists no
  fingerprint time for individuals (they still submit one card); RP figures are L257 "$100"/"$99".
- $6.53 (electronic individual) -> "paper": D06 L175 electronic individuals accrue no cost savings.
- Unquantified self-fingerprinting option (D06 L120, L350-351) -> "ATF's ... savings estimates"; ATF
  calls it currently unlikely and puts no figure on it.
- Other 2026 proposals: D03 (permits), D04 (NOTC), D05 (Form 4473) never mention Form 1/5320.1 or a
  making application; Form 4473 records licensee dispositions, and making involves no transfer.
  Searched all six texts for "Form 1", "5320.1", "CLEO", fingerprint/photograph dollar figures.
- Out-of-bundle ATF spouses rule (D06 fn8 L139) concerns spouses applying jointly, not an individual.

Necessity:
- D02 (page Doc 3): CLEO-copy time 0.25 h per application -> substitutes searched: D06 has 0 CLEO
  mentions; D04/D05 CLEO text concerns GCA NOTC notices -> without D02 the answer is $41.53.
- D06 (page Doc 6): $18 card, $17 photo, 0.25 h photo time for paper NFA individuals -> no other document
  prices prints or photos -> without D06 the answer is $6.53.
- $26.10 is stated in the question (also printed in D02 L159-160, D04 L302, D06 L239); not a necessity carrier.
After clarification: both documents still indispensable; the wording supplies only the wage.

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Scatter the pieces (each proposal prints only its part) and
State the rounding (stated wage separates $48.05 from the printed-sum $49 and the $26 route $48.00).
Rounding counterfactual recomputed: $49 and $48.00 differ from the gold. Not a which-items question.

Source risks: the photo-ID option is optional ("could realize", L220); ATF's own paper-individual
estimate counts it (L257 "would have a combined (rounded) savings of $42"), so "would save" follows
ATF's estimate. D06 Tables 1-15 are omitted graphics; every needed value is restated in prose
(L200, L220, L253, L257), so the gap does not affect premises, gold, uniqueness or necessity.

Author calculation (Decimal script, scratchpad i01_calc.py):
cash 18 + 17 = 35 dollars (D06); time 0.25 + 0.25 = 0.5 hours (D06, D02); 0.5 x 26.10 = 13.05 dollars;
35 + 13.05 = 48.05 dollars, exact, no rounding needed. Wrong routes: 41.53, 6.53, 49.00, 48.00,
49.36, 74.15, 106.15, 35.00 - all differ from the gold.
