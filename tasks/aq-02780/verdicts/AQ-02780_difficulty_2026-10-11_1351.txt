The FDIC proposal eliminates the public‑comment period and creates a “de minimis change in address” exception (§ 303.41(b)):

> Exchanging one facility for another within the same approximate location (line of sight, shared parking, contiguous properties, or same block) is neither a branch establishment nor a branch relocation—so no filing is required (only advance notice to customers and the FDIC office).
