## Question

An eligible State nonmember bank plans to merge in its mortgage subsidiary, open two branches, and move its main office and three branches next door. If the FDIC lets every deadline pass without acting, how many of the filings this requires stand approved under its merger-processing rule and 2025 branch proposal?

## Answer

3 filings

## Evidence

- D03 (jurisdiction, L334): "The FDIC has jurisdiction to act on merger applications that involve an IDI and any non-insured entity"
- D03 (merger expedited processing, L180): "Section 303.64 of the FDIC Rules and Regulations codifies the requirements for expedited processing of merger applications."
- D03 (no default approval, L181): "It is important to note that if the FDIC does not act within the expedited processing period, it does not constitute an automatic or default approval."
- D04 (proposed Sec. 303.43(a), L578): "Filings to establish a branch by an eligible depository institution as defined in Sec. 303.2(r) will be acknowledged in writing by the FDIC and will receive expedited processing."
- D04 (proposed Sec. 303.43(b), L581): "Filings for intrastate branch relocations or intrastate main office relocations will be acknowledged in writing by the FDIC and will receive expedited processing if the bank received an FDIC-assigned composite rating of 3 or better"
- D04 (proposed Sec. 303.43(b), L581): "A filing processed under expedited processing will be deemed approved on the third business day after receipt by the FDIC of a substantially complete filing."
- D04 (no removal from expedited processing, L256): "The proposed rule also would eliminate the FDIC's authority to remove such filings from expedited processing."
- D04 (proposed Sec. 303.41(b)(1), L546): "For the purposes of this subpart, a de minimis change in address is neither a branch establishment nor a branch relocation."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h14 (notes/hooks.md; the untested h6 of aq-02706 turned into a count), caller, 2026-10-10. The FDIC's branch proposal approves expedited filings by the lapse of three business days and removes its own discretion to stop that; its merger processing does not approve by lapse ("does not constitute an automatic or default approval"). The plan needs four filings: the merger application, two branch filings and the main-office filing; the three next-door branch moves need none. Approved by lapse: 2 + 1 = 3.
Ambiguity: 4 (merger approved by lapse) -> fails D03 L181. 6 (the three next-door branch moves treated as filings deemed approved) -> fails D04 L546-547, they need no filing. 7 (both). 2 (main office treated as de minimis, so no filing) -> fails D04 L547 "when a branch exchanges" and L581, which keeps main office relocations as filings. The merger is with the bank's own uninsured subsidiary so that no acquired offices raise a separate branch-approval count (D03 fn 26, L339); it still needs a Bank Merger Act application (D03 L334; fn 10, L171). "Eligible" gives the eligible depository institution status that proposed Sec. 303.43(a) requires and a composite rating of 1 or 2 (D04 L222), which meets the 3-or-better test for the main office. A branch in a new State would wait for the host State's confirmation (D04 L580) and is still approved by lapse after it.
Necessity: D03 -> the only statement that a lapsed merger period is no approval -> "automatic or default" has one hit in the bundle (D03 L181); D02's "deemed approved" is the OCC's former merger rule and points the other way -> without D03 the merger cell is unknown or taken as approved (4).
