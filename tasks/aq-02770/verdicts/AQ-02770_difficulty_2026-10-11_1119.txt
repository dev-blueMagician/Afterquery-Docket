Full answer
-----------

The final determination was published on December 23, 2022 (Document 2). For a gas-fired CWAF manufactured on that date—before the January 1, 2023 compliance date—the federal minimum thermal efficiency (TE) was 80.0% (the 1994–2022 standard).

From DOE’s TE2 analysis (Documents 3 and 4):

- Jacket-loss effect: up to about 2.5 percentage points lower for an outdoor unit (0.75% loss × 3.3 factor).
- Part-load effect: DOE observed part-load efficiency can be up to 2 percentage points lower than full-load efficiency. With DOE’s adopted 50/50 weighting that yields a 1.0 point TE2 swing, but NEEA’s modeling showed part-load operating shares as high as 75% (full-load only 25%). At that 75/25 weighting the part-load contribution to the swing is 1.5 points lower.

So the worst-case low is:

80.0% − 2.5 − 1.5 = 76.0%

Therefore, by DOE’s estimates, TE2 could be as low as 76.0%.
