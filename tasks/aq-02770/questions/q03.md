## Question

DOE published a final determination not to amend commercial warm air furnace standards. For a gas-fired furnace built that day at the federal minimum thermal efficiency, how low could TE2 be by DOE's estimates? Give one decimal place.

## Answer

76.5 percent

## Components

- c1: 76.5 percent

## Reasoning

Step 1. Source input (Doc 2, heading, Summary and Background): DOE's final determination not to amend the standards was published in the Federal Register on December 23, 2022. Its effective date, January 23, 2023, is later and is not the day it was published. Gas-fired commercial warm air furnaces manufactured before January 1, 2023 must have a thermal efficiency (TE) of not less than 80 percent, and the 81 percent minimum applies only to furnaces manufactured starting on January 1, 2023. A furnace built on the publication day at the federal minimum therefore has a TE of 80 percent.

Step 2. Source input (Doc 3, section II.C): DOE estimates that the TE2 test procedure could give a result as much as 3.5 percent lower than the industry test procedure that measures TE, for a model with high jacket loss and poor part-load performance. DOE's example shows that these are percentage points: a furnace with a TE of 81 percent could have a TE2 as low as 77.5 percent. Doc 4 repeats that example.

Step 3. Calculation (Doc 2 and Doc 3): 80 - 3.5 = 76.5 percent, the lowest TE2 for the furnace of Step 1. The printed 77.5 percent belongs to a furnace at the later 81 percent minimum, not to one built on the publication day.

## Evidence

- D02 (heading, publication date) [c1]: "Federal Register Volume 87, Number 246 (Friday, December 23, 2022)"
- D02 (Summary, the determination) [c1]: "DOE has determined not to amend the energy conservation standards for CWAFs"
- D02 (II.B Background, standards by manufacture date) [c1]: "for gas-fired CWAFs manufactured starting on January 1, 1994, until January 1, 2023, TE at the maximum rated capacity (i.e., rated maximum input) must be not less than 80 percent"
- D02 (II.B Background, the later level) [c1]: "For gas-fired CWAFs manufactured starting on January 1, 2023, the TE at the maximum rated capacity must be not less than 81 percent."
- D03 (II.C, DOE's estimate) [c1]: "between 3.5 percent lower (in a model with high jacket loss and poor part-load performance) to 1 percent higher"
- D03 (III Conclusion, DOE's example) [c1]: "have an actual range of efficiencies from 77.5 to 82 percent using the appendix B test procedure for TE2"
- D04 (II Discussion, the same example) [c1]: "have an actual range of efficiencies from 77.5 to 82 percent using the appendix B test procedure for TE2"
