## Question

In any order, which organizations, if any, filing alone or jointly, commented on DOE's April 2022 proposal to leave commercial warm air furnace standards unchanged and on analytic methods in response to its April 2025 Process Rule request for information, but not on its December 2024 tentative determination on TE2?

## Answer

American Gas Association, American Public Gas Association, American Council for an Energy-Efficient Economy, Lennox International

## Components

- c1: American Gas Association
- c2: American Public Gas Association
- c3: American Council for an Energy-Efficient Economy
- c4: Lennox International

## Reasoning

Step 1. Source input (Doc 2, Table II.1): written comments on the April 2022 proposed determination came from AHRI; from the American Gas Association and the American Public Gas Association, filing together; from a group called the Joint Advocates, made up of the Appliance Standards Awareness Project, the American Council for an Energy-Efficient Economy, the New York State Energy Research and Development Authority and the Natural Resources Defense Council; from the California Investor-Owned Utilities; from Lennox International; and from the Northwest Energy Efficiency Alliance.

Step 2. Source input (Doc 7, Table 1 and its footnote): the commenters on analytic methods in response to the April 2025 Process Rule request include AHRI, Lennox International and the Northwest Energy Efficiency Alliance. They also include a group again called Joint Advocates, here the Appliance Standards Awareness Project, the American Council for an Energy-Efficient Economy, the Consumer Federation of America and the National Consumer Law Center, and the Joint Gas Associations, which are the American Gas Association, the American Public Gas Association and the National Propane Gas Association. The New York authority, the Natural Resources Defense Council and the California utilities are not in that table.

Step 3. Matching member by member (Doc 2 and Doc 7): the organizations in both lists are AHRI, the American Gas Association, the American Public Gas Association, the Appliance Standards Awareness Project, the American Council for an Energy-Efficient Economy, Lennox International and the Northwest Energy Efficiency Alliance.

Step 4. Source input (Doc 4, Table I.1): the commenters on the December 2024 tentative determination were an individual, AHRI, the Appliance Standards Awareness Project and the Northwest Energy Efficiency Alliance filing jointly, and the manufacturers Trane Technologies, Johnson Controls and Daikin.

Step 5. Result (Doc 2, Doc 4 and Doc 7): removing AHRI, the Appliance Standards Awareness Project and the Northwest Energy Efficiency Alliance leaves the American Gas Association, the American Public Gas Association, the American Council for an Energy-Efficient Economy and Lennox International. Decoy: the Appliance Standards Awareness Project. It filed in the April 2022 and April 2025 proceedings inside groups that are both called Joint Advocates, but it also commented on the December 2024 tentative determination, so it is out while its co-filer the American Council for an Energy-Efficient Economy stays. The New York authority and the Natural Resources Defense Council fail the other way: they belong to the Joint Advocates of Doc 2 but not to the group of that name in Doc 7.
