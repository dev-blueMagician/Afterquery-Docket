## Question

How much did Kiwibank's home lending book grow in the first half of calendar 2023, to the nearest $0.1 billion?

## Answer

$0.5 billion

## Evidence

- D03 (L570, para 9.15): "Kiwibank announced its best results in the history of the bank for FY23"
- D03 (L570, para 9.15): "Home lending growth of $1.1 billion, (or 1.5x market)"
- D04 (L156, para 2.6(c)): "the bank grew its home lending book by $0.6b (1.5 x the banking system) during the first half [of FY23]"
- D04 (L161, footnote 7): "Kiwibank half-year results for the six months to 31 December 2022"
