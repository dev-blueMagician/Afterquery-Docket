## Question

Which of the banks named as holders of New Zealand's agricultural loans take part in the industry account-switching arrangements only through an agency banking arrangement?

## Answer

Rabobank and Heartland Bank

## Evidence

- D06 (L181-183, para 4.8): "agricultural loans are held by five banks (ANZ, BNZ, Rabobank, ASB, and Westpac)"
- D06 (L183, para 4.8): "almost of all that remainder held by two banks – TSB and Heartland"
- D02 (L804, Table 2): "The Cooperative Bank Heartland Bank* RABO Bank*"
- D02 (L808, Table 2 note): "*Participate via agency banking arrangements"
