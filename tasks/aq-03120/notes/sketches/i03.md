## Question

Which of the four fintechs that Consumer NZ said had not yet been able to serve New Zealanders had already launched there, and how many days before Consumer NZ's submission?

## Answer

Revolut, 66 days

## Evidence

- D05 (L173): "fintechs such as Revolut, Zeal, Jude and Douugh have not yet been able to provide services in New Zealand"
- D05 (L4): "7 September 2023"
- D03 (L1160): "prior to Revolut’s official launch on 3 July 2023"
