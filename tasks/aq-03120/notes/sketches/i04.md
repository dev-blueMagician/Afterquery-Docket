## Question

Among each bank's active personal customers in mid-2023, how many percentage points more of BNZ's than of ASB's did no online or mobile banking in the month measured?

## Answer

8.1 percentage points

## Evidence

- D03 (L1117, para 25.5): "In June 2023, 90% of personal customers who were active with ASB were digitally active in that month"
- D04 (L1223, para 6.13): "and 81.9% of BNZ's"
- D04 (L1223, para 6.13): "individual customers were digitally active"
- D04 (L1235, footnote 183): "customer is a customer that had a digital session in the given month"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h7. Each bank reports the share of its active personal customers who used digital channels in one
month. BNZ's sentence prints a sibling figure first (87.9% "digitally enabled", i.e. able to bank online), and ASB
prints two more (71% of over-65s; 77% in a 2017 benchmark). The asked quantity is the complement of the right pair.
Ambiguity: BNZ 87.9% -> "digitally enabled (i.e. they had a digital channel and were able to bank online when they
had a need)" measures access over all customers, not use by active individuals (Doc 4 L1223). ASB 71% -> customers
over 65 only (Doc 3 L1133). ASB 77% -> 2017 (Doc 3 fn 66). Months differ (June and July 2023): the question says
"mid-2023" and "the month measured".
Necessity: Doc 3 -> ASB's 90%; Doc 4 -> BNZ's 81.9% and its definition. Neither figure appears elsewhere (ANZ gives
sales origination and branch share, not active-customer use).
After clarification: both documents still needed.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Calculate across documents; look-alike measure. Wrong routes: with 87.9,
12.1 - 10 = 2.1; with ASB over-65s, 18.1 - 29 = -10.9; uncomplemented, 90 - 81.9 = 8.1 (same magnitude: weak).
Source risks: the two banks define "active" differently (BNZ defines it, ASB does not): the question asks only for the
difference of the two stated shares. Both figures sit past character 130,000 (two form number-check false alarms).
The complement does not change the magnitude, so the only live trap is the 87.9 sibling.
Author calculation: (100 - 81.9) - (100 - 90) = 18.1 - 10 = 8.1 percentage points (script).
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: mechanical screen clean (2026-10-11); platform quick test pending. Lower prior.
