# aq-03120 - cross-document hooks

Find facts that combine into a world answer before drafting wording. Record sources/locations,
input quantities and units/periods, the relationship, a possible ask and one or two selected levers.

Earlier captures: none for these bytes (hashes compared with every task folder, 2026-10-11). Same family
(parties' submissions on one NZ Commerce Commission paper): aq-02940 and aq-03066, both measured today.
What those runs showed, applied here before reading:

- A rate or share from one submission applied to another's base, in two wordings, was quick-test too easy
  (aq-02940 q01/q02). A derived share beside a printed look-alike pair was too easy (q03). So: no
  "A's percentage times B's base" design, however obscure the inputs.
- One dated case through two banks' requests, the deciding clause two paragraphs below the headline
  request: Low 0/5 (all five missed the clause), Medium 5/5 (all five applied it). A buried clause sets the
  low floor; the medium solver needs a second, independent gate (aq-02940 q04).
- Figures in submissions are mostly quoted third-party statistics; the same statistic repeats across
  submitters, so necessity fails unless each ticked document holds a period or scope the others lack.
- Measured Medium shapes to look for: a period-subtraction or scope carve-out decided by a sentence in the
  other document (aq-02673 q05); one scenario whose cells follow different stated rules (aq-02564 q02); a
  membership count where one source states a grouped item (aq-02749 q02).
