## Question

Which areas has EPA said need no failure-to-attain contingency measures because of an approved international-emissions demonstration? List alphabetically, or say none.

## Answer

Imperial County, Northern Wasatch Front, Phoenix-Mesa

## Evidence

- D04 (I.B, the interpretation): "the EPA interprets the CAA not to require the state to have EPA-approved contingency measures for failure to attain for the NAAQS at issue in an area with an approved section 179B(b) demonstration"
- D04 (fn 10, Imperial County): "proposing the same interpretation with respect to Imperial County for the 2008 ozone NAAQS"
- D04 (fn 10, final action): "85 FR 11817 (February 27, 2020) (finalizing the same)"
- D04 (III, Phoenix-Mesa): "the contingency measure requirements of CAA section 172(c)(9) would no longer apply to the Phoenix-Mesa Moderate 2015 ozone nonattainment area"
- D07 (III, Northern Wasatch Front): "the contingency measure requirements of CAA section 172(c)(9) would no longer apply to the NWF Moderate 2015 ozone NAA"
- D07 (fn 17, Imperial County): "proposing the same interpretation with respect to Imperial County for the 2008 ozone NAAQS"
