## Question

Which nonattainment areas' state-requested reclassifications, if any, left the tribal lands inside them at the lower classification? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

Coachella Valley, Greater Connecticut

## Evidence

- D03 (I.B): "the State requested a reclassification of Coachella Valley from"
- D03 (I.B): "the EPA did not reclassify any areas of Indian country within the boundaries of the nonattainment area"
- D05 (I.A.2): "At the request of CT DEEP, the Greater Connecticut area was voluntarily reclassified from Moderate to Serious"
- D05 (I.A.2): "However, the tribal portions of the Greater Connecticut area remained classified as Moderate."
