## Question

Which fibre quality dimensions did exactly two of Chorus, Spark and Vodafone want the input methodology to cover?

## Answer

Ordering, provisioning, switching and faults

## Evidence

- D08 (line 543): "We agree with CEPA’s six high level categories relating to:"
- D07 (line 637): "While quality dimensions should address the full lifecycle dimensions set out in the CEPA report"
- D01 (line 960, footnote 32): "Those product categories are ordering, provisioning, switching, faults, availability and performance."
- D01 (line 941): "the IMs should only focus on the end of the product lifecycle:"
- D01 (line 942): "specifically availability and performance."
