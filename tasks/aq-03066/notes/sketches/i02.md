## Question

If the Commerce Commission's illustrative loss asset were amortised straight-line over the longest time the first four regulatory periods could span, how much would the first period write off?

## Answer

257

## Evidence

- D03 (line 237): "a period consisting of a number of regulatory periods, such as 2, 3 or 4 periods"
- D03 (line 279, appendix table): "PV losses forward to 2021 (118) (117) (125) (139) (155) (202) (197) (184) (170) (136) (1,542)"
- D04 (line 163): "The initial regulatory period will be three years, followed by regulatory periods of 3-5 years."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2. The first regulatory period is fixed at three years and only later ones can reach five, so four periods span at most 3 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 18 years, and the first period carries 3 of those 18 years: 1,542 x 3 / 18 = 257 exactly. The three-year rule is used twice (span and share).
Ambiguity: a quarter per period (385.5) -> fails "straight-line" over time, since the periods differ in length. 4 x 5 = 20 years (231.3 with a three-year first period, 385.5 with a five-year one) -> fails D04 line 163. Later periods rather than the first four -> excluded by "first four". Unrounded table value 1,541.89 gives 256.98, 257 at whole units.
Necessity: D03 -> loss asset 1,542 and the straight-line, regulatory-period option (only source). D04 -> first period three years then 3-5 (only complete statement). Substitutes found: D03 line 461 "(3-5 years)" plus D01 line 1103 "short (three year) RP1 period" rebuild the rule, and the Act's period lengths may be recalled.
After clarification: two documents needed, but D04 is replaceable by D01 plus D03: stated Document-check risk before any difficulty run.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: calculate across documents; look-alike figure ("20" is a printed fixed-period alternative in the same D03 sentence). Wrong routes 385.5, 231.3, and 1,542 / 18 = 85.7 (one year, not the period).
Source risks: illustrative, unit-less figures; D04 dependency as above; sibling of q01 (same table), so at most one of the two is submitted.
Author calculation: 3 + 5 = 8; 8 + 5 = 13; 13 + 5 = 18; 1,542 x 3 = 4,626; 4,626 / 18 = 257. Every step terminates, so the form's number check can be met.
