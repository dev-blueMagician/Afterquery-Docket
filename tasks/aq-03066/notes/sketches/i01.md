## Question

At Crown Fibre Holdings' mid-point WACC for Enable, by what percentage does the opening loss asset in the Commerce Commission's illustrative calculation fall, to the nearest whole percent?

## Answer

6%

## Evidence

- D03 (line 279, appendix table): "Qualifying RAB – 20 68 140 226 302 369 428 579 723"
- D03 (line 231): "The discount rate we propose to apply is the same as the WACC value"
- D02 (line 110): "mid-point WACC of 9%, utilised by Crown Fibre Holdings Limited"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. The workshop pack's illustration fixes one WACC input (10%) that is used twice: return on capital = WACC x qualifying RAB, and the present-value factor to the end of 2021. CCHL alone prints the Crown Fibre Holdings mid-point (9%). Rerun at 9%: each year's loss falls by 1% of that year's qualifying RAB, and every loss is compounded at 9% instead of 10%.
Ambiguity: printed 1,542 -> fails "at the mid-point WACC Crown Fibre Holdings used" (table input is 10%, D03 line 277). 5.56% (same CCHL sentence) -> it is the Commission's copper mid-point, not Crown Fibre Holdings'. 8% or 10% -> range ends, not the mid-point. Keeping the 10% discount factor -> fails D03 line 231 (discount rate is the WACC value), and the table has a single rate input.
Necessity: D02 -> 9% (only occurrence in the bundle; grep "9%", "Crown Fibre Holdings", "mid-point WACC" over all eight texts) -> without it no rate. D03 -> table and method (only worked calculation in the bundle; D04 states the method in words without figures) -> without it no base.
After clarification: both still needed; the question names neither the rate nor any table figure.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: calculate across documents; printed same-label decoy (1,542). Wrong routes: return only at 9% = 1,506 (1,510); discount only at 9% = 1,485-1,486 (1,480 or 1,490); 0.9 x 1,542 = 1,388 (1,390); no deduction of government funding = 1,785.
Source risks: figures are "purely illustrative" and unit-less; the what-if joins a submitter's rate to the regulator's example; ten compounding terms by hand.
Author calculation: script scratchpad/whatif.py. Exact (unrounded table) 1,449.72. From printed integers: 1,451.07 to 1,452.00 by four routes (1,451 to 1,453 with yearly PVs rounded). All round to 1,450 at the nearest 10.
Form of the ask: first drafted as the rerun value to the nearest 10 (1,450). Promoted as the percentage fall (6%), because a rounded plain result cannot clear the form's reasoning number check while a percentage result may be rounded. Wrong routes become 2% (return only), 4% (discount only), 10% (scaling the total), 24% (the 5.56% copper mid-point).
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: mechanical screen passed 2026-10-11; promoted to q01 rev 0 (checked); quick test and difficulty run pending.
