## Question

The United States declined to intervene in a relator's qui tam over an Alaska company's PPP loan and in one over a probiotic's marketing. Counting days from each declination to the court order dismissing that relator's complaint, what is the probiotic suit's count minus the PPP suit's?

## Answer

190 days

## Evidence

- D01 (Background, p. 3; l.46): "On March 17, 2025, the United States notified the Court of its decision not to intervene in this action."
- D01 (Background, p. 4; l.66): "On November 21, 2025, the Court granted the motion but accorded Relator leave to file an amended complaint as to Counts 1, 2, and 4."
- D05 (Procedural Background, p. 7; l.117): "On June 20, 2025, the United States and the State Plaintiffs declined to intervene in this matter."
- D05 (caption, p. 1; l.43): "Dated: September 2, 2026"
