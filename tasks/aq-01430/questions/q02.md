## Question

Compared with July to December 2024, how much did China's fossil-fuel electricity generation change in July to December 2025? State it in TWh to one decimal place, showing whether it rose or fell.

## Answer

+2.8 TWh

## Components

- c1: +2.8 TWh

## Reasoning

Step 1 (Doc 4): The review of 2025 says "fossil generation declined by 56 TWh (-0.9%) in 2025" in China, so the change for the whole of 2025 against 2024 is -56 TWh.

Step 2 (Doc 6): The mid-year report compares January to June 2025 "with the same period last year". In its China section, "Coal generation fell by 56 TWh (-2%)" and "gas generation dropped by 2.7 TWh (-2%)". Coal and gas together: -56 - 2.7 = -58.7 TWh.

Step 3 (Doc 6): The same paragraph adds "Other fossil fuels fell by 0.1 TWh (-1.8%)". The report prints no total for China's fossil generation, so the first-half fossil change is -58.7 - 0.1 = -58.8 TWh.

Step 4 (Doc 4 and Doc 6): The July to December change is the full-year change less the first-half change: -56 + 58.8 = +2.8 TWh.

Step 5 (Doc 4 and Doc 6): Treating the first-half coal fall as the whole first-half fossil fall gives -56 + 56 = 0 TWh. That leaves out the gas and other fossil falls, so it is wrong.
