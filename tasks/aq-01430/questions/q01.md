## Question

How much did coal-fired electricity generation in the European Union change from 2019 to 2021? Give the answer in TWh with its sign.

## Answer

-34 TWh

## Components

- c1: -34 TWh

## Reasoning

Step 1 (Doc 3): In its European Union section, the review of 2024 says "Over the last five years, coal generation has fallen by 182 TWh (-40%)". The same paragraph dates that window: gas "was 139 TWh (-24%) lower in 2024 than it was in 2019". So EU coal generation fell 182 TWh from 2019 to 2024.

Step 2 (Doc 4): In its European Union section, the review of 2025 says EU fossil generation "declined by 24% (-257 TWh) between 2021 and 2025", and that coal generation accounted for "(-162 TWh) since 2021" of that decline. So EU coal generation fell 162 TWh from 2021 to 2025.

Step 3 (Doc 4): EU coal generation "declined by 5% (-14 TWh) in 2025". Removing 2025 from the 2021 window gives the change from 2021 to 2024: -162 + 14 = -148 TWh.

Step 4 (Doc 3 and Doc 4): The 2019 window and the 2021 window now both end in 2024, so the change from 2019 to 2021 is -182 + 148 = -34 TWh.

Step 5 (Doc 3 and Doc 4): Setting the two printed windows against each other without removing 2025 gives -182 + 162 = -20 TWh. That is wrong, because the 2021 window ends in 2025 while the 2019 window ends in 2024.
