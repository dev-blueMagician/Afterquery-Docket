## Question

In the calendar quarter when Chevron discovered oil at the Far South prospect, what was the year-over-year production growth of its Gulf of America operations minus that of its Permian Basin operations, in percentage points?

## Answer

8 percentage points

## Components

- c1: 8 percentage points

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 6: Chevron "Discovered oil at the non-operated Far South prospect in the deepwater Gulf of America in April 2025". April 2025 falls in the second quarter of 2025, the quarter after the one Doc 6 reports.

Step 2. Doc 8 reports second quarter 2025 results: worldwide production rose from a year ago "as growth at the company's Tengizchevroil (TCO) affiliate (34 percent), in the Gulf of America (22 percent), and in the Permian Basin (14 percent) more than offset the impacts of asset sales".

Step 3. Doc 8: Gulf of America growth minus Permian Basin growth = 22 - 14 = 8 percentage points.

Decoy check. Doc 6's own first quarter 2025 figures are "growth at TCO (20 percent), in the Permian Basin (12 percent), and in the Gulf of America (7 percent)", which would give 7 - 12 = -5 percentage points.
