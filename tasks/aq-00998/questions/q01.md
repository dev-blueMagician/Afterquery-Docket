## Question

By how much did Chevron's net debt change over the calendar quarter in which Chevron signed a 20-year agreement to supply electricity to a Microsoft data center? Give the answer in millions of U.S. dollars, with its sign.

## Answer

-$11,556 million

## Components

- c1: -$11,556 million

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 12 reports Chevron's second quarter 2026 results and says: "During the quarter, Chevron signed a 20-year power purchase agreement with Microsoft" for its data center in West Texas. The signing quarter is therefore the second quarter of 2026.

Step 2. Doc 11, the first quarter 2026 release, reports only an exclusivity agreement with Microsoft and Engine No. 1 "related to a proposed power generation and electricity offtake agreement". That is not a signed supply agreement, so it does not place the signing in the first quarter.

Step 3. Doc 11: net debt at March 31, 2026, the start of the signing quarter, was $40,101 million.

Step 4. Doc 12: net debt at June 30, 2026, the end of the signing quarter, was $28,545 million.

Step 5. Doc 11 and Doc 12: change over the quarter = 28,545 - 40,101 = -11,556 ($ millions), a decrease.

Decoy check. Doc 12 compares June 30, 2026 with December 31, 2025, so its two columns give a six-month change: 28,545 - 34,461 = -5,916. Its "$8.4 billion" reduction refers to total debt, not net debt.

Decoy check. Doc 11's own columns give the first quarter of 2026, the exclusivity quarter: 40,101 - 34,461 = 5,640.
