## Question

How many thousand barrels per day higher were Chevron's U.S. refinery crude unit inputs (crude oil plus other inputs) in the second quarter of 2026 than in the calendar quarter in which Chevron acquired its majority stake in ACES Delta?

## Answer

90 thousand barrels per day

## Evidence

- D04 (l.81, financial highlights): "absence of the third quarter 2023 acquisition of a majority stake in ACES Delta, LLC"
- D04 (l.161, U.S. Downstream table; columns 3Q 2024, 2Q 2024, 3Q 2023, 9M 2024, 9M 2023): "Refinery Crude Unit Inputs	MBD	995 900 980 925 965"
- D04 (l.167, U.S. Downstream bullet): "Refinery crude unit inputs, including crude oil and other inputs, increased 2 percent"
- D12 (l.163, U.S. Downstream table; columns 2Q 2026, 1Q 2026, 2Q 2025, 6M 2026, 6M 2025): "Refinery Crude Unit Inputs	MBD	1,070 1,054 1,051 1,063 1,034"
