## Question

In the calendar quarter in which Chevron discovered oil at its non-operated Far South prospect, by how many percentage points did the year-over-year production growth of Chevron's Gulf of America operations exceed that of its Permian Basin operations?

## Answer

8 percentage points

## Evidence

- D08 (l.17, title): "Chevron Reports Second Quarter 2025 Results"
