## Question

By how much did Chevron's net debt change over the calendar quarter in which Chevron signed a 20-year agreement to supply electricity to a Microsoft data center? Give the answer in millions of U.S. dollars, with its sign.

## Answer

-$11,556 million

## Evidence

- D12 (l.54, Wirth quote paragraph): "During the quarter, Chevron signed a 20-year power purchase agreement with Microsoft"
- D12 (l.341, Attachment 2 header): "SELECTED BALANCE SHEET ACCOUNT DATA (Preliminary) June 30, 2026 December 31,"
