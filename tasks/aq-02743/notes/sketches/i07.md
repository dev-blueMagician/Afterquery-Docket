## Question

Two HHS-funded towns each have fewer than 50,000 people; one has 15 or more employees, the other fewer than 15. After the 2026 extensions, how many days apart are the dates on which any federal rule first requires each to meet WCAG 2.1?

## Answer

351 days

## Evidence

- D03 (SUMMARY, L25): "The compliance date for public entities with a total population of less than 50,000, or any special district government, is extended from April 26, 2027, to April 26, 2028."
- D04 (SUMMARY, L26): "The compliance date for recipients with fifteen (15) or more employees is extended from May 11, 2026, to May 11, 2027."
- D04 (SUMMARY, L27): "(15) employees is extended from May 10, 2027, to May 10, 2028."
- D04 (overlap, L143): "There is significant overlap between covered entities under both rules since some recipients of financial assistance from HHS are also public entities governed by title II of the ADA."
