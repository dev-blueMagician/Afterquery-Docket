## Question

Discounting at 7% with compliance dates delayed by one year, what is DOJ's web rule's annualized benefits minus costs, divided by HHS's, as a whole percentage?

## Answer

524%

## Evidence

- D03 (footnote 82, L318): "The 2024 rule was expected to generate the 10-year average annualized benefits of $4,509.5 million, while incurring the 10-year average annualized costs of $3,249 million using a 7 percent discount rate."
- D04 (Table 1 annualized row, L354): "Annualized...................................... .............. 934.7 .............. 820.3 .............. -114.3"
- D04 (Table 3 annualized row, L368): "Annualized...................................... .............. 1,265.6 .............. 1,061.0 .............. -204.6"
