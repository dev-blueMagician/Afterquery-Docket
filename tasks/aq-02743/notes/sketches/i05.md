## Question

Two special district governments each have 15 or more employees. One holds an HHS grant; HHS pays the other only under a direct procurement contract. After the 2026 extensions, how many days apart must they first conform to WCAG 2.1?

## Answer

351 days

## Evidence

- D03 (SUMMARY, L25): "The compliance date for public entities with a total population of less than 50,000, or any special district government, is extended from April 26, 2027, to April 26, 2028."
- D04 (SUMMARY, L26): "The compliance date for recipients with fifteen (15) or more employees is extended from May 11, 2026, to May 11, 2027."
- D04 (definition, L260): "contract (other than a direct Federal procurement contract or a contract of insurance or guaranty)"
- D04 (scope, L49): "apply to programs and activities conducted by recipients of Federal financial assistance"
