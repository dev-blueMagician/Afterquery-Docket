## Question

When SEC proceedings on T. Rowe Price's crypto ETF proposal began, its minimum of shares outstanding at launch was how many times Nasdaq's proposed minimum for the Hashdex Nasdaq Crypto Index US ETF?

## Answer

2.5 times

## Evidence

- D06 (Trading Rules, L329): "A minimum of 100,000 Shares of the Fund will be outstanding at the commencement of trading on the Exchange."
- D06 (notice, L18): "notice is hereby given that on November 6, 2025, NYSE Arca, Inc."
- D02 (Initial and Continued Listing, L434): "A minimum of 40,000 Shares, or the equivalent of four Baskets, will be required to be outstanding at the time of commencement of trading on the Exchange."
