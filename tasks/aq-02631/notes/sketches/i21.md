## Question

On what date would a Hashdex trust portfolio held at the index weights of December 31, 2024 first have met proposed Rule 5712's 90% requirement?

## Answer

March 18, 2025

## Evidence

- D02 (proposed Rule 5712(e)(1)(ii), L62): "at least 90% of the weight of such holdings shall, on both an initial and continuing basis, consist of commodities and/or digital assets concerning which the Exchange is able to obtain information via the Intermarket Surveillance Group"
- D02 (surveillance discussion, L379): "Notably, the Coinbase Derivatives offers derivative products for three additional Index Constituents (as of the filing date): chainlink (LINK), litecoin (LTC), and avalanche (AVAX)."
- D02 (weight table heading, L127): "As of December 31, 2024, the Index Constituents and their weightings"
- D06 (SOL background, L144): "On March 18, 2025, SOL futures became available for trading on CME, a CFTC-regulated marketplace."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h13, round 2. A rule in one filing applied to a dated event that only an unrelated filing prints, with no pointer to it in the question. Nasdaq's test counts assets on which it can get information through ISG; D02 ties that to futures on ISG member markets (CME for bitcoin and ether, L352 and L492; Coinbase Derivatives for LINK, LTC, AVAX, L379-380). At the December 31, 2024 weights the uncovered constituents (Solana 4.78, XRP 5.02, Cardano 1.50, Uniswap 0.27) weigh 11.57, over the tenth allowed. The T. Rowe Price filing (D06) dates CME's Solana futures to March 18, 2025 and XRP's to May 20, 2025. With Solana covered the uncovered weight is 6.79, so the first date is March 18, 2025.
Ambiguity: May 20, 2025 (XRP's CME date) fails "first": Solana's futures come earlier and Solana alone is enough (11.57 - 4.78 = 6.79). September 18, 2025 (six months after the Solana launch) applies NYSE Arca's generic six-month criterion (D06 L64, L261), which is not part of Nasdaq's Rule 5712. "At once, after the sponsor's adjustment" fails "held at the index weights of December 31, 2024". The answer does not depend on whether LINK, LTC and AVAX count as covered: with only bitcoin and ether covered the uncovered weight falls from 12.82 to 8.04 on the same date. July 22, 2025 (D04's "currently lists and trades both XRP and Solana futures") is a later statement, not a launch date. Cardano or Uniswap alone could not cure it and no document dates futures for them.
Necessity: D02 -> rule, weights, which markets count -> "5712", "4.78", "Coinbase Derivatives" searched in all seven: D02 only. D06 -> the Solana launch date -> "SOL futures", "Solana futures", "futures became" searched: D06 L144 only (D04 L144 and D05 L145 say CME lists both without a date) -> without D06 no date can be given.
After clarification: the wording names neither Solana, CME, futures nor the T. Rowe Price filing.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Scatter the pieces; Calculate across documents. Wrong routes recomputed: XRP's date May 20, 2025; six-month route September 18, 2025.
Source risks: the step from "CME lists Solana futures" to "Nasdaq can obtain information on Solana through ISG" is D02's own reasoning for five other assets but is not stated for Solana anywhere; a solver may answer "cannot be determined". The weights are frozen by the question's own stipulation. Operands sit past character 20,000 in both documents (D02 table @27,797; D06 date @27,258).
Author calculation (Decimal, 2026-10-10): 4.78 + 5.02 + 1.50 + 0.27 = 11.57; 11.57 - 4.78 = 6.79 (covered 93.21); two-asset reading 12.82 - 4.78 = 8.04 (covered 91.96). March 18, 2025 is 28 days after the February 18, 2025 filing and 10 days before the March 28, 2025 comment deadline.
