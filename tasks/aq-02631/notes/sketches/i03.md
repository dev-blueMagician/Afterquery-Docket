## Question

How many days before the earliest day it could have approved the Bitwise 10 listing without a good-cause finding did SEC staff approve it?

## Answer

31 days

## Evidence

- D04 (Accelerated Approval, L169): "The Commission finds good cause to approve the Proposal prior to the 30th day after the date of publication of Amendment No. 1"
- D04 (order date, L187): "that the proposed rule change, as modified by Amendment No. 1 (SR-NYSEARCA-2024-98) be, and hereby is, approved on an accelerated basis."
- D05 (footnote 3, L26): "The proposed rule change, as modified by Amendment No. 1, was published for comment in the Federal Register on July 23, 2025."
- D05 (Introduction, L29): "On July 22, 2025, the Commission, acting through authority delegated to the Division of Trading and Markets"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h11. Linking across documents with a look-alike date. The staff order (D04, dated July 22, 2025) states the standard: good cause is needed to approve before the 30th day after Amendment No. 1's publication in the Federal Register. D04 was written before that publication happened, so it cites the amendment only by its release date, July 18, 2025 (fn 3). The publication date, July 23, 2025, is printed only in D05 fn 3. The 30th day after July 23, 2025 is August 22, 2025; the approval was dated 31 days earlier (it was issued a day before the amendment was even published).
Ambiguity: 26 days (30 days from the July 18, 2025 release date in D04 fn 3) fails "publication" in the standard the question points to. 33 days (30 days from D04's own Federal Register date, July 25, 2025) uses the order's publication, not the amendment's. 25 days (from the July 17, 2025 filing date, D05 L26) is the filing. "Earliest day ... without a good-cause finding": approving "prior to the 30th day" needs good cause, so the 30th day itself is the earliest day that does not. The original notice's publication (December 3, 2024, D05 fn 3) is not the one D04's standard names.
Necessity: D05 -> the amendment's Federal Register publication date -> "July 23" searched in all seven: D05 L26 only -> without D05 only July 18 (release) and July 25 (order's FR date) remain and the count changes. D04 -> the standard (30th day after publication of Amendment No. 1) -> "good cause", "30th" searched: D04 L169-170 only; D05 has no accelerated-approval section. Risk: the 30-day standard is statutory, so a solver who knows it may not need D04; the approval date July 22, 2025 is in both.
After clarification: the wording gives no date and does not say "30th day" or "publication"; both must come from D04, the date from D05.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Scatter the pieces; Calculate across documents. Wrong routes recomputed (Python 2026-10-10): 26, 33, 25.
Source risks: a date gap of two lookups and a stated rule; August 22, 2025 is also printed in D05 L29 as the deadline for statements on the stayed approval, so a solver can land on the right day for the wrong reason. D04's necessity rests on a rule experts know.
Author calculation: July 23, 2025 + 30 days = August 22, 2025; August 22, 2025 - July 22, 2025 = 31 days (datetime, elapsed days).
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: hold as a quick-test candidate from a different family than i01/i02.
