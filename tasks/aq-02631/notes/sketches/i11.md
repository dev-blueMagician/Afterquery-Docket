## Question

Under Hashdex's method for complying with proposed Rule 5712, counting as eligible only constituents whose futures markets Nasdaq's filing cites, by how many percentage points would Solana's portfolio weight at December 31, 2024 index weights exceed Bitwise 10's June 30, 2025 Solana weight (two decimals)?

## Answer

1.10 percentage points

## Evidence

- D02 (sponsor's method, L144): "The combined allocation of all digital assets other than those eligible under the 90% requirement will be limited to 10% of the portfolio, with their individual weights proportionally adjusted to reflect their relative weightings in the Index."
- D02 (surveillance discussion, L379): "Notably, the Coinbase Derivatives offers derivative products for three additional Index Constituents (as of the filing date): chainlink (LINK), litecoin (LTC), and avalanche (AVAX)."
- D02 (weight table, L127): "As of December 31, 2024, the Index Constituents and their weightings"
- D05 (Summary of the Proposal, L58): "As of June 30, 2025, the Trust's Portfolio Assets and their weightings were: 78.72% bitcoin (BTC), 11.10% ether (ETH), 4.97% XRP (XRP), 3.03% Solana (SOL)"
