## Question

On the last day of the temporary coastal-trading exemption for container and roll-on, roll-off voyages to Western Australia, how many dollars more per full import container was Patrick's stevedore infrastructure surcharge at Fremantle than DP World Australia's there?

## Answer

$12.69

## Evidence

- D06 (L152, permanent exemption section): "for voyages by container and roll-on, roll-off vessels to Western Australia until 31 March 2022"
- D02 (L174, Attachment A title): "INFRASTRUCTURE SURCHARGES - AS AT 25 January 2022 per full container / excluding GST"
- D02 (L178, DP World Fremantle row): "DP World Australia Fremantle $45.00 $45.00 1-Jan-21"
- D02 (L179, Patrick Fremantle row): "Patrick Fremantle $26.92 $53.85 1-Mar-21 28.84 57.69 7/03/2022"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1, two-row variant. Same anchor as i01 (D06 31 March 2022), Fremantle only.
Ambiguity: $8.85 (as-at 53.85 - 45.00) fails the date condition; export rows give -16.16 / -18.08 and fail
"import". Necessity: as i01 (D06 date only source; D02 rates only source).
Weakness found at screen time: a solver who applies every right-hand rate without checking dates also reaches
$12.69, because no Fremantle rate changes after 31 March. Only the stale reading fails, so the trap is one-sided.
i01 adds FACT's 1/04/2022 boundary, which defeats that shortcut ($120.28).
Author calculation: 57.69 - 45.00 = 12.69 (scratchpad calc_i01.py).
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: pending screen; kept as the simpler fallback, not promoted.
