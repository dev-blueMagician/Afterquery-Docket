## Question

If Port Botany's container throughput grew at one constant annual rate from its 2019 level to the volume expected in 2045, what would that rate be, as a percentage to one decimal place?

## Answer

4.6%

## Evidence

- D09 (L107, landside congestion): "Yet the port handled only 2.6 million TEU in 2019"
- D13 (L167, Port Botany 30 years plan): "Containers expected to increase from 2.7 million TEU (today) to 8.4 million per annum by 2045"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: forecast (D13) vs actual (D09) across documents; D13 also prints a "today" base (2.7 million) in the
same sentence as the forecast, a same-document decoy for the 2019 base.
Ambiguity: 4.5% (2.7 million base, 26 years) and 5.1% (2.7 million, 23 years) fail "from its 2019 level"; 4.4% or
4.8% (27 or 25 compounding years) fail "2019 to 2045" (26 annual steps); 8.6% is the linear rate, not "one constant
annual rate" of compound growth. Necessity: D09 is the only 2019 Port Botany figure; D13 the only 2045 expectation.
Source risks: NSW Ports plan figures may be recalled by a strong model (Document check); short route (two lookups and
one formula), so likely below Medium. Author calculation: (8.4 / 2.6)^(1/26) - 1 = 4.6137% -> 4.6%.
Screen decision: fallback only; not promoted.
