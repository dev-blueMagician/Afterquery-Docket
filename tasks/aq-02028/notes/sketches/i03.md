## Question

Taking the Port of Melbourne's annual throughput as stated by its newest container terminal, how many fewer truck trips a year would trucks loaded to estimated capacity, not the current average, need? Nearest thousand.

## Answer

329,000 truck trips

## Evidence

- D01 (L35, establishment of VICT): "VICT was awarded the concession to design, build and operate the third international container terminal in the Port of Melbourne"
- D01 (L33, Port of Melbourne): "It currently services over 2.88 million Twenty-foot equivalent Units (TEU) annually"
- D08 (L140, asset utilisation): "the current average is 2.5 TEU"
- D08 (L147, truck trips): "handled through Melbourne with a load factor of 2.5 TEU vehicle per trip resulting in approximately 1.2million truck trips pa"
- D08 (L247, load factor): "The estimated capacity is 3.5 TEU per vehicle"
