## Question

On the last day of the temporary coastal-trading exemption for container and roll-on, roll-off voyages to Western Australia, what was the mean stevedore infrastructure surcharge per full import container across the 13 container terminals at Adelaide, Brisbane, Fremantle, Melbourne and Sydney, in dollars to the cent?

## Answer

$117.39

## Evidence

- D06 (L152, permanent exemption section): "for voyages by container and roll-on, roll-off vessels to Western Australia until 31 March 2022"
- D02 (L174, Attachment A title): "INFRASTRUCTURE SURCHARGES - AS AT 25 January 2022 per full container / excluding GST"
- D02 (L175, Attachment A header): "STEVEDORE PORT EXPORT IMPORT EFFECTIVE"
- D02 (L137, narrative on Attachment A): "the latest increases in charges by Patrick Terminals are highlighted on the right-hand side which will be implemented on 1 March 2022"
- D02 (L176, FACT row): "Flinders Adelaide Container Terminal Adelaide $75.00 $75.00 1-Jul-21 79.50 112.50 1/04/2022"
- D02 (L178, Patrick Brisbane row): "Patrick Brisbane $84.90 $113.20 1-Mar-21 101.90 135.85 7/03/2022"
- D02 (L180, Patrick Melbourne row): "Patrick Melbourne $84.90 $129.50 1-Mar-21 101.90 155.40 7/03/2022"
