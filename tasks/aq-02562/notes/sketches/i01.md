## Question

What is the most MASC and ASCF combined could pay a four-partner general partnership if one partner's average AGI exceeded $900,000, all from farming?

(Screened first as "a general partnership of four U.S. citizens if one partner's 2021-2023 average AGI
exceeded $900,000" (28 words); promoted at 24 words: both programs fix the 2021-2023 years, and "most"
covers citizenship.)

## Answer

$1,250,000

## Evidence

- D02 (Average AGI Limitation and Payment Limitation): "a MASC payment if the average adjusted gross income of the person or legal entity exceeds $900,000 for 2021, 2022, and 2023, unless the person or legal entity's average adjusted gross farm income is at least"
- D02 (Average AGI Limitation and Payment Limitation): "75 percent of their average AGI."
- D02 (Average AGI Limitation and Payment Limitation): "A person or legal entity, other than a joint venture or general partnership, cannot receive, directly or indirectly, more than $125,000 in MASC payments."
- D02 (Average AGI Limitation and Payment Limitation): "A payment made to a legal entity will be attributed to those members who have a direct or indirect ownership interest in the legal entity"
- D07 (section VI): "is ineligible for ASCF Program payments, directly or indirectly, if the person's or legal entity's average adjusted gross income (AGI), using the average of the adjusted gross incomes for the 2021, 2022, and 2023 tax years, exceeds $900,000."
- D07 (Sec. 1414.109(a)): "A person, legal entity, or member of a joint venture or general partnership, as determined in 7 CFR part 1400 in effect for program year 2025 on June 1, 2026, cannot receive, directly or indirectly, more than $250,000 under this subpart."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: parallel payment rules applied to one stated case, asked as a total (measured Medium
recipe of aq-02486). Both programs bar average AGI over $900,000 for 2021-2023; only MASC (D02) lets
a person with at least 75 percent farm income back in. Both caps exclude general partnerships and bind
each partner (D02 attribution to members; D07 cap on each "member of a joint venture or general
partnership"). Independent authors A and B converged on this asymmetry; B's partnership form is used.
Ambiguity: $1,500,000 (MASC exception carried into ASCF) fails D07 sec. VI and 1414.109(b), which print
the bar with no exception; $1,125,000 (exception missed) fails "all from farming" against D02's
75 percent clause; $375,000 (each cap applied once to the partnership) fails both caps' "other than a
joint venture or general partnership" plus D02's attribution and D07's member cap; $500,000 / $750,000
are one program only ("combined"); $875,000, $625,000, $1,375,000, $750,000 are mixed two-error routes;
no route but the correct one reaches $1,250,000 with four partners (with three, a two-error route ties).
Unstated facts (other partners' AGI, shares, sales, acres) are settled by "most": per-member caps make
the supremum independent of ownership shares. Proration or a lowered MASC limit cannot raise the total.
Necessity: D02 -> $125,000 cap, attribution to members, 75 percent exception (searched "125,000",
