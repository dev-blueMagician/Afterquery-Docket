## Question

What was BHP's average realised steelmaking coal price, in US dollars per tonne, for the quarter ended 30 June 2025? Work it out from the realised prices and sales volumes BHP reports, without rounding intermediate values, and give one decimal place.

## Answer

177.3

Steps:

1. Neither review prints a price for the quarter ended 30 June 2025 (BHP's Q4 FY25): it is the FY25 price (D02, US$193.82/t) less the nine months to March 2025 (D01, US$200.12/t), each weighted by the volumes the price covers.
