## Question

In April–June 2026, how many more metric tons of ore per day did Freeport's Cerro Verde concentrator process than BHP's Escondida concentrators? Give the answer to the nearest thousand.

## Answer

22,000

## Evidence

- D04 (selected operating data, South America mill operations, three months ended June 30, 2026): "|Ore milled (metric tons per day)|416,400|404,800|418,400|408,100|"
- D04 (South America development activities): "At the El Abra operations in Chile, FCX has an attractive opportunity to expand the operation to include a major mill facility similar to the large-scale concentrator at Cerro Verde."
- D02 (Appendix 2, Escondida, quarter ended June 2026 column): "||Concentrator throughput|kt|36,490|36,721|35,628|34,225|35,862|142,436|137,160|4%|||||||||"
- D02 (Appendix 2, Escondida footnote): "1 Presented on a 100% basis. BHP interest in saleable production is 57.5%."

Steps:

1. Map April–June 2026 onto each report: BHP's quarter ended June 2026 (Q4 FY26, D02's "Jun 2026" column) and Freeport's second quarter (D04's three-month column, not its six-month one).
2. Identify the two concentrators: Freeport reports no Cerro Verde line, but its South American mill operations are Cerro Verde's (El Abra has no mill: Freeport could "include a major mill facility similar to the large-scale concentrator at Cerro Verde"): 416,400 t/day. Escondida's concentrator throughput, on the 100% basis its footnote states: 35,862 kt in the quarter.
3. Put Escondida on a per-day basis over the quarter's 91 days (30 + 31 + 30): 35,862,000 / 91 = 394,088 t/day; difference 416,400 - 394,088 = 22,312, so 22,000.

Decoy: 18,000 or 27,000 (Escondida's quarter spread over 90 or 92 days), or 24,000 (Freeport's six-month rate)
