## Question

What was BHP's average realised energy coal price, in US dollars per tonne, for the quarter ended 30 June 2025? Work it out from the realised prices and sales volumes BHP reports, without rounding intermediate values, and give one decimal place.

## Answer

Steps:

1. Neither review prints a price for the quarter ended 30 June 2025 (BHP's Q4 FY25): it is the FY25 price (D02, US$107.80/t) less the nine months to March 2025 (D01, US$115.99/t), each weighted by the volumes the price covers.
2. Take the volumes the price note defines (export sales only, including energy coal sold from the steelmaking coal mines): FY25 13,569 + 313 = 13,882 kt (D02); nine months 10,015 + 114 = 10,129 kt (D01); quarter 3,753 kt.
3. Compute (107.80 x 13,882 - 115.99 x 10,129) / 3,753 = 321,616.9 / 3,753 = 85.6959, so 85.7.
