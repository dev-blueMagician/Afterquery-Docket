## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h11 (notes/hooks.md). The two companies report copper on different consolidation bases: Freeport consolidates PTFI, Cerro Verde and El Abra at 100% and prints a "Net" row; BHP presents subsidiaries such as Escondida at 100% with the interest in a table note and equity-accounted Antamina already at its share. A like-for-like comparison has to apply both rules, and BHP never reports a calendar half.
Hard design (playbook section 4): L1 is quiet: BHP's group totals carry no ownership label, and only the Escondida table's note says the rows are 100% (BHP 57.5%); taking BHP's totals as its own gives -25.2. L2 is quiet: Antamina's table header says "BHP interest 33.75%", which invites a second scaling, but the end note's basis rule says equity-accounted volumes are already proportionate (7.4 if scaled again). L3: BHP's year runs to June; January-June is two quarter columns (FY26 gives 2.2; production instead of sales gives 0.4; Q2/June quarter alone 0.1). The question's words ("partners or minority shareholders", "belonged to") lead to ownership labels, which hold the traps as well as the settlements.
Ambiguity: (a) "reported copper sales": Freeport's consolidated "Sales, excluding purchases" (1,367) and BHP's group copper sales total (952.0, which equals the asset sum). BHP: Escondida 57.5%; Spence, Copper SA and Carajás 100% (their table headers); Antamina as reported (proportionate). (c) Carajás (divested 2 April 2026) counts at 100% for its March-quarter sales.
