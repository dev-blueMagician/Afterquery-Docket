## Question

Using the LME averages Freeport reports, by how many US cents per pound did BHP's average realised copper price for January to June 2026 exceed the average LME copper settlement price over those six months?

## Answer

27

Steps:

1. Map January-June 2026 onto BHP's year ending 30 June: it is the second half of FY26, US$6.21/lb (not FY26's 5.74 or Q4's 6.53).
2. Build the LME average for the same six months, which no document prints: Freeport's Q1 release gives the quarter's average of monthly settlements, $5.83, its Q2 release $6.05; (5.83 + 6.05) / 2 = 5.94.
