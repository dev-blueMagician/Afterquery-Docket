## Question

Freeport's April and July 2026 releases each estimate full-year 2026 molybdenum sales. By how many million pounds did the July-December 2026 sales implied by each release's own figures change from April to July?

## Answer

0

- D04 (highlights): "93 million pounds of molybdenum for the year 2026"

Steps:
