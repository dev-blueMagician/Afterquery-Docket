## Question

What is the least Reach can pay in total yearly salaries to the local democracy reporters allocated to it in July 2021?

## Answer

£1,875,000

## Evidence

- D04 (para 33): "Of the 165 reporters allocated in July 2021, Reach"
- D04 (para 33, continued after the page's footnotes): "Plc took 75, Newsquest 28.5, and JPI Media (now National World) 35.5."
- D02 (Q222, the BBC's answer, headed "Tom Morrison-Bell" in the transcript): "the minimum salary would be £24,000, but we would expect the salary for each individual role to be benchmarked against a senior reporter on the same publication"
- D02 (Q161, David Higgerson): "On the LDR scheme it is impossible for you to pay less than what you are paying your existing seniors."
- D02 (Q143, David Higgerson): "The base senior reporter salary is £25,000 now."
- D04 (para 49): "for a senior journalist it was £25,000"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h3. The scheme's stated minimum (£24,000) is not the figure that binds Reach: each role is
benchmarked to a senior reporter on the same publication, Reach's executive says it cannot pay a local
democracy reporter less than its existing seniors, and its senior minimum is £25,000. The count of reporters
is only in the report.

Ambiguity (readings reported, not cleared):
- The scheme-wide minimum applied to Reach: 75 x £24,000 = £1,800,000. Rejected by the benchmark rule (Doc 3
  Q222) and by Reach's own account (Q161), which put a Reach reporter at or above its senior minimum. This
  reading is defensible to a reader who takes "But £24,00 is the minimum" as the whole rule and the benchmark
  as an expectation; look at "One defensible answer" before any run. If it blocks, the one-reading wording is
  "On its own executive's evidence, what is the least Reach can pay ...".
- Senior pay in London is higher (Doc 1 para 49): raises some salaries, not the least total.
- "allocated to it in July 2021" against salaries stated in June 2022: the report calls July 2021 "the latest
  allocation" (para 50), and the question asks what Reach "can pay", present tense at the evidence.
- Reach's "additional roles" hired into the scheme (Doc 3 Q138) are not allocated reporters.

Necessity:
- D04 (Doc 1): only place with Reach's 75. Without it no total.
- D02 (Doc 3): only place with the scheme's pay rule and Reach's account of it. Without it no salary floor.
- Substitutes searched: Doc 1 has Reach's £25,000 senior minimum but no LDRS salary rule; Doc 2 and Doc 4
  give 165 reporters and no pay rule (Doc 4 Q234: a general "£24,000 to £25,000" wage).

After clarification: two indispensable documents; the wording supplies no figure.

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Calculate across documents; apply a rule to a case. Wrong routes
recomputed: 75 x 24,000 = 1,800,000 (scheme minimum); 165 x 25,000 = 4,125,000 (every reporter); 75 x 21,500
= 1,612,500 (trainee minimum).

Source risks: the rule rests on testimony; the BBC's answer carries the wrong speaker label. Doc 1's
sentence with the 75 is split by a page's footnotes. A lower bound nobody states: a reviewer may call it a
figure the documents do not connect, though both witnesses tie the reporter's salary to senior pay.

Author calculation: 75 reporters (Doc 1) x 25,000 pounds (Doc 3) = 1,875,000 pounds a year.
