## Question

How many Future News Pilot Fund applications went unfunded, and how much grant money did the average funded project receive? Answer in that order.

## Answer

158 applications and about £50,000

## Evidence

- D04 (para 41): "in October 2019 launched the £2 million Future News Pilot Fund (FNF), administered by Nesta"
- D04 (para 41): "It received 178 applications and issued grants to 20 projects."
- D03 (Q254, David Floyd): "That was £2 million and about £1 million was grant funding."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. The report gives the fund's size and the number of funded projects in adjacent sentences,
which looks sufficient for an average (£100,000). Only a witness who worked on the fund says, in another
document, that about half of it was grant funding. The gold needs that part, and the question does not say
where it is. Second ask (unfunded applications) is a number only the documents hold, added because the first
is a round value a closed-book guess could hit (aq-03169 q01).

Ambiguity (readings reported, not cleared):
- "grant money ... the average funded project" read as the whole fund over the projects: £2 million / 20 =
  £100,000. Rejected by "grant money": Doc 4 says only about £1 million of the £2 million was grant funding.
  Still the natural answer from Doc 1 alone; the form's "One defensible answer" check and the quick test will
  show whether solvers take it.
- "grants of tens of thousands of pounds" (Doc 4, same answer) describes two ICNN members' grants, not the
  average; it agrees with about £50,000 and not with £100,000.
- The 2015 Nesta fund "of around £2 million" (Doc 1 para 42) is a different fund with no project count.
- "applications went unfunded": 178 - 20 = 158, taking one grant per successful application. No document
  gives another count of applicants.
- Searched every mention of "Future News" and "Nesta" in all four documents (eleven and ten hits): no other
  amount, grant size or project count.

Necessity:
- D04 (Doc 1): only place with 178 applications and 20 projects. Without it neither part can be reached.
- D03 (Doc 4): only place separating grant funding from the fund's size. Without it the second part is
  £100,000, a different answer.
- Substitutes searched: Doc 2 and Doc 3 never mention the fund. Doc 1's "£1 million" is a Google grant to the
  NMA (para 45), not this fund.
