## Question

Darden's fiscal 2026 outlook, as it stood when that fiscal year's fourth quarter began, gave a range for reported diluted EPS from continuing operations. To the nearest cent, what was the actual full-year figure minus that range's midpoint?

## Answer

-$0.28 per share

## Components

- c1: -$0.28 per share

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 10 is Darden's release dated December 18, 2025. Its fiscal 2026 outlook reconciliation gives reported diluted net earnings per share from continuing operations of $10.62 to $10.82: 10.62 + 10.82 = 21.44.

Step 2. Doc 10: 21.44 / 2 = 10.72, the midpoint of that range.

Step 3. Doc 11 is dated March 19, 2026 and reports the third quarter ended February 22, 2026, so the fourth quarter had already begun when Doc 11 updated the outlook. Doc 11's later range was not yet in place at the start of the fourth quarter, and using it would give a different, wrong answer; the outlook in place was still the one in Doc 10.

Step 4. Doc 12: reported diluted net earnings per share from continuing operations for fiscal 2026 were $10.44.

Step 5. Doc 10 and Doc 12: 10.44 - 10.72 = -0.28.
