## Question

Leaving out the estimated legal-proceedings liability Chipotle booked within labor costs during 2026, what were its labor costs as a percentage of total revenue for the six months ended June 30, 2026? Give one decimal place.

## Answer

25.3%

## Evidence

- D01 (Doc 5, adjusted labor reconciliation line 333): "Legal proceedings-Labor(1) (11,875)"
- D01 (Doc 5, footnote): "Estimated liability recognized in labor on the condensed consolidated statements of income for legal matters that we expect to exceed typical costs for legal proceedings."
- D02 (Doc 6, six-month income statement line 145): "Labor 1,641,861 25.5 1,474,487 24.8"
- D02 (Doc 6, six-month income statement line 142): "Total revenue 6,436,804 100.0 5,938,646 100.0"
