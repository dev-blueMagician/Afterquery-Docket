## Question

By June 30, 2026, how much income tax benefit in total had Yum! recognized from its internal reorganization of the Pizza Hut legal entities, counting every quarter since that reorganization began? Answer in millions of dollars.

## Answer

$202 million

## Evidence

- D12 (Doc 1, special items tax table line 807): "Tax (Benefit) - Intra-entity transfers and valuations of intellectual property (89) (32) (89) (32)"
- D12 (Doc 1, note line 821): "The tax benefit recorded in the year ended December 31, 2025, which resulted from an internal reorganization to consolidate the Pizza Hut entities and assets into a single ownership structure"
- D11 (Doc 3, note line 722): "reflects tax benefits of $91 million and $113 million, respectively, resulting from an internal reorganization to consolidate the Pizza Hut legal entities and assets into two isolated ownership structures"
