## Question

Darden's fiscal 2026 outlook, as it stood when that fiscal year's fourth quarter began, gave a range for reported diluted EPS from continuing operations. By how much did the actual full-year figure fall short of that range's midpoint?

## Answer

$0.28 per share

## Evidence

- D04 (Doc 10, outlook reconciliation table line 139): "Reported diluted net earnings per share from continuing operations $10.62 to $10.82"
- D04 (Doc 10, dateline line 9): "ORLANDO, Fla., December 18, 2025 /PRNewswire/ -- Darden Restaurants, Inc. (NYSE:DRI) today reported its financial results for the second quarter ended November 23, 2025."
- D06 (Doc 12, fiscal 2026 highlights line 49): "Reported diluted net earnings per share from continuing operations were $10.44"
- D06 (Doc 12, dateline): "fourth quarter and fiscal year ended May 31, 2026, which included a 53rd week of operations"
