## Question

In eight federal rulings issued September 11-15, 2026, which defendants did the court say were within its personal jurisdiction, yet dismiss every claim against them in that same ruling?

## Answer

Lorenzo Vidino and Lewis Machining & Engineering, Inc.

## Evidence

- D02 (l.515): "Defendant Vidino, the American journalist over whom the Court has personal jurisdiction, is charged in only one count"
- D02 (l.551): "Therefore, the complaint fails to state the only claim against Vidino, and the case against him will be dismissed."
- D05 (l.80): "The Court has subject matter jurisdiction over this matter and personal jurisdiction over the Parties."
- D05 (l.156): "All claims asserted by Plaintiffs in their Complaint for Declaratory Judgment (Doc. 1)"

defendant; every claim against that defendant dismissed in the same ruling). Both gold items are non-salient: Vidino's

for lack of personal jurisdiction; Lewis Machining's appears in a formulaic consent-judgment finding (D05 l.80), and
the claims against it are dismissed with prejudice by the consent judgment (D05 l.68, l.156).
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Match then exclude; Bury one list item late. Decoys: OEC India, held within
