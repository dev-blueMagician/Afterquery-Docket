## Question

Which Pension Schemes Act 1993 subsection did alpha's rule on transfer payments to other schemes cite from 1 April 2022?

## Answer

Section 95(2)

## Evidence

- D02 (Doc 1, Annex E draft regulation 5, heading): "Amendment of regulation 142 (transfer of payments made to other schemes or pension arrangements)"
- D02 (Doc 1, Annex E draft regulation 5): "In regulation 142(4), for “95(2)” substitute “95(2A)”."
- D02 (Doc 1, Annex E draft regulation 1(2)): "These Regulations come into force on 1st April 2022"
- D01 (Doc 2, paragraph 4.61): "Cabinet Office has decided not to proceed with the amendment of regulation 142"
