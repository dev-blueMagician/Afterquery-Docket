## Question

How many days after the 2023 remedy consultation opened was its proposed last day for receiving opted-out service elections, barring extensions?

## Answer

573 days

## Evidence

- D04 (Doc 3, paragraph 6.5(c)): "received by the scheme manager after 1st October 2023 but before 30 September 2024, or later if the scheme manager considers there are reasonable circumstances to extend the deadline"
- D03 (Doc 4, Introduction): "which began on 6 March 2023 and closed on 14 May 2023"
