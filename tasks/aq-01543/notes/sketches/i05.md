## Question

Divide South Africa's existing nuclear generating capacity by the combined capacity of the nuclear reactors South Korea had in operation in late September 2025. What is the result in percent, rounded to two decimal places?

## Answer

7.79%

## Evidence

- D15 (Nuclear, L165): "Korea has 26 nuclear reactors, 23 of which are currently in operation"
- D15 (Table 2, L167): "Table 2: List of nuclear power plants in Korea"
- D15 (Table 2, L318): "(Data as of September 25, 2025)"
- D15 (Table 2, L177): "Taken offline on 3/8/23, 9/28/24, and 8/6/25 and waits for a decision on its operation license renewal"
- D14 (L20): "two reactors completed in the 1980s have a combined generating capacity of 1,830 MW"
