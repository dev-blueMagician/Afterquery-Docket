## Question

What is the combined annual processing capacity, in tons of waste, of the waste-to-energy plants in Israel and the United Arab Emirates that are planned or under construction but not yet operating?

## Answer

2,500,000 tons per year

## Evidence

- D08 (L38): "The Jerusalem Region Waste-to-Energy facility of 650k tons annually."
- D08 (L39): "The Tamar Region Waste-to-Energy facility of 650k tons annually."
- D08 (L73): "began construction in 2025 and is expected to process 300,000 tons of waste annually"
- D08 (L73): "Two additional facilities are planned, one in Jerusalem and another in southern Israel, each designed to treat 650,000 tons of waste per year."
- D17 (L46): "the project will have an expected annual processing capacity of 900,000 tons of waste"
- D17 (L48): "Sharjah Waste to Energy Facility, completed in 2022, has an annual processing capacity of 300,000 metric tons"
