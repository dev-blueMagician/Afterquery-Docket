## Question

In 2024, what was the share of the United Kingdom's electricity generation that came from renewable sources minus the same share for Mauritius, in percentage points?

## Answer

32.8 percentage points

## Evidence

- D18 (L12): "26% gas, 35% wind and solar, 14% nuclear, 16% other renewable, 1% coal and 8% other"
- D09 (L20): "18.2 percent (621.0 GWh) from renewable sources"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: grouped categories. The UK prints no single 2024 renewable share: wind and solar 35% plus
"other renewable" 16% = 51%. Mauritius prints 18.2% (621.0 of 3,417.6 GWh). 51 - 18.2 = 32.8.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: part vs total; decoys in D18: wind and solar 35% (-> 16.8), wind's
record 30% (L25, -> 11.8), "renewables/nuclear provided 25%" of 2023 total energy supply (L10, -> 6.8), clean
incl. nuclear 65% (-> 46.8). Mauritius decoys: 2030 target 60% (-> -9.0), 2028 aim "over 35 percent" (-> 16.0).
Source risks: UK 2024 renewable share is widely reported (about 50.8% in public statistics; recall gives 32.6,
a miss at one decimal); Mauritius 2024 share obscure. Likely Easy: a two-category sum is short.
Author calculation check and counting convention: 35 + 16 = 51; 51 - 18.2 = 32.8.
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: backup only.
