## Question

Taken together, what percentage of the electricity generated in South Korea and in mainland Portugal from January through November 2025 came from renewable sources? Give the percentage to two decimal places.

## Answer

15.46%

## Evidence

- D15 (Energy overview, L10): "with total generation estimated at 543.6TWh"
- D15 (Energy overview, L10): "Renewable generation remained at 57.6TWh, or 10.6%"
- D12 (Renewable Energy, L17): "a total of 44 303 GWh of electricity were generated in Mainland Portugal, with 75.1 % coming from renewable sources"
