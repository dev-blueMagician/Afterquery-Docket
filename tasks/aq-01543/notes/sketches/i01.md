## Question

For calendar year 2024, what was coal's share of Bulgaria's electricity production minus coal's share of South Korea's electricity generation, in percentage points to one decimal place?

## Answer

5.3 percentage points

## Evidence

- D02 (L10): "to some 37,963 GWh in 2024"
- D02 (L11): "Baseload power plants (coal, nuclear) generated a total of 28,46TWh of electricity in 2024"
- D02 (L13): "In 2024, nuclear power accounted for approximately 41.6% of Bulgaria’s total electricity generation"
- D15 (L9): "Coal and LNG each generated 167.2TWh, or 28.1%"
