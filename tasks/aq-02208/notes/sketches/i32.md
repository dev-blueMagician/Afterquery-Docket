## Question

How many more megahertz may carry launch-vehicle commands under FCC launch rules than the 2026 spacecraft-uplink band proposal?

## Answer

95 megahertz

## Evidence

- D03 (L80, part 26 direction limits kept from the Second R&O): "2025-2110 MHz band (Earth-to-space) and 2200-2290 MHz band (space-to-Earth)"
- D03 (L79, 2360-2395 MHz under part 26): "permit the use of the entire 2360-2395 MHz band for ground-to-launch vehicle and launch vehicle-to-ground communications"
- D05 (L183, 2026 proposal): "We propose to create a secondary allocation for SOS in the 2320-2345 MHz band in the non-Federal column of the United States Table in the Earth-to-space direction."

## Sketch notes

2320-2345 = 25 MHz (D05 L162, L183); 120 - 25 = 95.

band since the Third R&O); 65 (55 MHz of floated bands) fails "band proposal" (only 2320-2345 is proposed; others are
"seek comment").
