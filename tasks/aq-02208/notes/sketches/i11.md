## Question

How many more transmitter-characteristic items did the FCC's final space-launch frequency-coordination request list contain than its December 2024 proposed list?

## Answer

5 more items (17 in the final list versus 12 proposed)

## Evidence

- D02 (L172, proposed coordination request item 5): "5. Transmitter characteristics for each transmit station (center frequency):"
- D02 (L184, last proposed transmitter item): "l. Emission designator."
- D04 (L236, final coordination request item 5): "5. Transmitter characteristics for each transmit station (center frequency):"
- D04 (L253, last final transmitter item): "q. Emission designator;"
