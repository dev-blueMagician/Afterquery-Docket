## Question

Which items listed in the FCC Bureau's proposed space launch coordination-request data are required of no licensee under its final procedures?

## Answer

Distance to the nearest aircraft landing area, and natural formations or man-made structures that would shield the antenna from aircraft (in any order)

## Evidence

- D02 (line 195-199, proposed coordination-request group 7): "c. Distance to nearest aircraft landing area in kilometers; and"
- D02 (line 199): "d. Natural formations or existing man-made structures (hills, trees, water tanks, towers, etc.) which would tend to shield the antenna from aircraft."
- D04 (line 213, final fixed and base station registration): "4. Overall height above ground to tip of antenna in meters;"
- D04 (line 214, final fixed and base station registration): "5. Elevation of ground at antenna site above mean sea level in meters;"
- D04 (line 205, final station registration): "a justification for greater than five megahertz must be submitted in the initial station registration in ULS as a single attachment"
