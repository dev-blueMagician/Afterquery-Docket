## Question

On the basis used in Albemarle's Q2 2026 earnings release, by how much did its free cash flow (operating cash flow minus capital expenditures) for Q2 2026 exceed that for Q2 2025? Answer in millions of dollars to one decimal place.

## Answer

766.9

## Evidence

- D04 (free cash flow reconciliation, three months ended June 30, 2026): "Free cash flow $638,266"
- D04 (cash flow, six months ended June 30, 2025 column): "Net cash provided by operating activities 1,056,241 538,180"
- D04 (same table): "Capital expenditures (170,407) (302,252)"
- D02 (cash flow, three months ended March 31, 2025 column): "Net cash provided by operating activities 346,244 547,207"
- D02 (same table): "Capital expenditures (98,676) (182,624)"

## Lever stack

Shape: shape 2 (original against revised figures) on a period no document reports on its own: Q2 2025 cash flow exists only as the half-year less Q1, and Q1 2025 was quietly re-presented in the Q1 2026 release

- L1: Restated figures | trap: D01 "Net cash provided by operating activities 545,383 97,954" | settles: D02 "Net cash provided by operating activities 346,244 547,207" | missed gives: 765.1
- L2: Ask about a period no document reports on its own | trap: D03 "Cash from operations of $538 million in the first half of 2025" | settles: D04 "Six Months Ended June 30," | missed gives: 402.3
- L3: Exact table vs rounded prose | trap: D04 "free cash flow of $638 million" | settles: D04 "Free cash flow $638,266" | missed gives: 766.7

Steps:

1. Take Q2 2026 free cash flow from the Q2 2026 release's three-month reconciliation (D04: OCF 709,997 less capex 71,731 = 638,266 thousand).
2. Rebuild Q2 2025, which no release prints: the release's six-month 2025 column (OCF 538,180, capex 302,252) less Q1 2025 on the same basis, which only the Q1 2026 release's comparative prints (D02: OCF 547,207, capex 182,624): OCF -9,027, capex 119,628, FCF -128,655.
3. 638,266 - (-128,655) = 766,921 thousand, 766.9 million.

Decoy: 765.1 (Q1 2025 taken from its own release, D01: Q2 2025 FCF -126,831)
