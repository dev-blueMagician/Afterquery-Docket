## Question

In the US SIF Foundation's 2022 figures, by how many trillion dollars did assets in funds addressing climate change exceed assets in funds addressing the ESG issue that ranked second by fund assets? Give two decimal places.

## Answer

$1.65 trillion

## Components

- c1: $1.65 trillion

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 12: citing the US SIF Foundation's "2022 Report on US Sustainable Investing Trends", it says that of "the $8.4 trillion AUM in funds that consider ESG factors", "$3.45 trillion address climate change/carbon and $1.23 trillion apply fossil fuel divestment policies".
Step 2. Doc 7: citing US SIF's 2022 report (the same $8.4 trillion total), it groups climate change with fossil fuel divestment at "a combined $4.8 trillion" and says "The next largest ESG categories, military/weapons and tobacco, are addressed in funds with $1.8 trillion and $1.7 trillion in AUM".
Step 3. Doc 7 and Doc 12: the $4.8 trillion combines climate change with fossil fuel divestment, so the figure for climate change itself is Doc 12's climate change/carbon ($3.45 trillion), which ranks first. Fossil fuel divestment ($1.23 trillion) is smaller than both military/weapons ($1.8 trillion) and tobacco ($1.7 trillion), so military/weapons ranks second.
Step 4. Doc 12 and Doc 7: 3.45 - 1.8 = 1.65 trillion dollars.
Decoy (Doc 12): taking fossil fuel divestment, the other issue in the same sentence, as second gives 3.45 - 1.23 = 2.22, but Doc 7's military/weapons and tobacco figures are both larger.
Decoy (Doc 7): taking the grouped climate-and-fossil-fuel figure for climate change gives 4.8 - 1.8 = 3.0, but that figure also counts fossil fuel divestment.
