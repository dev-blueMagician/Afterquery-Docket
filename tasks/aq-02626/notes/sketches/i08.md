## Question

How many coal-ash landfills are in States whose CCR-program permits carry no set expiry?

## Answer

16

## Evidence

- D03 (Permit Requirements and Permitting Process, L190): "The permit is issued for the life of the facility (through termination of postclosure care)"
- D03 (Virginia CCR Units and Resources, L226): "(7 landfills and 18 surface impoundments)"
- D02 (Permit Requirements and Permitting Process, L204): "will be for the operating life of the facility through post-closure"
- D02 (Wyoming CCR Units and Resources, L267): "(3 landfills and 16 surface impoundments)"
- D06 (Guidelines for Permitting, L192): "There is no expiration for an operating license or plan of operation approval"
- D06 (Wisconsin CCR Units and Resources, L290): "(6 landfills and 3 surface impoundments)"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h24. Five parallel permit-duration statements with look-alike wording. Virginia, Wyoming and Wisconsin issue permits with no fixed term (7 + 3 + 6 = 16 landfills). Alabama's permit is "valid for the design life of the CCR facility, but for a period of no longer than ten years" (D05 L208) and Louisiana's is "issued for a maximum of 10 years" although the facility must hold a permit "for the active life of the facility" (D04 L194, L202). Wisconsin prints a ten-year figure too, for plan updates, not for the licence (D06 L192).
Ambiguity: 10 (Wisconsin left out for its ten-year plan update) fails "no set expiry" against "There is no expiration", D06 L192. 19 (Alabama or Louisiana added for "design life"/"active life") fails it against the ten-year maximum, D05 L208 and D04 L202. 22 is every State. Wyoming's existing landfills hold older solid waste permits that do expire (D02 L202); "CCR-program permits" names the chapter 18 permits of L204.
Necessity: D03, D02 and D06 are each the only source of their State's duration rule and landfill count; dropping one changes the sum. D04 and D05 carry only excluded States (unticked).
After clarification: the wording names no State and no term length.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Mentions it vs does it; Scatter the pieces. Decoys recomputed: 10, 13, 19, 22.
Source risks: each duration statement is one plain sentence; Wyoming has issued no chapter 18 permits yet (L302), so its rule is prospective.
Author calculation: 7 + 3 + 6 = 16. Python 2026-10-10.
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: sketch; quick test first. Shares its sentences with i09, so at most one of the two is written up.
