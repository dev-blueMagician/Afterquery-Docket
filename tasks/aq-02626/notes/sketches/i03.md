## Question

Per facility holding a State-issued coal-ash permit, how much more STAG funding had Alabama been awarded than Virginia had received, to the nearest dollar?

## Answer

$62,260

## Evidence

- D05 (Alabama CCR Units and Resources, L276): "ADEM has been awarded a total of $1,069,479 from EPA's CCR State and Tribal Assistance Grants (STAG) funding."
- D05 (Alabama's Permits Issued Under the State CCR Regulations, L398): "and (8) TVA Colbert Plant (Permit #17-11, issued October 25, 2022)."
- D03 (Virginia CCR Units and Resources, L226): "VADEQ has identified 25 disposal units that are currently or have been used for disposal of CCR wastes (7 landfills and 18 surface impoundments) at 8 facilities in Virginia."
- D03 (Virginia CCR Units and Resources, L226): "In total, VADEQ has received $571,396.00 in funding to develop its CCR permit program."
- D03 (Virginia's Permits, L329): "Subsequently, VADEQ issued permits to the owners and/or operators of all CCR units in the Commonwealth."
