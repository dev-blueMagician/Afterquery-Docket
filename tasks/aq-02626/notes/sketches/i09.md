## Question

Over its first 25 years, what is the fewest permit renewal applications a new coal-ash landfill would need in Alabama, Louisiana and Wisconsin combined?

## Answer

4

## Evidence

- D05 (Final Permit Determination, L208): "a CCR facility permit is valid for the design life of the CCR facility, but for a period of no longer than ten years."
- D05 (Permit Revocation, Denial, Suspension, Renewal or Transfer, L210): "requests for permit renewals are required to be submitted to ADEM at least 180 days prior to the expiration date of the existing permit"
- D04 (Duration of Permits, L202): "permits for CCR units are to be issued for a maximum of 10 years"
- D06 (Guidelines for Permitting, L192): "There is no expiration for an operating license or plan of operation approval"
- D06 (Guidelines for Permitting, L192): "the plan of operation be updated every 10 years during the landfill's active life"
