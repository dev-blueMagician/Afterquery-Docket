## Question

For a new coal-ash permit, how many more public-comment days in all does Wyoming guarantee with notices a week apart than Virginia with its hearing 40 days after notice?

## Answer

19 days

## Evidence

- D02 (Public Comment Period, L220): "All applicants for solid waste permits that submit a new, renewal, closure or major amendment permit application are required to complete two public comment periods during the permitting process."
- D02 (Public Comment Period, L221): "Each public comment period begins on the first day of the notice publication and continues until 30 days after the second publication."
- D02 (Public Comment Period, L221): "The applicant is required to publish each written notice once a week for two consecutive weeks in a newspaper of general circulation"
- D03 (Public Notice and Participation, L195): "The regulation requires that the public comment period last at least 45 days, with the public hearing scheduled at least 30 days following the public notice and the public comment period extending at least 15 days after the public hearing."
