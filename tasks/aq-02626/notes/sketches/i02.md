## Question

As of September 2026, how many days after Wyoming's Solid Waste Rules chapter 18 took effect did EPA last amend the federal coal-ash regulations?

## Answer

1,271 days

## Evidence

- D02 (Wyoming CCR Regulations, L278): "chapter 18 became effective on August 19, 2022"
- D06 (Background, L126): "November 8, 2024 (89 FR 88650), and February 10, 2026 (91 FR 5806)"
- D02 (Background, L127): "May 8, 2024 (89 FR 38950), and November 8, 2024 (89 FR 88650)."
