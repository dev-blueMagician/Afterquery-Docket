## Question

For a new coal-ash permit, how many more public-comment days in all does Wyoming guarantee, with notices a week apart, than Virginia?

## Answer

29 days

## Evidence

- D02 (Public Comment Period, L220): "All applicants for solid waste permits that submit a new, renewal, closure or major amendment permit application are required to complete two public comment periods during the permitting process."
- D02 (Public Comment Period, L221): "Each public comment period begins on the first day of the notice publication and continues until 30 days after the second publication."
- D03 (Public Notice and Participation, L195): "The regulation requires that the public comment period last at least 45 days"
