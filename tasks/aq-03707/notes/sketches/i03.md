## Question

For the Foodstuffs co-operative with the higher ROACE in the Commission's final report, what margin did it earn over the same years, of a kind that Foodstuffs' profitability consultant accepts as comparable between firms without adjusting for leasing or leverage?

## Answer

22.2% (its gross profit margin)

## Evidence

- D08 (l172; Doc 1): "12.8% for Foodstuffs SI and 13.1% for Foodstuffs NI"
- D03 (para 11, l187; Doc 11): "only the gross profit margin and EBITDAR margin can be benchmarked across firms without having to make an adjustment for the degree of renting or leverage"
- D12 (para 4.19, l702; Doc 8): "Cost of Goods Sold: 77.8% for FSNI and 77.6% for FSSI (measured on a WOB basis for the period 2015 to 2019)"
- D12 (para 4.17, l690; Doc 8): "FSNI submitted its NPAT margin is 4%"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h13 (independent author run, 2026-10-13; verified by the caller). Doc 1 ranks the two co-operatives (13.1% against 12.8%, averaged across 2015 to 2019). Doc 11 says only the gross profit margin and the EBITDAR margin compare across firms without an adjustment. Doc 8 prints no such margin for Foodstuffs North Island: it prints an NPAT margin (4%) and, in an argument about exchange rates, a cost-of-goods share of revenue (77.8%, whole of business, 2015 to 2019). The gross profit margin is the complement, 22.2%, printed nowhere. Every printed margin a solver can return is the wrong kind or the wrong firm.
Ambiguity: 4% (Doc 8 l690) -> an NPAT margin, which Doc 11 l185 says cannot be benchmarked without adjustment. 24.4% (Doc 8 l719) -> Woolworths NZ's gross profit margin, not a Foodstuffs co-operative. 22.4% (100 - 77.6) -> Foodstuffs SI, the lower ROACE. 15.00% and 10.00% (Doc 11 l138-139) -> the consultant's illustrative firm, not the co-operative. EBITDAR margin, the other accepted kind -> no document has data for any retailer ("EBITDAR" occurs only in Doc 11), so one answer. Searched "margin", "gross", "Cost of Goods", "COGS", "EBITDAR", "22.2", "2015 to 2019" in all 13.
Necessity: Doc 8 -> 77.8%, the only operand (no other document prints a cost share or a gross margin for Foodstuffs). Doc 1 -> which co-operative (ROACE values occur only at Doc 1 l172); without it 22.2 against 22.4 is undecided. Doc 11 -> which kind of margin; without it the printed 4% is the natural answer. Risk: a finance-literate reader may take "without adjusting for leasing or leverage" to mean gross margin unaided, so the Document check may call Doc 11 unnecessary; the lean wording i03b drops Doc 11 and keeps Docs 1 and 8.
After clarification: the wording names no kind of margin, no co-operative and no figure; "over the same years" points to the ROACE's years without printing them (the string "2015 to 2019" occurs in Doc 8 only in the cost-share sentence).
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Scatter the pieces; Calculate across documents. Not a which-items question. Look-alikes recomputed: 4, 24.4, 22.4, 15.00, 10.00; none equals 22.2.
Source risks: one document carries the only operand, the other two select; the last step is a complement; Doc 8 l602 queries how Foodstuffs built its whole-of-business figures ("Does COGS include private label?"), but 77.8% is the figure the council cites; 13.1 and 12.8 sit past character 22,000 of Doc 1 and 77.8 near character 95,000 of Doc 8 (form number check false alarms if written up).
Author calculation: Doc 8 l702: cost of goods sold 77.8 per cent of revenue (FSNI, whole of business, 2015 to 2019). Gross profit is sales less cost of goods sold (Doc 11 Table 1: 1,000 - 850 = 150, 15.00%; Doc 8 pairs 76% with a 24.4% gross profit margin for Woolworths NZ). 100 - 77.8 = 22.2 per cent. Python 2026-10-13: round(100 - 77.8, 1) = 22.2; round(100 - 77.6, 1) = 22.4.
