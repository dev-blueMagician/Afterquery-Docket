## Question

List, in alphabetical order, every country that any submission to the grocery market study says has four main supermarket players.

## Answer

Australia, Finland, Iceland, the Netherlands, Norway, Sweden, Switzerland

## Evidence

- D12 (para 3.12, l381; Doc 8): "Norway, Finland and Sweden which, like New Zealand, have a long thin land mass, all have four major supermarket chains. Iceland, which has a much smaller population, also has four retailers."
- D02 (para 86, l287; Doc 3): "for example Switzerland, Norway, Finland, Denver MSA, Orlando MSA, the Netherlands, Quebec, Ontario and elsewhere"
- D09 (evidence table, l89; Doc 5): "Even Aussie is a 4-player market."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2. Three submissions name four-player grocery markets: the suppliers' council (quoting its economists), Coriolis, and Monopoly Watch in one clause of a table row about property covenants. The union of countries is seven; Norway and Finland are in two of the lists.
Ambiguity: Ireland ("seven competing supermarket chains", "Ireland has 10") and Denmark (7), Doc 8 l365-381 -> not four. Quebec, Ontario, Denver MSA, Orlando MSA (Doc 3 l287) -> not countries. New Zealand (Doc 3 l285, four groups "by January 1, 2024") -> a proposal, not what it has. Australia is called a duopoly in Doc 10 l105, Doc 3 l248 and Doc 8 l207 -> the question says "any submission", and Doc 5 l89 says four.
Necessity: Doc 8 -> Sweden and Iceland. Doc 3 -> Switzerland and the Netherlands. Doc 5 -> Australia. Searched "four", "4", "player", "chains", "groups", "duopoly" in all 13: no other country is given four.
After clarification: all three still needed; the wording names no country and no document.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Scatter the pieces; Bury one list item late; Who belongs where. Decoys: Ireland, Denmark, the four sub-national markets, New Zealand's proposed four.
Source risks: a list, not the user's preferred number; the form's List contract advisory will show because the question cannot say "seven" without pointing at the member outside the two obvious lists; Australia's conflicting descriptions could draw a "One defensible answer" query.
Author calculation: not applicable (membership); universe above.
