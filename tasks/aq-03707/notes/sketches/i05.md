## Question

Which countries that a submission to the grocery study credits with four main supermarket players also appear among the Food and Grocery Council's examples of countries with grocery competition concerns? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

Australia and the Netherlands

## Evidence

- D09 (evidence table, l89; Doc 5): "Even Aussie is a 4-player market."
- D02 (para 86, l287; Doc 3): "for example Switzerland, Norway, Finland, Denver MSA, Orlando MSA, the Netherlands, Quebec, Ontario and elsewhere"
- D12 (para 3.12, l381; Doc 8): "Norway, Finland and Sweden which, like New Zealand, have a long thin land mass, all have four major supermarket chains. Iceland, which has a much smaller population, also has four retailers."
- D12 (para 4.11a, l632; Doc 8): "ACCC Chair Rod Sims identified supermarkets as a market dominated by a small number of providers"
- D12 (para 4.11c, l638; Doc 8): "c. Netherlands"
