The co-operative with the higher ROACE in the Commission’s final report is Foodstuffs North Island (FSNI), with an average ROACE of 13.1% over 2015–2019 (versus 12.8% for Foodstuffs South Island and 12.7% for Woolworths NZ).

However, the specific profit-to-sales figure reported for FSNI in the profitability analysis (and discussed by Incenta in comparing New Zealand retailers with international peers) is a net profit after tax (NPAT) margin of about 4% (submitted for FSNI, compared with about 2.4% for Woolworths NZ). Incenta notes that, while this 4% figure is the reported NPAT margin, EBITDAR or gross profit margins are the technically appropriate comparable metrics because they do not require adjustments for capital structure or leasing arrangements.
