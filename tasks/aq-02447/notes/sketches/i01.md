## Question

How many distinct ECCNs does BIS's July 2026 UAE rule name in § 742.6(a)(6)(iii)(B) that its January 2023 Macau rule's § 742.6(a)(6) did not?

## Answer

10 ECCNs

## Evidence

- D06 (L256, amendment 7): "7. Sec. 742.6 is amended by revising paragraph (a)(6)(iii)(B) to read as follows:"
- D06 (L264, revised (iii)(B)): "A license is required to export, reexport, or transfer (in-country) items specified in ECCNs 3A001.z.1.b, z.2.b, z.3.b, z.4.b; 3A090.b;"
- D01 (L224, amendment 9): "9. Section 742.6 is amended by revising paragraphs (a)(6) and (b)(10) to read as follows:"
- D01 (L229, revised (a)(6)(i)): "A license is required for items specified in ECCNs 3A090, 3B090, 4A090, 5A992 (that meet or exceed the performance parameters of ECCNs 3A090 or 4A090)"
