## Question

Counting the industry-levy funding and the further capital its owners are adding, how much capital stands behind each cell site the Rural Connectivity Group plans to build, to the nearest thousand dollars?

## Answer

$433,000

## Evidence

- D10 (para 72, L285): "funding of $150 million from the industry, via the Telecommunications development Levy (TDL)"
- D08 (set-asides section, L1131): "investment the members of the RCG are contributing (a further $75m capex and substantial ongoing capex)"
- D05 (para 232, L1169): "The RCG plans to deploy 520 cell sites"
