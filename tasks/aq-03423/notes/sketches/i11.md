## Question

Which organisations that made submissions to the Commerce Commission's mobile market study, if any, had become an MVNO or had tried to, including any whose attempt only another submitter reports? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

Trustpower, Vocus, WISPA NZ

## Evidence

- D08 (answer on Q12, L468): "We understand Trustpower has also received a competing MVNO offer from Spark."
- D08 (answer on Q20, L597): "Vocus claims its mobile offer is constrained by MNOs"
- D11 (answer on Q14, L39): "WISPA has requested negotiations to establish a MVNO from the two largest MNOs."
