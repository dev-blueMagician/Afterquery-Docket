## Question

The mobile connections New Zealand added during 2017 were how many times the country's whole MVNO subscriber base a year earlier, to one decimal place?

## Answer

13.5 times

## Evidence

- D05 (para 104, L566): "reaching 6.4 million connections in 2017, an increase of 5.1% on the previous year"
- D06 (section 2.1.3, L83): "The total number of MVNO subscribers increased in 2016 but remained low at around 23,000"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h11. The connections added in 2017 are printed nowhere: Doc 1 gives the 2017 level and its growth "on the previous year", so the increase is 6.4 million less 6.4 million / 1.051, not 5.1% of 6.4 million. Nothing in the question points at that step. Doc 5 alone gives the MVNO base, for 2016.
Ambiguity: 14.2 (5.1% applied to the 2017 level, 326,400) -> fails "added during 2017": the 5.1% is "on the previous year" (Doc 1 L566). 14.8 (Vocus's own 21,000 services, Doc 4 L64) -> fails "whole MVNO subscriber base a year earlier": it is one MVNO in November 2017. No other connection count or MVNO total is printed (searched "connections", "million", "23,000", "21,000", "subscribers" in all texts; Doc 7's 1.3 million is 2degrees' own base).
Necessity: Doc 1 -> 6.4 million and 5.1% -> only at L566. Doc 5 -> 23,000 in 2016 -> only at L83. Without Doc 5 the only MVNO count is Vocus's 21,000, which gives 14.8.
After clarification: both still needed; the wording supplies no figure.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Calculate across documents; Temporal alignment. Wrong routes recomputed: 14.2, 14.8. Neither equals 13.5.
Source risks: the documents compare MVNOs with the market as a share, not with a year's growth, so the comparison is the question's own framing (i07 asks the share instead).
