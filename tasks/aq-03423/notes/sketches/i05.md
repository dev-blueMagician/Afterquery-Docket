## Question

If the 2100MHz band were shared equally among the three mobile network operators, as 2degrees asks, which operator would hold the most spectrum across the sub-1GHz, 1800MHz and 2100MHz bands, and how many megahertz, to one decimal place?

## Answer

Spark, 156.7 MHz

## Evidence

- D05 (para 139, L750): "in the sub 1GHz bands, Spark holds a total of 70MHz spectrum (700MHz/850MHz), Vodafone holds a total of 60MHz spectrum (700MHz, 900MHz), and 2degrees holds a total of 40MHz spectrum (700MHz/900MHz)"
- D05 (para 139, L750): "in the 2100MHz band, Spark and 2degrees each own 30MHz, while Vodafone owns 50MHz"
- D08 (answer on Q21, L624): "redistributing the 2100MHz spectrum equally between national operators (currently Vodafone has 2x25MHz, while Spark and 2degrees have 2x15MHz each)"
