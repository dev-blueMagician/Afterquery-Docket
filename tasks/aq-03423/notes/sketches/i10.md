## Question

In the Commerce Commission's 2015 survey of businesses, where did mobile providers rank among the five sectors compared, first for being seen as the most competitive and then for the share of businesses that had switched supplier in the previous two years?

## Answer

Second for being seen as most competitive, fourth for switching

## Evidence

- D08 (footnote 6, L256): "44% of respondents considered mobile providers as the most competitive service providers, behind only power companies (47%)"
- D05 (para 195, L995): "26% of respondents had switched power companies in the previous two years"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h13. One survey, reported in two documents: how competitive each sector is thought to be (Doc 7 only) and how many businesses had actually switched (Doc 1 only). Mobile is second on the first and fourth on the second.
Ambiguity: the five sectors are the same in both (power, mobile, insurers, fixed-line or fixed broadband providers, banks). Switching order from Doc 1: power 26%, fixed broadband 16%, insurance 15%, mobile 14%, banks 5%. Doc 1's "11% ... likely to switch in the next 12 months" -> fails "had switched ... in the previous two years".
Necessity: Doc 7 -> the perception ranking -> only in footnote 6. Doc 1 -> the four other sectors' switching rates -> only at L995.

Source risks: two plain lookups and a sort.
