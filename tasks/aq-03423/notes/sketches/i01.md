## Question

On the Commerce Commission's December 2017 monitoring figures, what share of the fixed broadband market, to the nearest percent, did New Zealand's two largest mobile network operators hold together?

## Answer

71%

## Evidence

- D07 (para 81, L307): "the three MNOs collectively have 75% of the fixed broadband market"
- D07 (fn 14, L316): "Annual Telecommunications Monitoring Report: Key Facts, 20 December 2017"
- D08 (fn 7, L320): "estimated broadband retailer market share by connections was 13% versus 4% for 2degrees: Commerce Commission, 2017 Annual Telecommunications Monitoring Report, 20 December 2017"
