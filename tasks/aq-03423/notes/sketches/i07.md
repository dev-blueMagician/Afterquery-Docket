## Question

In 2016, what share of New Zealand's mobile connections were MVNO subscribers, to two decimal places?

## Answer

0.38%

## Evidence

- D05 (para 104, L566): "reaching 6.4 million connections in 2017, an increase of 5.1% on the previous year"
- D06 (section 2.1.3, L83): "The total number of MVNO subscribers increased in 2016 but remained low at around 23,000"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h11, asked as the share the documents themselves talk about ("MVNO subscriber share", "less than one percent"). The MVNO count is for 2016 and the only connection count is for 2017, so the 2016 base has to be backed out of the 5.1% growth first.
Ambiguity: 0.36% (23,000 over the 2017 level of 6.4 million) -> fails "In 2016". "Less than one percent" (Doc 1 L383, Doc 2, Doc 4) and "1%" (Doc 7 L561) -> not to two decimal places and not dated 2016.
Necessity: Doc 1 -> 6.4 million and 5.1% -> only at L566. Doc 5 -> 23,000 in 2016 -> only at L83.
After clarification: both still needed; the wording supplies no figure.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Temporal alignment; Calculate across documents. Wrong route recomputed: 0.36%. Backing out with 6.4 x (1 - 0.051) still gives 0.38%.
Source risks: both inputs carry two significant figures, as does the answer. "In 2016" names the year the denominator has to be moved to, which may make the step plain.
Author calculation: 6,400,000 / 1.051 = 6,089,439; 23,000 / 6,089,439 = 0.3777%, 0.38% to two decimal places.
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: round 2 sketch, quick test pending. Same hook as i06: at most one of the two can become a question.
