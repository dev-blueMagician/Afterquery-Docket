## Question

When Abbott's board declared the quarterly dividend payable in November 2025, what full-year 2025 adjusted diluted EPS range was the company's guidance projecting at that time?

## Answer

$5.10 to $5.20

## Evidence

- D04 (l.293, dividend): "On Sept. 19, 2025, the board of directors of Abbott declared"
- D04 (l.293, dividend): "payable Nov. 17, 2025"
- D04 (l.21, dateline): "ABBOTT PARK, Ill., Oct. 15, 2025"
- D04 (l.287, guidance): "narrows the range to $5.12 to $5.18"
- D03 (l.21, dateline): "ABBOTT PARK, Ill., July 17, 2025"
- D03 (l.299, guidance): "adjusted diluted earnings per share of $5.10 to $5.20"
