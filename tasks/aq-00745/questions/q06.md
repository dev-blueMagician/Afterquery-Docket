## Question

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 8: "On March 23, 2026, Abbott completed its acquisition of Exact Sciences". Doc 8 itself is dated April 16, 2026, so the full-year range it gives ($5.38 to $5.58) was issued after the closing and was not yet in force on the day the acquisition closed.

Step 2. Doc 7: the range issued on Jan. 22, 2026, the latest full-year 2026 range issued before the closing, is $5.55 to $5.80, so the low end in force on the day of the closing was $5.55.

Step 3. Doc 10 and Doc 8: Doc 10, dated July 16, 2026, raised the range to $5.45 to $5.60 "compared to previous range of $5.38 to $5.58", which is Doc 8's April 16, 2026 range. So on June 30, 2026, the range in force was still $5.38 to $5.58, with a low end of $5.38.

Step 4. Doc 7 and Doc 8: change in the low end = 5.38 - 5.55 = -0.17.

Conclusion: the low end of the range moved by -$0.17.

Decoys (Doc 7, Doc 8 and Doc 10): taking Doc 8's own April range as the one in force on the closing day gives 5.38 - 5.38 = 0.00; taking Doc 10's July range as the one in force on June 30 gives 5.45 - 5.55 = -0.10; making both errors gives 5.45 - 5.38 = +0.07.
