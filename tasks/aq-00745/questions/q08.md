## Question

By how much did the low end of Abbott's full-year 2026 adjusted diluted EPS guidance change from the range in force when it completed its acquisition of Exact Sciences to the range in force on the payment date of the dividend its board declared in June 2026? Give the sign, in dollars to the nearest cent.

## Answer

-$0.10

## Components

- c1: -$0.10

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 8: "On March 23, 2026, Abbott completed its acquisition of Exact Sciences". Doc 8 itself is dated April 16, 2026, so the full-year range it gives ($5.38 to $5.58) was issued after the closing and was not yet in force on the day the acquisition closed.

Step 2. Doc 7: the range issued on Jan. 22, 2026, the latest full-year 2026 range issued before the closing, is $5.55 to $5.80, so the low end in force on the day of the closing was $5.55.

Step 3. Doc 10: "On June 12, 2026, the board of directors of Abbott declared the company's quarterly dividend" and the dividend "is payable Aug. 17, 2026", so its payment date is Aug. 17, 2026. Doc 10, dated July 16, 2026, issued a full-year range of $5.45 to $5.60, so on Aug. 17, 2026 the range in force was $5.45 to $5.60, with a low end of $5.45.

Step 4. Doc 7 and Doc 10: change in the low end = 5.45 - 5.55 = -0.10.

Conclusion: the low end of the range moved by -$0.10.

Decoys (Doc 7, Doc 8 and Doc 10): taking Doc 8's own April range as the one in force on the closing day gives 5.45 - 5.38 = +0.07; using the June 12 declaration date or the July 15 record date instead of the payment date puts the second range at Doc 8's April range and gives 5.38 - 5.55 = -0.17; making both errors gives 5.38 - 5.38 = 0.00.
