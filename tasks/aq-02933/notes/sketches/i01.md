## Question

On CMS's counts, Texas's average yearly gain in enrolled hospices was what percentage of its average yearly loss of enrolled home health agencies? One decimal place.

## Answer

144.8%

## Evidence

- D06 (Doc 5, Table 1, Texas row, offset 16,123): "Texas........................................................... 718 1,081 51"
- D06 (Doc 5, Table 1 title, offset 15,561): "Table 1--Number of Enrolled Hospices in CY 2019 and CY 2023"
- D05 (Doc 6, footnote 23, offset 36,907): "Between 2019 and 2025, the number of enrolled HHAs in Michigan dropped from 485 to 361 (over 25 percent) and from 2,269 to 1,893 in Texas (roughly 17 percent)."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h3. Texas is the only State with counts in both May notices. Hospices rose by 363 over four years and HHAs fell by 376 over six, so the raw changes look alike while the yearly paces do not.
Ambiguity: (1) 96.5, the raw changes divided (363 / 376): fails "average yearly", since the spans are four and six years. (2) Table 2's Texas figures (164, 67) count newly enrolling hospices, not enrolled hospices. (3) A ratio of printed percentages (51 against roughly 17) is not a gain or loss in number. Only two enrolled-hospice counts and two enrolled-HHA counts exist for Texas, so the periods are fixed by the documents.
Necessity: Doc 5 -> Texas's hospice counts and their years; Doc 6 -> Texas's HHA counts and their years; neither document prints the other's -> each ratio term is unreachable without its document.
After clarification: no count or span is in the wording.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: temporal alignment. Wrong route recomputed: 363 / 376 = 96.5.
Source risks: once both passages are found this is arithmetic; it shares Texas's hospice counts with i03, so the two are not independent questions. Footnote 23 sits at character 36,942 (form number check may flag 2,269 and 1,893).
Author calculation: 1,081 - 718 = 363 over 2023 - 2019 = 4 years, 90.75 a year. 2,269 - 1,893 = 376 over 2025 - 2019 = 6 years. At 90.75 a year hospices gain 90.75 x 6 = 544.5 in six years; 544.5 / 376 = 1.4481 -> 144.8%. Script check 2026-10-11: 144.814.
