## Question

At the yearly enrollment paces CMS cites, how many would-be enrollees would six months keep out: medical supply companies plus hospices outside its five outlier States?

## Answer

383

## Evidence

- D03 (Doc 3, section II.F.1, offset 42,309): "approximately 600 medical supply companies enroll in Medicare each year (or 300 over a 6-month period)"
- D06 (Doc 5, section V.C.1, offset 57,790): "Excluding the aforementioned outliers in Arizona, California, Georgia, Nevada, and Texas, between CY 2023 and CY 2025 the combined nationwide number of new enrollments was roughly 500 (166 per year)"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2. Each moratorium notice sizes the would-be entrants its six months keep out. Doc 3 prints the six-month figure (300) and Doc 6 does too (150 to 200), but Doc 5 prints only a three-year total and a yearly figure, so its six-month figure (83) has to be worked out.
Ambiguity: (1) 466, the two printed parentheticals added: 166 is "per year", and the question says six months. (2) 800, with the "roughly 500": that is the CY 2023 to CY 2025 total. (3) 766, both yearly figures: fails "six months". (4) Hospices nationwide: no document gives a pace for the five excluded States together (Table 2 has three of them, Nevada is a range, Georgia is absent), so the question limits the hospice part to States outside the five. (5) Doc 5's next sentence, "a similar number would seek to enroll during the moratorium", names no figure; the question asks at the yearly pace, so half of 166. (6) 500 / 3 / 2 = 83.3, which also gives 383 to the whole number. Searched: "per year", "each year", "6-month", "enroll" in Docs 3, 5, 6.
Necessity: Doc 3 -> the medical supply pace (600 a year, 300 per six months); Docs 5 and 6 mention the DMEPOS moratorium but print no pace for it -> without Doc 3 that term is unknown. Doc 5 -> the hospice pace (166 a year outside five States); no other document prints it -> without Doc 5 that term is unknown.
After clarification: "five outlier States" tells the solver which hospice figure is meant but supplies no number; both documents still needed.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: unit normalization with a calculated figure beside printed ones. Wrong routes recomputed: 300 + 166 = 466; 300 + 500 = 800; 600 + 166 = 766.
Source risks: both inputs are stated as approximations ("approximately", "roughly"), so the gold is a whole number of companies and hospices, not a finer figure. Every input sits past character 42,000 of its document; the form's reasoning number check has only been seen to pass numbers up to character 19,581, so it may flag 600, 300 and 166.
Author calculation: Doc 3 300 per six months (also 600 / 2). Doc 5 166 per year; 166 / 2 = 83. 300 + 83 = 383. Script check 2026-10-11: 383.0.
