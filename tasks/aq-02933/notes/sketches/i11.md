## Question

From 2019 to 2025, across States with CMS-reported enrolled totals for both years, by how many did the home health agencies lost exceed the hospices gained?

## Answer

315 providers

## Evidence

- D05 (Doc 6, footnote 23, offset 36,866): "Between 2019 and 2025, the number of enrolled HHAs in Michigan dropped from 485 to 361 (over 25 percent) and from 2,269 to 1,893 in Texas (roughly 17 percent)."
- D06 (Doc 5, section II.C, offset 45,838): "In Georgia, there have been sharp increases in the number of hospices there--from 221 in CY 2019 to 273 in CY 2025, or a 24 percent rise"
- D06 (Doc 5, section II.C, offset 45,069): "The total number of enrolled Nevada hospices continues to rise at a rate well beyond that of the beneficiary population--from 138 in CY 2023 to 188 in CY 2025, or 36 percent."
- D06 (Doc 5, Table 1, Nevada row, offset 16,047): "Nevada.......................................................... 55 138 151"
