## Question

How did FERC's assumed hourly cost of respondents' time change from its 2024 reactive-power proposal to its 2026 IBR modeling order? Give the signed dollar change.

## Answer

-$36.48 per hour

## Evidence

- D01 (fn124, information collection statement): "Based on the Commission's Fiscal Year 2024 average cost of $207,786/year (for wages plus benefits, for one full-time employee), $100/hour is used."
- D06 (fn64, burden tables): "The estimated hourly cost (salary plus benefits) is a combination of the following categories from the BLS website"
- D06 (fn64, burden tables): "for a total ($53.39 + $10.13 = $63.52/hour)"
