## Question

How many megawatts of coal-fired capacity at plants covered by EPA's ash-pond deadline proposal lie within the geographic markets DOJ alleged Constellation's Calpine deal would harm?

## Answer

650 MW

## Evidence

- D04 (L184, ERCOT heading): "3. Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) Coleto Creek Power Plant (Coleto Creek) located in Texas."
- D04 (L185): "associated with the 650 MW coal-fired steam generating unit"
- D05 (L178, complaint para. 55): "likely would lessen competition substantially for wholesale electricity in ERCOT and PJM Coastal Mid-Atlantic"
- D05 (L129, para. 33): "The PJM Coastal Mid-Atlantic geographic area is a distinct area within the PJM region that includes southeastern Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, and the eastern shores of Maryland and Virginia."
