What the bundle is, for hook purposes:

- D05, D06, D07 are three editions of one CDC order (June 21, July 13, August 12, 2026). Each prints a new DRC
  case count with an as-of date and restates one earlier count; each has its own comment deadline and term.
  All of it is 2026 material, so nothing here is recallable.
- D06 and D07 each report dated events that fell while the previous order was still in force (D06: a U.S.
  aid worker's positive test announced July 10; D07: Uganda's end-of-outbreak declaration July 28, a discharge
  July 16, a travel-notice escalation August 4).
- D04 is the interim final rule the orders rest on for lawful permanent residents; it has an expiry clause.
- D01-D03 are one rulemaking chain (2024 proposal, 2024 EOIR rule, 2025 partial withdrawal of the 2020
  Security Bars rule). They are qualitative except D01's fiscal-year statistics, which no other document
  prints. D04-D07 never cite D01-D03.
