## Question

Which sections of 8 CFR parts 1003, 1208 and 1235 are amended by either EOIR's December 2024 interim rule on bars in fear reviews or the provisions of the 2020 Security Bars rule that survived the December 2025 partial withdrawal?

## Answer

8 CFR 1003.42, 1208.13, 1208.16, 1208.31, 1208.33 and 1208.35

- D02 (regulatory text, L378): "4. Amend Sec. 1208.31 by:"
- D02 (regulatory text, L406): "5. Amend Sec. 1208.33 by:"
- D02 (regulatory text, L442): "6. Amend Sec. 1208.35 by removing the reference to ``Sec. 214.11 of this title'' in paragraph (a)(2)(i)(C) and adding in its place ``Sec."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h13. A list built from two rulemakings' amendatory instructions, with members that look excluded and are not. Measured shape: the late member beside a look-alike statement (aq-02501 q01, Low 0/5, Medium 2/5 then 1/5) and the complement-side membership count (aq-02749 q02, Low 0/5, Medium 3/5). EOIR's rule amends four sections; its preamble walks through three of them and its footnote 8 says it is "unnecessary to also amend" Sec. 1208.35, yet instruction 6, the last 300 characters of an 84,000-character document, amends Sec. 1208.35 for a cross-reference. The 2025 rule withdraws the 2020 rule's amendments to Sec. 1003.42, 1208.16(f), 1208.30 and 1235.6 and keeps 1208.13(c)(10) and 1208.16(d)(2).
Membership, eight candidates in the three parts: 1003.42 in (EOIR amends it; the 2020 rule's own amendment is withdrawn); 1208.13 in (survives); 1208.16 in (paragraph (d)(2) survives, paragraph (f) withdrawn); 1208.30 out (2020 amendment withdrawn; EOIR's rule leaves it as it is); 1208.31 in; 1208.33 in; 1208.35 in (instruction 6); 1235.6 out (withdrawn).
Ambiguity: "amended by" is settled by numbered amendatory instructions and by the 2025 rule's own list of what survives. Footnote 8 is about the bars-review clarification; the regulatory text and the preamble's "two non-substantive corrections ... in 8 CFR 1208.33(a)(3)(i)(C) and 1208.35(a)(2)(i)(C)" (L183-187) both show Sec. 1208.35 is amended. The authority citations "continue to read" and are not sections.
Necessity: D02 -> 1003.42, 1208.31, 1208.33, 1208.35 (D03 never names 1208.31, 1208.33 or 1208.35). D03 -> 1208.13 and 1208.16, and that 1208.30 and 1235.6 drop out (D01 and D02 name 1208.13 and 1208.16 only as existing rules). D01 is not used: it is DHS's proposal for part 208.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Bury one list item late; match, then exclude. Wrong routes: without 1208.35 (footnote 8 taken at its word); with 1208.30 (named 21 times in D03, 5 in D02); without 1208.16 (its paragraph (f) withdrawal read as the whole section); without 1003.42 (D03's withdrawal read as the last word); part 208 sections added.
Source risks: the last instruction carries its own header "Sec. 1208.35 [Amended]", so a solver that scans the regulatory text finds it; a list question, not the numeric shape the user prefers; a reviewer may see a list of section numbers as dry.
Author calculation: not applicable (membership); eight candidates checked one by one, six in.
