## Question

How many days after Uganda declared its Ebola outbreak over was the comment deadline for the CDC entry-suspension order then in force?

## Answer

3 days

## Evidence

- D07 (III.B, L125): "On July 28, 2026, the Republic of Uganda Ministry of Health declared the outbreak over."
- D07 (SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION, L41): "The ASH again continued the Order without change on July 13, 2026, for 30 days with a 15-day comment period, and no comments were received."
- D06 (DATES, L27): "This action took effect July 13, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. EDT. Written comments must be received on or before July 31, 2026."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2. Edition trap plus a label that is not the rule. The event (July 28) is reported only in the August order (D07). The order in force that day is the July 13 order (D06), whose comment deadline, July 31, is printed only in D06. D07 describes that order as having "a 15-day comment period", and July 13 + 15 days is July 28 itself; the 15 days in fact ran from publication (July 16), as each order's DATES line shows.
Ambiguity: 0 days (July 13 + 15) rests on a start date no document states and fails against D06's printed deadline; 35 days (September 1, D07 L28) and 15 days (the order's end, August 12) fail "comment period ... on the order then in force": D07 took effect August 12 (L27). Elapsed days July 28 -> July 31 = 3; "on or before July 31" makes July 31 the closing day.
Necessity: D07 -> July 28 declaration ("declared the outbreak over": D07 only) -> not in D05/D06 -> without D07 no date to count from. D06 -> July 31 deadline ("July 31": D06 only) -> D07 gives only the 15-day label -> without D06 the label yields July 28 (0 days) or nothing.
After clarification: the wording gives no date; both documents still needed.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Same series, right edition (wrong edition 35); mentions it vs does it (label route 0).
Source risks: a reader may hold that the label supports 0 days; D06's DATES line is explicit, and D05 (June 25 publication -> July 10) and D07 (August 17 -> September 1) show the same pattern.
Author calculation: July 31 - July 28 = 3 days (Python date difference, 2026-10-10). Label route: July 13 + 15 = July 28 -> 0. Wrong edition: September 1 - July 28 = 35.
