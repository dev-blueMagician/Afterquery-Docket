## Question

On June 25, 2026, CDC received 30 letters and 18 portal comments on its interim quarantine rule and 21 letters on its June 21 Ebola entry order; on July 10 it received 22 more portal comments on each. How many met CDC's requirements on method and timing?

## Answer

61 comments

## Evidence

- D04 (DATES, L30): "Comment date: Comments must be received by June 26, 2026."
- D04 (ADDRESSES, L33): "ADDRESSES: You may submit comments, identified by CDC-2026-0991, by the following method:"
- D04 (ADDRESSES, L39): "Because of staff and resource limitations, all comments must be submitted electronically to www.regulations.gov."
- D05 (DATES, L27): "Written comments must be received on or before July 10, 2026."
- D05 (ADDRESSES, L29): "by either of the methods listed below. Do not submit comments by email. CDC does not accept comments by email."
- D05 (ADDRESSES, L32): "Mail: Division of Global Migration Health, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, 1600 Clifton Road NE, MS H16-4, Atlanta, GA 30329."
