## Question

When CDC announced a U.S. aid worker's Ebola infection, its entry-suspension order then in force cited two DRC confirmed-case counts. By what percentage did the later count exceed the earlier, to one decimal place?

## Answer

819.8%

## Evidence

- D06 (III.B, L156): "On July 10, 2026, CDC announced that an additional U.S. citizen working for a humanitarian organization in DRC had tested positive for Bundibugyo virus disease."
- D06 (DATES, L27): "This action took effect July 13, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. EDT."
- D05 (SUMMARY, L25): "This Order was issued on June 21, 2026, and shall remain in effect through 4:59 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) on Tuesday, July 21, 2026."
- D05 (III.B, L125): "On May 22, 2026, when the Amended Order was issued, DRC reported 91 confirmed cases across 11 health zones. As of June 15, 2026, DRC reports 837 confirmed cases across 31 health zones"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. Edition trap in the aq-00964 layout. The event (July 10) is reported only in the July order (D06), whose own counts are dated after it (837 on June 15 and 1,873 as of July 12, order effective July 13). The order in force on July 10 is the June 21 order (D05), whose two counts are 91 (May 22) and 837 (June 15). D06 restates 837 but never 91.
Ambiguity: 123.8% (D06's own pair) and 130.5% (D07's pair) fail "then in force": D06 took effect July 13 (L27), D07 August 12; "more than 800 percent" (D05 L125) fails "to one decimal place"; 919.8% (837 as a share of 91) fails "exceed". D05 prints exactly two DRC confirmed-case counts (L125); its Uganda figures (L126) are not DRC counts. Only one order is in force on July 10: the June order "continues" the earlier ones and runs to July 21 unless replaced, and the next took effect July 13.
Necessity: D06 -> the July 10 announcement ("tested positive": D06 only) -> D05 and D07 never mention it -> without D06 no order can be picked. D05 -> 91 confirmed cases on May 22 ("91 confirmed": D05 only) -> D06 restates only 837 -> without D05 the earlier count is unknown and the only computable pairs give 123.8% or 130.5%.
After clarification: the wording gives no date, count or order; both documents still needed.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Same series, right edition (wrong edition 123.8% / 130.5%); exact figure vs rounded prose (prose "more than 800 percent" is not 819.8%).
Source risks: the Amended Order's end date is printed nowhere; not needed, since the June order was issued June 21 and took effect that day.
Author calculation: (837 - 91) / 91 = 746 / 91 = 8.19780... -> 819.8% (Python, 2026-10-10). Wrong edition: 1,036 / 837 = 123.8%; 2,445 / 1,873 = 130.5%.
