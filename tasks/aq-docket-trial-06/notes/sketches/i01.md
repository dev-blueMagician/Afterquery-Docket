## Question

In 2003 GAO made unannounced visits to privately insured credit unions to check deposit-insurance notices. In the state whose bank regulator caps its assessments at $2.20 per $1,000 of assets, what percentage of the privately insured credit unions got at least one visit? Answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

77.3%

- D02 (Table 1 note b, 431): "visits at three branch locations. For another credit union, in addition to conducting a site visit at the main location, we conducted a site visit at a branch location."
