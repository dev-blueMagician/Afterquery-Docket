## Question

In 2003 GAO made unannounced visits to privately insured credit unions to check deposit-insurance notices. Across the states whose bank regulators GAO interviewed while reviewing OCC's 2004 preemption rules, what percentage of the privately insured credit unions got at least one visit? Answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

40.5%

## Evidence

- D05 (L183): "state banking departments, consumer groups, state bankers associations, and national and state banks in six states (California, Georgia, New York, North Carolina, Idaho, and Iowa)"
- D05 (L797): "Table 4 provides more detailed information on the funding arrangements for six state bank regulators that we interviewed."
- D02 (Table 3, L467): "Alabama 3 2 0 0 California 22 18 3 17 Idaho 20 7 5 71 Illinois 40 15 8 53"
- D02 (Table 1, L423): "Alabama 3 5 a 3 60 California 22 21 b 6 29 Illinois 40 10 c 4 40 Indiana"
- D02 (Table 1 note b, L431): "visits at three branch locations. For another credit union, in addition to conducting a site visit at the main location, we conducted a site visit at a branch location."
- D02 (L407): "We conducted unannounced site visits to 57 locations of privately insured credit unions (49 main and 8 branch locations) in five"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h2 with a set bridge. D05 names the six states where GAO interviewed state bank regulators (California, Georgia, New York, North Carolina, Idaho, Iowa). D02 Table 3: of these, only California (22) and Idaho (20) had privately insured credit unions (the eight are AL, CA, ID, IL, IN, MD, NV, OH). D02 site visits covered AL, CA, IL, IN, OH only, so Idaho got none. California's 21 sites cover 17 credit unions (note b). (17 + 0) / (22 + 20) = 17/42 = 40.48% -> 40.5%.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Linking across documents (set membership), Part vs total (sites vs credit unions). Decoys: 50.0% (21/42 sites), 77.3% (17/22, California only), 95.5% (21/22), 24.1% (51/212, D02's own 2003 interview states / all eight), 28.5% (51/179), 39.5% (17/43 with Idaho's uninsured credit union).
Source risks: D05 defines the population; without it the probe must guess a state set (D02's own nine-state interview list is the nearest look-alike and gives 24.1%). GAO-fieldwork subject on both sides (rule 10 borderline). Two steps a careless reader can drop (Idaho's zero visits; California's repeat visits).
Author calculation check and counting convention: 17/42 = 0.404762 -> 40.5%; privately insured counts as of December 2002 (Tables 1 and 3); a credit union counts once however many of its locations were visited.
