## Question

At the end of 2002, what percentage of all U.S. credit unions that were not federally insured were located in the state whose bank regulator caps its assessment at $2.20 per $1,000 of assets? Answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

10.3%

## Evidence

- D05 (Appendix VI, Table 4, California row, text line 805): "The California Department of Financial"
- D05 (Appendix VI, Table 4, California row, text lines 807-809): "minimum of $5,000 and a maximum of $2.20 per"
- D02 (Table 3, text line 467): "California 22 18 3 17"
- D02 (Appendix I, text line 1179): "As of December 2002, 214 state- chartered credit unions lacked federal deposit insurance, and all but two were privately insured"
- D02 (footnote 8, text line 239): "we identified two uninsured credit unions, one was located in Idaho and the other was located in New Hampshire"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: q02 corrected for the entity coincidence. The bridge (D05 Table 4, only California's
regulator has the $2.20-per-$1,000 cap) selects California; D02 gives California 22 privately insured
credit unions and no uninsured ones; the national denominator must include the two uninsured credit
unions (214, D02 1179), not just the 212 privately insured.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: scatter the pieces; bury one item late (the remainder in the
denominator). Wrong route 22 / 212 = 10.38% -> 10.4%. Coincidence table: no other state has 22 (shares
1.4, 9.8, 9.8, 18.7, 2.3, 3.7, 0.5, 43.5). "Not federally insured" avoids D02's scope term "lacking
federal deposit insurance" (its shorthand for privately insured credit unions), though the meaning is
the same; D02 1179 counts all 214 as lacking federal insurance.
Source risks: weaker trap than q02 (only the denominator changes); a solver searching the question's
concept may land on 1179 directly. Rounding: 10.28 and 10.38, both clear of 10.35.
Author calculation check and counting convention: 22 / 214 = 10.280% -> 10.3%; 22 / 212 = 10.377% -> 10.4%.
