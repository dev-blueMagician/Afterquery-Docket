## Question

What percentage of the privately insured credit unions in the state whose bank regulator adds a $100 charge per branch office to its assessment had Web sites that did not tell consumers they were not federally insured, as GAO found in 2003? One decimal.

## Answer

25.0%

## Evidence

- D05 (Appendix VI, Table 4, Idaho row, text line 819): "The Idaho Department of Finance is"
- D05 (Appendix VI, Table 4, Idaho row, text line 821): "on total assets and $100 per branch office"
- D02 (Table 3, text line 467): "Idaho 20 7 5 71"
- D02 (text line 455): "39 of the 78 sites had not included language to notify consumers that the credit union was not federally insured"
