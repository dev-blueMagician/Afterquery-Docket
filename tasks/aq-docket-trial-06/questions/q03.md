## Question

By what percentage did the number of U.S. credit unions at the end of 2002 exceed the number of U.S. banks on September 30, 2005, measured against the bank count? Answer to two decimal places.

## Answer

31.31%

## Components

- c1: 31.31%

## Reasoning

1. Doc 2: as of December 2002, 9,688 credit unions were federally insured, and 214 state-chartered credit unions lacked federal deposit insurance (212 privately insured plus two uninsured ones, in Idaho and New Hampshire). Federally chartered credit unions must be federally insured, so these two groups are all credit unions: 9,688 + 214 = 9,902 credit unions.
2. Doc 5: as of September 30, 2005, there were 1,846 federally chartered banks and 5,695 state-chartered banks: 1,846 + 5,695 = 7,541 banks.
3. Doc 2 and Doc 5: 9,902 - 7,541 = 2,361 more credit unions than banks.
4. Doc 2 and Doc 5: 2,361 / 7,541 = 31.31% (to two decimal places), measured against the bank count.
5. Wrong routes: leaving out the two uninsured credit unions named in a footnote, counting only the federally insured credit unions, measuring against the credit-union count, or counting one bank charter type each gives a different percentage at two decimal places.
