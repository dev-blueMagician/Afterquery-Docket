## Question

At the end of 2002, what percentage of all U.S. credit unions without federal deposit insurance were located in the state whose bank regulator adds a $100 charge per branch office to its asset-based assessment? Answer to one decimal place.

## Answer

9.8%

## Components

- c1: 9.8%

## Reasoning

1. Doc 5: among the state bank regulators GAO describes, only the Idaho Department of Finance has an assessment structure that "includes a graduated base fee and excess fee based on total assets and $100 per branch office", so the state is Idaho.
2. Doc 2: as of December 2002, 214 state-chartered credit unions lacked federal deposit insurance, and all but two were privately insured; the two uninsured ones were located in Idaho and New Hampshire. Idaho had 20 privately insured credit unions, so Idaho had 20 + 1 = 21 credit unions without federal deposit insurance.
3. Doc 2 and Doc 5: 21 / 214 = 9.8% (to one decimal place).
4. Wrong routes: using only the privately insured credit unions (Idaho's privately insured count over the national privately insured total), or adding the uninsured credit union to only one side of the fraction, gives a different value at one decimal place.
