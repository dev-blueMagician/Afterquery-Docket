## Shared ground between D04 and D05 (navigation only)

- Common data program: both use the BLS Current Employment Statistics program. D04: "Current
  Employment Statistics (CES) survey" (D04 line 11), note 1 survey size and "seasonally adjusted
  unless otherwise noted" (D04 line 3334). D05: "Current Employment Statistics program" (line 9),
  "Current Employment Statistic program data" (line 11), note 1 basis (line 3273). Classification:
  D04 rail = 2017 NAICS 482 (D04 line 3334); D05 state manufacturing under NAICS from 1990 (line
  11) with NAICS 311-337 codes (lines 3241-3263).
- Publication relationship: both BLS Monthly Labor Review articles of 2021 with consecutive DOIs
  (D04 October 2021, mlr.2021.21, D04 line 3330, date 10/27/2021 D04 line 5; D05 November 2021,
  mlr.2021.22, line 3269, date 11/10/2021 line 5). Neither cites the other. D05 belongs to a
  four-part regional manufacturing series (line 11); D04 is not described as part of one.
- Overlapping periods: every month Jan 1990 - Dec 2019 has a value in D04 Chart 1 (rail,
  thousands, SA) and in D05 Charts 1 and 5 (manufacturing, thousands, SA). D04 Charts 2, 4, 5
  (Jan 2018 - Dec 2020) overlap D05 for Jan 2018 - Dec 2019; D04 Chart 3 (Jan 2016 - Dec 2020)
  overlaps D05 for 2016-2019. D05 Charts 2-4 give 1990 and 2019 only. 2020 values exist only in
  D04; D04 Chart 1 also covers 1980-1989, which D05 does not.

- Business-cycle and recession dates, and who dates them: only D05 gives cycle months (November
  2001 end of the 2001 recession; December 2007 peak; June 2009 trough, line 1465; February 2010
  called "the trough of the recession" for manufacturing, line 1467). D05 names no dating body in
  captured text (NBER only as a journal title, line 3283). D04 gives no cycle months. Both name
  the pandemic recession without dates: D04 "The COVID-19 recession" (D04 line 3010); D05 "the
  recession that was ushered in by the coronavirus disease 2019 pandemic" (line 11). "Great
  Recession": D05 text (lines 1465, 3255); D04 only in a cited title (D04 note 16, line 3364).
  D04 Chart 1 has values for all D05 cycle months (table above).
- Industries in both: D04 mentions manufacturing only as a freight-demand sector (with
  agriculture) affected by 2019 U.S.-China disputes (D04 line 3010). D05 never mentions rail,
  coal or freight (keyword sweep). The word "transportation" names different industries: D04
  transportation and warehousing / rail transportation (NAICS 482, D04 Table 1 lines 1991-2002)
  vs D05 transportation equipment manufacturing (NAICS 336, line 3241). "Truck transportation"
  (D04 line 1996) vs trucks as transportation-equipment output (line 3241).
- China and trade: D04 U.S.-China tariff disputes 2019, phase-one deal January 2020 (D04 lines
  3008-3010, notes 10-15); D05 China's permanent normal trade relations in 2000 (line 1465,
  note 2).
