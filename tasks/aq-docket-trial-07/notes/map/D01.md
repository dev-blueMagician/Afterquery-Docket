### Operative definitions, rules and scope exceptions

- Legal definition (L15): Indian Country "is defined in 18 U.S.C. § 1151 and 40 CFR § 171.3 as
  “(a) all land within the limits of any Indian reservation under the jurisdiction of the United
  States Government, notwithstanding the issuance of any patent, and, including rights-of-way
  running through the reservation, (b) all dependent Indian communities within the borders of the
  United States whether within the original or subsequently acquired territory thereof, and whether
  within or without the limits of a state, and (c) all Indian allotments, the Indian titles to which
  have not been extinguished, including rights-of-way running through the same.”" "This definition
  has been accepted into practice by federal agencies." (note 2: EPA page, L232).
- Scope difference (L15): "the latter [federal legal definition] applies only to federally
  recognized tribes, whereas the former [Census Bureau measurement] includes state-recognized
  tribes as well." L17: Census, "unlike the BIA, includes state-designated tribal service areas";
  Hawaii "has no federally recognized tribes".
- Service population (LFR quote, L109): "The tribe’s estimate of all American Indians and Alaska
  Natives who are living on or near the tribe’s reservation (or tribal area) during the 2010
  calendar year and who are eligible to receive services funded by Indian Affairs. ... The service
  population of a tribe is not the same as the members (or “enrollment”) of the tribe." L153: the
  measure "was not meant to include entire tribal enrollments".
- Home ownership (L45): "(the proportion of households who own their dwellings)".
- Domain (L51): "a consistent analytical “domain,” which defines exactly what it is that is being
  studied."
- Tribal service area and tribal statistical area (L101): second domain "an expansion of the
  first, to include a wider geographic area in which households may be located and receive services
  from the tribe"; "A similar geographic area is what the U.S. Census Bureau has defined, in
  consultation with the tribes, as a tribal statistical area."
- Population basis (L125): "identifies people as either enrolled tribal members or individuals who
  self-identify (in a U.S. Census Bureau survey) as AIAN or AIAN-AOIC."
- Institutional basis (L135): "institutions that are either tribal government enterprises or
  businesses and nonprofit organizations owned by a tribal member."
- Tribal establishments and coverage (Table 3 notes, L188), quoted under Tables.
- SSA study AIAN (L161): "individuals described as AIAN in our analysis are those who listed their
