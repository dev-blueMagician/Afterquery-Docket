## Question

For the federal bank regulator whose officials told GAO they had built a review program to flag high-risk types of lending, list the four outcome categories of the consumer complaints it resolved in calendar years 2000 through 2004, in order from most to least frequent.

## Answer

Additional information provided, bank not in error, withdrawn or tabled for litigation, bank in error

## Components

- c1: Additional information provided
- c2: bank not in error
- c3: withdrawn or tabled for litigation
- c4: bank in error

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 5: "FDIC officials told us that they have developed a review program to identify high-risk lending areas and evaluate risk management and underwriting approaches." The same passage gives the other regulators different steps (NCUA contacted its largest credit unions, OTS reviewed its most active alternative mortgage lenders, OCC began reviewing lenders' marketing materials), so the regulator is FDIC.

Step 2. Doc 4: GAO sorted the complaint resolutions of OCC, the Federal Reserve, FDIC and OTS for calendar years 2000 through 2004 into four categories: regulators provide consumers additional information, complaint is withdrawn or tabled due to litigation, regulators determine that bank was not in error, and regulators determine that bank was in error. "Between 2000 and 2004, the most common resolution of complaints handled by all federal regulators was that consumers were provided more in-depth or specific information", so additional information ranks first for FDIC.

Step 3. Doc 4: under "Regulators Determine That Bank Was Not in Error", this outcome "was third for OCC in terms of frequency, while it was second for the other regulators", so it ranks second for FDIC.

Step 4. Doc 4: the withdrawn or litigation outcome was "the least common for the Federal Reserve and OTS and the third most common for FDIC", so it ranks third for FDIC.

Step 5. Doc 4: "Bank Made an Error" "was the least common outcome for complaints resolved by OCC and FDIC", so it ranks last for FDIC.

Conclusion: additional information provided, bank not in error, withdrawn or tabled for litigation, bank in error.

Decoy: Doc 4's overall list, "as shown in order of decreasing frequency" (additional information, withdrawn, bank not in error, bank in error), is OCC's order, not FDIC's. The Federal Reserve and OTS put the withdrawn outcome last.
