## Question

For the federal bank regulator whose officials said they had set up a program to pinpoint high-risk lending areas, list the four outcome categories of the consumer complaints it resolved in 2000 through 2004, in order from most to least frequent.

## Answer

Consumer given additional information, bank found not in error, complaint withdrawn or in litigation, bank found in error

## Evidence

- D03 (L189, agency-specific AMP steps): "FDIC officials told us that they have developed a review program to identify high-risk lending areas"
- D04 (L225, outcome 1 for all regulators): "Between 2000 and 2004, the most common resolution of complaints handled by all federal regulators was that consumers were provided more in-depth or specific information"
- D04 (L249, under the heading "Regulators Determine That Bank Was Not in Error"): "was third for OCC in terms of frequency, while it was second for the other regulators"
- D04 (L241): "the least common for the Federal Reserve and OTS and the third most common for FDIC"
- D04 (L253): "was the least common outcome for complaints resolved by OCC and FDIC"
