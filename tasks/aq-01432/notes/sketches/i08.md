## Question

The UK's short business guide to unfair commercial practices groups several banned practices under after-sales care. Which paragraphs of Schedule 20 to the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 set out those practices? Give the paragraph numbers in ascending order.

## Answer

8, 9 and 29

## Evidence

- D10 (After sales care, L95): "honouring promises you have made about after care being available in a particular language or from a particular location, and by responding to correspondence where they are trying to exercise statutory rights."
- D09 (Banned practice 8, L487): "Undertaking to provide an after-sales service to consumers and then making such service available only in a language which is different to the language used in communication with the consumer"
- D09 (Banned practice 9, L495): "Falsely claiming or creating the false impression that an after-sales service is available, including falsely claiming that it is available in, or accessible from, any particular country or location."
- D09 (Banned practice 29, L719): "or failing systematically to respond to correspondence, to dissuade a consumer from exercising the consumer’s contractual rights."
- D09 (Banned practice 11, L511): "Presenting rights given to consumers by law as a distinctive feature of the trader’s offer."
