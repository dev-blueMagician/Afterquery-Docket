## Question

Which paragraphs of Schedule 20 to the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 set out the banned practices that the UK's brief business guide on unfair trading lists under misleading promotional claims? Give the paragraph numbers in ascending order.

## Answer

5, 6, 7, 11, 17, 22, 23

## Components

- c1: 5
- c2: 6
- c3: 7
- c4: 11
- c5: 17
- c6: 22
- c7: 23

## Reasoning

Step 1 (Doc 11): The short guide says "The banned practices fall into the following themes." Under "Misleading promotional claims" it names: bait advertising; bait and switch; pretending that a special offer will finish soon; claiming to be about to cease trading or move premises; saying a product is free when it isn't; telling someone they have won a prize when that is not true; pretending that consumers' statutory rights are a distinctive part of the offer.

Step 2 (Doc 9): The full guidance numbers each banned practice as in Schedule 20 ("paragraph 13 of Schedule 20"). The guide's behaviours match: an invitation to purchase at a price the trader has reasonable grounds to believe it cannot supply, without disclosing that (banned practice 5, bait advertising); an invitation to purchase followed by refusing to show, take orders for or deliver the item "with the intention of promoting a different product" (banned practice 6, bait and switch); falsely stating that a product will only be available for a limited time (banned practice 7); claiming to be about to cease trading or move premises (banned practice 17); creating the false impression that the consumer has won a prize (banned practice 22); describing a product as free when the consumer has to pay more than unavoidable costs (banned practice 23); presenting rights given to consumers by law as a distinctive feature of the offer (banned practice 11).

Step 3 (Doc 11 and Doc 9): In ascending order the paragraphs are 5, 6, 7, 11, 17, 22, 23.

Decoy (Doc 9): banned practice 21, claiming to offer a competition or prize promotion without awarding the prizes, also concerns prizes, but the guide's clause is about telling someone they have won, which is banned practice 22.
