answer: Three services would expose the provider. All three are the Chapter 6 "procure for traders" examples in CMA208:
(1) Setting up and running social-media groups that recruit people to post fake reviews (or concealed incentivised reviews) about a trader's products. This counts for its fake-review limb.
(2) Selling reviews that look as if individual consumers wrote them but were in fact generated by software or bots.

None of the Chapter 7 "facilitation" services would expose the provider.
