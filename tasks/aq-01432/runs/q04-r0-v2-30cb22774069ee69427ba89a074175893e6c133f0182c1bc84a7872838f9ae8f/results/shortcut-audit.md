answer: Three services, all from CMA208 Chapter 6 ("Offering to procure banned reviews and information for traders"):
(1) Setting up and running social-media groups that recruit people willing to post fake or concealed incentivised reviews about a trader's products. Only the fake-review part makes the provider a fake-review broker selling reviews under §465.2(a). §465.4 could also apply if the incentives require a positive review.
(2) Selling reviews generated by bots or other software that look as if individual consumers wrote them (§465.2(a)).
(3) A search engine optimisation (SEO) service that boosts a trader's rating partly by generating fake reviews (§465.2(a)).
The two Chapter 7 "facilitation" examples are not covered by the rule: services that let accounts be set up so they avoid or reduce the risk of detection, and running a platform or social-media site while knowingly allowing fake-review services to be sold on it.
