## Question

One firm that BIS added to the Entity List in September 2025 has premises on the same Shanghai road as a chip plant run by a company on BIS's October 2024 list of validated end users. Which firm is it, and which validated end user runs the plant?

## Answer

Sino IC Technology Co., Ltd. and Shanghai Huahong Grace Semiconductor Manufacturing Corporation

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h14. Two long flattened tables of names and addresses: the validated end-user list
(D03, about 50 site addresses) and the Entity List additions (D04, 29 organisations, about 150 addresses).
One road joins a trusted fab and a listed firm: Guoshoujing Road, Shanghai (Shanghai Huahong Grace's HFab 2
at No. 668; Sino IC Technology at No. 351). The page says it is short of "One figure, found precisely";
this is the two-document form of that shape. Two look-alike pairs fail the test in the question.

Step audit (steps no word in the question points at): none. The question says what to compare. What it
does not give is the road, either name, or a search word: neither table is ordered by address, and D03
prints the fab's address interleaved with the item column. The difficulty is retrieval in two tables and
two decoy pairs, which the workspace does not count as reasoning difficulty. Said plainly so it is not
read as a lead.

Ambiguity (readings reported, not cleared):
- "Jicun Semiconductor Technology (Shanghai) and Lam Research": Jicun (added) is at No. 45, Feila Road;
  Lam's WGQ bonded warehouse is at No. 55, Fei La Road. Rejected by "chip plant run by": the Lam site is a
  bonded warehouse and Lam Research Service is not a chip maker. The road is also spelled two ways.
- "Hexin Xingtong and Lam Research": both on Bi Bo Road, Shanghai. Rejected twice: Hexin Xingtong is a
  corrected entry (first added May 9, 2024, D04 L123), not one added in September 2025, and the Lam site is
  a service office.
- "Chip plant" for HFab 2: D03 never uses those words; it labels the site "HFab 2" of a "Semiconductor
  Manufacturing Corporation". A reader could ask whether an "HFab" is a plant.
- Every road name on D03's list was searched in D04 (map, "Added 2026-10-13"): no other match, so no
  other added firm qualifies under any reading of "plant".

Necessity:
- D03: only source of the validated end users' site addresses. D04: only source of the added firms' addresses.
- Each document carries its own answer part (the fab's operator from D03, the firm from D04).

Delete-and-guess: D03 gone, a solver has about ten added firms with Shanghai addresses and no road to match;
a guess from the name alone would favour a Zhangjiang chip firm such as Shanghai Fudan Microelectronics (not
the gold). D04 gone, nothing identifies the firm. Street addresses on these two lists are not recallable detail.

After clarification: two indispensable documents; the wording gives no road and no name.

Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Who belongs where; Scatter the pieces. Decoys named and failed:
the Feila / Fei La pair (wrong kind of site) and the Bi Bo Road pair (wrong kind of entry and of site).

Source risks: a reviewer may call a shared road a coincidence rather than reasoning. A solver that searches
D04 for each of D03's five fab roads finds it mechanically.
