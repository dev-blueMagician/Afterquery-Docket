## Question

Judging by all five of these BIS rules together, which named companies listed under, or described as based in, the UAE still needed an export licence for advanced computing chips after the UAE joined Country Group A:5 in July 2026? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

HAS General Trading LLC, MGX, Smart Mail Services

## Evidence

- D04 (preamble, Additions, L58): "The ERC determined to add HAS General Trading LLC, under the destination of the UAE, to the Entity List"
- D04 (preamble, Additions, L59): "The entity is added with a license requirement for all items subject to the EAR."
- D04 (preamble, Additions, L55): "The ERC determined to add one entity to the Entity List: Smart Mail Services under the destinations of Iran and the United Arab Emirates."
- D06 (Enforcement section, L81): "BIS will continue enforcing the license requirement for exports, reexports, and transfers (in-country) of advanced computing items specified in Sec. 742.6(a)(6)(iii)(A) to or within the UAE, except when the ultimate consignee and all end users are UAE Government entities or approved commercial entities listed in supplement no. 8 to part 740"
- D06 (Enforcement section, L87): "In addition, BIS intends to favorably review export license applications involving the UAE-headquartered company MGX, for the export of semiconductors and servers to the UAE."
- D06 (Enforcement section, L83): "two UAE-based AI companies are also approved recipients of such items license-free"
- D06 (Supplement 8, table to paragraph (b), L191): "Group 42 Holding Ltd d/b/a/G42,"
- D06 (Enforcement section, L85): "approval in supplement no. 8 to part 740 does not overcome the end-use and end-user based license requirements in"
