## Question

One BIS rule put two firms on the Entity List under the UAE. A later one moved the UAE into Country Group A:5. How many days after the first rule took effect did the second take effect?

## Answer

301 days

## Evidence

- D04 (DATES, L32): "DATES: This rule is effective September 12, 2025."
- D04 (SUMMARY, L28): "and the United Arab Emirates (UAE) (2)"
- D06 (DATES, L30): "DATES: This rule is effective July 10, 2026."
- D06 (SUMMARY, L28): "BIS is removing the UAE from Country Groups D:3 and D:4 and adding the UAE to Country Group A:5."
