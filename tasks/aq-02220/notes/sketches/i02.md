## Question

What percentage of England's 2035 heat-network target would the impact assessment's preferred zoning option's 2035 deployment equal, to one decimal place?

## Answer

46.3%

## Evidence

- D01 (L18, Introduction): "In the Warm Homes Plan we are setting a target to more than double the amount of heat demand met via heat networks in England to 7% (27TWh) by 2035"
- D02 (L1497, Table 12): "Table 12 – Deployment under different policy options (in addition to existing heat networks and"
- D02 (L1534-1544, Table 12 Medium and High pairs): "Medium High (preferred) 0.1 0.1 4.2 6.3 8.3 12.5"
