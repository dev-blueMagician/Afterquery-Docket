## Question

How many TWh of England's projected 2050 heat demand are heat networks now expected to supply, to one decimal place?

## Answer

87.8 TWh

## Evidence

- D01 (L18, Introduction): "with them expected to provide a fifth of all heat by 2050"
- D02 (L1495, Results): "The total heat demand for England, presented in Opportunity Areas for District Heating Networks in the UK3, is estimated to be 439 TWh in 2050"
