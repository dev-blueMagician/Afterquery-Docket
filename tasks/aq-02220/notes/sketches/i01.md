## Question

With the impact assessment's heat-network efficiencies, what percentage of the zone emissions benchmark's electricity and gas input is gas, to one decimal place?

## Answer

40.0%

## Evidence

- D01 (L116, Emissions in Heat Network Zones): "This will be based on a benchmark scenario assuming 85% of heat is delivered by low-carbon air source heat pumps using electricity and 15% is supplied by gas boilers"
- D02 (L2230, Annex 1 Table 24): "Table 24 - Thermal Efficiency (factual and counterfactual)"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. Doc 4's benchmark gives shares of heat; Doc 2's Table 24 efficiencies turn heat into
electricity and gas input (heat-network ASHP 321%, back-up gas boiler 85%). Gas input 15/85 = 17.647% of heat,
electricity 85/321 = 26.480%, gas share 40.0%.
Ambiguity: heat share 15.0% fails "electricity and gas input"; Table 24's individual commercial (84%) and
domestic (86%) gas boilers fail "heat-network" (Table 21 lists the Back-up Gas Boiler among heat-network
technologies); ASHP is the only air-source entry. Rounding intermediates to one decimal gives 39.9%.
Necessity: Doc 4 only source of the 85/15 benchmark (searched benchmark, 85%, 15%, gas boilers in all four);
Doc 2 only source of any efficiency (searched efficiency, COP, coefficient, 321). Without Doc 2 a typical COP 3
gives 38.4%; without Doc 4 no benchmark.
