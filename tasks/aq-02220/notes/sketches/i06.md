## Question

With the impact assessment's central generation mix, what share of the consultation's economic waste-heat supply would its preferred option use in 2050, to the nearest percent?

## Answer

42%

## Evidence

- D02 (L979, technology mix): "This study proposed that 19% of heat network heat demand could be met with waste heat sources"
- D02 (L1893-1894, sensitivity): "which is based on achieving 31 TWh/yr of additional deployment by 2050"
- D03 (L927, heat sources): "The results show waste heat supplying 14 TWh of a total 76 TWh of heat being met by district heat networks in England"
