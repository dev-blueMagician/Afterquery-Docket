## Question

Which South American country's fiscal year 2021 State Department- and USAID-managed aid included the largest dollar amount of American Rescue Plan Act money, and what was the percentage change from its fiscal year 2021 aid without that money to the Biden Administration's fiscal year 2024 aid request for it? Give the sign, to the nearest whole percent.

## Answer

Peru (+26%)

## Components

- c1: Peru
- c2: +26%

## Reasoning

Step 1: Doc 6, Table 2 note a: its FY2021 country figures "include some supplemental assistance appropriated in the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (P.L. 117-2)", $97.2 million for the region (Table 1 note a).

Step 2: Doc 3 and Doc 6: Doc 3's FY2021 column leaves out supplemental emergency appropriations, P.L. 117-2 among them (Table 2 note a), so each country's ARP money is its Doc 6 FY2021 figure minus its Doc 3 FY2021 figure, in thousands of dollars.

Step 3: Doc 3 and Doc 6, check: the country differences add up to Doc 6's $97.2 million, and the two FY2021 regional totals differ by that amount plus the $5 million OAS contribution that Doc 6 counts and Doc 3 leaves out.

Step 4: Doc 3 and Doc 6, Peru: 98,885 - 85,535 = 13,350.

Step 5: Doc 3 and Doc 6, Colombia: 478,192 - 465,092 = 13,100.

Step 6: Doc 3 and Doc 6, the other South American countries are smaller: Brazil 27,950 against 19,450, Paraguay 9,600 against 4,400, Ecuador 28,750 against 23,750, Uruguay 900 against 400; Bolivia has no Doc 3 row and a Doc 6 FY2021 figure of 4,000; Argentina (700), Chile (455), Guyana (200), Suriname (200) and Venezuela (38,000) are the same in both. So Peru's is the largest dollar amount.

Step 7: Doc 6, Table 2: Peru's FY2024 request was 107,635 (Table A-3: 107.6 million).

Step 8: Doc 3 and Doc 6: (107,635 - 85,535) / 85,535 = +26% (to the nearest whole percent).

Step 9: Doc 3 and Doc 6, decoys: with the ARP money left in the base, 107,635 / 98,885 = 1.0885; the printed +7.2% in Peru's Doc 6 row compares with the FY2023 estimate; and Colombia, the largest recipient overall, had less ARP money and a falling request (444,025 in Doc 6 against 465,092 in Doc 3).
