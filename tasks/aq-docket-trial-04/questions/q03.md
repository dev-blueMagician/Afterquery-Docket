## Question

The priorities in the Biden Administration's fiscal year 2025 State Department- and USAID-managed aid request for Latin America and the Caribbean made up nearly 75% of that request. By how many percentage points did those priorities' share change from the fiscal year 2023 request to the 2025 request? Give the sign, to one decimal place.

## Answer

-6.6 percentage points

## Components

- c1: -6.6 percentage points

## Reasoning

Step 1: Doc 8 lists the FY2025 priorities and says they "account for nearly 75%" of the FY2025 request: the Central America root-causes strategy (at least $632.6 million), Colombia ($413.3 million), Haiti ($356.7 million), Mexico ($109.7 million), the Caribbean Basin Security Initiative ($81.0 million) and Venezuela ($53.8 million).

Step 2: Doc 8: 632.6 + 413.3 + 356.7 + 109.7 + 81.0 + 53.8 = 1,647.1.

Step 3: Doc 8, Table 1 total for the FY2025 request (2,205.2): 1,647.1 / 2,205.2 = 74.69%.

Step 4: Doc 3 gives the FY2023 requests for the same priorities. Of the $986.8 million requested for the Central America strategy, $959.0 million fell within the Latin America and Caribbean regional request; the other $27.8 million was in global accounts outside the regional total. The other requests were $63.5 million for the Caribbean Basin Security Initiative, $462.9 million for Colombia, $274.8 million for Haiti, $141.6 million for Mexico and $55.0 million for Venezuela.

Step 5: Doc 3: 959.0 + 63.5 + 462.9 + 274.8 + 141.6 + 55.0 = 1,956.8.

Step 6: Doc 3, Table 1 total for the FY2023 request (2,407.4): 1,956.8 / 2,407.4 = 81.28%.

Step 7: Doc 3 and Doc 8: 74.69% - 81.28% = -6.6 percentage points (to one decimal place).

Step 8: Doc 3 and Doc 8, decoys: Doc 8's Table 2 prints FY2023 estimated allocations, not requests, beside the FY2025 request; Doc 3's headline $986.8 million includes the $27.8 million outside the regional request; and the rounded totals ("more than $2.4 billion" in Doc 3, "$2.2 billion" in Doc 8) or the printed "nearly 75%" give different values at one decimal place.
