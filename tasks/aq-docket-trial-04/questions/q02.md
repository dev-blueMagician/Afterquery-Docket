## Question

Which Latin American and Caribbean countries saw the Biden Administration's requested State Department- and USAID-managed aid decline from its fiscal year 2023 request to its fiscal year 2024 request and decline again in its fiscal year 2025 request? List them alphabetically.

## Answer

Colombia, Mexico, Peru, Venezuela

## Components

- c1: Colombia
- c2: Mexico
- c3: Peru
- c4: Venezuela

## Reasoning

Step 1: Doc 3 (FY2023 edition, Table 2, "FY2023 (req.)" column, thousands of dollars) gives each country's FY2023 request: Colombia 462,875, Mexico 141,575, Peru 109,870, Venezuela 55,000, El Salvador 124,800.

Step 2: Doc 6 (FY2024 edition, Table 2, "FY2024 (request)" column) gives the FY2024 requests: Colombia 444,025, Mexico 111,400, Peru 107,635, Venezuela 54,000, El Salvador 124,800.

Step 3: Doc 8 (FY2025 edition, Table A-1, "FY2025 (request)" column) gives the FY2025 requests: Colombia 413,275, Mexico 109,700, Peru 94,800, Venezuela 53,750, El Salvador 87,700.

Step 4: Colombia, Mexico, Peru and Venezuela fall at both steps. Every other country rose, stayed level, or rose before falling (Brazil, Dominican Republic, Guatemala, Honduras and Jamaica rose for FY2024 and then fell).

Step 5: Decoy: El Salvador's FY2024 request equals its FY2023 request (124,800), so it declines only once. State Central America Regional falls twice (447,765, 417,800, 295,800) but is a regional program, not a country. The "% Change" columns in Doc 6 and Doc 8 compare each request with the FY2023 estimated allocation, not with the previous request; read that way, Costa Rica, Honduras, Jamaica, Nicaragua, Panama and Trinidad and Tobago would wrongly qualify.
