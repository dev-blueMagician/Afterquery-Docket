## Question

Which South American country's fiscal year 2021 State Department- and USAID-managed aid included the most American Rescue Plan Act money, and by what percentage did the Biden Administration's fiscal year 2024 aid request for that country exceed its fiscal year 2021 aid without that money, to the nearest whole percent?

## Answer

Peru (26%)
