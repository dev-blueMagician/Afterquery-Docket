## Question

The Biden Administration said the priorities in its fiscal year 2025 State Department- and USAID-managed aid request for Latin America and the Caribbean took nearly 75% of it. By how many percentage points did those priorities' share change from the fiscal year 2024 request to the 2025 request? Give the sign, to one decimal place.

## Answer

-2.7 percentage points

## Evidence

- D08 (Summary): "These priorities account for nearly 75% of the total foreign assistance funding that the Administration requested for Latin America and the Caribbean for FY2025."
- D08 (Summary): "$413.3 million for Colombia"
- D08 (Table 1): "Total 2,104.4a 2,043.3 2,205.2b +7.9%"
- D06 (Summary): "$444.0 million for Colombia"
- D06 (Table 1): "Total 1,896.5a 2,104.4b 2,043.3 2,470.7 +20.9%"
- D06 (Venezuela section): "The Biden Administration requested $54.0 million for FY2024"
