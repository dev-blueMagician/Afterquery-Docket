## Question

Which country had the largest percentage decline in requested State Department- and USAID-managed aid from the Biden Administration's FY2023 request to its FY2025 request, and what was that decline, to the nearest whole percent?

## Answer

Honduras (35% decline)

## Evidence

- D05 (Table 2, FY2023 (req.) column, thousands): "Honduras 56,906 73,365 74,002 108,306 133,450 +23.2%"
- D05 (Table A-3, FY2023 Request, millions): "Honduras 126.7 — 6.0 — — — 0.8 — 133.5"
- D08 (Table A-1, FY2025 (request) column, thousands): "Honduras 79,902a 117,878c 141,774 86,300 -39.1%"
- D08 (Central America section): "$86.3 million for Honduras"
