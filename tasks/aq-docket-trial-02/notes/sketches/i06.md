## Question

By how much did full-year net sales of the operations Colgate moved from its Africa/Eurasia segment into its Europe, Middle East & Africa segment change from 2024 to 2025, in millions of dollars? Give the sign.

## Answer

+$69 million

## Evidence

- D11 (l.85, note 1): "The results of the Europe and Africa/Eurasia (excluding Russia and Belarus) reportable operating segments and the skin health business (previously within the North America reportable operating segment) have been combined in a new Europe, Middle East & Africa reportable operating segment."
- D11 (l.85, same note): "Russia and Belarus, which were previously reported within the Africa/Eurasia reportable operating segment, are now reported within the Asia Pacific reportable operating segment."
- D09 (l.463, Table 5 net sales; FY2025 and FY2024 columns): "Asia Pacific | 722 | 724 | 2,814 | 2,858"
- D09 (l.464, Table 5 net sales; FY2025 and FY2024 columns): "Africa/Eurasia | 309 | 269 | 1,172 | 1,095"
- D10 (l.28, recast 2025): "Asia Pacific | 3,038 | 1,137 | 1,101 | (20) | 820"
- D10 (l.59, recast 2024): "Asia Pacific | 3,074 | 1,144 | 1,061 | (23) | 892"
