## Question

In the year Colgate took a charge to write down goodwill and intangible assets tied to its skin health business, what were that business's net sales, in millions of dollars?

## Answer

$366 million

## Evidence

- D09 (l.71, impairment paragraph): "During the fourth quarter, the Company took a non-cash, after-tax charge of $794 million to adjust the carrying values of goodwill and intangible assets related to the skin health business."
- D11 (l.85, note 1): "the skin health business (previously within the North America reportable operating segment) have been combined in a new Europe, Middle East & Africa reportable operating segment"
- D09 (l.460, Table 5 net sales, twelve months 2025): "North America | $ 1,021 | $ 1,036 | $ 4,045 | $ 4,113"
- D10 (l.25, recast year ended December 31, 2025): "North America | $ 3,679 | $ 1,510 | $ 1,375 | $ 4 | $ 790"
