## Question

As of June 30, 2026, Colgate's cumulative pretax charges under its Strategic Growth and Productivity Program equaled what percentage of the midpoint of the program's charge estimate in effect when its first charges were recorded? Give it to two decimal places.

## Answer

127.20%

## Evidence

- D07 (l.41, programme announcement, August 1, 2025): "The productivity program is projected to result in cumulative pre-tax charges, once all initiatives are approved and implemented, totaling between $200 and $300 million over the course of the three-year program."
- D09 (l.703, Table 9 fourth quarter 2025, income before income taxes column): "Restructuring programs(1)	13 2 11"
- D09 (l.903, Table 9 note): "The restructuring program charges relate to the Strategic Growth and Productivity Program in 2025"
- D11 (l.39, SGPP expansion paragraph): "cumulative pretax charges related to the SGPP, once all projects are approved and implemented, are now estimated to be $350 million to $550 million, increased from $200 million to $300 million"
- D12 (l.800, Table 9 six months 2026, income before income taxes column): "Strategic Growth and Productivity Program	305 61 244 2 242"
