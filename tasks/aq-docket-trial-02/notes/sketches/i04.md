## Question

Measured as Colgate measures segment operating profit, what was its skin health business's result in the quarter in which it took the goodwill and intangible assets impairment charge on that business, in millions of dollars?

## Answer

-$4 million

## Evidence

- D09 (l.71, impairment paragraph): "During the fourth quarter, the Company took a non-cash, after-tax charge of $794 million to adjust the carrying values of goodwill and intangible assets related to the skin health business."
- D09 (l.491, Table 5 note): "Corporate Operating profit (loss) for the three months ended December 31, 2025 included goodwill and intangible assets impairment charges of $919"
- D09 (l.477, Table 5 operating profit, three months 2025): "North America | $ 203 | $ 206 | $ 784 | $ 839"
- D10 (l.25, recast 2025): "North America | $ 3,679 | $ 1,510 | $ 1,375 | $ 4 | $ 790"
- D10 (l.333, recast nine months 2025): "North America | $ 2,745 | $ 1,119 | $ 1,042 | $ 2 | $ 583"
- D11 (l.85, note 1): "the skin health business (previously within the North America reportable operating segment) have been combined in a new Europe, Middle East & Africa reportable operating segment"
