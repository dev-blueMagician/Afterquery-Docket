## Question

How much of Colgate's full-year 2025 net sales in its Europe, Middle East & Africa segment came from operations it had previously reported in its Africa/Eurasia segment, in millions of dollars?

## Answer

$948 million

## Evidence

- D11 (l.85, Q1 2026 division table note 1): "The results of the Europe and Africa/Eurasia (excluding Russia and Belarus) reportable operating segments and the skin health business (previously within the North America reportable operating segment) have been combined in a new Europe, Middle East & Africa reportable operating segment."
- D11 (l.85, same note): "Russia and Belarus, which were previously reported within the Africa/Eurasia reportable operating segment, are now reported within the Asia Pacific reportable operating segment."
- D09 (l.463, Table 5 net sales, twelve months 2025): "Asia Pacific | 722 | 724 | 2,814 | 2,858"
- D09 (l.464, Table 5 net sales, twelve months 2025): "Africa/Eurasia | 309 | 269 | 1,172 | 1,095"
- D10 (l.28, recast year ended December 31, 2025): "Asia Pacific | 3,038 | 1,137 | 1,101 | (20) | 820"

## Sketch notes

The former Africa/Eurasia part of EMEA = A/E 2025 (1,172, D09) minus Russia/Belarus 2025, which is
never printed: recast AP 3,038 (D10) - original AP 2,814 (D09) = 224. 1,172 - 224 = 948.
Cross-route: EMEA 4,276 (D10) - Europe 2,962 (D09) - skin health (NA 4,045 D09 - 3,679 D10 = 366) = 948.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: match then exclude (carve-out of Russia/Belarus stated only in
a parenthetical and the next sentence of D11's note); scatter the pieces. Omitting the exclusion gives
1,172 (the printed A/E figure); treating EMEA as Europe + A/E gives 1,314; both differ from 948.
Source risks: D11 l.85 is the only composition statement; segment rows are pipe-table rows (form
