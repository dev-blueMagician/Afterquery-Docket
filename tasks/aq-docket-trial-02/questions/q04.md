## Question

What was the operating profit margin of Colgate's North America segment, as the company defined that segment from 2026, for the twelve months ended June 30, 2026? Give it as a percentage of the segment's net sales, to two decimal places.

## Answer

20.33%

## Components

- c1: 20.33%

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 12: the segment tables were recast to conform to the reporting structure effective for the quarter ended March 31, 2026. North America had net sales of 1,779 and operating profit of 333 in the six months ended June 30, 2026, against recast net sales of 1,823 and operating profit of 384 in the six months ended June 30, 2025 (dollars in millions).

Step 2. Doc 10 and Doc 12: the recast segment exhibit in Doc 10 already shows the Europe, Middle East & Africa segment, and its six-month 2025 North America row (net sales of 1,823 and operating profit of 384) matches Doc 12's recast comparatives, so it follows the structure used from 2026. On that basis North America had full-year 2025 net sales of 3,679 and operating profit of 790.

Step 3. Doc 10 and Doc 12: net sales for the second half of 2025 were 3,679 - 1,823 = 1,856.

Step 4. Doc 10 and Doc 12: net sales for the twelve months ended June 30, 2026 were 1,856 + 1,779 = 3,635.

Step 5. Doc 10 and Doc 12: operating profit for the second half of 2025 was 790 - 384 = 406.

Step 6. Doc 10 and Doc 12: operating profit for the twelve months ended June 30, 2026 was 406 + 333 = 739.

Step 7. Doc 10 and Doc 12: the operating profit margin was 739 / 3,635 = 20.33% (to two decimal places).

Decoy: Doc 9 reports full-year 2025 North America net sales of 4,045 and operating profit of 784 beside separate Europe and Africa/Eurasia segments, which shows the structure in place before 2026. Combining those figures with the recast six-month figures gives a different and wrong margin.
