## Question

## Answer

## Components

- c1: $132 million

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 11: after the Board approved an expansion of the program on April 30, 2026, cumulative pretax charges "are now estimated to be $350 million to $550 million, increased from $200 million to $300 million". No later document changes this estimate.

Step 2. Doc 11: the two ends of the latest range add to 350 + 550 = 900.

Step 3. Doc 11: the midpoint of the latest range is 900 / 2 = 450.

Step 4. Doc 9: the full-year 2025 reconciliation shows "Restructuring programs" charges of 13 before income taxes, and its note says these charges relate to the Strategic Growth and Productivity Program in 2025. No charges for the program appear before the fourth quarter of 2025.

Step 5. Doc 12: the six-month 2026 reconciliation shows program charges of 305 before income taxes, made up of operating-profit charges of 300 and 5 in non-service related postretirement costs.

Step 6. Doc 9 and Doc 12: pretax charges recorded through June 30, 2026 were 13 + 305 = 318.

Step 7. Doc 9, Doc 11 and Doc 12: the unrecorded part of the midpoint was 450 - 318 = 132.

Decoy: Doc 12's segment note gives charges of 300 for the six months, but those are only the charges inside operating profit; the pretax total for the period is 305.
