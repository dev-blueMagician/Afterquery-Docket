## Question

What was the 2024 segment operating profit margin of the Africa/Eurasia operations that Colgate moved into its Europe, Middle East & Africa segment? Give it as a percentage of their net sales, to two decimal places.

## Answer

19.68%

## Components

- c1: 19.68%

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 11: the realignment effective for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 combined "the Europe and Africa/Eurasia (excluding Russia and Belarus) reportable operating segments and the skin health business" into the new Europe, Middle East & Africa segment, and moved Russia and Belarus from Africa/Eurasia into Asia Pacific. So the Africa/Eurasia operations moved into the new segment are Africa/Eurasia without Russia and Belarus, and the recast Asia Pacific figures exceed the original ones by those of Russia and Belarus.

Step 2. Doc 9: under the old structure, Africa/Eurasia had 2024 net sales of 1,095 and operating profit of 253 while Asia Pacific had 2024 net sales of 2,858 and operating profit of 812 (dollars in millions).

Step 3. Doc 10: the recast Asia Pacific figures for 2024 were net sales of 3,074 and operating profit of 892.

Step 4. Doc 9 and Doc 10: Russia and Belarus net sales in 2024 were 3,074 - 2,858 = 216.

Step 5. Doc 9 and Doc 10: Russia and Belarus operating profit in 2024 was 892 - 812 = 80.

Step 6. Doc 9 and Doc 10: the moved operations had 2024 net sales of 1,095 - 216 = 879.

Step 7. Doc 9 and Doc 10: the moved operations had 2024 operating profit of 253 - 80 = 173.

Step 8. Doc 9 and Doc 10: their operating profit margin was 173 / 879 = 19.68% (to two decimal places).

Decoy: the whole Africa/Eurasia segment in Doc 9 earned 253 on net sales of 1,095 in 2024 but that total still includes Russia and Belarus, which went to Asia Pacific rather than to the new segment.
