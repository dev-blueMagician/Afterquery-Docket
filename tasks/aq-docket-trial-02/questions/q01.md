## Question

By what percentage did full-year net sales of the operations Colgate moved from its Africa/Eurasia segment into its Europe, Middle East & Africa segment change from 2024 to 2025? Give the sign, to two decimal places.

## Answer

+7.85%

## Components

- c1: +7.85%

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 11: the realignment effective for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 combined "the Europe and Africa/Eurasia (excluding Russia and Belarus) reportable operating segments and the skin health business" into the new Europe, Middle East & Africa segment, and moved Russia and Belarus from Africa/Eurasia into Asia Pacific. So the operations moved from Africa/Eurasia into the new segment are Africa/Eurasia without Russia and Belarus, and the recast Asia Pacific figures exceed the original ones by Russia and Belarus.

Step 2. Doc 9: under the old structure, full-year net sales of Africa/Eurasia were 1,172 in 2025 and 1,095 in 2024, and of Asia Pacific 2,814 in 2025 and 2,858 in 2024 (dollars in millions).

Step 3. Doc 10: the recast full-year net sales of Asia Pacific were 3,038 in 2025 and 3,074 in 2024.

Step 4. Doc 9 and Doc 10: Russia and Belarus net sales in 2025 were 3,038 - 2,814 = 224.

Step 5. Doc 9 and Doc 10: Russia and Belarus net sales in 2024 were 3,074 - 2,858 = 216.

Step 6. Doc 9 and Doc 10: the moved operations had 2025 net sales of 1,172 - 224 = 948.

Step 7. Doc 9 and Doc 10: the moved operations had 2024 net sales of 1,095 - 216 = 879.

Step 8. Doc 9 and Doc 10: the change from 2024 to 2025 was 948 - 879 = 69.

Step 9. Doc 9 and Doc 10: as a share of 2024 net sales, 69 / 879 = 7.85% (to two decimal places), an increase.

Decoy: Doc 9 reports a 7.0% net sales increase for the whole Africa/Eurasia segment, but that segment still includes Russia and Belarus, which went to Asia Pacific rather than to the new segment.
