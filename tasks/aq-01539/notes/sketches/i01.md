## Question

## Answer

3

- D03 (L62): "Kroll Settlement Administration, LLC is appointed as the Settlement Administrator"

- D01 (L66): "Fourteen actions on the motion and twelve potential tag-along actions are pending in this district."
- D01 (L67): "Even counting all actions in the District of Montana as a single action and all actions in the Eastern District of North Carolina as a single action, there still are 42 related actions pending in twelve districts."
