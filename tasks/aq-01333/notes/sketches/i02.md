## Question

HPE says its Networking segment's operating profit margin fell from 29.7% in fiscal Q1 2025 to 23.7% a year later. With the segment defined as in that comparison, what was Networking's operating profit margin for fiscal 2025 as a whole, to two decimal places?

## Answer

24.39%

## Evidence

- D01 (l.45, Q1 FY26 segment results; Doc 4): "with 23.7% operating profit margin, compared to 29.7% from the prior-year period"
- D01 (l.415, footnote 5; Doc 4): "transferring the Telco and Instant On businesses to Corporate Investments and Other from Networking"
- D01 (l.326, segment net revenue; Doc 4): "Networking $2,706 $2,722 $1,076"
- D01 (l.331, segment earnings before taxes; Doc 4): "Networking $640 $638 $320"
- D05 (l.464, nine-month segment net revenue; Doc 6): "Networking $8,289 $3,814"
- D05 (l.469, nine-month segment earnings before taxes; Doc 6): "Networking 1,858 956"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h02, recast carve-out with a printed same-label decoy (trial-02 q01 shape). The 29.7% is the recast Q1 FY25 figure (D01 l.374); the originally reported Q1 FY25 Intelligent Edge margin was 27.4% (D02 l.516), so the comparison uses the FY26 segment definitions. FY25 on that basis = (9M recast EBT 956 + Q4 recast 638) / (9M recast revenue 3,814 + Q4 recast 2,722) = 1,594 / 6,536.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Same series, right edition (recast vs original); State the rounding. Decoys: D06 l.539 FY25 Networking 23.3% (old basis, printed); D05 l.536 9M FY25 recast 25.1%; Q4 original mix 24.21%; Q3 original mix 24.03%; equal-weight quarterly margins 25.05%; revenue-weighted printed margins 24.37%.
Source risks: pipe-row quotes (form verbatim check unconfirmed). Removal estimates: without D05 (Q3 FY25 recast only there) 24.03-24.37%; without D01 (Q4 FY25 recast only there) 24.21-24.52%; at 2 dp none equals 24.39. D03 adds only Q2 FY25 recast, derivable from D05 9M minus D01 Q1 and D05 Q3.
Author calculation check and counting convention: 1,594 / 6,536 = 24.3880% (Fraction) -> 24.39 (2 dp); 1 dp would be 24.4, where some removal estimates collide.
Screen decision/actual preliminary feedback: pending local closed-book and blind screens; wording risk "defined as in that comparison" may be read as the segment-margin basis rather than the segment composition.
