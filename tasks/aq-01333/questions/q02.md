## Question

HPE says its Networking segment's operating profit margin fell from 29.7% in fiscal Q1 2025 to 23.7% a year later. On that same segment structure, what was Networking's operating profit margin for fiscal 2025 as a whole, to two decimal places?

## Answer

24.39%

## Components

- c1: 24.39%

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 4: Networking had "23.7% operating profit margin, compared to 29.7% from the prior-year period". Its segment note explains the structure behind that comparison: from fiscal 2026 HPE moved "the Telco and Instant On businesses to Corporate Investments and Other from Networking" and reflected the change in its segment information retrospectively.
Step 2. Doc 1: under the former structure the same quarter (three months ended January 31, 2025) showed an Intelligent Edge margin of 27.4%, so the comparison in Step 1 uses the new structure.
Step 3. Doc 6: on the new structure, Networking for the nine months ended July 31, 2025 had net revenue of 3,814 and earnings before taxes of 956 (dollars in millions).
Step 4. Doc 4: on the new structure, Networking for the three months ended October 31, 2025 had net revenue of 2,722 and earnings before taxes of 638.
Step 5. Doc 4 and Doc 6: fiscal 2025 net revenue = 3,814 + 2,722 = 6,536.
Step 6. Doc 4 and Doc 6: fiscal 2025 earnings before taxes = 956 + 638 = 1,594.
Step 7. Doc 4 and Doc 6: operating profit margin = 1,594 / 6,536 = 24.39% (to two decimal places).
Decoy (Doc 3): the fiscal 2025 Networking margin printed for the year ended October 31, 2025 is 23.3%. That figure is for the pre-2026 segment, which still included Telco and Instant On: 1,596 / 6,850 = 23.30%.
