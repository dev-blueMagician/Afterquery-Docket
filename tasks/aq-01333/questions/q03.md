## Question

On the day HPE closed its Juniper Networks acquisition, it had a forecast range for fiscal 2025 non-GAAP operating profit growth. How many percentage points above that range's midpoint did HPE's actual fiscal 2025 growth in that measure come in? Give one decimal place.

## Answer

9.3 percentage points

## Components

- c1: 9.3 percentage points

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 2: "HPE closed its acquisition of Juniper Networks Inc. on July 2, 2025." This release is dated September 3, 2025, so the fiscal 2025 growth range it prints (4% to 7%) was issued after the closing.
Step 2. Doc 1: this release, dated June 3, 2025, is the last one issued before the closing. Its fiscal 2025 outlook puts "non-GAAP operating profit(1)(4) growth to be negative 7% to 0%", so the midpoint lies below zero by 7 / 2 = 3.5 percentage points.
Step 3. Doc 3: non-GAAP earnings from operations were 3,353 for the year ended October 31, 2025 and 3,168 for the year ended October 31, 2024 (dollars in millions).
Step 4. Doc 3: the increase was 3,353 - 3,168 = 185.
Step 5. Doc 3: actual fiscal 2025 growth was 185 / 3,168 = 5.84%.
Step 6. Doc 1 and Doc 3: actual growth came in above the midpoint by 5.84 + 3.5 = 9.3 percentage points (to one decimal place).
Decoy (Doc 2): its own range of 4% to 7% was issued on September 3, 2025, after the closing, so it was not the forecast HPE had on the day of the closing.
