## Question

Measured at the midpoints, how much higher in percent was HPE's September 2026 full-year forecast for fiscal 2026 non-GAAP diluted net EPS than the full-year forecast it was guiding to when it finished selling its remaining H3C stake? Round to one decimal place.

## Answer

58.3%

## Components

- c1: 58.3%

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 6: "In May 2026, the Company sold the remaining equity interest in H3C."
Step 2. Doc 5: this release is dated June 1, 2026 and says the divestiture "was completed on May 28", so the full-year range it gives was published after the sale and was not yet the forecast HPE was guiding to at completion.
Step 3. Doc 4: this release, dated March 9, 2026, is the last one published before the sale. Its full-year fiscal 2026 non-GAAP diluted net EPS range is "$2.30 to $2.50", so the ends add to 2.30 + 2.50 = 4.80.
Step 4. Doc 4: the midpoint is 4.80 / 2 = 2.40.
Step 5. Doc 6: this release, dated September 2, 2026, gives the full-year range "$3.75 to $3.85", so the ends add to 3.75 + 3.85 = 7.60.
Step 6. Doc 6: the midpoint is 7.60 / 2 = 3.80.
Step 7. Doc 4 and Doc 6: the September midpoint is higher by 3.80 - 2.40 = 1.40.
Step 8. Doc 4 and Doc 6: relative to the earlier midpoint, 1.40 / 2.40 = 58.3% (to one decimal place).
Decoy (Doc 5): its own full-year range, $3.35 to $3.45, sits in the same release as the completion, but that release is dated June 1, 2026, after the sale, so the range was not yet in force.
