## Question

What is FLRA's FOIA fee minus NRC's for 1,400 paper pages after a one-hour search, for a nonprofit advocacy group willing to pay all fees?

## Answer

$65

## Evidence

- D01 (2411.13(c)(4), line 202): "except that the first 100 pages of duplication and the first two hours of search time shall be furnished without charge"
- D01 (2411.13(d)(4), line 206): "Twenty-five cents per page for paper-copy duplication of documents"
- D03 (9.37(a)(1), line 660): "the NRC will charge a requester the fees for the cost of printing or copying records at the rate of $0.20 per page"
- D03 (9.37(c)(4), lines 680-681): "except that the NRC will provide the first"
