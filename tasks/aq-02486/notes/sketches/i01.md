## Question

Absent fee waivers, what is ONCD's proposed FOIA charge minus the Access Board's, to the cent, for 225 photocopied pages sent to a news reporter?

## Answer

$12.50

## Evidence

- D05 (2200.9(b)(3), line 284): "Records will be photocopied at a rate of ten cents ($0.10) per page."
- D05 (2200.9(b)(3), line 284): "Duplication fees shall not be charged for the first 100 pages of copies unless the copies are requested for a commercial use."
- D07 (1120.10(c)(2), line 298): "Where photocopies are supplied, the agency will provide one copy per request at a cost of $0.20 per page."
- D07 (1120.10(d)(3)(i), lines 310-311): "Except for requesters seeking records for a commercial use, the agency will provide without charge:"
- D07 (1120.10(d)(4), line 313): "a total fee calculated under paragraph (c) of this section is $25.00 or less for any request, no fee will be charged."

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h1. Both proposals give a non-commercial requester 100 free pages, but only the Access
Board waives a remaining total of $25.00 or less; ONCD mentions $25 only as a notice threshold
(2200.9(d), line 286) and has no small-fee or collection-cost rule. ONCD: 125 x $0.10 = $12.50 charged.
Access Board: 125 x $0.20 = $25.00, which is "$25.00 or less", so nothing is charged. Gold +$12.50:
the agency with half the per-page rate charges more.

Ambiguity:
- -$12.50 (Access Board charges $25.00) -> fails D07 (d)(4) line 313 ("$25.00 or less", inclusive).
- $0 (ONCD waives too) -> D05 has no waiver; 2200.9(d) line 286 is notice only ("shall notify").
- -$22.50 (no free pages) -> both give a news reporter 100 free pages (D05 284; D07 310-311).
- +$22.50 (ONCD Privacy Act rule, all pages once 4 or more, D05 435) -> part 2201 covers individuals'
  Privacy Act requests for their own records, not a reporter's FOIA request.
- Fee waiver: ONCD 2200.10 and D07 (k) public-interest waivers -> excluded by "Absent fee waivers".
- Search/review: news media pay no search fee (D05 281; D07 291/305); review only for commercial use.
- Missed time limits: would bar news-media duplication fees (D05 314; D07 306); no premise of delay.
- D07 (d)(5) collection-cost rule (314): moot, Access Board's fee is already $0. ONCD has none.
- Old Access Board rule ($0.20, 1990) -> question says "proposed"; same rate anyway.
Necessity:
- D05 -> ONCD's $0.10 rate, 100 free pages, no waiver -> ONCD appears nowhere else -> charge unknown.
- D07 -> Access Board's $0.20 rate, 100 free pages, inclusive $25.00 waiver -> nowhere else -> charge unknown.
  Risk: the waiver is DOJ-template wording also in D02 (482-483); a solver without D07 who assumes the
  Access Board follows it at any rate <= $0.20 lands on the gold. Probe without D07 (concrete issue).
After clarification: the wording supplies neither agency's rate, free pages nor waiver; both documents stay needed.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: mentions it vs does it ($25 in both rules, waiver only in D07);
sign of a difference. Wrong routes recomputed (scratchpad i01_i02_calc.py): -12.50, 0, -22.50, +22.50.
Source risks: closed-book recall of the 1990 Access Board rule; template inference for D07 (above).
Author calculation: D05 284: (225 - 100) pages x $0.10/page = $12.50. D07 298, 311: (225 - 100) x $0.20
= $25.00; D07 313: $25.00 <= $25.00 -> $0. $12.50 - $0 = $12.50. Fractions, exact.
