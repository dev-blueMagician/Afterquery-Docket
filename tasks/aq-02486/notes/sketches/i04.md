## Question

For a nonprofit advocacy group's 5-hour-22-minute FOIA search, how many more minutes would count toward fees at the NRC than at the Peace Corps?

## Answer

45 minutes

## Evidence

- D03 (9.37(a)(2), line 661): "The NRC will charge the following hourly rates for search and review of agency records by NRC personnel, rounded up to the nearest full hour"
- D03 (9.37(c)(4), lines 680-681): "except that the NRC will provide the first"
- D02 (303.16(d)(1)(ii), line 462): "For each quarter hour spent by personnel searching for requested records"
- D02 (303.16(e)(3), line 478): "No search or review fees will be charged for a quarter-hour period unless more than half of that period is required for search or review."
- D02 (303.16(e)(4)(ii), line 481): "The first 2 hours of search."
