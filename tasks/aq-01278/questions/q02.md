## Question

Chubb said its North American commercial insurance net premiums written grew 2.8% in the quarter ended March 31, 2026. On that same basis, what was their year-over-year percentage change in the quarter ended June 30, 2026? Give the sign, to one decimal place.

## Answer

-1.3%

## Components

- c1: -1.3%

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 4 (highlights): "North America was up 4.1%, including growth of 8.3% in personal insurance and 2.8% in commercial insurance". The North America table comprises NA Commercial P&C Insurance, NA Personal P&C Insurance and NA Agricultural Insurance.
Step 2. Doc 4 (North America table, net premiums written, 2026 vs 2025): Total North America P&C 6,887 and 6,615; North America Personal 1,681 and 1,552. 6,887 - 1,681 = 5,206
Step 3. Doc 4: 6,615 - 1,552 = 5,063
Step 4. Doc 4: 5,206 - 5,063 = 143
Step 5. Doc 4: 143 / 5,063 = 2.8%, the stated growth, so the 2.8% covers North America outside personal insurance.
Step 6. Doc 4: the North America Commercial P&C Insurance segment alone (4,895 against 4,787) grew only 2.3%; adding North America Agricultural Insurance gives 4,895 + 311 = 5,206, so the stated basis is commercial plus agricultural.
Step 7. Doc 9 (North America table, quarter ended June 30): Total North America P&C 8,424 and 8,394; North America Personal 2,054 and 1,938. 8,424 - 2,054 = 6,370
Step 8. Doc 9: 8,394 - 1,938 = 6,456
Step 9. Doc 9: 6,370 - 6,456 = -86
Step 10. Doc 9: -86 / 6,456 = -1.3% (to one decimal place)
Step 11. Doc 9 and Doc 4: the second-quarter release's "North America Commercial was down 2.3%" measures the commercial segment without agriculture, the measure that grew 2.3% rather than 2.8% in the first quarter, so it is not the stated basis.
