## Question

By what percentage did Travelers' worldwide personal automobile net written premiums change from the second quarter of 2025 to the second quarter of 2026? Give the sign, to two decimal places.

## Answer

-10.41%

## Components

- c1: -10.41%

## Reasoning

Step 1. Doc 8 (Personal Insurance net written premiums table): the Automobile row sits under the Domestic heading and shows 1,858 for the second quarter of 2026 and 1,968 for the second quarter of 2025. The International row shows 178 for the 2025 quarter and nothing for 2026, and the text says the prior year quarter "included $178 million related to the Canadian operations divested by the Company in the first quarter of 2026". So worldwide personal automobile premiums in the 2026 quarter are the domestic 1,858.
Step 2. Doc 3 (Selected Statistics - Personal Insurance - Automobile): automobile net written premiums for 2Q2025 were 2,074, while the Net Written Premiums - Personal Insurance page shows domestic Automobile of 1,968 for 2Q2025. 2,074 - 1,968 = 106
Step 3. Doc 3 (Selected Statistics - Personal Insurance - Homeowners and Other): net written premiums for 2Q2025 were 2,592 against domestic Homeowners and Other of 2,520. 2,592 - 2,520 = 72
Step 4. Doc 3: 106 + 72 = 178, the International total for 2Q2025, so the product pages include the international business, and worldwide personal automobile premiums in that quarter were 2,074.
Step 5. Doc 8 and Doc 3: 1,858 - 2,074 = -216
Step 6. Doc 8 and Doc 3: -216 / 2,074 = -10.41% (to two decimal places)
