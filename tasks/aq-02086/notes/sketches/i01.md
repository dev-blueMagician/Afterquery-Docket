## Question

Under the consultation-stage impact assessment's assumptions, how many staff hours a year would the Ishak family's landlord spend preparing additional written summaries?

## Answer

2,400 hours

## Evidence

- D02 (L1047, Annex C glossary): "Resident and employee co-owned housing society, with 12,000 homes throughout Rochdale. This was Housing Association the Ishak family rented their social home from."
- D03 (L482, written summaries): "we use the upper bound of the Housemark estimate, that 4 repairs per property are carried out per year"
- D03 (L483, written summaries): "Using the EHS estimate that 40% of faults are classified as requiring an urgent repair as a proxy for the proportion of responsive repairs requiring a report to be provided to tenants"
- D03 (L484, written summaries): "we have assumed that only 50% of these reports will be an additional burden to the sector"
- D03 (L474, preparation costs): "we have assumed that it will take 15 minutes of staff time per report sent"
