## Question

On BRE's estimates, what would mitigating the most serious dampness cost per affected English home, on average, to the nearest £10?

## Answer

£3,590

## Evidence

- D02 (L1115, footnote 12): "According to BRE, dampness and mould hazards in homes in England cost the NHS £38 million annually and would cost a total of £269 million to mitigate"
- D03 (L624, savings to the NHS): "Dampness is also a common and costly issue for the NHS, with 75,000 homes in England suffering from the most serious dampness in 2018, leading to a £38m annual bill for the NHS"

## Sketch notes

Hook/mechanism: h4. The same BRE damp estimate appears in two documents, tied by its GBP 38m NHS bill: Doc 1's footnote gives the total mitigation cost (GBP 269m), Doc 2 the number of homes (75,000). 269,000,000 / 75,000 = 3,586.67 -> GBP 3,590.
Ambiguity: Doc 1 L275's 935,000 homes with "damp problems" (any severity, 2021) -> fails "most serious" -> GBP 287.70; Doc 1 L34's 177,000 social homes with damp and mould -> fails "most serious" and "English home" -> GBP 1,519.77; NHS bill per home GBP 38m / 75,000 = GBP 506.67 -> fails "mitigating"; Doc 1 L281 Regulator "<0.2% of social homes" with the most serious damp and mould -> no count, social only.
Necessity: Doc 1 -> GBP 269m (only mitigation cost in the bundle; grep "269", "mitigat") -> nothing else gives a remediation total. Doc 2 -> 75,000 homes (only count of most-serious-damp homes; grep "75,000") -> without it the only damp counts are 935,000 and 177,000.
After clarification: "most serious dampness" is Doc 2's term; the link to Doc 1's GBP 269m rests on the shared GBP 38m, which the question does not state.
Selected lever(s) and applicable tests: Linking across documents; Bury one list item late (footnote 12 at the end of Doc 1). Wrong routes: 288, 1,520, 507.
Source risks: a reviewer may doubt that the GBP 269m covers exactly the 75,000 homes (both are BRE, both GBP 38m); precision limited to GBP 10.
Author calculation: 269 / 0.075 = 3,586.666... -> GBP 3,590 to the nearest GBP 10.
