## Question

Expressed per post-split share, what was the midpoint of the GAAP diluted EPS guidance KLA had in force when its stock split took effect?

## Answer

$0.966

## Evidence

- D14 (L16, highlights): "On June 11, 2026, after the market close, the company effected a ten-for-one stock split of its common stock"
- D13 (L20, dateline): "MILPITAS, Calif., April 29, 2026"
- D13 (L38, guidance heading): "The following details our guidance for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 ending in June"
- D13 (L39, GAAP EPS guidance): "GAAP diluted EPS is expected to be in a range of $9.66 +/- $1.00"
