## Question

For its fiscal quarter ended June 30, 2026, by what percentage did KLA's non-GAAP gross profit exceed the gross profit implied by multiplying the midpoints of the revenue and non-GAAP gross margin guidance it gave for that quarter? Round to one decimal place.

## Answer

3.3%

## Evidence

- D13 (L38, guidance heading): "The following details our guidance for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 ending in June"
- D13 (L39, revenue guidance): "Total revenues are expected to be in a range of $3.575 billion +/- $200 million"
- D13 (L39, non-GAAP gross margin guidance): "Non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be in a range of 61.75% +/- 1.00%"
- D14 (L101, statement of operations, three months ended June 30, 2026 first column): "Total revenues 3,657,556 3,174,741 13,579,476 12,156,162"
- D14 (L104, statement of operations): "Costs of revenues 1,413,108 1,207,286 5,255,060 4,751,867"
- D14 (L224, pre-tax impact table, three months ended June 30, 2026): "Costs of revenues $ 36,200"
